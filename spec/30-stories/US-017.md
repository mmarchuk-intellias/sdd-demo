# US-017 · EVSE allocated shortly before the slot and sent to the driver

As a charging driver, I want a specific EVSE assigned to my booking only shortly before my slot, and its EVSE and stall
sent to me, so that I drive straight to an EVSE that is free and suits my vehicle when I arrive.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Allocation at the site's allocation offset, with a notice to the charging driver
  Given a confirmed booking at a site
  When the site's configured allocation offset before the slot is reached
  Then the booking is allocated to one free EVSE whose connector the vehicle can use
  And the charging driver gets a message on their channel naming the EVSE and the stall

Scenario: S2 (failure) No EVSE is assigned at booking time
  Given a booking was confirmed well before its slot
  When the booker or the charging driver views the booking before the site's allocation offset
  Then the booking shows no EVSE
  And no EVSE has an allocation for that booking, so it counts only against the site's pool
  And no message naming an EVSE has been sent for it

Scenario: S3 (failure) No free compatible EVSE at allocation time
  Given no EVSE at the site whose connector the vehicle can use is free when the allocation offset is reached
  When the platform tries to allocate the booking
  Then no EVSE is allocated and no message naming an EVSE is sent
  And the booking is handled as a full site (JS-019)

Scenario: S4 (happy path) The lowest-power EVSE that meets the vehicle's maximum power
  Given the free compatible EVSEs at a site have different maximum powers, and some of them meet the vehicle's maximum DC charging power
  When the booking is allocated
  Then it gets the lowest-power EVSE among those that meet the vehicle's maximum DC charging power

Scenario: S5 (edge) No free compatible EVSE meets the vehicle's maximum power
  Given every free compatible EVSE at the site has a maximum power below the vehicle's maximum DC charging power
  When the booking is allocated
  Then it gets the highest-power free compatible EVSE

Scenario: S6 (edge) The notice goes to the charging driver when the booker is someone else
  Given a booking whose booker and charging driver are different numbers
  When the booking is allocated, and later reallocated to another EVSE
  Then each time the charging driver gets a message naming the EVSE and the stall

## Notes

- Depends on: A-002 (allocation offset about T-15, configured per site), A-008 and Q-034 (the driver's channel; WhatsApp
  is unconfirmed, U-10), Q-050 (accessible EVSEs may need their own allocation rule).
- "Free" comes from live status at pattern A and B sites (REQ-104, EP-05; Q-012 for JFK) and from the attendant view at
  LIC (REQ-158, US-031). Stale status is REQ-168 (EP-05).
- No two overlapping allocations on one EVSE: US-011 (REQ-007). Reallocation when the EVSE is occupied: JS-018.
