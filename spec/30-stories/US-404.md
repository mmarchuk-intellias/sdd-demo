# US-404 · Raw pilot export kept read-only and for named people only

As the Amperlane CTO, I want the raw pilot export stored read-only, open only to the people I name, and deleted when
the retention period counsel sets for it ends, so that a file full of driver names and phone numbers cannot be
altered, spread or kept longer than Amperlane is allowed to keep it.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A named person reads the export
  Given the raw pilot export is stored and Priya Nair is named as allowed to read it
  When she opens it
  Then she can read it

Scenario: S2 (failure) A person who is not named tries to open it
  Given an engineer who holds a production administrator role but is not named for the raw pilot export
  When the engineer tries to open it, through the platform or directly in its storage
  Then access is refused

Scenario: S3 (failure) A named person tries to change it
  Given a person named for the raw pilot export
  When that person tries to change, overwrite or delete any part of it before its retention period ends
  Then the change is refused
  And the export is unchanged

Scenario: S4 (edge case) The retention period ends
  Given counsel has set a retention period for the raw pilot export
  When that period ends
  Then the export is deleted
  And the deletion is recorded with its time
  And nobody, including the people named for it, can open it afterwards

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Counsel has not set a period yet
  Given counsel has not yet set a retention period for the raw pilot export
  When the platform's retention check runs
  Then the export is not deleted
  And the data register shows its retention period as not set

  (A-058, Q-022).
