# US-013 · Vehicle data only with the fleet's authorisation

As a fleet manager, I want Amperlane to read data from my fleet's vehicles only after my fleet has recorded its
authorisation as account owner, and to stop as soon as we revoke it, so that our vehicle data is never used without the
account owner's consent.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Data is read for an authorised fleet
  Given a fleet's authorisation as account owner is recorded
  And a vehicle data source is connected for that fleet
  When the platform reads vehicle data
  Then it reads the battery level and charging state of that fleet's vehicles
  And it reads no vehicle location unless the vehicle's charging driver has also opted in (REQ-255)

Scenario: S2 (failure) No authorisation, no reading
  Given a vehicle data source is connected for a fleet whose authorisation is not recorded
  When readings for the fleet's vehicles would be due
  Then no data is read for them
  And their bookings proceed without vehicle data

Scenario: S3 (failure) Revoking stops reading at once
  Given a fleet's authorisation is recorded and its vehicles' data is being read
  When the fleet revokes its authorisation
  Then no data is read for its vehicles from the moment of revocation
  And their confirmed bookings stay confirmed

Scenario: S4 (edge) A vehicle that changes fleet follows the new fleet's authorisation
  Given a vehicle is transferred from an authorised fleet to a fleet whose authorisation is not recorded
  When readings for that vehicle would be due
  Then no data is read for it

## Notes

- R7: no vehicle data source is confirmed for the MVP. Geotab access is unconfirmed (U-9, Q-014) and its integration
  is I (REQ-075); Tesla Fleet API and Smartcar are parked (REQ-311, REQ-312, CF-004, Q-015). The story is testable
  against any connected source, a simulated one included.
- Depends on: A-024 (vehicle state on the fleet's authorisation; location also needs the driver's opt-in, REQ-255),
  Q-016 (how the fleet authorises access).
- S2 and S3 "bookings proceed" relies on REQ-019 (no vehicle data needed to book, EP-06).
- Step 3.1 red-team pass: S1 no longer says "reads data" and "shows each reading with its time". The first let a fleet's
  authorisation alone unlock vehicle location, which contradicts REQ-255 and A-024 (location also needs the driver's
  opt-in); the second was behaviour no linked requirement states.
- Who records the fleet's authorisation (fleet manager in the fleet portal, or ops from the fleet agreement) is a
  design detail for Step 3.3.
