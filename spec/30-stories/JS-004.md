# JS-004 · Next free slot offered and held when the slot is full

When the slot I picked has just been taken, I want to get the next free compatible slot at the same site offered and
held for me, so I can book it with one more confirmation instead of searching again between rides.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The next free compatible slot is offered and held
  Given a driver's booking attempt for a slot at a site fails because the slot is full
  And a later slot at the same site has room for the driver's vehicle and the chosen duration
  When the platform answers the attempt
  Then the answer offers the next free compatible slot at that site, for the same vehicle and duration
  And no start time between the requested one and the offered one would have accepted a hold for that vehicle and duration
  And that slot is held for the driver for the site's configured hold time
  When the driver confirms within that time
  Then the booking is confirmed for the offered slot

Scenario: S2 (failure) The offer is not confirmed in time
  Given the driver was offered the next free compatible slot and it is held for the driver
  When the site's configured hold time runs out without a confirmation
  Then the hold on the offered slot expires
  And its capacity is offered to other bookers again

Scenario: S3 (failure) A free slot the vehicle cannot use is not offered
  Given the next slot with room at the site has room only on EVSEs whose connectors the driver's vehicle cannot use
  When the driver's attempt fails because the requested slot is full
  Then that slot is not offered
  And the offer is the next slot with room on an EVSE the vehicle can use

Scenario: S4 (failure) No later slot at the site has room
  Given no later slot at the site has room for the driver's vehicle and duration
  When the driver's attempt fails because the requested slot is full
  Then the answer says the slot is full
  And nothing is held for the driver

Scenario: S5 (edge) Two drivers lose the same slot
  Given two drivers' attempts for the same full slot are received one after the other
  And the next free compatible slot has room for one more booking only
  When both attempts are answered
  Then the driver whose attempt was received first is offered that slot and it is held for that driver
  And the other driver is offered the next free compatible slot after it, or told that the slot is full

## Notes

- Depends on: A-001 (hold time), A-034 (every attempt that loses a contended slot gets an alternative or an explicit
  "full" answer). Order of decision: REQ-102 (JS-010).
- How far ahead the next free compatible slot is searched (same day, within the site's bookable hours) is a design
  detail for Step 3.3. Offers at other sites exist only when the site is full at station-hold time or at the
  pre-arrival check (REQ-148 v2, JS-019), not here.
