# US-321 · Fleet pays its invoice through Stripe without card data reaching Amperlane

As a fleet finance contact, I want to pay the monthly invoice through Stripe and enter card details only on Stripe's
own pages, so that the fleet keeps paying Amperlane the way it does today and its card details are never held by
Amperlane.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Paying the invoice
  Given an issued fleet invoice submitted to Stripe
  When the fleet finance contact opens the invoice's payment page and pays
  Then the payment is collected by Stripe
  And the invoice is shown as paid in the platform once Stripe reports the payment

Scenario: S2 (failure) A declined payment
  Given an issued fleet invoice
  When the fleet's card is declined on Stripe's page
  Then the invoice stays unpaid in the platform
  And the fleet can pay again from the same payment page

Scenario: S3 (failure) Card numbers never reach Amperlane
  Given a fleet finance contact entering card details to pay an invoice
  When the platform's requests, logs, database and error reports are inspected
  Then no card number appears in any of them
  And every card field the fleet finance contact uses is served by Stripe

Scenario: S4 (edge case) Paying during the platform's maintenance window
  Given the platform is in its planned maintenance window
  When the fleet pays its invoice on Stripe's page
  Then the payment is collected by Stripe
  And the invoice is shown as paid once the platform is back

## Notes

- Fleets pay Amperlane through Stripe Billing today (CTX-051, integrations.md I-12); A-007 keeps Stripe as the provider.
  Keeping Stripe replaceable (REQ-111) is an enabler for Step 3.3.
- Only fleet invoice payments are covered. Payouts to station operators and Stripe Connect are parked (Q-001; REQ-278).
- Which payment methods Stripe offers the fleet is a Stripe setting decided in design. The maintenance window is
  constitution N5 (A-028); catching up a missed payment update is JS-322 (REQ-110).
