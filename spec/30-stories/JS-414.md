# JS-414 · Settle a disagreement over caps or terms from the change history

When a station operator, a fleet or Amperlane disagree about which capacity rule, fleet policy, partner term, user role
or EVSE setting applied at a given time, I want every change to it recorded with who made it, when, and its before and
after values, so I can settle the disagreement from the record instead of from memory.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A station operator lowers a cap
  Given Kinship Charging lowers the cap of the weekday afternoon capacity rule at Gowanus in the station portal
  When the change is saved
  Then the change is recorded with the Kinship user, the time, the old cap and the new cap
  And ops can read which cap applied at any earlier time

Scenario: S2 (failure) Ops changes a rule on behalf of a station operator
  Given an ops person changes the capacity rule at JFK on behalf of Jamaica Bay Energy
  When the change is saved
  Then the change is recorded with the ops person as actor, not the station operator

Scenario: S3 (failure) Someone tries to alter the change record
  Given a recorded change to a fleet policy
  When any user tries to edit or delete that record
  Then the attempt is refused
  And the record is unchanged

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A value changed twice in one day
  Given the grace period configured for LIC is changed twice on one day
  When ops looks at the history for that day
  Then both changes are shown with before and after values
  And the value that applied at any time of that day can be read from them

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A per-EVSE setting
  Given an EVSE at Gowanus is marked as reservable after a firmware fix
  When the setting is saved
  Then the change is recorded with the actor, the time and the before and after values of the setting

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A user's role is changed
  Given an ops person changes the role of a Kinship Charging user of the station portal
  When the change is saved
  Then the change is recorded with the ops person, the time, the user's role before and the role after

## Notes

- Station operators change caps themselves (CTX-065). What happens to bookings already confirmed above a lowered cap is A-018,
  not this story. Partner terms include the per-site grace period (A-003).
- The record is part of the audit trail (constitution N17); role changes are also security events (JS-413).
- Red team: S6 covers user roles, the one REQ-262 item no scenario named. Partner terms are covered by the per-site
  grace period (S4).
