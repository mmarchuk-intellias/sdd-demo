When a booked driver has not arrived by the end of the site's grace period, I want to have the booking released
automatically, with nobody phoning anyone, so I can give the EVSE back to walk-ups straight away.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · No arrival by the end of grace releases the booking
  Given a booking at a site with the default grace period of 10 minutes
  And no arrival is recorded by 10 minutes after the booking start
  When the grace period ends
  Then the booking is released as a no-show with no human action
  And its capacity returns to the slot calendar
  And the booking's history shows the release with the system as actor and its reason code

Scenario: S2 · happy path · An arrival within grace keeps the booking
  Given arrival was recorded on a booking before the end of its grace period
  When the grace period ends
  Then the booking is not released

Scenario: S3 · failure · A late grace timer after an arrival does not release
  Given arrival was recorded on a booking before the end of its grace period
  And the grace timer fires late, for example after planned maintenance
  When the timer fires
  Then the booking is not released

Scenario: S4 · failure · A late grace timer with no arrival releases once and goes to ops review
  Given no arrival was recorded on a booking by the end of its grace period
  And the grace timer fires late
  When the timer fires
  Then the booking is released once
  And no no-show fee is created automatically, and the booking appears in the ops exception queue (JS-110)

Scenario: S5 · edge case · A site with its own grace period
  Given a site configured with a grace period other than 10 minutes
  When a booking at that site has no arrival by the end of that period
  Then the booking is released at the end of that site's grace period
  And its no-show and fee decisions use that site's value

Scenario: S6 · edge case · A grace period changed after confirmation
  Given a booking was confirmed before the site's grace period was changed
  When the booking's grace period ends with no arrival
  Then the booking is released at the end of the grace period in force when it was confirmed

Scenario: S7 · edge case · An ops extension delays the release
  Given ops extended the booking's grace period with a reason code (US-108)
  When the original grace period ends with no arrival
  Then the booking is not released until the extended grace period ends

## Notes

- A-003, Q-004: the grace period is stored per site. A-028 and constitution N5: timers may fire late during
  maintenance but are never lost and take effect once (REQ-133, FT-14.2).
- S4 applies REQ-180 (JS-110). S6 follows REQ-146 (FT-01.7): earlier bookings keep their values.
- Telling site staff to clear the stall is REQ-035 (FT-02.3). The same rule applies at all supply patterns (REQ-028).
