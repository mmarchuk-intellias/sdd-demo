# US-032 · Attendant sees who is due and confirms arrivals

As an attendant at LIC Garage Depot, I want to see which driver is due at which EVSE and when, and confirm a driver's arrival, so that
I mark the right stall and a driver who has arrived is not released as a no-show.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The attendant sees the drivers due
  Given bookings at LIC Garage Depot are allocated for the current day
  When the attendant opens the attendant view
  Then it lists each allocated booking with its EVSE, stall, start time and the driver due, showing only the driver data allowed for site staff

Scenario: S2 (happy path) The attendant confirms an arrival
  Given a driver due at an EVSE arrives before the end of the booking's grace period
  When the attendant confirms the driver's arrival in the attendant view
  Then the booking's arrival is recorded with its time and the attendant as its source
  And the booking is not released when grace ends

Scenario: S3 (failure) An arrival confirmed after the booking was released
  Given a booking at LIC Garage Depot was already released as a no-show after grace
  When the attendant confirms the driver's arrival
  Then the booking stays recorded as a no-show
  And the arrival time is stored with it as a late arrival

Scenario: S4 (failure) The attendant cannot confirm an arrival at another site
  Given the attendant is signed in for LIC Garage Depot
  When the attendant tries to confirm an arrival for a booking at another site
  Then the confirmation is refused

Scenario: S5 (edge) A reallocation updates the list
  Given a booking in the attendant's list is reallocated to another EVSE
  When the attendant views the list
  Then the booking is shown at its new EVSE and stall

## Notes

- Depends on: A-021, Q-010, Q-049 and A-054 (Hunters Point Garages provides the attendant's device); R7: U-13 (the
  attendant's adoption is unconfirmed). Q-048 decides which driver data site staff see; A-053 covers it until it is
  answered (the plate and the charging driver's first name only, never the phone number). S1 holds under any answer,
  since it shows "only the driver data allowed for site staff" (constitution R6). REQ-258 (EP-13) limits the view to
  the current day at the site.
- S2 relies on REQ-177 (no release after arrival, EP-03); S3 on REQ-037 (late arrival, EP-03).
- The window in which an attendant's confirmation counts as arrival (REQ-043 and REQ-174 set one for the QR scan and
  the CSMS session start only) is a design detail for Step 3.3.
