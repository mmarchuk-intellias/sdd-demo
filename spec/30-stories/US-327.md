# US-327 · Monthly completed bookings for NYC, with pilot data marked

As Amperlane's CEO, I want completed bookings reported per month for NYC sites under the configured definition, with
migrated pilot data marked and shown separately, so that I can track the climb towards 10,000 completed bookings a
month by June 2027 on figures that hold up in front of investors.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Monthly figures for NYC
  Given completed bookings at NYC sites over several months
  When the CEO opens the completed-bookings report
  Then it shows one figure per month, counted under the configured definition

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Pilot data marked, with and without
  Given months that include migrated pilot bookings
  When the CEO opens the report
  Then pilot bookings are marked as pilot data and shown separately from platform bookings
  And the report can be shown with or without them

Scenario: S3 (failure) Sites outside NYC are not counted
  Given completed bookings at a site outside NYC
  When the report is shown
  Then they are not in any figure

Scenario: S4 (failure) Pilot statuses are not read with their old meaning
  Given a migrated pilot booking recorded as "cancelled" that the migration re-classified as a no-show
  When the report is shown
  Then the booking is treated as a no-show, under the same status definitions as platform data

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Month boundaries in the site's local time
  Given a booking that starts late on the last evening of a month in the site's time zone, when it is already the next month in UTC
  When the report is shown
  Then the booking counts in that month

Scenario: S6 (edge case) The definition changes
  Given ops has changed the completed-booking definition during the current month
  When the CEO opens the report
  Then the current month is counted under the new definition
  And every month's figure states the definition it was counted under

## Notes

- The 10,000-a-month target is NYC only (CTX-138; constitution N12).
- Depends on Q-017 (definition, via REQ-224 and US-326), A-026 (migration and re-classification, REQ-228), CF-018 and
  Q-029 (the pilot figures are uncleaned).
- Whether figures already reported for past months are recounted after a definition change, or stay frozen under the
  definition then in force (figures already given to investors), is Q-054, a client decision that depends on Q-017.
  The red team rewrote S6 so it no longer decides it: it fixes only the current month and requires each figure to
  state its definition, which holds under either answer.
