# US-400 · Pilot history queryable with new bookings

As the Amperlane CTO, I want the pilot history since October 2025 (bookings, no-shows, arrival times, drivers and the
EVSE each booking used) to live in the platform's own data model, linked to the same driver, fleet, site and EVSE
records as new bookings, so that the only real dataset on how fleet drivers behave can be analysed together with live
bookings and put in the Series A data room, instead of sitting in a CSV dump.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A pilot driver's history and new bookings sit under one record
  Given the pilot history has been migrated
  And a Borough Green driver booked at Gowanus in the Amperlane Concierge and has booked again in the platform since launch
  When the Amperlane CTO queries that driver's bookings
  Then the pilot bookings and the platform bookings are returned together in one result, under the same driver, fleet
  and site records
  And each pilot booking shows its slot, its outcome, its arrival time where the pilot recorded one, and its EVSE
  And that EVSE is the same EVSE record that platform bookings at Gowanus are allocated to

Scenario: S2 (failure) A pilot booking names a site the platform does not know
  Given a pilot booking whose site matches none of Gowanus, JFK and LIC
  When the migration runs
  Then the booking is not attached to a guessed site
  And it is on the migration's rejected list with the reason

Scenario: S3 (failure) A pilot booking sits in a Sheet column that maps to no EVSE
  Given a pilot booking placed in a column of the site's pilot Google Sheet that maps to no EVSE in the platform
  When the migration runs
  Then the booking is not linked to an invented EVSE
  And it is on the migration's rejected list with the reason

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A pilot booking with no EVSE recorded
  Given a pilot booking that exists in Airtable but has no cell in the site's pilot Google Sheet
  When the migration runs
  Then the booking is loaded with its driver, fleet, site and slot
  And its EVSE is shown as not recorded rather than filled in
  And it is in the migration's conflict list as held only in Airtable (US-403 S2)

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A pilot driver is registered after the migration ran
  Given a pilot driver whose mobile number was not registered to any fleet when the migration ran
  When the driver's fleet registers that number in the platform
  Then the driver's pilot bookings and later platform bookings are returned under the same driver record

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A driver who drove for both pilot fleets
  Given a pilot driver whose Airtable bookings name Borough Green for some and Five Boroughs for others
  When the history is queried for one fleet
  Then only the bookings Airtable records for that fleet are returned, under the same driver record

Scenario: S7 (happy path) One EVSE's pilot and platform bookings together
  Given the pilot history has been migrated
  And an EVSE at Gowanus that pilot bookings were mapped to and platform bookings have since been allocated to
  When the Amperlane CTO queries that EVSE's bookings for a range of months that spans the launch
  Then the pilot bookings and the platform bookings on that EVSE are returned together in one result

Scenario: S8 (failure) The early, messy months are not left out
  Given pilot bookings from October 2025 to January 2026 whose statuses use the early status values
  When the migration runs
  Then each of them is loaded or on the rejected list with its reason, like every other record
  And none is left out for being early (they carry the data-quality flag, US-402)

## Notes

- Depends on A-026 (Airtable and the Sheets since October 2025; Stripe Billing and Twilio logs are REQ-314, parked V)
  and Q-020 (migration scope). A pilot Sheet column is mapped to an EVSE by A-004 ("one port = one EVSE = one
  stall" at the pilot sites; Q-013). A driver is identified by the registered mobile number (A-006) and may drive for several
  fleets (A-016).
- Which outcome a pilot booking gets after re-classification is US-402; loading or rejecting every record, and the
  reconciliation of counts, is US-401. Reporting with pilot data marked separately is REQ-225 (EP-11).
- Red team: "queryable" could be met by a separate pilot table joined only by name. S1 and S5 require the same
  driver record for pilot and platform bookings; S1 and S7 the same EVSE record. S8 stops the migration from starting
  in February 2026, where the data becomes decent (CTX-054), instead of October 2025 (REQ-096).
- The pilot records the fleet per booking, not the vehicle (CTX-042), so S6 separates fleets by the fleet Airtable
  records.
