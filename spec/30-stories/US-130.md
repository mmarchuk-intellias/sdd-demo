As Amperlane ops, I want to place a maintenance hold that takes EVSEs or capacity at a site out of booking for a time
window, with bookings on a held EVSE rebooked as on a fault, so that planned work never sends a driver to an EVSE that
is out of service and never counts as a no-show.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · Hold an EVSE for a window
  Given EVSE A at a site has no bookings in a time window
  When an ops user places a maintenance hold on EVSE A for that window, with a reason code
  Then no booking is allocated to EVSE A in that window
  And EVSE A does not count as capacity for new bookings in that window
  And the hold is recorded with its window, the ops user, the reason code and the time

Scenario: S2 · happy path · Allocated bookings are rebooked
  Given a booking is allocated to EVSE A inside the window
  When an ops user places the maintenance hold on EVSE A
  Then the booking is rebooked as on a charger fault, with the reason code 'moved — maintenance'
  And no no-show is recorded for it

Scenario: S3 · failure · A booking that cannot be placed
  Given a booking allocated to EVSE A inside the window has no other compatible EVSE free at the site
  When the maintenance hold is placed
  Then its driver gets alternatives and the booking appears in the ops exception queue, as on a fault

Scenario: S4 · failure · Confirmed bookings that no longer fit
  Given confirmed bookings in the window are not yet allocated
  When the maintenance hold is placed
  Then each one that no longer fits the site's remaining compatible EVSEs is handled as unplaced before its allocation time, as on a fault

Scenario: S5 · failure · An invalid hold is refused
  Given an ops user enters a maintenance hold whose window ends before it starts, or has no reason code
  When the ops user places it
  Then the hold is refused

Scenario: S6 · edge case · A capacity hold without a named EVSE
  Given ops placed a maintenance hold on capacity, not on a named EVSE, at a site for a window
  When a driver books a slot in that window
  Then the held capacity counts against the site's capacity like a booking

Scenario: S7 · edge case · The hold ends
  Given a maintenance hold on EVSE A ends at the end of its window
  When the window has ended
  Then EVSE A is back in the pool for new allocations

## Notes

- A-018, Q-035: a maintenance hold takes EVSEs out physically, so affected bookings are rebooked as on a fault (A-017,
  JS-119). Maintenance is kept out of no-show figures (CTX-036, REQ-092).
- The station operator's notice is REQ-051 (FT-09.2). Fault return-to-service rules (JS-118) do not apply to a
  maintenance hold, which ends with its window.
