# US-229 · Messages only after the driver agrees; STOP ends them at once

As a driver, I want Amperlane to message me only once I have agreed to booking messages, and to stop on a channel
the moment I reply STOP there, so that I decide what reaches my phone.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Agreement is recorded
  Given a driver's agreement to booking messages is recorded with its time and how it was given
  When the driver's booking is confirmed
  Then the driver receives the confirmation on their channel

Scenario: S2 (failure) No agreement is recorded
  Given a registered driver with no recorded agreement to booking messages
  When a booking is confirmed with that driver as charging driver
  Then the booking is made
  And no SMS or WhatsApp message is sent to the driver

Scenario: S3 (failure) The driver replies STOP by SMS
  Given a driver who agreed to booking messages on SMS and WhatsApp has an upcoming booking
  When the driver replies STOP by SMS
  Then no further SMS is sent to the driver from that moment, including the booking's reminder
  And messages on WhatsApp continue

Scenario: S4 (failure) A message is queued when STOP arrives
  Given a reminder for a driver is about to be sent by SMS
  When the driver's STOP by SMS is received before the reminder is handed to the messaging provider
  Then the reminder is not sent by SMS

Scenario: S5 (edge case) STOP in lower case
  Given a driver who agreed to booking messages
  When the driver replies "stop"
  Then it is treated as STOP

Scenario: S6 (edge case) The agreement record
  Given a driver's agreement to booking messages was recorded
  When ops looks at the driver's record
  Then it shows when the agreement was recorded and how it was given

## Notes

- US carrier rules for application-to-person messaging; Q-047 covers the Twilio sender registration.
- How the agreement is given and recorded before the first message ("how it was given" has no agreed values), whether
  a pilot driver's past use of the pilot number counts, and whether the rule covers booker-only numbers (REQ-088 sends
  them confirmations) are Q-056, a client decision. The AC hold under any answer: they fix only that the time and
  form are recorded and that nothing is sent without them.
- How a driver who replied STOP starts receiving messages again is a design detail for Step 3.3 (subject to Q-056).
- WhatsApp: Q-034, A-008. A driver who stopped every channel still has a valid booking; undelivered time-critical
  messages are listed for ops (REQ-219, JS-226).
