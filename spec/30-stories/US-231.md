# US-231 · Directions in the driver's own navigation app and one tap back to Uber or Lyft

As a rideshare driver, I want to open directions to the site in my own navigation app from my booking, and get back
to the Uber Driver or Lyft Driver app with one tap, so that charging fits between rides without retyping an address
or hunting for apps.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Directions to the site
  Given a driver has a booking at Gowanus open in the driver page
  When the driver taps directions
  Then the phone's own navigation app opens with Gowanus as the destination

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Back to Uber or Lyft
  Given a driver is on the driver page
  When the driver taps the control for the Uber Driver app, or the one for the Lyft Driver app
  Then that driver app opens with that one tap, not the Uber or Lyft rider app

Scenario: S3 (failure) The driver has not opted in to location
  Given a driver who has not opted in to location
  When the driver taps directions
  Then the navigation app opens with the site as the destination
  And the platform reads and stores no location of the driver

Scenario: S4 (failure) Nothing is shared with Uber or Lyft
  Given a driver taps the control for the Uber Driver app or the Lyft Driver app
  When that app opens
  Then the link that opened it carries no booking, vehicle or driver data
  And the platform receives no data back from Uber or Lyft

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The booking was rebooked to another site
  Given a driver's booking was rebooked from Gowanus to JFK
  When the driver taps directions on that booking
  Then the navigation app opens with JFK as the destination

## Notes

- Deep links only in the MVP (CF-005, CTX-098); trip and vehicle data from Uber and Lyft is REQ-322 (V). Estimated
  arrival time is REQ-065 (I). Maps provider for in-page maps: A-030, Q-027, CF-020.
- What the driver sees when the navigation app, the Uber Driver app or the Lyft Driver app is not installed is a
  design detail for Step 3.3.
- S3: location is used only with the driver's opt-in (A-024, constitution N18).
