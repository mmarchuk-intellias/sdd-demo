# US-317 · A fleet's owed, paid and outstanding at any past date

As Amperlane finance, I want to see for any fleet and any past date what it owed, had paid and had outstanding, so
that I can answer a fleet's question about its account, or check a month end, from the ledger alone.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A fleet's position at a past date
  Given a fleet with charges, payments and a reversal over several months
  When a finance user asks for the fleet's position at a past date
  Then the platform shows what the fleet owed, had paid and had outstanding at the end of that day
  And each figure equals the sum of the fleet's ledger entries recorded up to the end of that day

Scenario: S2 (failure) A later correction does not rewrite the past
  Given a fee reversed after a past date
  When the fleet's position at that past date is shown
  Then it is the same as it was before the reversal was recorded

Scenario: S3 (failure) A future date is refused
  When a finance user asks for a fleet's position at a date in the future
  Then no figures are shown and the user is told that only past and current dates are available

Scenario: S4 (failure) Other organisations cannot see the fleet's position
  When a station operator, or a fleet manager of another fleet, asks for the fleet's position
  Then the request is refused

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A date before the fleet's first entry
  Given a fleet whose first ledger entry is later than the date asked for
  When a finance user asks for its position at that date
  Then owed, paid and outstanding are all zero

## Notes

- Fleet receivables exist in every business model (CF-024, KYIV reason of REQ-086). The cash gap and per-fleet credit
  limits are parked (REQ-280, REQ-281; Q-021).
- "End of that day" is Eastern time (constitution, spec conventions row 11).
- The double-entry structure that makes S1 hold without stored balances is REQ-085 (enabler, Step 3.3).
- Whether a fleet manager sees its own fleet's position is not stated here; accrued charges for the fleet are REQ-208 (I).
