# US-022 · Reservable flag per EVSE set by ops

As a member of Amperlane ops, I want to set each EVSE's reservable flag myself without a software release, so that EVSEs start
getting charger reservations as soon as their firmware is fixed, and stop at once if a charger starts rejecting them.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Turning the flag on
  Given an EVSE at Gowanus FastHub is flagged as not reservable
  When ops sets its reservable flag in the ops console
  Then every booking on that EVSE whose station-hold offset is reached after the change gets a charger reservation, including bookings allocated before the change
  And no software release took place
  And the audit trail records the change with ops as actor and its time

Scenario: S2 (failure) Turning the flag off
  Given an EVSE at Gowanus FastHub is flagged as reservable and its charger has started rejecting reservations
  When ops clears its reservable flag
  Then every booking on that EVSE whose station-hold offset is reached after the change gets a station-hold request to site staff instead of a charger reservation, including bookings allocated before the change

Scenario: S3 (failure) Only ops can change the flag
  Given a station operator or a fleet manager is signed in
  When that user tries to change an EVSE's reservable flag
  Then the change is refused
  And the flag stays as it was

Scenario: S4 (edge) The flag is per EVSE, not per site
  Given two EVSEs at the same site with the same reservable flag
  When ops changes the flag of one of them
  Then the flag of the other is unchanged

## Notes

- Depends on: Q-009 (which chargers are on the reservation list and when the firmware is fixed), Q-013 and A-004
  (reservations work on "10 of 16 chargers", CTX-060: whether that is per charger or per EVSE).
- The flag in force when a booking's station-hold offset is reached decides the method (REQ-024, REQ-026); S1 and S2
  say so, because "bookings allocated afterwards" would have let a booking allocated just before the change keep a
  method its charger now rejects (Step 3.1 red-team pass).
- The flag decides between US-021 (charger reservation) and US-025 (site staff). Audit of per-EVSE settings with
  before-and-after values is REQ-262 (EP-13).
