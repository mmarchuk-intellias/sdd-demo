As a driver, I want to record my arrival by scanning the QR code posted at an EVSE of my booking's site, so that the
platform knows I am on site and does not release my booking as a no-show, even though I share no location.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · A scan inside the arrival window records arrival
  Given a driver has a confirmed booking at a site
  And the time is after the set time before the slot start and before the end of the booking's grace period
  When the driver scans the QR code posted at an EVSE of that site
  Then arrival is recorded on the booking at the time of the scan
  And the booking's history shows the scan with the EVSE, the booking and the time

Scenario: S2 · failure · A scan before the arrival window opens does not count
  Given a driver has a confirmed booking whose arrival window has not opened yet
  When the driver scans the QR code posted at an EVSE of the booking's site
  Then the scan is stored with the EVSE, the booking and the time
  And no arrival is recorded on the booking

Scenario: S3 · failure · A scan at another site does not count
  Given a driver has a confirmed booking at site A and is inside its arrival window
  When the driver scans the QR code posted at an EVSE of site B
  Then no arrival is recorded on the booking at site A

Scenario: S4 · failure · A scan after the end of grace does not count as arrival
  Given a booking's grace period ended with no arrival recorded
  When the driver scans the QR code posted at an EVSE of the booking's site
  Then the scan is stored with the EVSE, the booking and the time
  And the booking is not recorded as arrived within grace

Scenario: S5 · edge case · A scan at an EVSE other than the allocated one still counts
  Given a booking is allocated to EVSE A at a site and is inside its arrival window
  When the driver scans the QR code posted at EVSE B of the same site
  Then arrival is recorded on the booking
  And the booking's history names EVSE B as the EVSE scanned

Scenario: S6 · edge case · A second scan does not change the arrival
  Given arrival was recorded on a booking from a QR scan
  When the driver scans a QR code at the same site again
  Then the second scan is stored
  And the booking's arrival time stays the time of the first scan

## Notes

- A-024: location is opt-in and off by default, so the QR scan is the default arrival evidence. The start of the
  arrival window is a set time before the slot (a configured value).
- The codes come from US-102. Arrival from the CSMS session start is JS-103; from the attendant, REQ-176 (FT-02.4).
  What follows a scan after grace (late arrival, re-offer) is JS-106.
- How a scan is matched to the driver's booking when the phone that scans has not opened a link to that booking
  (A-006) is a design detail for Step 3.3.
- S5 follows REQ-043 ("an EVSE of the booking's site"). Checked in the Step 3.1 red-team pass: arrival is evidence that
  the driver is at the site, while the station hold secures the allocated EVSE; a scan at another EVSE does not move the
  allocation, so the two do not conflict.
- A photo of a site's QR code, scanned anywhere, passes S1 – S6. Whether station operators accept the scan alone as
  arrival or want it corroborated (for example by the EVSE's status changing to charging soon after) is Q-052. S1 – S6
  hold under either answer: REQ-043 makes the scan the arrival evidence, and a "corroborate" answer would change it
  through a change request. Disputes rest on the scan (A-024).
