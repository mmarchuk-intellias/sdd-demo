# US-314 · Station operator sees what each fault cost in bookings

As a station operator, I want to see, for each fault at my sites, how many bookings it forced to be rebooked, offered
alternatives or sent to ops, so that I can show my maintenance team what each fault costs.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Counts per fault
  Given a charger fault on one EVSE that affected bookings
  When the station operator opens the site's faults in the station portal
  Then the fault is listed with its EVSE, when it started and when it ended
  And with how many bookings were rebooked, how many drivers were offered alternatives and how many bookings were sent to ops

Scenario: S2 (failure) Only fault-caused changes are counted
  Given bookings at the same site rebooked for a site-full case or a maintenance hold while the EVSE was faulted
  When the station operator opens the fault
  Then those bookings are not counted against the fault

Scenario: S3 (failure) Another station operator's faults stay hidden
  When a station operator opens the faults of a site that is not theirs
  Then nothing is shown

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A fault still in progress
  Given the EVSE is still faulted
  When the station operator opens the fault
  Then it is shown as ongoing, and its counts grow as further bookings are affected

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A booking with more than one outcome
  Given a booking whose driver was offered alternatives and which was then sent to ops
  When the station operator opens the fault
  Then the booking counts once in the fault's total of affected bookings

## Notes

- The station operator view the COO asked for: "charger 7 is down and it's costing you six bookings" (SRC-C2@00:35:14).
- The three categories can overlap (REQ-050: an unplaced booking goes to ops with alternatives offered to its driver);
  whether a booking counts in one category, by its final outcome, or in each is a design detail for Step 3.3. S5
  fixes only the total.
- Depends on A-017 and CF-022 (automatic same-site rebooking, ops for the rest) and Q-038 (covered by A-050). S3
  relies on REQ-258.
