# US-031 · Attendant sets each EVSE to free, occupied or faulted

As an attendant at LIC Garage Depot, I want to set each EVSE at my site to free, occupied or faulted in the attendant view, so that
drivers and ops act on the site's real status even though its chargers have no API.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The attendant sets an EVSE to occupied
  Given the attendant is signed in for LIC Garage Depot
  When the attendant sets an EVSE to occupied
  Then the EVSE's status is occupied
  And every view of it shows "last updated by attendant at" the time of the change

Scenario: S2 (failure) The attendant sets an EVSE to faulted
  Given an EVSE at LIC Garage Depot is free and has bookings later that day
  When the attendant sets it to faulted
  Then the EVSE's status is faulted, shown with the attendant's update time
  And the EVSE is handled as faulted for those bookings (REQ-192)

Scenario: S3 (failure) Status the attendant has not updated for a long time is not shown as live
  Given the attendant last updated an EVSE hours ago
  When ops or a driver views that EVSE's status
  Then it is shown as "last updated by attendant at" that earlier time

Scenario: S4 (failure) The attendant cannot set status at another site
  Given the attendant is signed in for LIC Garage Depot
  When the attendant tries to set the status of an EVSE at another site
  Then the change is refused

Scenario: S5 (edge) An occupied status at the pre-arrival check leads to reallocation
  Given a booking at LIC Garage Depot is allocated to an EVSE
  And the attendant sets that EVSE to occupied before the pre-arrival check
  When the pre-arrival check runs
  Then the booking is reallocated as in JS-018, or handled as a full site (JS-019) if no compatible EVSE is free
