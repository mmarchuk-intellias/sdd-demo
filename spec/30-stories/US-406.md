# US-406 · Pilot sites and fleets run on the platform from launch

As the Amperlane CEO, I want every new booking at the three pilot sites for the two pilot fleets made and run in the
platform from the NYC production launch on 2027-03-01, with no spreadsheet in the critical path, so that real sites
and real fleets run on Amperlane Platform v1 and the ramp towards the Series A milestone does not depend on ops'
phones and spreadsheets.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Bookings after launch run end to end in the platform
  Given the platform is live in NYC production since the launch date
  When a Borough Green driver and a Five Boroughs driver each book a slot at each of Gowanus, JFK and LIC
  Then each booking is made, confirmed, allocated, given its station hold and closed in the platform
  And nothing about any of them is written to or read from Airtable, Retool, a pilot Google Sheet or any other
    spreadsheet kept by ops

Scenario: S2 (failure) A driver texts a booking request after launch
  Given the platform is live
  When a Borough Green driver texts a site code and a time, as drivers did in the pilot
  Then the request is answered by the platform
  And no request is dropped into Airtable

Scenario: S3 (failure) A fleet phones ops to book for a driver
  Given the platform is live
  When a pilot fleet phones ops to book a slot for one of its registered drivers
  Then ops makes the booking in the ops console on the driver's behalf
  And the booking exists in the platform with ops recorded as booker
  And Airtable, the pilot Google Sheets and every other spreadsheet kept by ops are unchanged

Scenario: S4 (failure) The platform is unavailable during the availability window
  Given the platform is live and becomes unavailable during the availability window
  When drivers arrive for their bookings
  Then ops honour the next bookings from the outage copy (JS-419)
  And no booking is taken in Airtable, the pilot Google Sheets or any other spreadsheet

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A booking taken in the pilot for a slot after launch
  Given a booking taken in the Amperlane Concierge before the cut-over for a slot after it
  When the cut-over completes
  Then the booking is run in the platform (JS-407)

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A pilot booking whose slot started before the cut-over
  Given a booking in the Amperlane Concierge whose slot started before the cut-over and ends after it
  When the cut-over completes
  Then the Amperlane Concierge finishes that booking
  And it is invoiced by the pilot process (US-408)

## Notes

- Launch definition: "Real stations, real fleets, no more spreadsheets in the critical path" (SRC-C1@00:29:06);
  "Nobody opens Airtable" (SRC-C1@00:33:22). New NYC sites and fleets by June 2027 are Q-007.
- S2 assumes drivers keep texting the number the pilot used: A-057 (the platform takes over the pilot's Twilio number
  at cut-over; validated with Q-047). SMS booking itself is REQ-061 (EP-06); booking on behalf is REQ-055 and "no
  pilot tool for ops" is REQ-058 (EP-07). S2 – S4 check only that nothing falls back to the pilot tools.
- S3: REQ-009 v2 (CR-002) records the fleet-portal or ops user who made a booking as its booker, so "ops recorded as
  booker" (REQ-055) agrees with it.
- S1 says "the launch date", not 2027-03-01: whether the launch waits for the written go-live criteria (REQ-269,
  US-409) is CF-027 (needs-decision), and S1 holds under every option. The cut-over instant is a design detail for
  Step 3.3.
- The Series A milestone (10,000 completed bookings a month by June 2027) is constitution N12; its sizing is REQ-132
  (enabler).
- Red team: "Gowanus, JFK or LIC" let one site and one fleet pass S1; S1 now needs both fleets at all three sites.
  "No spreadsheets in the critical path" (REQ-142) is not met by retiring the pilot Sheets and keeping a new one, so
  S1, S3 and S4 name any spreadsheet kept by ops. The LIC CSV export (REQ-162) is a session-data feed, not a
  spreadsheet kept by ops. S3 does not name Five Boroughs: a booking made by ops counts against JS-330 (REQ-113).
