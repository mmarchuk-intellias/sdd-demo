# US-323 · Finance sees each invoice's payment status and the overdue ones

As Amperlane finance, I want the payment status of every fleet invoice tracked and every invoice still unpaid after its
due date flagged, so that I follow up only on invoices that are really overdue.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Payment status per invoice
  Given issued fleet invoices
  When a finance user opens the fleet invoices
  Then each shows its fleet, month, amount, due date and payment status as last reported by the payment provider

Scenario: S2 (happy path) An unpaid invoice past its due date is flagged
  Given an invoice still unpaid at the end of its due date
  When a finance user opens the fleet invoices the next day
  Then that invoice is flagged as overdue

Scenario: S3 (failure) An invoice paid on its due date is not flagged
  Given an invoice paid on its due date
  When a finance user opens the fleet invoices the next day
  Then that invoice is not flagged

Scenario: S4 (failure) A failed payment attempt
  Given a fleet's payment attempt failed before the due date and the fleet did not pay again
  When the due date has passed
  Then the invoice is shown as unpaid and flagged as overdue

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Paid after being flagged
  Given an invoice flagged as overdue
  When the fleet pays it
  Then the flag is cleared and the invoice shows when it was paid

## Notes

- The due date is net-30 from issue (REQ-083). "End of the due date" is Eastern time (constitution, spec conventions
  row 11).
- Status stays in step with the provider through the scheduled check (REQ-110, JS-322).
- Q-021: per-fleet credit limits and an alert after a set number of overdue days are parked (REQ-280, REQ-281); this
  story only flags.
