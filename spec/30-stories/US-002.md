# US-002 · Capacity caps per time window protect walk-up capacity

As a station operator, I want each time window at my site to carry a cap on bookable EVSEs that Amperlane holds and
bookings never exceed, so that bookings never take more of my EVSEs than I agreed to give and the rest stay open to
walk-up drivers who pay the public price.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A hold within the cap is accepted
  Given a site's capacity rule sets days of the week, a time window and a cap on bookable EVSEs
  And for every slot a requested booking covers, the holds and bookings overlapping the slot are below the cap of the rule for that slot
  When a booker places a hold for that booking
  Then the hold is accepted

Scenario: S2 (failure) A hold that would exceed the cap in any slot it covers is refused
  Given a requested booking covers several slots
  And in one of them the holds and bookings overlapping it already equal the cap of the rule for that slot
  When a booker places a hold for that booking
  Then the hold is refused
  And the holds and bookings overlapping that slot still equal the cap

Scenario: S3 (failure) Capacity rules that leave a slot uncovered or cover it twice are rejected
  Given a site's bookable hours on a day of the week
  When a set of capacity rules is saved in which a slot within those hours falls under no rule, or under more than one rule
  Then the set is rejected
  And the capacity rules in force at the site stay unchanged

Scenario: S4 (edge) An unconfirmed hold counts against the cap until it expires
  Given the holds and bookings overlapping a slot equal its cap, and one of them is a hold that has not been confirmed
  When another booker places a hold for that slot
  Then that hold is refused
  And once the unconfirmed hold expires, the same request is accepted

Scenario: S5 (edge) A booking across two rules is checked against each
  Given a requested booking starts in the time window of one capacity rule and ends in the time window of the next rule
  When a booker places a hold for it
  Then the hold is accepted only if the slots in the first window stay within the first rule's cap and the slots in the second window stay within the second rule's cap

## Notes

- Depends on: Q-005 (the caps and windows per site come from partner agreements). CTX-064 gives the order of size
  (30–50% of EVSEs at peak); REQ-005 expresses the cap as a number of EVSEs.
- Who edits the rules: the station operator in the station portal (REQ-077, EP-09) and ops at onboarding (REQ-165,
  US-014). Lowering a cap below confirmed bookings is REQ-186 (A-018, Q-035, EP-09) and not covered here.
- Overbooking stays off: the opt-in policy is parked (REQ-327, V, Q-006).
- Feasibility against compatible EVSEs at confirmation is REQ-147 (US-003); this story covers the cap only.
- For a capacity rule whose time window runs past midnight (e.g. the Gowanus off-peak window, SRC-D1#7), the day of
  the week the slots after midnight belong to is a design detail for Step 3.3.
