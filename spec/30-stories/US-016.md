# US-016 · Booking times in the site's time zone

As a driver, I want every booking time shown in the site's local time with the time zone indicated, so that I arrive at
the right time even across a daylight-saving change or when my phone is set to another zone.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Times are shown in the site's zone
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub, whose time zone is America/New_York
  When the driver views the booking in the driver PWA or in a booking message
  Then its start and end are shown in Eastern time with the zone indicated

Scenario: S2 (failure) The viewer's own time zone does not change what is shown
  Given the driver's phone is set to a time zone other than the site's
  When the driver views a booking at a NYC site
  Then its start and end are still shown in the site's time zone with the zone indicated

Scenario: S3 (failure) A repeated local hour is not ambiguous
  Given the autumn daylight-saving change repeats an hour of local time at the site
  When a booking is made for a start in that hour
  Then the booking records one instant
  And it is shown with the zone abbreviation that tells the two occurrences of that hour apart

Scenario: S4 (edge) A booking across the spring change keeps its absolute duration
  Given a booking whose window spans the spring daylight-saving change at the site
  When the booking is shown and its timers run
  Then it lasts exactly its booked duration in absolute time
  And its end is shown in the site's local time after the change

## Notes

- Depends on: A-013 (per-site time zone; times stored as instants). The daylight-saving changes happen at night, so S3 and S4
  rely on overnight bookings: REQ-152 (US-033, A-028; CF-015 is needs-decision).
- A site in another time zone end to end is US-014 S4 (REQ-138).
