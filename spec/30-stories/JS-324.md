# JS-324 · Match bookings to session records and set aside what does not match

When a station operator's session records arrive, by API or in the LIC CSV export, I want each booking matched to its
record with energy, start and end, and every booking or record that does not match set aside, neither invoiced nor
judged a no-show, so I can invoice and decide no-shows only on evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A booking is matched to its session record
  Given a booking with a recorded arrival on its allocated EVSE
  When the session record for that EVSE and the booking's window arrives
  Then within the set time of the record arriving, the booking is matched to it with its energy in kWh, start and end

Scenario: S2 (failure) A booking ends with no matching session record
  Given a booking that ended after a recorded arrival, with no matching session record
  When a finance user opens the reconciliation queue
  Then the booking is in the queue
  And it is neither invoiced nor marked a no-show while it is there
  And it leaves the queue once its session record arrives and is matched

Scenario: S3 (failure) A session record on a booked EVSE with no matching booking
  Given a booking released as a no-show at the end of grace
  When a session record arrives for its EVSE within its booked window
  Then the record and the booking are put in the reconciliation queue
  And the booking's no-show fee line is not put on an invoice while the case is open

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A walk-up session on an EVSE with no booking
  Given a session record on an EVSE that had no booking in that window
  When it is ingested
  Then it is not put in the reconciliation queue

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The same session record arrives twice
  Given the LIC export repeats a session row already received
  When it is ingested again
  Then the booking stays matched to one record
  And nothing is added to the reconciliation queue

## Notes

- R7: per-session records are unconfirmed at all three pilot sites (U-6, U-7): Q-009 (AMPECO), Q-012 (ChargeLab), Q-011
  (LIC CSV fields). Until they are confirmed, bookings at a site without records end up in the queue (S2); red team to
  weigh the ops and finance load.
- The match is evidence for the completed-booking definition (REQ-224, Q-017). "Set time" is a configured value.
- Who resolves a queue item (finance or ops) and with which outcomes (match by hand, confirm the no-show, accept the
  record as a walk-up) is a design detail for Step 3.3.
- S3 meets REQ-036 (a fee line at release) and REQ-180 (ops review). Red team on the order of events: if the record
  arrives after the fee line was already invoiced, the issued invoice is unchanged (REQ-213), and a later reversal
  becomes a credit on the next invoice (US-320); S3 covers only a fee line not yet invoiced.
