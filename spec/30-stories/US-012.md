# US-012 · Optional manual SoC in the booking flow

As a driver of a vehicle with no telematics, I want to enter my vehicle's current SoC when I book, if I choose to, so
that I can give Amperlane my battery level in the booking flow, as I tell ops by text today, while I can still skip it
when I am in a hurry.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The driver enters the SoC
  Given a driver is booking a vehicle with no telematics in the driver PWA
  When the driver enters the vehicle's current SoC as a percentage and confirms the booking
  Then the booking is confirmed
  And the booking shows the SoC the driver entered

Scenario: S2 (failure) The driver gives no SoC
  Given a driver is booking a vehicle in the driver PWA
  When the driver confirms without entering an SoC
  Then the booking is confirmed with no SoC recorded

Scenario: S3 (failure) A value that is not a valid percentage is refused
  Given a driver is booking a vehicle in the driver PWA
  When the driver enters an SoC outside the range a percentage can take
  Then the value is refused
  And the booking can still be confirmed without an SoC

Scenario: S4 (edge) Skipping the SoC keeps booking within two taps
  Given a registered driver opens the open-slot view from a message link on a device that may book without a one-time code (REQ-241)
  When the driver books without touching the SoC entry
  Then the booking takes no more than the two taps of constitution N23, pick a slot and confirm

## Notes

- Booking never requires SoC (REQ-019, EP-06).
- No K or Y requirement uses the entered SoC. Its stated use, suggesting a duration from the SoC (REQ-017,
  SRC-C2@00:38:05), is I. The Step 3.1 red-team pass rewrote the "so that", which promised that Amperlane "knows how much
  charge the vehicle needs", to the benefit the committed scope delivers: the driver reports the battery level in the
  flow instead of by text (SRC-C1@00:06:52). Triage raised no new question: REQ-018 is Y for the agreed "Geotab plus
  manual SoC" cut (CTX-099) and REQ-017 is I; whether REQ-018 stays Y without REQ-017 is a Step 5 input (constitution
  P1).
- S4's precondition keeps it consistent with REQ-241: a device without a recent one-time code shows only the booking's
  site, time and one-tap actions, and entering SoC is not one of them (REQ-241 v2, CR-001).
- SoC is vehicle data in the data register (constitution SC4).
