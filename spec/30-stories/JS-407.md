# JS-407 · Open pilot bookings carried over at cut-over

When the platform takes over from the Amperlane Concierge at cut-over, I want every pilot booking for a slot after
the cut-over already in the platform with its driver, site and window, and each of those drivers told in their language
how to manage it, so I can honour every booking promised in the pilot without opening Airtable or the Sheets, and
drivers know where to cancel or say they are running late.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Open pilot bookings are in the platform and drivers are told
  Given bookings in the Amperlane Concierge for slots after the cut-over
  When the cut-over completes
  Then each of those bookings exists in the platform as a confirmed booking with its driver, site and window
  And each driver receives one message in their stored language saying how to cancel the booking or say they are running late
  And when its slot comes, each of those bookings is allocated and given its station hold like a booking made in the platform

Scenario: S2 (failure) The driver's number is not registered to any fleet
  Given a pilot booking for a slot after the cut-over whose driver's mobile number is not registered to any fleet in the platform
  When the cut-over completes
  Then the booking still exists in the platform with its driver, site and window
  And it is listed in the cut-over result for ops with the reason "driver not registered"

Scenario: S3 (failure) No agreement to booking messages is recorded for the driver
  Given a pilot booking for a slot after the cut-over whose driver has no recorded agreement to booking messages
  When the cut-over completes
  Then no SMS or WhatsApp message is sent to that driver
  And the booking is listed in the cut-over result for ops with the reason "driver not told"

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A pilot window that does not fit the site's configured durations
  Given a pilot booking whose window is not one of the site's configured booking durations
  When the cut-over completes
  Then the booking exists in the platform with the window the pilot promised

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The pilot over-promised a slot
  Given pilot bookings for one slot at a site that exceed the cap of the site's capacity rule for that slot
  When the cut-over completes
  Then every one of those bookings exists in the platform
  And none is dropped or rebooked by the cut-over
  And they are listed in the cut-over result for ops as above the site's cap, so ops act on each one

Scenario: S6 (edge case) No language is stored for the driver
  Given a pilot booking whose driver has no stored language
  When the cut-over completes
  Then the driver's message is in English

Scenario: S7 (edge case) The carry-over is run a second time
  Given the carry-over of pilot bookings has completed and drivers have been told
  When the carry-over is run again, for example after a failure part-way through
  Then no pilot booking exists twice in the platform
  And no driver receives a second cut-over message for the same booking

## Notes

- The person in the situation is Amperlane ops, who honours pilot promises at launch. The cut-over instant is a design
  detail for Step 3.3; it moves with CF-027 (needs-decision), and the AC hold for whatever instant it is. At cut-over
  the platform takes over the pilot's SMS number (A-057, Q-047).
- S3 follows REQ-248 (EP-06): no message without recorded agreement. Whether a pilot driver's use of the pilot SMS
  number counts as that agreement is Q-056; S3 holds under any answer (if it does not count, every carried-over driver
  falls into S3 and ops reach them another way).
- S4 and S5 follow A-055, which extends A-018 to pilot bookings carried over above a site's cap or outside its
  configured durations: they stand and are listed for ops, who act on each one; nothing is dropped silently.
- S6 follows A-042. WhatsApp is unconfirmed (U-10, Q-034); SMS is in use (A-008).
- Invoicing of these bookings is US-408 (by the platform, because their slots are after the cut-over).
- Red team: bookings loaded as rows with no timers would pass "exists in the platform" and never be allocated or
  held; S1 now requires them to run at their slot (US-406 S5). S5 lists over-promised bookings for ops, as A-018 does
  for bookings above a lowered cap. S7 follows constitution N8: the pilot already sends duplicate confirmations
  (CTX-044).
- Cross-story: a pilot driver registered at cut-over may also be due a welcome message (US-225); how one message
  covers both is a design detail for Step 3.3.
