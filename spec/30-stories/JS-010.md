# JS-010 · Fair and quick answers at the shift-change burst

When the shift change sends many drivers after the same few EVSEs in the same slot, I want to get my booking attempt
answered at once and decided in the order I sent it, so I can know straight away whether the cab will be charged for
the next shift and never lose the slot to someone who asked after me.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The earlier attempt wins the last unit of capacity
  Given one unit of capacity is left in a slot at a site during the shift-change burst
  And the platform receives attempt A for that slot before attempt B
  When both attempts are decided
  Then attempt A gets the hold
  And attempt B is told the slot is full

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Answers stay within the latency target during the burst
  Given the shift-change burst at the launch sites
  When booking API response times are measured as A-032 defines, per 10-minute window
  Then 95% of the responses take under 300 ms, including the database constraint check

Scenario: S3 (failure) An attempt received later does not overtake an earlier one
  Given attempt A for a slot is received by the platform before attempt B for the same slot, through a different channel
  And the slot has room for one of them only
  When both attempts are decided
  Then attempt A gets the hold and attempt B does not, whichever of the two could be processed sooner

Scenario: S4 (failure) Every losing attempt gets an answer, not an error
  Given more attempts arrive for a slot than it has capacity left
  When they are decided
  Then every attempt that loses gets an alternative or an explicit answer that the slot is full
  And none of them is left without an answer

Scenario: S5 (edge) Attempts for different slots do not compete
  Given attempts A and B are for different slots at the same site and both slots have room
  When both attempts are decided
  Then both get a hold, whatever order they were received in

Scenario: S6 (edge) Five times the modelled launch burst
  Given a load test sends 2,000 booking attempts in 10 minutes, five times the modelled launch burst of about 400, with heavy contention on a few sites, using the profile in A-034
  When the test runs on staging
  Then booking API responses take under 300 ms at the 95th percentile
  And there are zero overlapping allocations
  And there are zero duplicate bookings among the retries that reuse an idempotency key
  And at most 0.1% of the attempts end in a server error (A-034)
  And every attempt that loses a contended slot gets an alternative or an explicit answer that the slot is full

## Notes

- Depends on: A-032 (which operations, measured where), A-034 (contention profile and pass criteria, including at most
  0.1% server errors; S6 states all of them, so that the latency target cannot be met by answering quickly with
  errors), A-009 (burst concentrated on a few sites), CF-026 (400 is the modelled launch burst, not the
  observed one). Constitution N3 and N6 hold the same targets.
- The alternative offered to a loser is JS-004 (REQ-008). Retries with the same idempotency key are constitution N8
  (REQ-103, EP-14). The database constraint behind "zero overlapping allocations" is REQ-101, an enabler for step 3.3;
  the allocation rule itself is US-011 (REQ-007).
