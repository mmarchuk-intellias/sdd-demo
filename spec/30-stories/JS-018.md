# JS-018 · Allocated EVSE occupied, booking reallocated and walk-up left alone

When the EVSE allocated to my booking is still occupied by a walk-up or an overstay shortly before my slot, I want to be
reallocated to another free compatible EVSE at the same site and told which one, so I can still charge on time without
anyone being asked to unplug.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Occupied at station-hold time
  Given a booking is allocated to an EVSE that is occupied when the site's station-hold offset is reached
  And another EVSE at the site whose connector the vehicle can use is free
  When the station hold falls due
  Then the booking is reallocated to the free compatible EVSE
  And the station hold is placed on that EVSE, not on the occupied one
  And the charging driver gets a message naming the new EVSE and stall

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Occupied by an overstay at the pre-arrival check
  Given the allocated EVSE was free at station-hold time
  And at the pre-arrival check it is still occupied by the previous session
  And another compatible EVSE at the site is free
  When the pre-arrival check runs
  Then the booking is reallocated to the free compatible EVSE, its station hold is placed there and the charging driver is told the new EVSE

Scenario: S3 (failure) A walk-up on the allocated EVSE is left alone
  Given a walk-up is charging on the EVSE allocated to a booking
  When the platform handles the booking at station-hold time or at the pre-arrival check
  Then the platform sends no stop command for the walk-up's session
  And it sends site staff no station-hold request for that EVSE
  And no message to site staff, ops or the driver asks for the walk-up to leave

Scenario: S4 (failure) No other compatible EVSE is free
  Given the allocated EVSE is occupied by a walk-up and no other compatible EVSE at the site is free
  When the station hold falls due
  Then the walk-up is still left alone as in S3
  And the booking is handled as a full site (JS-019)

Scenario: S5 (edge) Reallocation away from a reserved EVSE at a pattern A site
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub had a charger reservation sent for its first EVSE
  When the booking is reallocated because that EVSE is occupied at the pre-arrival check
  Then the charger reservation on the first EVSE is cancelled
  And a charger reservation is sent for the new EVSE if it is flagged reservable, otherwise site staff get a station-hold request for it

## Notes

- Depends on: A-002 (station hold about T-15, pre-arrival check about T-10, configured per site), A-012 (site staff do
  physical station holds), Q-012 and A-031 (whether JFK status is fresh enough to see the occupancy; U-8), A-021 and
  Q-049 at LIC (occupancy known only from the attendant view; U-13).
- S5 relies on REQ-153 and REQ-035 (US-027) for clearing the old station hold and on REQ-024/REQ-026 (US-021, US-025)
  for the new one.
- REQ-155 (JS-023) sends a signage request when a charger reservation is rejected; if the rejection is because a
  walk-up occupies the EVSE, REQ-020 forbids a request for that EVSE. Step 3.1 red-team pass: the specific rules
  (REQ-020, REQ-021: occupied at station-hold time) take precedence over the general one (REQ-155); JS-023 S4 applies
  this.
