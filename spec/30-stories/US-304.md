# US-304 · Fleet adapter policy with a per-vehicle override

As a fleet manager, I want to set once whether the fleet's vehicles may use adapters, and override it for single
vehicles, so that my drivers are sent to an EVSE that needs an adapter only where the fleet accepts that, without my
editing every vehicle.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The fleet allows adapters
  Given the fleet's policy allows adapters
  And a NACS vehicle of the fleet records an allowed CCS1 adapter
  When its driver looks for a slot at a site whose free EVSEs are all CCS1
  Then slots at that site are offered for the vehicle

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A vehicle override allows what the fleet forbids
  Given the fleet's policy forbids adapters
  And one NACS vehicle has an override that allows its recorded CCS1 adapter
  When drivers look for slots at a site whose free EVSEs are all CCS1
  Then slots are offered for that vehicle
  And no slots are offered for the fleet's other NACS vehicles

Scenario: S3 (failure) Adapters forbidden: no adapter-only slot is offered or allocated
  Given the fleet's policy forbids adapters and the vehicle has no override
  When its driver looks for a slot at a site whose only free EVSEs need an adapter for that vehicle
  Then no slot is offered
  And no booking for the vehicle is allocated to an EVSE whose connector differs from the vehicle's native connector

Scenario: S4 (failure) Drivers and other fleets cannot change the policy
  When a driver of the fleet, or a fleet manager of another fleet, tries to change the fleet's adapter policy or a vehicle override
  Then the request is refused and the policy is unchanged

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A vehicle override outlives changes of the fleet default
  Given a vehicle whose override allows adapters while the fleet forbids them
  When the fleet manager changes the fleet's policy to allow adapters and later back to forbid them
  Then the vehicle's override still applies after each change
  And once the fleet manager removes the override, the vehicle follows the fleet's policy again

## Notes

- The connector matching that uses this setting is REQ-013 (EP-01); the vehicle's recorded adapters are REQ-015.
- A policy change applies to new bookings only: confirmed bookings it affects, for example one accepted only through
  an adapter the fleet now forbids, stand and are listed for the fleet manager or ops to act on (A-055). These AC do
  not test it.
- Policy changes are audited with before and after values (REQ-262, EP-13).
- Whether ops may set the policy for a fleet without portal users is not stated; REQ-206 covers drivers and vehicles.
