As a driver, I want to book, arrive and charge without sharing my location, and to have it collected only if I opt in
and only around my booking, so that my whereabouts stay private between rides and I still keep my booked slot.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · A driver who never opted in completes a booking
  Given a registered driver whose location opt-in is off, as it is by default
  When the driver books a slot, confirms it and scans the QR code at an EVSE of the site inside the arrival window
  Then the booking is confirmed and arrival is recorded from the QR scan
  And no location reading of the driver or of the vehicle is collected

Scenario: S2 · failure · The phone blocks location access
  Given a driver who has not opted in to location
  And the driver's browser blocks location access
  When the driver books, cancels, reports running late or records arrival
  Then each step completes
  And no step asks the driver for location access

Scenario: S3 · failure · A withdrawn opt-in stops collection
  Given a driver opted in to location and then withdrew the opt-in
  When the pre-slot reminder of the driver's next booking goes out
  Then no location reading of the driver is collected from the moment of withdrawal

Scenario: S4 · failure · No collection outside the booking's window
  Given a driver has opted in to location
  When the time is before the booking's pre-slot reminder or after the end of its grace period
  Then no location reading of the driver is collected

Scenario: S5 · edge case · No QR scan and no session start
  Given a driver who has not opted in, at a site whose CSMS sends no session-start event
  And the driver did not scan a QR code
  When the attendant confirms the driver's arrival in the attendant view
  Then arrival is recorded on the booking from the attendant

Scenario: S6 · edge case · A fleet's authorisation is not the driver's opt-in
  Given the driver's fleet has authorised access to its vehicles' data
  And the driver has not opted in to location
  When the pre-slot reminder of the driver's booking goes out
  Then no location of the driver's vehicle is collected

## Notes

- A-024, Q-016: any location, from the phone or the vehicle, needs the driver's opt-in, off by default, and only from
  the pre-slot reminder until grace ends. Counsel approves the consent wording.
- REQ-255 sits in FT-03.2; it is linked here because REQ-204 and REQ-255 describe the same driver choice. REQ-256
  (keeping location only as arrival events) is an enabler.
- In the committed plan no K or Y requirement reads location (REQ-173 and REQ-175 are I). Until one is admitted, S3, S4
  and S6 hold because nothing collects location. No K or Y requirement defines the opt-in and withdrawal control
  itself; REQ-255 (K) and A-024 cover it, and the control arrives with REQ-173 or REQ-175 if they are admitted
  (constitution P7).
- S5 depends on the attendant view (REQ-176, FT-02.4), which is unconfirmed for LIC (U-13; A-021, Q-049).
