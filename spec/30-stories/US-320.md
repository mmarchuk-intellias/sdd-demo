# US-320 · A fee reversed after invoicing returns as a credit on the next invoice

As a fleet finance contact, I want a fee reversed after its invoice was issued to come back as a credit line on our
next invoice, so that I never have to replace or re-check an invoice we have already processed.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A credit line on the next invoice
  Given an issued invoice that carries a no-show fee line
  When the fee is reversed after a dispute
  Then the fleet's next invoice has a credit line for that fee, naming the original invoice and booking
  And the issued invoice is unchanged

Scenario: S2 (failure) An issued invoice cannot be edited
  Given an issued fleet invoice
  When anyone tries to change or remove one of its lines
  Then the attempt is refused and the invoice is unchanged

Scenario: S3 (failure) A fee reversed before its invoice is issued needs no credit
  Given a no-show fee reversed or waived before its month's invoice is issued
  When that invoice is issued
  Then the fee is not charged on it
  And no credit line for it appears on any later invoice

Scenario: S4 (failure) No double credit
  Given a credit line was already created for a reversed fee
  When month close runs again
  Then no second credit line for that fee appears

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A reversal after the following invoice is also issued
  Given the fee's invoice and the next month's invoice are both issued
  When the fee is reversed
  Then the credit line appears on the first invoice issued after the reversal

## Notes

- Depends on Q-002 (fee payer and amount) and A-047 (a fleet can dispute within 30 days of the booking, so reversals
  after the invoice are expected).
- Reversal in the ledger: US-316. Waiving before invoicing: REQ-182 (EP-03).
- How a credit larger than the next invoice's charges is handled (negative total, or carried forward) is a design
  detail for Step 3.3.
