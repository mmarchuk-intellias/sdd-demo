# JS-019 · Whole site full before the slot, alternatives and ops exception

When no compatible EVSE at my site is free shortly before my slot, I want to get alternatives at once, at the same site
and at nearby sites, so I can still charge soon instead of driving to a full site.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Full at station-hold time
  Given a booking is allocated at JFK Lot 9
  And when the site's station-hold offset is reached, no EVSE at the site whose connector the vehicle can use is free
  When the station hold falls due
  Then the charging driver immediately gets the next compatible slot at JFK Lot 9 and the nearest compatible free slots at other sites
  And an exception for the booking appears in the ops queue within the set time

Scenario: S2 (happy path) The driver accepts an alternative
  Given the charging driver was offered alternatives because the site was full
  When the driver accepts one of them
  Then the booking is rebooked to the accepted slot with the reason code 'moved — site full'
  And the booking is not recorded as a no-show

Scenario: S3 (failure) Full at the pre-arrival check
  Given the allocated EVSE was free at station-hold time
  And at the pre-arrival check neither it nor any other compatible EVSE at the site is free
  When the pre-arrival check runs
  Then the charging driver immediately gets the next compatible slot at the site and the nearest compatible free slots at other sites
  And an exception for the booking appears in the ops queue within the set time

Scenario: S4 (failure) Only compatible alternatives are offered
  Given the nearest free slot at another site is free only on EVSEs whose connectors the vehicle cannot use
  When the alternatives are offered
  Then that slot is not among them

Scenario: S5 (edge) Full while no ops user is signed in
  Given no ops user is signed in to the ops console
  And a booking's site is full at station-hold time
  When the station hold falls due
  Then the charging driver gets the alternatives as in S1
  And the exception is in the ops queue within the set time, and stays there until an ops user handles it
