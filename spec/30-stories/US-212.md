# US-212 · Book in two taps from a message link

As a registered driver, I want to book from the open-slot view in two taps, picking a slot and confirming, with
site, vehicle and duration already filled in, so that booking a charge fits between two rides.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Two taps from the open-slot view
  Given a registered driver opens the driver page from a message link on a phone that has completed a one-time code
    for the driver within the set period (REQ-241)
  And the open-slot view shows a site, a vehicle and a duration already filled in
  When the driver taps a free slot
  And taps confirm
  Then the booking is confirmed after exactly these two taps
  And the page shows the confirmed booking

Scenario: S2 (happy path) The driver changes a preset
  Given the open-slot view shows a site, a vehicle and a duration already filled in
  When the driver changes the site to another partner site, the vehicle to another vehicle the driver may book, or
    the duration to another of the site's booking durations
  Then the open-slot view shows the free slots for the changed site, vehicle or duration
  And picking a slot and confirming books it with the changed site, vehicle or duration

Scenario: S3 (failure) The slot is taken before the driver picks it
  Given the open-slot view shows a slot as free
  And another booker takes the last capacity in that slot
  When the driver taps that slot
  Then no hold is placed for that slot
  And the driver is told the slot was taken and sees the slots free at that moment

Scenario: S4 (failure) The hold expires before the driver confirms
  Given the driver has picked a slot
  And the site's hold time passes without a confirmation
  When the driver taps confirm
  Then no booking is created
  And the driver is told the hold expired and sees the slots free at that moment

Scenario: S5 (failure) Confirm is sent twice on a bad connection
  Given the driver has picked a slot
  When the confirm request reaches the platform twice with the same idempotency key
  Then exactly one booking exists
  And the driver sees one confirmed booking

Scenario: S6 (edge case) No free slot for the presets
  Given no compatible slot is free at the preset site for the preset vehicle and duration on that day
  When the driver opens the open-slot view
  Then the view says that no slot is free there
  And lets the driver change the site, the vehicle or the duration

Scenario: S7 (edge case) A phone that has not completed a one-time code
  Given a registered driver opens the driver page from a message link on a phone that has not completed a one-time
    code for the driver within the set period
  When the driver picks a slot and confirms
  Then no booking is created until a one-time code sent to the registered number is entered on that phone
  And once the code is entered, the booking is confirmed for the slot the driver picked

## Notes

- Baseline: constitution N23 (two taps), N3 (booking API latency), N8 (idempotency, REQ-103).
- How the site, vehicle and duration are preset (for example the driver's last booking or the fleet's default) is a
  design detail for Step 3.3.
- S7: REQ-241 (Y) puts a one-time code before any change on an unverified phone, so the first booking on a new phone
  takes more than two taps; booking is not a one-tap action, so REQ-241 v2 (CR-001) leaves S7 unchanged. Where the
  code is asked (before the open-slot view or at confirm) and whether N23 counts the two taps from after it are a
  design detail for Step 3.3; S7 holds under either answer.
- S3 and S4 rely on REQ-145 (FT-01.2). Offering the next free compatible slot when a slot is taken is REQ-008 (Y,
  FT-01.2); the red team took it out of S3 so this K story does not depend on a Y requirement.
