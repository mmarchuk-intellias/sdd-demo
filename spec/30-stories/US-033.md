# US-033 · Book a slot that starts overnight

As a booker, I want to book slots that start between 01:00 and 05:00 Eastern time (REQ-152), so that a vehicle that
works the night shift can be charged with a guaranteed slot in the site's quiet hours.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) An overnight slot is offered and booked
  Given a site whose bookable hours include the night and whose capacity rule for the night has room
  When a booker opens the open-slot view for that site and the coming night
  Then start times between 01:00 and 05:00 Eastern time are shown as open
  And a hold placed on one of them can be confirmed as a booking

Scenario: S2 (failure) The night's capacity rule is applied as by day
  Given the holds and bookings overlapping a slot between 01:00 and 05:00 Eastern time already equal the cap of the site's capacity rule for that slot
  When a booker places a hold that covers that slot
  Then the hold is refused

Scenario: S3 (failure) A site whose bookable hours exclude the night
  Given a site whose bookable hours do not include 01:00 to 05:00 Eastern time
  When a booker requests a slot at that site starting in that period
  Then no start time in that period is shown as open
  And the request is refused

Scenario: S4 (edge) A booking that runs into the overnight period
  Given a site whose bookable hours and capacity rules cover both the evening and the night
  When a booker books a slot that starts before 01:00 Eastern time and ends after it
  Then the booking is accepted only if every slot it covers, before and after 01:00, stays within the cap of the capacity rule for that slot
