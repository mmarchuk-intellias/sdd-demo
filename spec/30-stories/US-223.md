# US-223 · One confirmation per booking, in the reader's language

As a booker or charging driver, I want exactly one confirmation in my language with the site, date, start time,
duration and vehicle, so that I know what is booked without calling ops and never wonder whether two confirmations
mean two bookings.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The booker is the charging driver
  Given a driver whose language is Spanish books a slot for themself
  When the booking is confirmed
  Then the driver receives one confirmation in Spanish
  And it names the site, the date, the start time in the site's time zone (A-013), the duration and the vehicle

Scenario: S2 (happy path) The booker and the charging driver differ
  Given a day driver whose language is English books a slot for a night driver whose language is Bengali
  When the booking is confirmed
  Then the day driver receives one confirmation in English
  And the night driver receives one confirmation in Bengali
  And both name the same site, date, start time, duration and vehicle

Scenario: S3 (failure) The confirmation step runs twice
  Given a booking whose confirmation step is retried after a platform error or a duplicate inbound message
  When the retry completes
  Then the booker and the charging driver each still have exactly one confirmation for the booking

Scenario: S4 (failure) Confirm is tapped twice
  Given a driver has picked a slot
  When the driver taps confirm twice and both requests reach the platform with the same idempotency key
  Then one booking exists
  And the driver receives one confirmation

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A booking confirmed during the maintenance window
  Given a booking is confirmed while the platform is in planned downtime between 01:00 and 05:00 ET
  When the platform resumes
  Then the confirmation is sent, possibly late, and exactly once (constitution N5)

Scenario: S6 (failure) A confirmation with no delivery status is not sent again
  Given a driver with SMS and WhatsApp registered was sent the booking's confirmation by SMS
  And no delivery status arrived for it within the set time
  When the SMS is delivered later
  Then no confirmation for the booking was sent on WhatsApp
  And the driver holds exactly one confirmation for the booking

## Notes

- The pilot sends two confirmations about twice a week (CTX-044); inbound deduplication is REQ-090 (JS-216).
- REQ-219 v2 (CR-005) resends a confirmation on the other channel only when it is reported undelivered, never because
  no delivery status arrived, so a late first delivery cannot give the driver two and REQ-089 holds (S6; JS-226 S5,
  S6). "The set time" in S6 is REQ-219's configured value.
- When one confirmation step confirms two bookings (afternoon and overnight, REQ-010), whether the booker gets one
  message per booking or one for both is a design detail for Step 3.3.
- Language choice is REQ-199 (US-227); the link in the message is REQ-063 (US-211).
