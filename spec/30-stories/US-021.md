# US-021 · Charger reservation shortly before the slot

As a driver booked at a site whose chargers accept reservations, I want my allocated EVSE reserved at the charger from
shortly before my slot until my grace period ends, so that a walk-up cannot plug in just before I arrive.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The reservation is sent at the station-hold offset and outlasts grace
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub is allocated to an EVSE flagged as reservable
  When the site's configured station-hold offset before the slot is reached
  Then the platform sends a charger reservation for that EVSE through the CSMS
  And the reservation's expiry is no earlier than the end of the booking's grace period

Scenario: S2 (failure) An EVSE not flagged as reservable gets no charger reservation
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub is allocated to an EVSE whose charger rejects reservations and is not flagged as reservable
  When the station-hold offset is reached
  Then no charger reservation is sent
  And site staff get a station-hold request for it instead (US-025)

Scenario: S3 (failure) No charger reservation before the station-hold offset
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub was confirmed well before its slot
  When its allocated EVSE is checked at the CSMS before the station-hold offset
  Then no charger reservation exists for that booking

Scenario: S4 (edge) The expiry follows the site's own grace period
  Given a site whose grace period is configured to a value other than the default of A-003
  When a charger reservation is sent for a booking at that site
  Then its expiry is no earlier than the end of that booking's grace period under the site's value

Scenario: S5 (edge) A reallocated booking is reserved on its new EVSE
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub is reallocated at station-hold time to another EVSE flagged as reservable
  When the station hold is placed
  Then the charger reservation is sent for the new EVSE, not for the first one

## Notes

- Depends on: A-002 (station-hold offset about T-15, configured per site), A-003 (grace defaults to 10 minutes per
  site), Q-009 (AMPECO sandbox and the list of chargers that accept reservations). The AMPECO reservation endpoint is
  confirmed on 10 of 16 chargers (CTX-060, integrations.md I-1); the API itself is REQ-031 (EP-05).
- Rejected or unanswered reservations: JS-023 (REQ-155). Cancelling the reservation when the booking ends: US-027
  (REQ-153). The reservable flag: US-022 (REQ-025).
- If ops extends a booking's grace (REQ-042) after the charger reservation was sent, whether the reservation's expiry
  is extended is a design detail for Step 3.3.
