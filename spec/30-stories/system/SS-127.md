# SS-127 · Grace and auto-release: release at the end of grace without arrival, never after arrival

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to release a booking automatically at the end of its grace period when no
arrival is recorded, and never once arrival is recorded, so that the EVSE goes back to walk-ups with nobody phoning
anyone and a driver who arrived keeps the stall.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall take a booking's end of grace as its slot start plus the grace
   period of its site fixed at confirmation (10 minutes unless the site is configured otherwise) plus any ops extension
   (SS-130).
2. [Event] When a booking's end of grace is reached with no arrival recorded, the Arrival & no-show component shall
   release the booking as a no-show with no human action, with the system as actor and the auto-release reason code,
   return its capacity to the slot calendar and emit a release event for clearing its station hold (SS-120) and for the
   fee decision (SS-131).
3. [Unwanted] If arrival is recorded on the booking before its end of grace, then the Arrival & no-show component shall
   not release it, even when the grace timer fires late.
4. [Unwanted] If the grace timer fires more than the configured timer lateness tolerance after the end of grace and no
   arrival is recorded, then the Arrival & no-show component shall release the booking once and mark it
   "grace timer late", so that no no-show fee is created for it automatically (SS-131).
5. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall apply clauses 1 – 4 alike at sites of supply patterns A, B and C,
   each booking with its own site's values.

## Notes
- A-003 and Q-004 (grace per site, 10 minutes by default per REQ-034); REQ-146 via SS-032: a grace period changed after
  confirmation does not affect the booking (JS-105 S6). Ops extensions (JS-105 S7) are SS-130.
- A-028 and constitution N5: timers may fire late during maintenance but are never lost and take effect once (SS-034).
  Clause 4 is the "grace timer fired late" exclusion of REQ-180; its tolerance is a new parameter of SS-026, default in design.md §8 (A-059).
- An arrival with no charging session (JS-111 S3) keeps the booking by clause 3. JS-330 S2 and US-205 S3 are clauses 2
  and 5.
- P3.4 final pass (2026-10-28, handoff H3 from critic Z): JS-324 S2 ("nor marked a no-show while it is there") holds
  through clause 3, since that booking has a recorded arrival. Parent JS-324 (Y) added; KYIV stays K, epic stays EP-03.
