# SS-330 · Directions in the driver's navigation app and one tap back to the Uber Driver or Lyft Driver app

As the Driver PWA, I need to open directions to the booking's site in the phone's own navigation app and to return the
driver to the Uber Driver or Lyft Driver app with one tap, exchanging no data with either, so that charging fits
between rides without retyping an address or hunting for apps.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the driver taps directions on a booking, the Driver PWA shall open the phone's navigation app by a deep
   link with the booking's current site as the destination.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall put only the destination in the directions link and read no location of the phone
   to build it.
3. [Event] When the driver taps the control for the Uber Driver app or for the Lyft Driver app, the Driver PWA shall
   open that app, not the Uber or Lyft rider app, by a deep link with that one tap.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall put no booking, vehicle or driver data in a deep link to the Uber Driver or Lyft
   Driver app and accept no data from those apps.
5. [Unwanted] If the navigation app, the Uber Driver app or the Lyft Driver app cannot be opened, then the Driver PWA
   shall keep the driver page open with the site's address shown and the booking unchanged.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item #32 (clause 5).
- Deep links only (ADR-013, CF-005, CTX-098); trip and vehicle data from Uber and Lyft is REQ-322 (V); arrival ETA is
  REQ-065 (I). Maps provider for any in-page map: A-030, Q-027, CF-020; none is needed here. The exact deep-link
  addresses of the Uber Driver and Lyft Driver apps are checked in Phase 0.
- "Current site" follows a rebook to another site (US-231 S5). Location: SS-331 (US-231 S3).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 5 no longer states "leave it to the phone's own link handling", which is not a testable
  response of the PWA.
