# SS-437 · Runbook for every alert and external system, used by Amperlane before hypercare ends

As the Platform & operations component, I need a runbook register that ties every production alert rule and every
external system to a written runbook and records who used each runbook and how, and a handover report built from it,
so that Amperlane's two engineers can restore service without Intellias once hypercare ends.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Unwanted] If a change adds a production alert rule without a link to a written runbook, then the Platform &
   operations component shall fail the pipeline run.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall keep in the runbook register every external system the
   platform depends on, AMPECO, ChargeLab, the LIC CSV export, Twilio, Stripe, Amperlane's single sign-on and the AWS
   services included, each with its outage runbook.
3. [Event] When an engineer records a use of a runbook in a drill or an incident, the Platform & operations component
   shall keep in the register who used it, when, whether they followed it end to end, and whether any step needed
   Intellias staff.
4. [Event] When the handover report is produced at the end of hypercare, the Platform & operations component shall
   list as an open handover item each alert rule or external system whose runbook is missing, has a step that needs
   Intellias staff, or has no recorded end-to-end use by a named Amperlane engineer before hypercare ended.
