# SS-227 · Station portal fault and maintenance outcomes per affected booking

As the station portal (Partner portals), I need to list, for each fault or maintenance hold at a station operator's
site, every booking it changed and what happened to it, so that the station operator knows which booked drivers are
still coming and to which EVSE.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When Allocation & rebooking emits the per-booking outcomes of a charger fault (SS-108 clause 8) or of a
   maintenance hold (SS-113 clause 6) at a site, the station portal shall list for that fault or hold each booking in
   those outcomes with its slot, its former EVSE and its outcome as emitted: rebooked to another EVSE at the site, or
   handed to unplaced handling, shown as sent to ops.
2. [Ubiquitous] The station portal shall show with each rebooked booking in the list its reason code, 'moved — charger
   fault' or 'moved — maintenance'.
3. [Event] When Allocation & rebooking emits the final outcome of a booking listed as sent to ops (SS-108 clause 10),
   the station portal shall show that final outcome in the list, with the new site, time and EVSE where it has one.
4. [Unwanted] If a booking at the site is not among the per-booking outcomes of a fault or hold because it still fits
   the remaining EVSEs, then the station portal shall not list it as changed.
5. [Ubiquitous] The station portal shall show no driver data for a listed booking beyond the vehicle's plate and the
   charging driver's first name, and never a phone number.

## Notes
- Covers JS-313 S1 – S5 for the station portal. The notice within the set time (REQ-051) is an SMS to the station
  operator's registered contact, SS-313 (bucket-5 #39).
- The rebooking itself is SS-108 (fault) and SS-113 (maintenance); unplaced bookings SS-109; bookings that still fit
  stay (data-model INV-2, A-050).
- Clause 5: REQ-258 (phone numbers) and A-053 (Q-048). Who may open which site is SS-323. The count of bookings each
  fault cost is SS-234 (REQ-080).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28, cross-batch fix): clauses 1, 3 and 4 consume the per-booking outcomes of SS-108 clauses 8 and 10 and
  SS-113 clause 6 instead of recomputing which bookings changed and how they ended.
