# SS-339 · De-identified data outside production: exports, data-room extracts, staging seeds

As the Audit & data protection component, I need driver names and phone numbers removed from every field of data that
leaves production, free text included, with a final check before release, so that Amperlane can share and test with
real booking data without exposing a single driver.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When data is prepared to leave production, as an export to a party outside Amperlane, a data-room extract or
   a staging seed, the Audit & data protection component shall remove every driver name and phone number from every
   field, free text included.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall recognise phone numbers in any written format, with or
   without country code, spaces or punctuation.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall keep booking ids, sites, slots and outcomes in the output.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall replace each driver with a random surrogate id that is the
   same for all of that driver's records in one output and is not derived from the driver's name or phone number.
5. [Event] When an output is about to be released, the Audit & data protection component shall scan it for any phone
   number and for the name of every driver that the platform or the migrated pilot history holds, drivers no longer
   registered included.
6. [Unwanted] If the scan finds a match, then the Audit & data protection component shall not release the output.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall give the drivers of a staging seed phone numbers from the
   configured test range, which reaches no real phone.
8. [Event] When the scan finds a match, the Audit & data protection component shall report to the person who requested
   the output the field where the match was found.

- A hash of the phone number used as driver id would be reversible by trying every number (JS-405 S6); clause 4 forbids
  it.
