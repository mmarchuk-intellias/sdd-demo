# SS-230 · Monthly completed bookings for NYC, pilot data marked and separable

As the reporting module, I need to report completed bookings per month for NYC sites under the configured definition,
with migrated pilot data marked and separable, so that the climb to 10,000 completed bookings a month holds up in
front of investors.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the completed-bookings report is requested, the reporting module shall give one figure per month for
   NYC sites, counted under the configured completed definition (SS-229).
2. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall place each booking in the month in which its final slot started, in the
   site's time zone.
3. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall mark migrated pilot bookings as pilot data, show them separately from
   platform bookings, and give every figure with and without them.
4. [Unwanted] If a site is not in NYC, then the reporting module shall leave its bookings out of every figure of this
   report.
5. [Unwanted] If a migrated pilot booking was re-classified by the migration, then the reporting module shall count it
   by its re-classified status under the same status definitions as platform bookings, never by its original value.
6. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall count the current month under the definition in force when the report is
   produced and state with each month's figure the definition it was counted under.

## Notes
- Covers US-327 S1 – S6.
- Clause 2: bucket-5 #49 (slot start in the site's time zone, migrated bookings included) and #44 (a rebook keeps the
  same booking, so it counts once, in the month of its final slot).
- Clause 4: whether a site is in NYC comes from its configured address and time zone (REQ-165, SS-028).
- Past months after a definition change: Q-054 (client decision, with Q-017); clause 6 holds either way.
- Depends on Q-017 (definition), A-026 and REQ-228 (re-classification, SS-418), CF-018 and Q-029 (pilot figures
  uncleaned). The 10,000 target is NYC only (CTX-138, constitution N12).
