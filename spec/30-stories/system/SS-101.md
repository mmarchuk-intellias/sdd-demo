# SS-101 · EVSE choice: compatible, lowest sufficient power, free (walk-ups never displaced)

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to choose, for every allocation and reallocation, a free EVSE whose
connector the vehicle can use and whose power suits it, so that the driver arrives at an EVSE they can plug into and no
walk-up is ever displaced.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall allocate or reallocate a booking only to an EVSE that has at
   least one connector matching the vehicle's native connector or an adapter allowed for that vehicle, the vehicle's own
   adapter setting overriding the fleet's policy (SS-039).
2. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall treat an EVSE as free for a booking only when its last known
   status (SS-202) is available, so it is not in use (occupied or charging), faulted or unknown, it is under no
   maintenance hold for the booking's slot, and no active allocation on it overlaps the booking's time range (SS-102).
3. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall choose, among the free compatible EVSEs whose maximum power
   meets the vehicle's maximum DC charging power, the one with the lowest maximum power.
4. [Unwanted] If no free compatible EVSE meets the vehicle's maximum DC charging power, then the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall choose the free compatible EVSE with the highest maximum power.
5. [Unwanted] If a booking names no vehicle when its allocation offset is reached (a booking carried over at cut-over
   without one, SS-003 clause 9), then the Allocation & rebooking component shall treat no EVSE as compatible with it,
   so that it is not allocated and goes to site-full handling with its ops exception (SS-100 clause 4, SS-105).

## Notes
- Clause 2 is the walk-up rule of REQ-020 at allocation (data-model INV-12): an EVSE in use by anyone, walk-up or
  overstay, is never chosen. The ban on stop commands and on requests to make a walk-up leave is SS-104 clause 4 and
  SS-118 clause 7.
- With a stale status feed, clause 2 uses the last known status and the status service raises the outage exception
  (SS-203, REQ-168). At LIC the status is the attendant's (SS-207, A-021, U-13).
- Q-050: accessible EVSEs may need their own allocation rule. Q-013 and A-004: an EVSE with connectors of both types
  counts as compatible for either vehicle under clause 1 (US-006 S5).
- A-055: a vehicle or adapter-policy change after confirmation applies to new bookings only; the bookings it affects are
  listed for explicit action (SS-038, SS-039).
- Refusing a booking whose vehicle no EVSE fits (US-006 S2 – S4) is the Booking engine's (SS-004, SS-006).
- Clause 5 is CR-009 with A-061 (Gate 3, 2026-10-28): pilot records name no vehicle (CTX-042), so a carried-over
  booking may have none; ops set it before the allocation offset (SS-421 clause 12), and one still without a vehicle
  is handled as a booking no compatible EVSE can take (A-055, REQ-022). No vehicle is guessed: a guess repeats the
  pilot's connector mismatches (CTX-049). SS-105 clause 2's compatible alternatives need a vehicle, so for such a
  booking ops act on the exception. JS-407 S1 ("allocated … like a booking made in the platform") rests on A-061.
