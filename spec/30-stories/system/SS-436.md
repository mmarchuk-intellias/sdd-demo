# SS-436 · Outage copy of the next bookings per site, outside the platform, ops-only

As the Platform & operations component, I need a copy of each site's next bookings kept outside the platform's
application, database and identity service, refreshed at the configured outage-copy refresh interval and open only to
ops, so that ops can honour bookings by phone during a platform outage instead of going back to Airtable and the Sheets.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the configured outage-copy refresh interval elapses and the platform returns complete data, the Platform
   & operations component shall replace the outage copy with, per site, every booking whose slot starts within the
   configured outage-copy horizon, with the charging driver, the driver's contact number, the window and the allocated
   EVSE where there is one.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall serve the outage copy from hosting separate from the
   platform's application, database and identity service, so that it opens while all three are unavailable.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall show on the outage copy the time of its last complete
   refresh.
4. [Unwanted] If the last complete refresh is older than the configured outage-copy refresh interval, then the Platform
   & operations component shall show on the outage copy that it is out of date, so that ops know later bookings may be
   missing.
5. [Unwanted] If a refresh cannot reach the platform or returns incomplete data, then the Platform & operations
   component shall keep the last complete refresh.
6. [Event] When an ops person opens the outage copy, the Platform & operations component shall sign them in through
   Amperlane's single sign-on, which runs outside the platform, with a read-only ops role.
7. [Unwanted] If a person without an ops role tries to open the outage copy, then the Platform & operations component
   shall refuse access and record the attempt in the access log (SS-433).
8. [Unwanted] If Amperlane's single sign-on is unavailable, then the Platform & operations component shall allow access
   only to a named ops person through the break-glass step of the outage runbook (SS-437) and record each such access
   with the person and the time.
