# SS-401 · No-show fee line, exactly one per no-show, separate from energy

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need to post exactly one no-show fee line for each no-show fee that the no-show
decision requests, at the terms fixed for the booking and apart from any energy charge, so that a station operator is
paid for an EVSE held for a driver who never came and a disputed fee can be handled on its own.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the Arrival & no-show component requests a no-show fee for a booking (SS-131), the Ledger & invoicing
   component shall post one no-show fee line linked to the booking, at the fee amount and with the station operator
   share fixed for that booking at its confirmation from its own site's terms (SS-027).
2. [Unwanted] If a no-show fee is requested for a booking that already has a no-show fee line, for example after a
   retried timer or at the end of an ops grace extension, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall post no second
   line and shall return the existing line.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall post each no-show fee as a line of its own kind and shall never
   combine it with an energy charge in one line.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall post a no-show fee line only on a fee request from the Arrival &
   no-show component, automatic or an ops fee decision with a reason code, and never from a booking's state on its own.

## Notes
- Covers US-109 S1 (clauses 1, 3), S2 (clause 4; the decision itself is SS-131), S3 and S5 (clause 2), S4 (clause 1,
  parameterised over two sites); US-316 S1 (clauses 1, 3). US-316 S2 – S5 are SS-402.
- Contract (ss-plan §5.1, fee line): SS-131 decides and requests one fee, or sends the booking to ops review for a
  REQ-180 exclusion; this SS records the line; SS-027 fixes the terms at confirmation (bucket-5 #17); reversals and
  waivers are SS-402; credits SS-406.
- An agreed "no fee yet" is an explicit zero (#17, SS-027): the one line is posted at zero, so REQ-036's "exactly one
  line" holds at such a site too.
- Q-002 (payer) and Q-005 (amounts and shares) are open: the line exists in every option. The account the fee is
  charged to follows the fleet policy's payer once Q-002 is decided; tests use the fleet as payer, the only payer the
  baseline invoices (REQ-083). Energy lines exist only where the business model sells energy (Q-001; REQ-270 is
  parked), so clause 3 is tested with the sample months of REQ-210 (SS-400).
- Clause 2 is the business rule; it holds even for two requests with different idempotency keys (constitution N8).
