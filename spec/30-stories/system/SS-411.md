# SS-411 · Finance views of invoices, reconciliation and disputes across every fleet and site

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need to give Amperlane finance read-only views of fleet invoices with their
payment status, of the reconciliation queue and of disputes with their fee outcomes, across every fleet and site, so
that finance can run month-end checks themselves while operational settings stay with ops, station operators and
fleets.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall give finance users a view of fleet invoices listing, for each
   invoice, its fleet, month, amount, due date, payment status, overdue flag (SS-409) and any submission error
   (SS-412).
2. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall give finance users a view of the reconciliation queue listing
   each case with its booking and its session record (SS-408).
3. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall give finance users a view of disputes listing each dispute with
   its outcome, the fee line it affected and the reversal or credit entry it produced (SS-402, SS-406).
4. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall list in the finance views the invoices of every fleet and the
   reconciliation cases of every site, with no organisation filter.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall offer the finance views as read operations only, with no
   action that changes a booking, a fee, a capacity rule or a fleet policy.
6. [Unwanted] If a fleet-manager or station-operator user requests a finance view, through a portal or directly
   through the API, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall return no data and the out-of-scope response of
   SS-323 clause 7.

## Notes
- Covers US-325 S1 (clauses 1 – 3), S4 (4, 6). US-325 S2 and S3 (finance refused on booking, capacity-rule and
  fleet-policy commands, also directly through the API) are SS-440 (Identity & access), added in batch 5: the refusal
  belongs to the finance role's permissions, checked on every API call (constitution SC3), not to the settlement
  module.
- P3.4 critique: clause 6 said "refuse"; SS-323 clause 7 gives every out-of-scope request the response for an item
  that does not exist. Clause 6 now uses that response, so the two SS cannot answer one request
  differently.
- Finance actor: A-010, CF-011 (resolved-provisional), Q-037. If the client answers "exports and e-mails only", a CR
  changes this SS.
- A finance user's view of a driver's personal data is audited (REQ-263 v2, SS-335). Payout views are parked (Q-001;
  REQ-287 – REQ-289). Dispute records come from SS-134.
