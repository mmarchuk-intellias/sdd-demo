# SS-121 · Station-hold record per booking: method, requests, answers and their times

As the Station-hold dispatcher, I need to record for every booking each station-hold attempt, its method, every request
and answer and their times, so that ops can show what was done to hold the stall when a no-show is disputed.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Station-hold dispatcher shall record for every booking each station-hold attempt with its EVSE, its
   method (charger reservation, site staff or ops), each request with the time it was sent, and each answer (accepted,
   rejected, delivered, acknowledged, not delivered or not answered) with its time, and each charger-reservation
   cancellation request with its result from the CSMS integration (cancelled, not answered or cancellation
   unconfirmed, SS-212 clauses 7, 10 and 11) and its time.
2. [Event] When a charger reservation is rejected or unanswered and the fallback site-staff request is sent, the
   Station-hold dispatcher shall record both attempts and their answers in the order they happened.
3. [Unwanted] If a request is not delivered or not answered within its configured timeout, then the Station-hold
   dispatcher shall record that outcome with the time the timeout passed.
4. [Event] When a booking is reallocated, the Station-hold dispatcher shall keep the attempts made on every EVSE the
   booking had, each with its EVSE and time.
5. [Event] When a blocked-stall report is stored for a booking (SS-133), the Station-hold dispatcher shall add it to the
   booking's station-hold record as a failed station hold with the report's time.
6. [Event] When the charger rejects a charger reservation, the Station-hold dispatcher shall write an audit event
   (SS-332) with the attempt, its rejection, the system as actor and the time.
7. [Event] When the dispute view (SS-135) asks for a booking's station-hold record, the Station-hold dispatcher shall
   return every attempt and answer in time order.
