# SS-402 · Append-only ledger; fee reversal or waiver by a new entry

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need posted entries to be unchangeable, and every reversal or waiver of a no-show
fee to be a new entry that leaves the fee entry and every energy line untouched, so that the ledger always shows what
was charged, when, and why it was undone.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall refuse every change to, and every deletion of, a posted entry,
   from any user or platform process, and leave the entry unchanged.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall make every correction as a new entry that references the entry
   it corrects.
3. [Event] When the Arrival & no-show component reports a dispute decision to reverse a no-show fee (SS-134), the
   Ledger & invoicing component shall post one reversing entry for the fee's full amount that references the fee line
   and records the decision's reason code, actor and time.
4. [Event] When an Amperlane ops user waives a no-show fee line that is on no issued invoice and gives a reason code,
   the Ledger & invoicing component shall post one waiver entry that references the fee line and records the ops user,
   the reason code and the time.
5. [Unwanted] If a waiver comes without a reason code, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall refuse it with the
   code REASON_CODE_REQUIRED and post nothing.
6. [Unwanted] If a waiver is requested for a fee line that is on an issued invoice, then the Ledger & invoicing
   component shall refuse it with the code FEE_ALREADY_INVOICED, post nothing and leave the issued invoice unchanged.
7. [Unwanted] If a user who is not Amperlane ops requests a waiver, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall refuse
   it and post nothing.
8. [Unwanted] If a reversal or a waiver is requested for a fee line that has already been reversed or waived, then the
   Ledger & invoicing component shall refuse it with the code FEE_ALREADY_REVERSED and post no further entry.
9. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall leave every energy line unchanged when it posts a reversal or a
   waiver of a no-show fee.
10. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall count a reversal or a waiver from its own posting time only,
    so that any figure as of an earlier time still includes the fee.
11. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall report a reversed or waived fee line with a net amount of zero
    from the posting time of its reversal or waiver, so that no invoice built after that time charges it.

## Notes
- Covers US-316 S2 (clauses 3, 9), S3 (clauses 1, 2), S4 (clause 8), S5 (clause 10; the position view is SS-403);
  US-115 S1 (clauses 4, 11), S2 (5), S3 (6), S4 (7), S5 (8), S6 (9); US-113 S1 (clauses 3, 9). US-113 S6 (credit line
  on the next invoice, issued invoice unchanged) is SS-406.
- Clause 6 sends an invoiced fee down the dispute path: ops reverse it through a dispute decision (SS-134), and the
  credit line follows (SS-406, REQ-213). The dispute window, raiser and decision are SS-134; reason codes come from
  SS-020's catalogue.
- Clause 1 is checked by constitution N19's database grant test (the application role cannot update or delete ledger
  entries). The waiver, a manual ops action, is also in the audit trail (constitution N17, SS-332); not restated.
- Clause 7: a fleet manager (US-115 S4) and a finance user (SS-440) are refused; the ops role is held in Identity &
  access.
- Q-002: the payer is undecided; reversals and waivers hold in every option. ADR-008.
