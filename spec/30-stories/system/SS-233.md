# SS-233 · Per-vehicle monthly fleet report with matched energy

As the reporting module, I need to give a fleet a monthly report per vehicle of its bookings, no-shows, completed
bookings and the energy of its matched session records, so that the fleet can see how each vehicle uses Amperlane and
spot vehicles that book but do not charge.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet manager opens the vehicle report for a month, the reporting module shall give one row for each
   of the fleet's vehicles with a booking in that month, with its bookings, no-shows, completed bookings and the
   energy in kWh of its matched session records.
2. [Unwanted] If a booking has no matched session record, then the reporting module shall add no energy for it and show
   in the vehicle's row how many of its bookings have no matched session record yet.
3. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall place each booking in the month in which its final slot started, in the
   site's time zone, and count no-shows by the rules of SS-232.
4. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall count completed bookings in the vehicle report under the configured
   completed definition (SS-229) and state that definition in the report.
5. [Unwanted] If a vehicle was retired during or after the month, then the reporting module shall keep its row for that
   month.
6. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall include in a fleet's vehicle report only the fleet's own vehicles and their
   bookings, so that a shared driver's bookings on another fleet's vehicles never appear in it.

## Notes
- Covers US-308 S1 – S6 (S5 with clause 4 and SS-229: the current month counts under the definition in force).
- Clause 6 states the report's content; access to a fleet's data in general is SS-323 (REQ-258, A-016).
- R7: per-session records are unconfirmed at all three sites (U-6, U-7: Q-009, Q-011, Q-012); where none arrive,
  energy stays empty and clause 2 applies. Matching: SS-407. Month boundaries: A-013.
- Past months after a definition change: Q-054 (client decision, with Q-017).
