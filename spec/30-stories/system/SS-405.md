# SS-405 · Invoice lines itemised and traceable to ledger entries and bookings

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need every invoice line to itemise one charge with its booking id, site, date,
vehicle and charging driver, and to reference the ledger entries and booking it comes from, so that a fleet can check
each line against its own records and finance never has to reconcile an invoice later.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall put each charge of the month on its own invoice line, showing
   the booking id, the site, the date of the booking's final slot start in the site's time zone, the vehicle and the
   charging driver.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall make every invoice line reference, when the invoice is issued,
   the ledger entries it was built from and the booking they belong to.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall make each invoice's total equal the sum of its line amounts.
4. [Unwanted] If a charge of the month has ledger entries that reference no booking, then the Ledger & invoicing
   component shall issue the fleet's invoice at that close without the charge, so that no issued line lacks its
   booking.
5. [Event] When a reconciliation case holding a charge left off an invoice for want of a booking is closed with the
   charge linked to its booking (SS-408 clause 11), the Ledger & invoicing component shall put the charge on the
   fleet's next invoice as an adjustment line naming the original month and booking.

## Notes
- Covers US-318 S2 (clauses 1, 3), S5 (clause 1: the charging driver, not the booker; REQ-009 v2 records the booker);
  JS-319 S2 (2), S4 (4, 5; the case that holds the charge is SS-408 clause 10).
- P3.4 critique: clause 4 had three behaviours (leave off, queue, issue without); the queue part is now SS-408
  clause 10, which had no clause to receive such a charge, and clause 5's "traced" names the SS-408 outcome that does
  it (clause 11). Clause 1 uses the final slot, as SS-404 clause 4 does.
- Settles bucket-5 #52: an untraceable charge never holds the K monthly invoice (REQ-083); it waits in the
  reconciliation queue and returns as an adjustment once traced (REQ-218).
- Adjustment lines (SS-404) and credit lines (SS-406) also name their booking and original month or invoice, so every
  line stays traceable.
- Driver names on a fleet's invoice are that fleet's own drivers (REQ-258, SS-323).
