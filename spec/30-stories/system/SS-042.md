# SS-042 · Booker-only numbers that book for the fleet but never charge

As the Fleet & vehicles module, I need a fleet to register numbers that may book for it without being drivers, so that
an owner can book for the other shift's driver, and the booking's messages still go to the driver who charges.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet manager or ops register a mobile number as a booker-only number of a fleet, the Fleet &
   vehicles module shall store it in E.164 and list it among the fleet's booker-only numbers, apart from its drivers.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall report a booker-only number as allowed to book for its fleet and never
   as a driver of that fleet.
3. [Unwanted] If a number is registered as a booker-only number of a fleet in which it is already a driver, or as a
   driver of a fleet in which it is already a booker-only number, then the Fleet & vehicles module shall refuse it and
   say which role the number already holds in that fleet.
4. [Unwanted] If the number is not a valid phone number, then the Fleet & vehicles module shall not register it and
   shall name the number and the reason.

## Notes
- Covers JS-007 S2 and S8 (the registration side; the booking rules of S3 and S8 are SS-005).
- A-023 and Q-028: a booker-only number books for the fleet and names the driver who will charge. A number may be a
  booker-only number in one fleet and a driver in another (A-016).
- Status messages to booker-only numbers are SS-315; their one-time codes are bucket-5 #37 (SS-321).
