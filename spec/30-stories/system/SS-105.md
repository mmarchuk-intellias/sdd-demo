# SS-105 · Site full before arrival: ops exception, immediate alternatives, 'moved — site full'

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to detect a booking for which no compatible EVSE at its site is free
shortly before the slot, offer its driver alternatives at once and alert ops, so that the driver can still charge soon
and ops can act before the slot.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When no compatible EVSE at the site is free for a booking at its allocation offset, at its station-hold
   offset or at the pre-arrival check, the Allocation & rebooking component shall mark the booking site-full and raise a
   'site full' ops exception for it (SS-219) within the configured site-full exception time.
2. [Event] When the Allocation & rebooking component marks a booking site-full, the Allocation & rebooking component
   shall in the same step request for the charging driver the next compatible slot at the same site (SS-009) and the
   nearest compatible free slots at other sites (SS-010), to be sent by Messaging (SS-316).
3. [Event] When the charging driver accepts an alternative offered for a site-full booking (SS-011), the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall record the rebooking with the reason code 'moved — site full', record no no-show for the
   original slot and mark the site-full exception resolved.
4. [Unwanted] If a site-full booking's slot start is reached with no alternative accepted, then the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall keep the booking on its slot, keep its site-full mark and keep the site-full exception
   open, so that grace and auto-release run as for any booking (SS-127) and no no-show fee is created automatically
   (SS-131).
5. [State] While no ops user is signed in to the ops console, the Allocation & rebooking component shall request the
   alternatives and raise the exception as in clauses 1 and 2.
6. [Event] When the pre-arrival check runs for a site-full booking that has no allocation and has not been rebooked, the
   Allocation & rebooking component shall allocate it under SS-101 if a free compatible EVSE now exists, and mark its
   site-full exception resolved.
