# SS-133 · Blocked-stall report: stored with evidence, ops exception, counts as a failed station hold

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to store a driver's report that the allocated stall was blocked, with its
time and the arrival evidence, alert ops and count the booking as a failed station hold, so that the driver gets help
and is not charged automatically for a stall they could not use.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the charging driver or the booker of an active allocated booking reports the allocated stall blocked,
   the Arrival & no-show component shall store the report with its time and the booking's arrival evidence at that
   moment, and raise a 'blocked stall' ops exception for the booking with the report (SS-219).
2. [Event] When the Arrival & no-show component stores a blocked-stall report, the Arrival & no-show component shall
   record the booking as a failed station hold for the fee decision (SS-131) and in the station-hold record (SS-121).
3. [Event] When the Arrival & no-show component stores a blocked-stall report, the Arrival & no-show component shall
   request reallocation of the booking at its site (SS-106).
4. [Unwanted] If the reporter is neither the booking's charging driver nor its booker, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall refuse the report and leave the booking unchanged.
5. [Unwanted] If no arrival evidence exists when the report is made, then the Arrival & no-show component shall store
   the report with its time and "no arrival evidence" and shall not treat the report as arrival, so that without a QR
   scan, session start or attendant confirmation the booking is released at its end of grace (SS-127) and its fee goes
   to ops review (SS-131).
6. [Unwanted] If the booking was already released or cancelled, then the Arrival & no-show component shall refuse the
   report.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 15 (clause 5).
- Q-005: liability for a blocked stall is a partner-agreement term. A-024: arrival evidence without location is a QR
  scan, a CSMS session start or the attendant.
- The report is a one-tap action with no one-time code (REQ-241 v2, CR-001; SS-327); sender authorisation is SS-322
  (constitution SC3).
- The exception stays open whether or not the booking is reallocated (SS-106 clause 4).
