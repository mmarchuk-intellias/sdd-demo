# SS-226 · Station portal upcoming bookings with allocated EVSE, no driver phone numbers

As the station portal (Partner portals), I need to list the upcoming bookings at a station operator's sites with the
EVSE each one is allocated to, without driver phone numbers, so that site staff know which EVSEs are booked and when.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a station operator opens a site's upcoming bookings, the station portal shall list each confirmed
   upcoming booking at the site with its slot in the site's time zone and the EVSE it is allocated to, or "not yet
   allocated" until its allocation.
2. [Event] When an upcoming booking is reallocated to another EVSE, the station portal shall show the new EVSE.
3. [Unwanted] If an upcoming booking is cancelled, released or rebooked to another site, then the station portal shall
   remove it from the site's upcoming bookings, from its former EVSE's entry included.
4. [Ubiquitous] The station portal shall show no driver data for a booking beyond the vehicle's plate and the charging
   driver's first name, and never a phone number.

## Notes
- Covers US-315 S1, S2, S3 and S5. S4 (other station operators' sites) is SS-323 (REQ-258).
- Clause 4: REQ-258 bars phone numbers; A-053 (Q-048) limits the rest to plate and first name. This SS states the
  view's fields; who may open which site is SS-323.
- Late allocation at the site's allocation offset: A-002, REQ-003 (SS-100); reallocation: REQ-021 (SS-104).
