# SS-341 · Location consent guard: no location stored without an active opt-in inside the window

As the Audit & data protection component, I need every location reading, from the phone or the vehicle, to pass a
consent guard before it is stored or used, so that no driver's location is kept without an active opt-in or outside
the booking's window.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall pass every location reading, from the phone or from a
   vehicle's telematics, through the consent guard before it is stored or used.
2. [Unwanted] If the driver has no active location opt-in, then the Audit & data protection component shall discard the
   reading without storing it.
3. [Unwanted] If the reading falls before the booking's pre-slot reminder or after the end of its grace period, then
   the Audit & data protection component shall discard it without storing it.
4. [Event] When the driver withdraws the location opt-in, the Audit & data protection component shall discard every
   location reading for that driver from that moment.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall not count a fleet's authorisation of vehicle data as the
   driver's location opt-in.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall keep every driver's location opt-in off until the driver
   turns it on, and record with it the consent text version the driver accepted (SS-305).

## Notes
- A-024, Q-016: opt-in, off by default, only from the pre-slot reminder until grace ends; counsel approves the wording.
  In the committed plan no K or Y requirement collects location (REQ-173, REQ-175 are I), so US-104 S3, S4 and S6 hold
  because nothing collects it; this guard keeps them true once a feature is admitted.
- The PWA side (no prompt, every step without location) is SS-331; minimisation is SS-340; vehicle-data authorisation
  by the fleet is SS-043.
