# SS-414 · Invoice payment on Stripe-hosted pages, no card data at Amperlane

As the Payments integration (Stripe), I need fleet invoices to be paid only on Stripe-hosted payment pages reachable
from each invoice, so that fleets keep paying Amperlane through Stripe as they do today and no card number ever enters
Amperlane's systems.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the provider accepts an invoice (SS-412), the Payments integration shall record the invoice's
   Stripe-hosted payment page link with the invoice, for the fleet finance contact to pay there.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Payments integration shall collect fleet invoice payments only through Stripe.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Payments integration shall serve no card field of its own and send every card entry to a page or
   field hosted by Stripe.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Payments integration shall store, log and pass on no card number, in any request, record, log line
   or error report.
5. [Unwanted] If a provider event or response carries card details, then the Payments integration shall drop every
   card detail other than the card brand before storing or logging it.
6. [Unwanted] If a payment is declined on the Stripe page, then the Payments integration shall keep the invoice's
   payment page link unchanged, so that the fleet can pay again from the same page.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Payments integration shall direct fleets to a payment page that depends on no platform service, so
   that a fleet can pay while the platform is down or in its maintenance window.

## Notes
- Covers US-321 S1 (clauses 1, 2), S2 (6; the invoice staying unpaid is SS-413 and SS-409), S3 (3, 4, 5), S4 (7; the
  status catch-up afterwards is SS-413 clause 7).
- Fleets pay Amperlane through Stripe Billing today (CTX-051, integrations.md I-12); A-007 keeps Stripe as provider.
  The invoice reaches the fleet finance contact through the provider as today; whether it is also shown in the fleet
  portal depends on Q-037 (A-010).
- Which payment methods Stripe offers the fleet is a Stripe setting (US-321 note). Payouts and Stripe Connect are
  parked (Q-001; REQ-278).
- Clause 4 is also checked by constitution SC5's log-scrubbing test (no payment data in logs).
