# SS-119 · Unacknowledged site-staff request: ops exception at the pre-arrival check, booking stays valid

As the Station-hold dispatcher, I need to alert ops when site staff have not acknowledged a station-hold request by the
pre-arrival check, while the booking stays valid, so that ops can still get the stall marked before the driver arrives
and the driver is not charged for a station hold nobody confirmed.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the pre-arrival check runs for a booking whose site-staff station-hold request has not been
   acknowledged, the Station-hold dispatcher shall raise a 'contact the site' ops exception for the booking (SS-219).
2. [Ubiquitous] The Station-hold dispatcher shall leave a booking confirmed and allocated whether or not its site-staff
   request is acknowledged, so its arrival is recorded as for any booking (SS-124 – SS-126).
3. [Event] When site staff acknowledge a request after its 'contact the site' exception was raised, the Station-hold
   dispatcher shall record the acknowledgement with its time and mark the exception resolved.
4. [Event] When the pre-arrival check runs for a booking whose site-staff request was acknowledged, or whose charger
   reservation the charger accepted, the Station-hold dispatcher shall raise no 'contact the site' exception for it.
5. [Unwanted] If a booking's site-staff request, a fallback after a failed charger reservation included, is neither
   acknowledged by site staff nor recorded as arranged by ops (SS-123) before the booking's end of grace, then the
   Station-hold dispatcher shall record the booking's station hold as failed, so that no no-show fee is created for it
   automatically (SS-131).

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 10 (clause 5): an unacknowledged request counts as a failed station hold unless site staff or
  ops record it as placed before grace ends.
- A-046 and Q-041 (JFK), A-048 and Q-059 (Gowanus), A-012, A-002 (pre-arrival check about T-10), Q-023 (the exception is
  raised whether or not ops is on duty).
- JS-026 S2 (arrival by QR scan with the exception open) is clause 2 with SS-124. Ops recording a station hold as
  arranged with the site (US-129 S3) is SS-123 clause 4.
