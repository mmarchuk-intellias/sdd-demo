# SS-325 · Booking link opens the booking in the phone's browser, with nothing to install

As the Driver PWA, I need to open the booking a message link points to straight in the phone's browser, so that
drivers see and act on a booking between rides with no app-store download and no account.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a driver opens a booking link from an SMS or WhatsApp message, the Driver PWA shall open the driver
   page for that booking in the phone's browser.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall have no step that asks the driver to install an app from an app store, sign up or
   enter a password.
3. [Unwanted] If the Identity & access component refuses the link because it expired, is bound to another device or was
   altered (SS-320), then the Driver PWA shall show no booking data and show how to get a new link by texting the link
   keyword from the registered number.
4. [State] While a booking is cancelled, released or ended, the Driver PWA shall show its state and offer no action
   that would change it.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall make the page opened from a message link usable within 3 s at p75 on a throttled 4G
   profile (constitution N24).
6. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall make the page opened from a message link usable on a 360 px wide screen without
   horizontal scrolling (constitution N24).

## Notes
- The PWA is the agreed channel (CF-001, ADR-009); native apps are REQ-321 (V). Driver links without a password follow
  A-006 (SS-320).
- Every booking message carries the link: SS-302 (US-211 S2). How long a link works: SS-320 (REQ-240). WhatsApp is
  unconfirmed (U-10, Q-034, A-008); clause 1 holds for both channels (US-211 S5).
- Clause 5 numbers come from constitution N24 (A-043).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clause 5 (load time and screen width) split into clauses 5 and 6.
