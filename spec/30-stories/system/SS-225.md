# SS-225 · Today's arrival and charging per booked vehicle

As the fleet portal (Partner portals), I need to show for each vehicle booked today whether arrival was recorded and
whether a charging session started, updated as the evidence arrives, so that a fleet can sort out a cab that is not
charging before the next shift needs it.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet user opens today's bookings, the fleet portal shall show for each vehicle of the fleet booked
   today whether arrival was recorded and whether a charging session started, each with its time.
2. [Event] When arrival or a session start is recorded for a booking shown, the fleet portal shall show it with its
   time in an open view without a reload.
3. [Unwanted] If a booking reaches the end of grace with no arrival, then the fleet portal shall show it as released
   with no arrival, with the time of release.
4. [Event] When SS-107 reports that no charging session started within the session-start wait after a shown booking's
   arrival, the fleet portal shall show the booking as arrived and not charging.
5. [Optional] Where a site's session-start evidence comes from the attendant, the fleet portal shall show a booking with
   a recorded arrival, no attendant report of charging and no charge-not-started report (SS-107) as arrived, charging
   not yet reported, with the time of the last update.
6. [Event] When a booking shown is rebooked, the fleet portal shall follow it to its new EVSE, site or time and show the
   rebook's reason code.
7. [Ubiquitous] The partner portals shall serve the same arrival and charging evidence, with the same times, to the
   fleet portal, to the booking page of a verified booker (REQ-241) and to the ops console.
8. [Event] When the outcome of a charge that did not start is decided for a shown booking (a rebook at the site or a
   fault case with ops, SS-107), the fleet portal shall show that outcome.

## Notes
- Covers JS-309 S1 and S3 – S6, and the read side of S2 (the verified booker's booking page and ops look-up). The

  EVSE's status changing to charging or occupied (SS-200), and the attendant's "occupied" at LIC (SS-207).
- R7: session-start events, ChargeLab freshness and the attendant's device are unconfirmed (U-8, U-13, U-14): Q-009,
