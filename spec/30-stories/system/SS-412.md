# SS-412 · Idempotent invoice submission, retried while the provider is down

As the Payments integration (Stripe), I need to submit each issued fleet invoice to the payment provider with an
idempotency key, keep retrying while the provider is unavailable, and never let a retry create a second invoice, so that
each fleet gets exactly one invoice a month through outages, timeouts and restarts.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the Ledger & invoicing component issues a fleet invoice (SS-404), the Payments integration shall submit
   it to the payment provider with its lines, issue date and due date, with no manual step, file or data entry.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Payments integration shall send every attempt to submit one invoice, the first and every retry,
   with the same idempotency key, derived from the platform's invoice id.
3. [State] While the payment provider is unavailable, the Payments integration shall accept each submission request
   as pending and answer its caller without waiting for the provider, so that bookings, arrivals and ledger postings
   are recorded as usual.
4. [Unwanted] If the provider is unavailable, times out or answers with a server error, then the Payments integration
   shall keep the submission pending and retry it at the configured payment retry interval, while the submission is
   younger than the provider's configured idempotency-key retention, until the provider accepts it.
5. [Event] When the provider accepts a submission, the Payments integration shall record the provider's invoice id
   against the platform invoice and stop retrying it.
6. [Unwanted] If a pending submission is older than the provider's configured idempotency-key retention, then the
   Payments integration shall, before any further attempt, look up the provider for an invoice that carries the
   platform's invoice id, record that invoice if it finds one, and submit again only if it finds none.
7. [Unwanted] If the lookup of clause 6 cannot be made, then the Payments integration shall keep the submission pending
   without submitting it and show it to finance as blocked (SS-411).
8. [Unwanted] If the provider rejects a submission as invalid, then the Payments integration shall stop retrying it
   and show it to finance with the provider's error code (SS-411).
9. [Event] When the platform restarts with invoice submissions pending, the Payments integration shall resume each of
   them with its original idempotency key.

## Notes
- P3.4 critique: clause 3 described an architecture ("outside the booking and ledger paths") and is now the state it
  must hold; clause 4 no longer contradicts clauses 6 and 7 ("retry until accepted" against "do not submit");
  clause 6 says what happens when the lookup finds nothing; clause 9 is an event, not a storage design.
- Covers JS-322 S1 (clauses 3, 4), S2 (2, 5), S5 (9; clause 6 and 7 also cover the red team's outage longer than the
  key retention); JS-319 S1 (clause 1: submission at close with no manual step).
- Contract (ss-plan §5.1): idempotency of invoice submission lives here. One invoice per fleet and month on the
  platform side is SS-404 clause 3.
- R7 and A-007: Stripe Billing invoices are in use (integrations.md I-12). How long Stripe keeps an idempotency key,
  and whether the integration can find an invoice by the platform's invoice id, are not in the sources: Q-062 (2); the
  provider idempotency-key retention in design.md §8 (A-059) comes from Stripe's documentation, not a presale source.
  Fallback: clause 7, no blind resubmission.
- Zero-total invoices (#41, SS-404): Q-062 (1).
- The retry runs as a durable workflow timer (constitution N10). The payment retry interval is the platform parameter of
  REQ-245 in SS-026's catalogue; defaults for both configured values are in design.md §8 (A-059).
- The payout parts of REQ-244 apply only if Q-001 puts Amperlane in the money flow (ADR-011; REQ-278 is parked); they
  are not tested here, and a CR would add their clauses. The provider sits behind the interface of SS-415.
