# SS-134 · No-show disputes: raised within the window, ops decision with a reason code

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to open a dispute on a no-show raised by the booking's fleet or driver within
the dispute window, and to record ops' decision with a reason code and send it to whoever raised it, so that nobody pays
a no-show fee the evidence does not support.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet manager of the booking's fleet, or the booking's charging driver, raises a dispute on a no-show
   within the configured dispute window, the Arrival & no-show component shall open the dispute with that raiser and
   raise an 'open dispute' ops exception for it (SS-219).
2. [Event] When an ops user records a dispute on behalf of a driver, or of a fleet without portal users, the Arrival &
   no-show component shall open it with that driver or fleet as the raiser and the ops user as the person who recorded
   it.
3. [Unwanted] If the dispute window of the no-show has closed, then the Arrival & no-show component shall open no
   dispute and tell the raiser that the window has closed.
4. [Unwanted] If the raiser is a fleet manager of another fleet, or a driver who is not the booking's charging driver,
   then the Arrival & no-show component shall open no dispute.
5. [Unwanted] If a dispute is already open on the no-show, then the Arrival & no-show component shall open no second
   dispute.
6. [Event] When an ops user decides an open dispute, reverse or keep the fee line, with a reason code from the
   catalogue, the Arrival & no-show component shall record the decision with the ops user, the reason code and the
   time, and close the dispute.
7. [Unwanted] If an ops user's decision has no reason code from the catalogue, then the Arrival & no-show component
   shall refuse it and keep the dispute open.
8. [Event] When ops decide to reverse the fee line, the Arrival & no-show component shall request its reversal from the
   Ledger & invoicing component (SS-402 clause 3; a credit line if it was invoiced, SS-406).
9. [Event] When a dispute is decided, the Arrival & no-show component shall request that the decision and its reason
   be sent to the raiser, in the driver's language for a driver.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 19 (clause 2): a driver raises a dispute from the booking page (behind REQ-241's one-time code)
  or by message to ops; a fleet without portal users raises it through ops (REQ-243), who record the fleet as raiser.
- Q-043 is covered by A-047: a fleet or a driver, within 30 days of the booking; the window is a configured value with
  that default. Q-002: the payer is undecided; the flow holds in every option.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clause 6 held four behaviours (record, reverse, close, notify); the reversal request and the
  notice to the raiser are now clauses 8 and 9. The dispute window is a platform-wide parameter of SS-026 (default
  A-047).
- Organisation scoping (US-113 S4) is SS-323; the evidence view is SS-135; the fee reversal without touching energy
  lines is SS-402 (constitution N19).
- P3.4 final pass (2026-10-28): clause 9's notice to a driver raiser is sent by SS-314 clause 7. For a fleet raiser,
  the fleet portal shows the decision (SS-224 clause 6, A-062, Gate 3 2026-10-28), which covers US-113 S1's last Then.
