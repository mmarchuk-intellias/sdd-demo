# SS-006 · Booking vehicle registered to the fleet, allowed and not retired

As the Booking engine, I need every booking to name one vehicle that is registered to the booking's fleet, allowed
for its charging driver and not retired, so that matching and allocation use a real vehicle's connector profile and
the booking belongs to the right fleet.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall record exactly one vehicle on every booking and set the booking's fleet to
   that vehicle's fleet, except a booking carried over at cut-over (SS-003 clause 9).
2. [Event] When a booker asks which vehicles can be booked, the Booking engine shall return every vehicle that is not
   retired, belongs to a fleet the booker may book for, and that fleet's vehicle policy allows the charging driver to
   book, including, for a number registered to several fleets, the vehicles of each of them.
3. [Unwanted] If the named vehicle is not registered to a fleet the booker may book for, then the Booking engine shall
   refuse the request with the code VEHICLE_NOT_IN_FLEET and place no hold.
4. [Unwanted] If the named vehicle is retired, then the Booking engine shall refuse the request with the code
   VEHICLE_RETIRED and place no hold, whatever channel the request came through.
5. [Unwanted] If the fleet's vehicle policy does not allow the charging driver to book the named vehicle, then the
   Booking engine shall refuse the request with the code VEHICLE_NOT_ALLOWED and keep the vehicles the policy allows
   bookable.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall use, for matching and for the allocation request, the vehicle's connector,
   adapter and power profile as the Fleet & vehicles module reports it at the moment of each check.

## Notes
- Covers US-006 S4; US-009 S3 and S5; US-218 S1, S2, S4; US-303 S3 (no new booking for a retired vehicle on any
  channel; its past bookings shown as retired are SS-038 and SS-223).
- The vehicle policy of clauses 2 and 5 is SS-045, added in this batch because no catalogued SS held it (ss-log-1).
  Depends on A-023 and Q-028 (how a driver is authorised for a fleet's vehicle), A-016 (several fleets).
- Clause 6 with A-055: a profile change applies to new checks; confirmed bookings stand and are listed (SS-038).
- Vehicle records: SS-038; retirement: SS-038.
- CR-009 (Gate 3, 2026-10-28): REQ-231 v2 keeps a pilot booking carried over at cut-over also where no registered
  vehicle is known for it; clause 1 excepts it, and SS-003 clause 9 records the fleet and the vehicle the cut-over
  request names, or none. How the vehicle is then set, and what allocation does without one, is A-061 (SS-421 clause
  12, SS-101 clause 5). REQ-012 is unchanged.
