# SS-124 · QR arrival: a valid scan within the arrival window records arrival

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to record a driver's arrival from a scan of the QR code at any EVSE of the
booking's site within the booking's arrival window, so that a driver who shares no location keeps the booking and is not
released as a no-show.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall take a booking's arrival window as running from the site's
   configured arrival-window start before the slot start to the booking's end of grace, ops extensions included
   (SS-127).
2. [Event] When a phone with a link session for a booking (SS-320) scans a valid QR code of an EVSE at the booking's
   site inside the booking's arrival window, the Arrival & no-show component shall record arrival on the booking at the
   scan's time, with the scanned EVSE and the QR scan as its source.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall store every valid scan with its EVSE, booking and time, whether or
   not it counts as arrival.
4. [Unwanted] If a scan comes before the booking's arrival window opens, then the Arrival & no-show component shall
   record no arrival.
5. [Unwanted] If the scanned EVSE is at another site than the booking's, then the Arrival & no-show component shall
   record no arrival on the booking.
6. [Unwanted] If a scan comes after the booking's end of grace with no arrival recorded, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall record no arrival within grace and hand the scan to late-arrival handling (SS-128).
7. [Event] When a scan comes after arrival was already recorded on the booking, the Arrival & no-show component shall
   keep the first arrival and its time.
8. [State] While a driver has more than one active booking at the site, the Arrival & no-show component shall apply a
   scan to the booking whose arrival window contains the scan's time.
9. [Unwanted] If the scanning phone has no link session for any of the driver's bookings, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall ask the driver to open the booking's link or to text for a new one from the registered number
   (SS-320), and record arrival at the scan's time if the booking page then opens inside the arrival window.
10. [Unwanted] If a scanned code fails its signature check or names no known EVSE (SS-031), then the Arrival & no-show
    component shall reject the scan and record nothing.
11. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall record arrival from a QR scan without asking for or storing any
    location.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 16 (clause 9).
- A-024: location is opt-in and off by default, so the QR scan is the default arrival evidence (REQ-204). Q-052: whether
  station operators accept a scan alone as arrival; clauses 2 – 7 hold under either answer (a "corroborate" answer comes
  through a change request). A-006: drivers are identified by signed links.
- A scan at an EVSE other than the allocated one counts (US-101 S5): arrival shows the driver is at the site; it does
  not change the allocation.
- The same arrival window applies to the CSMS session start (SS-125) and to the attendant (SS-126, item 13).
