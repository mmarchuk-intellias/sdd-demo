# SS-403 · Fleet position at any past date: owed, paid, outstanding

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need to give a fleet's owed, paid and outstanding amounts as at the end of any
past or current day, derived only from the entries posted up to then, so that finance can answer a fleet's question
about its account, or check a month end, from the ledger alone.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a finance user asks for a fleet's position at a date that is today or in the past, the Ledger &
   invoicing component shall return what the fleet owed, had paid and had outstanding at the end of that day in
   Eastern time (or now, for today), each equal to the sum of the fleet's entries of that kind posted up to then.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall report a fleet's owed amount as its charges net of the
   reversals, waivers and credits posted against them, and its outstanding amount as owed minus paid.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall leave out of a fleet's position at a day every entry posted
   after the end of that day, a later reversal of an earlier fee included.
4. [Unwanted] If the requested date is in the future, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall return no figures,
   with the code DATE_IN_FUTURE and the message that only past and current dates are available.
5. [Event] When a finance user asks for a fleet's position at a date earlier than the fleet's first ledger entry, the
   Ledger & invoicing component shall return zero for owed, paid and outstanding.

## Notes
- Covers US-317 S1 (clauses 1, 2), S2 (3), S3 (4), S5 (5). S4 (another organisation is refused) is organisation
  scoping, SS-323 (contract, ss-plan §5.1).
- Charges come from SS-401 and SS-404, reversals and waivers from SS-402, credits from SS-406, payments from SS-409.
  No stored balance: SS-400.
- "End of that day" is Eastern time (spec conventions row 11). Who may ask: finance (SS-411, SS-440) and ops.
- Q-021: credit limits and the cash-gap view are parked (REQ-280, REQ-281). Accrued charges for fleet managers are
  REQ-208 (I).
