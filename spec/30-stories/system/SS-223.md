# SS-223 · Fleet portal bookings: the fleet's upcoming and past bookings with state

As the fleet portal (Partner portals), I need to list every upcoming and past booking of the fleet with its charging
driver, vehicle, site, time and current state, whoever made it, so that a fleet manager can answer questions about
the fleet's charging without calling Amperlane.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet manager opens the fleet's bookings, the fleet portal shall list every upcoming and past booking
   of the fleet's vehicles with its charging driver, vehicle, site, date and time, and current state, whether it was
   made by a driver, a booker-only number, a fleet manager or ops.
2. [Event] When a booking of the fleet changes state, the fleet portal shall show the new state on the next view, and
   for a rebook its reason code and the EVSE, site and time it was rebooked to.
3. [Ubiquitous] The fleet portal shall show each booking's start and end in the site's time zone, each with its own
   date.
4. [Unwanted] If a booking's charging driver has been removed from the fleet, then the fleet portal shall keep listing
   the booking, with the driver marked as removed.

## Notes
- Covers US-306 S1, S2 and S5. S3 and S4 (other fleets' bookings, a shared driver's bookings on other fleets'
  vehicles) are organisation scoping, SS-323 (REQ-258); "the fleet's bookings" are the bookings of the fleet's
  vehicles (A-016).
- Clause 4 follows A-052: a removed driver's future bookings stand and are listed for the fleet manager to reassign or
  cancel (Q-045). Reassigning is SS-319's area.
- Booking states and reason codes come from SS-020 (REQ-053); time zones from SS-021 (A-013). Migrated pilot bookings
  among past bookings follow REQ-115 (SS-416); not covered here.
