# SS-219 · Exception queue: every exception type, dedupe, grace-critical first, close

As the ops console, I need to hold every open exception raised anywhere in the platform in one queue, once per problem,
with the ones ops must handle before a booking's grace ends first, so that ops always handle the booking that needs
them soonest and never watch several tools to find problems.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a component raises an exception of a type in the exception-type catalogue, the ops console shall add it
   to the single exception queue with its type, its site, its EVSE and booking where it has them, the time it was
   raised and the details its raising component supplies.
2. [Unwanted] If an exception is raised with the same type as an open exception and the same booking, EVSE, site, fault
   or maintenance hold, message or report as that exception, by the type's level in the catalogue (clause 11), then the
   ops console shall keep one open exception and record the repeat on it.
3. [Ubiquitous] The ops console shall list first the open exceptions that concern a booking whose grace has not ended,
   or a site or EVSE at which a booking is still to be allocated or within grace that day, ordered by the slot start of
   that booking, or for a site- or EVSE-level exception of the next booking due for allocation at its site, earliest
   first; and after them all other open exceptions, oldest first.
4. [State] While an exception is open, the ops console shall keep it in the queue, whether or not its booking's slot has
   passed, until an ops user closes it or its raising component reports it resolved.
5. [Event] When an ops user closes an exception with a reason code, the ops console shall remove it from the queue and
   record the closure with the ops user, the reason code and the time on the exception and, where it has a booking, in
   that booking's history.
6. [Unwanted] If an ops user tries to close an exception without a reason code, then the ops console shall refuse the
   closure and keep the exception open.
7. [Event] When the raising component reports an exception resolved (a driver's answer, an ops decision taken in that
   component, or recovery), the ops console shall remove it from the queue and record the resolution with its actor,
   reason code and time.
8. [Event] When the raising component updates an open exception (for example the alternatives offered and the driver's
   answer), the ops console shall show the update on it without the ops user reloading the queue.
9. [State] While no ops user is signed in, the ops console shall accept, deduplicate and order raised exceptions as at
   any other time, so each is at its place in the order when an ops user signs in.
10. [Unwanted] If a user without the Amperlane ops role requests the exception queue, then the ops console shall refuse
    access.
11. [Ubiquitous] The ops console shall hold in its exception-type catalogue these types, each with its deduplication
    level: by booking 'charger reservation failed' (SS-117), 'station-hold request undelivered' (SS-118, SS-122),
    'contact the site' (SS-119), 'station hold not cleared' (SS-120), 'site full' (SS-105), 'unplaced after fault'
    (SS-109), 'fault rebooking incomplete' (SS-036), 'charge not started' (SS-107), 'blocked stall' (SS-133), 'fee
    decision' (SS-131, SS-132) and 'open dispute' (SS-134); by EVSE 'fault' (SS-208) and 'silent EVSE' (SS-204); by site
    'status feed down' (SS-203); by fault or maintenance hold 'operator notice undelivered' (SS-313); by message
    'undelivered message' (SS-307) and 'message not understood' (SS-312); by report 'driver fault report' (SS-210).

## Notes
- Covers US-124 S1 – S7, US-121 S1 and S4 (with SS-109), JS-019 S1, S3 and S5 (with SS-105), JS-110 S1 – S3 and S6
  (with SS-131, which decides the fee and reports the exception resolved; clause 6 refuses a plain close without a
  reason code) and JS-210 S2 and S6 (with SS-203).
- REQ-189 v2 (CR-003) sets the order. Clause 3 reads "exceptions ops must handle before a booking's grace ends" as those
  that still concern a booking before or within its grace (by booking, or by the site's next allocation for site- and
  EVSE-level exceptions); a site- or EVSE-level exception with no booking due that day, a fee decision and a dispute
  follow, oldest first. Slot starts are compared as instants, so sites in different time zones order correctly.
  Dana Whitfield confirms the order (CR-003).
- Exception-type catalogue: clause 11 (ss-plan §5.1 plus the types the writers added: 'fault' SS-208, 'operator notice
  undelivered' SS-313, 'fee decision' also from SS-132, 'station-hold request undelivered' also from SS-122, 'fault
  rebooking incomplete' SS-036; names as proposed in ss-log-2). Reconciliation items go to finance (SS-408), not to this
  queue.
- Levels for clause 2 are those of clause 11. 'Undelivered message' dedupes per message (SS-307 clause 9 records a
  late delivery on it), 'message not understood' per inbound message (SS-312), 'driver fault report' per report
  (SS-210 clause 3).
- Q-023 (ops hours) is an undecided client decision; out-of-hours escalation is REQ-316 (parked V). The queue holds in
  every option. Organisation scoping in general is SS-323; clause 10 is the ops-only role check (constitution SC3).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 11 added (cross-batch fix: every raised type listed, 'fault rebooking incomplete' of SS-036
  clause 3 included; levels made testable); clause 2 refers to it.
