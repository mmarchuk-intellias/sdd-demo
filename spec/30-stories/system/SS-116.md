# SS-116 · Charger reservation at the station-hold offset, expiry no earlier than the end of grace

As the Station-hold dispatcher, I need to place each booking's station hold at the site's station-hold offset by the
method its allocated EVSE supports, a charger reservation lasting until grace ends where the EVSE is reservable, so that
a walk-up cannot take the EVSE just before the driver arrives.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the site's configured station-hold offset before the slot start of an active allocated booking is
   reached and its EVSE is flagged reservable and not in use (SS-104), at a site not in manual mode (SS-123) and, at
   Gowanus, outside degraded mode (SS-122), the Station-hold dispatcher shall request a charger reservation for that
   EVSE through the site's CSMS integration (SS-212), with an expiry no earlier than the booking's end of grace.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Station-hold dispatcher shall take a reservation's expiry from the booking's own end of grace: its
   slot start plus the grace period fixed for it at confirmation plus any ops extension recorded when the request is
   sent.
3. [Unwanted] If the allocated EVSE is not flagged reservable, then the Station-hold dispatcher shall send no charger
   reservation for it and leave its station hold to the site-staff request of SS-118 (or to ops' list in manual mode,
   SS-123).
4. [Ubiquitous] The Station-hold dispatcher shall send no charger reservation for a booking before its station-hold
   offset is reached.
5. [Event] When a booking is allocated or reallocated after its station-hold offset has been reached (for example under
   SS-104, SS-105 or SS-106), the Station-hold dispatcher shall place the station hold at once on the new EVSE by that
   EVSE's method and none on the earlier EVSE.
6. [Event] When ops extend the grace period of a booking whose charger reservation is in place (SS-130), the
   Station-hold dispatcher shall extend or re-send the reservation so that its expiry is again no earlier than the new
   end of grace, and handle a rejection of that request as in SS-117.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Station-hold dispatcher shall place station holds at sites of supply patterns A, B and C at each
   site's own station-hold offset, by charger reservation for a reservable EVSE and by site-staff request otherwise, and
   shall change no booking state or timer differently by pattern.
8. [Event] When ops set an EVSE's reservable flag, the Station-hold dispatcher shall send a charger reservation under
   clause 1 for every booking on that EVSE whose station-hold offset is reached after the change, bookings allocated
   before the change included.
9. [Event] When ops clear an EVSE's reservable flag, the Station-hold dispatcher shall send a site-staff station-hold
   request (SS-118) instead of a charger reservation for every booking on that EVSE whose station-hold offset is reached
   after the change, bookings allocated before the change included.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 8 (clause 6).
- Clauses 8 and 9 cover US-022 S1 and S2: the flag in force when the station-hold offset is reached decides the method
  (REQ-024, REQ-025, REQ-026). The flag itself, its audit and who may change it (US-022 S1 audit, S3, S4) are SS-029.
- A-002 (station hold about T-15, configured per site), A-003 (grace 10 minutes by default, per site), Q-009 (AMPECO
  sandbox, which chargers accept reservations); U-1 and integrations.md I-1: reservations work on 10 of 16 Gowanus
  chargers. The reservable flag is SS-029; the AMPECO call is SS-212.
- An EVSE in use at the station-hold offset is reallocated first (SS-104). Manual mode (SS-123) and Gowanus degraded
  mode (SS-122) suspend clause 1.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 1 said "send a reservation" whenever the EVSE is reservable, while SS-104 clauses 1 and 5
  (EVSE in use), SS-122 clause 1 (degraded mode) and SS-123 clause 1 (manual mode) answer the same trigger with no
  reservation; the exceptions now sit in the clause, not only in this note. Clause 3 hands over to SS-118 instead of
  restating it, so manual mode is not contradicted there either.
- Q-060 (extends Q-009): whether AMPECO caps a reservation's expiry below the end of grace plus the maximum grace
  extension, and which identifier the reservation carries so that the booked driver, and no walk-up, can start a
  session on the reserved EVSE. Fallback: a rejected reservation follows REQ-155 (SS-117).
