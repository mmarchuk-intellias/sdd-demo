# SS-235 · Ops-free booking measure for Five Boroughs from the audit trail

As the reporting module, I need to measure from the audit trail which Five Boroughs bookings ran with no manual ops
action, and which ops actions were allowed exceptions, so that the CEO's first launch-success sentence ("nobody on my
team picks up the phone") can be checked booking by booking.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the ops-free measure is produced for a month, the reporting module shall classify each Five Boroughs
   booking whose slot started in that month as ops-free if it reached arrival, auto-release or an automatic rebook and
   its audit trail holds no manual ops action.
2. [Unwanted] If a Five Boroughs booking's audit trail holds a manual ops action, that is an action with a person in the
   Amperlane ops role as actor, then the reporting module shall count the booking against the measure and list the
   action with its person, time and reason code, unless clause 3 applies.
3. [Unwanted] If a manual ops action was taken on a booking while it had an open site-full exception, or an unplaced
   fault-affected booking exception with no free compatible EVSE at its site, then the reporting module shall count the
   action as an allowed exception and not against the measure.
4. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall not treat actions by the system, a driver, a booker or site staff, the
   attendant included, as manual ops actions.
5. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall give with the measure, per month, the number of Five Boroughs bookings, how
   many were ops-free, how many had only allowed exceptions, and the bookings counted against it with their actions.

## Notes
- Covers JS-330 S1 – S6 as a measure over the trail; the behaviours themselves are SS-124 – SS-127 (arrival and
  auto-release), SS-108 (automatic rebook) and SS-105, SS-109 (exceptions).
- Answered in the triage: ops booking on behalf and ops action on an unacknowledged station hold count against REQ-113
