# SS-045 · Vehicles each registered driver may book under the fleet's policy

As the Fleet & vehicles module, I need each fleet to say which of its vehicles each registered driver may book, every
vehicle unless the fleet limits a driver, so that a driver can book any vehicle the fleet allows and nothing else.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall report every non-retired vehicle of a fleet as allowed for every
   registered driver of that fleet, except where the fleet has limited that driver to named vehicles.
2. [Event] When a fleet manager or ops limit a registered driver of the fleet to named vehicles of the fleet, the Fleet
   & vehicles module shall report from that moment only those vehicles, while not retired, as allowed for that driver.
3. [Event] When a fleet manager or ops remove a driver's limit, the Fleet & vehicles module shall report every
   non-retired vehicle of the fleet as allowed for that driver from that moment.
4. [Unwanted] If a limit names a vehicle that is retired or belongs to another fleet, then the Fleet & vehicles module
   shall refuse the limit and keep the driver's policy unchanged.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall answer whether a driver may book a vehicle under the policy of that
   vehicle's fleet only, so a driver of two fleets gets each fleet's own answer.
6. [Event] When a new limit leaves a driver's confirmed future booking on a vehicle the driver is no longer allowed,
   the Fleet & vehicles module shall keep the booking and list it for the fleet manager, or for ops where the fleet has
   no portal user, to act on.

## Notes
- Added in Step 3.3 (ss-log-1): US-218 S4 ("a vehicle the fleet policy does not allow") and REQ-198 ("any vehicle
  those fleets' policies allow") need a policy that no catalogued SS held. It is used by SS-006 (clauses 2 and 5).
  Covers US-218 S2 (clause 5) and S4 (clause 2).
- A-023 and Q-028 (how a driver is authorised to book a fleet's vehicle); A-016. Clause 1's default keeps today's
  behaviour: a registered driver books any vehicle of the fleet. Spend controls (allowed sites, bookings per day) are
  parked (REQ-294, REQ-295, Q-032) and are not part of this policy.
- Clause 6 applies A-055's principle (nothing dropped silently) to a policy change; A-055 names vehicle edits and
  adapter-policy changes, not this one (ss-log-1). Changes are audited (REQ-262 fleet policies, SS-333).
