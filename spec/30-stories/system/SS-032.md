# SS-032 · Per-booking workflow with timers from the booking's fixed parameters

As the Booking workflows module, I need one workflow per hold and booking that schedules every booking timer from the
parameters fixed at confirmation and moves them when the booking changes, so that each step of a booking runs at the
right time for that booking and never on another booking's or a later version's values.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a hold is placed, the Booking workflows module shall start one workflow for it, keyed by the hold's
   booking id, with a hold-expiry timer at the hold's expiry instant.
2. [Event] When the hold-expiry timer fires and the hold has not been confirmed, the Booking workflows module shall
   request the Booking engine to expire the hold.
3. [Event] When a booking is confirmed, the Booking workflows module shall schedule, from the parameter version fixed
   at its confirmation, the reminder, allocation at the allocation offset, the station hold at the station-hold offset
   and the pre-arrival check at the pre-arrival check offset before the slot start, and the end of grace at the grace
   period after the slot start.
4. [Event] When a booking timer fires, the Booking workflows module shall request the component that owns that step to
   act for the booking: allocation and the pre-arrival check (Allocation & rebooking), the station hold (Station-hold
   dispatcher), the end of grace (Arrival & no-show) and the reminder (Messaging, SS-033).
5. [Ubiquitous] The Booking workflows module shall not request allocation or a station hold for a booking before the
   booking's allocation offset or station-hold offset is reached.
6. [Event] When a booking is confirmed after one of its allocation, station-hold or pre-arrival check points has
   passed, the Booking workflows module shall run that step at once and in that order.
7. [Event] When a booking is rebooked to another slot or site, or ops extend its grace, the Booking workflows module
   shall cancel the timers set for the old times and schedule them for the new slot and grace end.
8. [Event] When a booking is cancelled, released or ends, the Booking workflows module shall cancel its remaining
   timers so that no later step runs for it.
9. [Unwanted] If a site's parameters change after a booking was confirmed, then the Booking workflows module shall keep
   that booking's timers at the values fixed at its confirmation.
10. [Ubiquitous] The Booking workflows module shall schedule the same timers from the same parameters for a booking at
    a site of any supply pattern.
11. [Event] When the Pilot migration & cut-over component requests the workflow of a carried-over booking (SS-421
    clause 10), the Booking workflows module shall start one workflow for it, keyed by the booking id and with no
    hold-expiry timer, and handle it under clauses 3 and 6 as a booking confirmed at that moment with the parameter
    version recorded on it (SS-003 clause 9).
