# SS-418 · Status re-classification with original value, rule and evidence; pre-2026-02-01 flag

As the Pilot migration & cut-over component, I need migrated outcomes re-classified so that rebooked, no-show and
cancelled are distinct, each re-classified record keeping its original status with the rule and evidence used, and
records from before 2026-02-01 flagged, so that data-room figures are not inflated by pilot rebooks or skewed by early
status values, and every changed figure can be traced to its evidence.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall give every migrated booking one outcome in which
   rebooked, no-show and cancelled are distinct, and keep the record's original status beside it.
2. [Event] When a migration run classifies a record whose original status is no-show and whose site's pilot Sheet shows
   the same driver placed in another slot that day after a charger fault, the Pilot migration & cut-over component
   shall set its outcome to rebooked and attach the rule applied, with its id and version, and the Sheet evidence.
3. [Event] When a migration run classifies a record whose original status is cancelled and whose comment says the
   driver did not arrive, the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall set its outcome to no-show and attach the
   rule applied, with its id and version, and the comment as evidence.
4. [Unwanted] If no documented re-classification rule matches a record on its evidence, then the Pilot migration &
   cut-over component shall give it the outcome its original status maps to and attach no rule or evidence.
5. [Unwanted] If anyone tries to change or remove a migrated booking's original status, rule or evidence, then the
   Pilot migration & cut-over component shall refuse it and leave them unchanged.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall flag for data quality every migrated booking whose slot
   started before 2026-02-01 in the site's time zone, and no booking whose slot started on or after that date, whether
   or not it was re-classified.

## Notes
- P3.4 critique: clauses 2 and 3 had a condition as their trigger; they now start from the migration run.
- Covers US-402 S1 (clause 2), S2 (3), S3 and S7 (4: no evidence, and a real cancellation, both keep the outcome their
  status maps to), S4 (5), S5 (6), S6 (2, 6).
- "Rebooked" is the glossary term for the pilot's "moved" bookings (REQ-228). Evidence in the sources: Airtable still
  shows bookings rebooked after the Gowanus fault as no-shows (SRC-C2@00:52:53); early no-shows were "cancelled" with a
  comment (SRC-C2@00:05:22).
- The rules come from a documented, versioned list that Priya Nair validates on the raw export (Q-020, Q-029).
  Depends on CF-018 (resolved-provisional) and A-026.
- US-402 S5 is written in Eastern time; the three pilot sites are all in Eastern time (A-013), and #49 applies the
  boundary to the slot start in the site's time zone.
- Reporting with or without pilot data: REQ-225 (Reporting); the data-room report: REQ-095.
