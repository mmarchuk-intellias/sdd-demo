# SS-326 · Two-tap booking from the open-slot view with presets, SoC optional

As the Driver PWA, I need booking from the open-slot view to take two taps with site, vehicle and duration preset, and
to ask for nothing about the vehicle except an optional SoC, so that booking fits between two rides and works for cabs
with no telematics.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a driver session with full scope opens the open-slot view, the Driver PWA shall show the free slots for
   the preset site, vehicle and duration (SS-022), each preset changeable.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall book with exactly two taps from the open-slot view: a tap on a free slot places the
   hold, and a tap on confirm confirms it.
3. [Event] When the driver changes the site, the vehicle or the duration, the Driver PWA shall show the free slots for
   the changed values and book with them.
4. [Unwanted] If the hold is refused because the slot was taken, then the Driver PWA shall tell the driver the slot was
   taken and show the slots free at that moment.
5. [Unwanted] If the confirmation is refused because the hold expired, then the Driver PWA shall tell the driver the
   hold expired and show the slots free at that moment.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall send one idempotency key with a confirmation and the same key with every repeat of
   it, so a second tap or a network retry shows the same booking.
7. [Event] When the confirmation succeeds, the Driver PWA shall show the confirmed booking.
8. [Unwanted] If no compatible slot is free for the presets on that day, then the Driver PWA shall say so and offer to
   change the site, the vehicle or the duration.
9. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall offer the SoC as an optional percentage from 0 to 100 that adds no tap and never
   blocks the confirmation.
10. [Unwanted] If the entered SoC is not a valid percentage, then the Driver PWA shall refuse the value and keep the
    confirmation available without it.
11. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall ask for no location or vehicle data other than the optional SoC at any booking
    step.
12. [Event] When a booking is confirmed with an SoC, the Driver PWA shall show that SoC on the booking.
13. [Event] When a session with limited scope asks for the open-slot view, the Driver PWA shall ask for the one-time
    code first (SS-321).
14. [Event] When a refused hold comes back with a same-site offer held for the driver (SS-002 clause 3, SS-009
    clause 2), the Driver PWA shall also show the offered slot, marked as held for the driver until its hold expires,
    with confirm available on it.
