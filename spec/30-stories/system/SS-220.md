# SS-220 · Live site board: bookings by state, EVSE status with age, open exceptions

As the ops console, I need to show one live board per site for the current day with its bookings by state, each EVSE's
status with its age, and the site's open exceptions, so that ops no longer keep a hand-coloured sheet per site open.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user opens a site's board, the ops console shall show the site's bookings of the current day
   grouped by state, each EVSE of the site with its status, observed-at time and stale flag or attendant update time
   (SS-202), and the site's open exceptions.
2. [Event] When a booking at the site changes state, an EVSE status changes or turns stale, or an exception at the site
   is raised or closed, the ops console shall update an open board without the ops user reloading it.
3. [State] While the site's status feed is unavailable, the ops console shall show every EVSE of the site on the board
   as stale with its age, together with the site's open status-feed-down exception.
4. [Ubiquitous] The ops console shall take the board's current day and show its times in the site's time zone, the
   current day being the bookings whose slot starts on the current calendar day there or runs into it.
5. [Unwanted] If an open board loses its live update connection, then the ops console shall mark the board as not live
   with the time of its last update until the connection is back and the board has been refreshed.

## Notes
- Covers US-125 S1 – S6 (S3 and S5 through the SS-202 status read; S4 with SS-203).
- Clause 5 guards the risk the board replaces: a view that silently stops updating looks live. It is the board's own
  failure path; staleness of each EVSE status stays SS-202.
- Time zones: A-013, REQ-151 (SS-021). LIC status depends on the attendant view (U-13; A-021, Q-049).
