# SS-022 · Booking presets for the driver page and text booking

As the Booking engine, I need to preset the site, the vehicle and the duration of a driver's booking and read a texted
time in the site's time zone, so that a driver books in two taps from the open-slot view, or with one text, without
filling anything in.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a registered driver's open-slot view is requested without a site, a vehicle or a duration, the Booking
   engine shall preset the vehicle of the driver's last booking if the driver may still book it, else the only vehicle
   the driver may book; the site of the booking the message link was sent about, else the site of the driver's last
   booking; and the shortest of that site's configured booking durations.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall let every open-slot query and hold request override each preset with another
   partner site, another vehicle the driver may book or another of the site's configured durations.
3. [Event] When a text booking names a site code and a time but no vehicle or duration, the Booking engine shall use the
   vehicle preset of clause 1 and the site's shortest configured duration, and read the time as the site's local time.
4. [Event] When a texted time is earlier than the current local time at the site, the Booking engine shall use the next
   occurrence of that time, on the following day, and return the date with the result.
5. [Unwanted] If no vehicle can be preset because the driver may book more than one vehicle and has no last booking
   with a vehicle still allowed, then the Booking engine shall book nothing from a text booking and answer
   VEHICLE_CHOICE_NEEDED, so the reply asks the driver to pick one through the link.
6. [Unwanted] If no site can be preset for the open-slot view, then the Booking engine shall return the live partner
   sites with an EVSE compatible with the preset vehicle for the driver to pick one, and hold nothing.

## Notes
- Covers US-212 S1 and S2 (presets, changeable) and US-214 S1 (the vehicle and duration a text booking uses). The two
  taps themselves are SS-326; the text parsing and replies are SS-311.
- Settles bucket-5 #25: preset the vehicle of the driver's last booking (or the only vehicle the fleet allows), the
  site of the last booking and the site's shortest configured duration, all changeable (REQ-100); a text time means its
  next occurrence in the site's time zone and the reply names the date; if no vehicle can be preset, the reply asks the
  driver to pick one through the link and books nothing.
- A driver's first booking on the PWA without a message about a booking has no site to preset (clause 6), so it takes
  more than two taps; no requirement covers it (GAP in ss-log-1).
- Which vehicles the driver may book: SS-006, SS-045. Site codes are onboarding data (SS-028).
