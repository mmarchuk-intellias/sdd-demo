# SS-234 · Fault cost per fault: bookings rebooked, offered alternatives or sent to ops

As the reporting module, I need to show a station operator, for each charger fault at its sites, how many bookings the
fault affected and what became of them, each booking counted once, so that the station operator can show its
maintenance team what each fault costs.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a station operator opens a site's faults, the reporting module shall list each fault with its EVSE, the
   time it started (its fault event, SS-208), the time it ended (its return to service, SS-209) and the total of
   bookings in that fault's per-booking outcomes (SS-108 clause 8).
2. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall count each booking in a fault's per-booking outcomes once, in exactly one
   category by its final outcome as emitted (SS-108 clauses 8 and 10): rebooked by the platform at the site, rebooked to
   an alternative its driver accepted, or handled by ops (every other final outcome of a booking handed to unplaced
   handling).
3. [Unwanted] If a booking at the site was rebooked for another cause, such as a full site or a maintenance hold, while
   the EVSE was faulted, then the reporting module shall leave it out of the fault's counts, because it is not among
   that fault's per-booking outcomes.
4. [State] While a fault is ongoing, the reporting module shall show it as ongoing, with counts that include every
   booking affected so far.
5. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall show with each fault, apart from the outcome categories, how many of its
   bookings' drivers were offered alternatives, that is the bookings handed to unplaced handling (SS-109).

## Notes
- Covers US-314 S1, S2, S4 and S5. S3 (other station operators' faults) is SS-323 (REQ-258).
- Settles bucket-5 item #40: each fault-affected booking counts once, by its final outcome (rebooked at the site,
  accepted an alternative, with ops), next to the total. Every booking the platform could not place was offered
  alternatives and sent to ops (REQ-049, REQ-050), so REQ-080's "offered alternatives" is read as the drivers who
  took one; the rest are "handled by ops".
- A fault runs from its fault event (SS-208) to its return to service (SS-209). Rebooking: SS-108, SS-109, SS-110.
- Depends on A-017, CF-022 and A-050 (Q-038).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28, cross-batch fix): clauses 1 – 3 count from the per-booking outcomes of SS-108 clauses 8 and 10 (also
  emitted for an ops one-action fault, SS-112) instead of recomputing them. Clause 5 covers US-314 S1's "how many
  drivers were offered alternatives" as a figure beside the three categories of bucket-5 item #40, so no booking counts
  twice.
