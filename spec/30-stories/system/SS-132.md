# SS-132 · Cancellation before the site's cut-off carries no no-show fee

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to keep a booking cancelled before its site's cancellation cut-off free of
any no-show fee and to send a later cancellation to ops for a fee decision, so that a driver can give a slot back
without paying for it and the EVSE returns to walk-ups before its slot.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a booking is cancelled before the cancellation cut-off fixed for it at confirmation, the Arrival &
   no-show component shall request no no-show fee for it.
2. [Event] When the Booking engine records a cancellation as a late cancellation (SS-013 clause 6), the Arrival &
   no-show component shall request no no-show fee line for it automatically.
3. [Event] When the Booking engine records a late cancellation (SS-013 clause 6), the Arrival & no-show component shall
   raise a 'fee decision' ops exception for the booking (SS-131) naming the late cancellation, for ops to create or
   waive the fee with a reason code (SS-131 clause 3).
4. [Unwanted] If a cancellation is requested for a booking already released as a no-show, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall keep the booking a no-show and leave its fee decision and fee line unchanged.

## Notes
- Q-042 is covered by A-049: the cut-off is a per-site value that defaults to the slot start; a cancellation after it is
  a late cancellation that goes to ops as a fee decision, with no fee line created automatically (clause 3). The cut-off
  in force at confirmation is the one the confirmation states (US-112 S1, SS-300).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clause 2 recorded the late cancellation in the booking's history, as SS-013 clause 6 already
  does on the same trigger (two components, one history entry each). The Booking engine keeps that record; clauses 2
  and 3 now act on it.
- Q-002: the payer is undecided; the rule holds in every option. The cancel command itself, refused for a booking that
  is no longer active, is SS-013.
