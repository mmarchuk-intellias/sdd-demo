# SS-033 · Reminder at the site's lead time to the charging driver

As the Booking workflows module, I need to request one reminder for each booking at its site's reminder lead time
before the slot, addressed to the charging driver, so that a driver out on rides heads to the site in time, or cancels
or reports running late while the slot can still go to someone else.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a confirmed booking's reminder lead time before its slot start is reached, using the lead time of its
   site fixed at confirmation, the Booking workflows module shall request Messaging to send one reminder for that
   booking to its charging driver.
2. [Unwanted] If a booking is confirmed, or rebooked to a new slot, after the reminder point for its slot has passed,
   then the Booking workflows module shall request no reminder for that slot.
3. [Unwanted] If the reminder timer fires late, for example after planned downtime, then the Booking workflows module
   shall request the reminder once, unless the slot has already started.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Booking workflows module shall request at most one reminder per booking and slot.

## Notes
- Covers JS-224 S1, S4, S5, S6. S2 and S3 (no reminder after a cancellation; the reminder moves with a rebooking) are
  SS-032. Content, language, link and channel are SS-302 (REQ-063, REQ-199); a STOP reply blocks the channel (SS-304).
- Settles bucket-5 #30: a booking made after its reminder point gets no separate reminder; the confirmation stands in
  for it (clause 2). For a rebooking, the allocation notice or the acceptance reply stands in.
- Clause 3's "unless the slot has already started" keeps a reminder from arriving after the booking began; the grace
  and fee rules for a late timer are SS-127 and SS-131 (REQ-180).
- A-011: the lead time is a per-site parameter (SS-026); its default is in design.md §8 (A-059).
