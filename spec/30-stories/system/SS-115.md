# SS-115 · Gowanus fault replay passes on the simulator (nine bookings at the shift change)

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to pass an automated replay of the Gowanus fault on the simulator, so
that fault rebooking is proven on the case that cost ops an afternoon and stays proven release after release.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the replay faults the simulated Gowanus EVSE at 1:50 PM ET with its nine affected bookings around the
   shift change, the Allocation & rebooking component shall end with each of the nine bookings either rebooked at
   Gowanus or handed to unplaced handling with alternatives offered to its driver.
2. [Event] When the replay runs, the Allocation & rebooking component shall rebook each of the two bookings whose
   vehicles need NACS or an adapter only to an EVSE with a connector that vehicle can use, and offer it only such
   alternatives.
3. [Event] When the replay runs, the Allocation & rebooking component shall record none of the nine bookings as a
   no-show.
4. [Event] When the replay's fault has been handled, the Allocation & rebooking component shall return a fault summary
   (SS-111) that lists all nine bookings with their outcomes.
5. [Event] When the replay's fault has been handled, the Allocation & rebooking component shall have emitted the outcome
   of each of the nine bookings for the station operator's view (SS-227).
6. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall pass the replay on every release that changes booking,
   inventory or workflows (constitution D6).
7. [Unwanted] If the replay ends with any of the nine bookings neither rebooked nor handed to unplaced handling, or
   recorded as a no-show, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall fail the replay, which blocks the release
   (constitution D6).
