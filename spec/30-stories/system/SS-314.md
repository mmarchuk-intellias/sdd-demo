# SS-314 · Ops message to a driver on the driver's channel, recorded on the booking

As the Messaging component, I need to send an ops user's templated or free-text message to a driver on the driver's
channel and record it on the booking, and to tell a driver who raised a dispute the decision ops recorded, so that
ops can answer or warn a driver from the console instead of texting by hand.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user sends a templated message about a booking to its charging driver or booker, the Messaging
   component shall send it to that person on their preferred channel, in their language.
2. [Event] When an ops user sends a free-text message about a booking to its charging driver or booker, the Messaging
   component shall send the text as written on that person's preferred channel.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall record each ops message against the booking with the ops user, the
   template or the text, the recipient, the channel, the time and the delivery status.
4. [Unwanted] If no agreement is in force on the recipient's preferred channel (SS-304), then the Messaging component
   shall not send the message on that channel.
5. [Unwanted] If the sender of an ops message (clauses 1, 2) is not signed in with an ops role, then the Messaging
   component shall refuse to send the message.
6. [Event] When the Messaging component does not send an ops message for want of agreement (clause 4), the Messaging
   component shall tell the ops user why and whether another channel of the recipient has an agreement in force.
7. [Event] When the Arrival & no-show component requests a dispute decision notice for a dispute a driver raised
   (SS-134 clause 9), the Messaging component shall send that driver one notice with the decision and its reason, in
   the driver's stored language (SS-303) on the driver's channel (SS-308).

## Notes
- The driver search that finds the booking is SS-221 (US-126 S1, S2, S4, S6). Templates exist in English, Spanish and
  Bengali (SS-302); free text is sent as the ops user wrote it.
- US-126 S5 follows REQ-248 (SS-304). The full message history with delivery status is REQ-196 (I) and is not assumed;
  clause 3 keeps only the ops messages on the booking.
- Channels: SMS, and WhatsApp once enabled (SS-308; U-10, Q-034, A-008). Views of a driver's data are logged by SS-335.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clause 4 split into clauses 4 and 6 (US-126 S5).
- P3.4 final pass (2026-10-28, handoff from critic X): SS-134 clause 9 requests the decision notice for the raiser, and
  no Messaging SS delivered it (US-113 S2). Clause 7 sends it to a driver who raised the dispute; the agreement gate
  (clause 4, SS-304) applies to it as to any message. Clause 5 now names the ops messages of clauses 1 and 2, so it does
  not refuse the notice, which no signed-in person sends. Parent US-113 and requirement REQ-045 added (both Y; KYIV

  fleet manager receives the decision") gets no message: the fleet portal shows the decision (SS-224 clause 6, A-062).
