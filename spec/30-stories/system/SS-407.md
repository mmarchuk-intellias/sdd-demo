# SS-407 · Session matching: booking to session record within the set time

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need every session record a CSMS integration delivers stored and matched, within
the configured session match time, to the booking that charged on its EVSE and window, with its energy, start and end,
and every booking that ends with no record marked as such, so that invoicing and no-show decisions rest on the station
operator's own record of the charge.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a CSMS integration delivers a session record (SS-213, SS-215), the Ledger & invoicing component shall
   store it with its source, external id, EVSE, start, end, energy and the time it was received, retrievable by EVSE
   and time window.
2. [Event] When a session record is stored, the Ledger & invoicing component shall, within the configured session match
   time, match it to the booking with a recorded arrival that was allocated to that EVSE and whose window, from the
   start of its arrival window to its end, contains the record's start, and keep the record's energy in kWh, start and
   end with the match.
3. [Event] When a match is made, the Ledger & invoicing component shall emit a session-matched event with the booking
   id and the record's energy, start and end, for Reporting (SS-229).
4. [Unwanted] If a session record arrives again from the same source with the same external id, then the Ledger &
   invoicing component shall keep one stored record, keep the booking matched to it, and add nothing to the
   reconciliation queue.
5. [Unwanted] If more than one booking, or more than one session record, qualifies for one match, then the Ledger &
   invoicing component shall make no automatic match and hand each of them to the reconciliation queue (SS-408).
6. [Event] When the window of a booking with a recorded arrival ends and no session record is matched to it, also at a
   site whose CSMS provides no session records, the Ledger & invoicing component shall mark the booking as without a
   session record, which opens its reconciliation case (SS-408 clause 1).
