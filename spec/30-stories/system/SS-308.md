# SS-308 · WhatsApp channel: booking and booking messages, templates outside the 24-hour window

As the Messaging component, I need to carry booking requests, replies and booking messages on WhatsApp for drivers who
prefer it, within WhatsApp's template rule, so that those drivers deal with Amperlane in the messaging app they already
use.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Optional] Where the WhatsApp channel is enabled, the Messaging component shall process an inbound WhatsApp message
   from a registered number as it processes an SMS (SS-309 – SS-312) and reply on WhatsApp.
2. [Optional] Where the WhatsApp channel is enabled, the Messaging component shall send booking messages on WhatsApp, in
   the driver's language (SS-303), to a driver whose preferred channel is WhatsApp and who has an agreement in force on
   WhatsApp.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall reply to an inbound message on the channel it arrived on.
4. [Event] When the Messaging component sends a WhatsApp message, the Messaging component shall check at that moment
   whether the driver's last inbound WhatsApp message is less than 24 hours old, and otherwise send the message as a
   pre-approved template in the driver's language.
5. [Unwanted] If no approved template exists for that message in the driver's language, then the Messaging component
   shall report the message undelivered on WhatsApp (SS-307) instead of sending it as a free-form message.
6. [State] While the WhatsApp channel is not enabled, the Messaging component shall send every driver message by SMS.
7. [State] While the WhatsApp channel is not enabled, the Messaging component shall refuse WhatsApp as a driver's
   preferred channel.

## Notes
- R7: WhatsApp is unconfirmed (integrations.md U-10; Q-034); Twilio stays the provider (A-008). "Enabled" is a
  platform setting switched on once the Twilio WhatsApp sender and the templates in English, Spanish and Bengali are
  approved (external dependency). Clause 6 is the fallback: SMS only.
- Clause 4 judges the window when the message is sent, not when it was scheduled (US-215 S6).
- Undelivered WhatsApp messages and the resend to SMS: SS-307 (US-215 S4, S5). Booking by WhatsApp works like SMS:
  text booking SS-311, keywords SS-310 (US-215 S1).
- Who sets a driver's preferred channel is not stated by any requirement (GAP in the Step 3.3 log).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clause 6 split into clauses 6 and 7; clause 5 reworded to one response.
