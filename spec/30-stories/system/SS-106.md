# SS-106 · Blocked-stall reallocation to a free compatible EVSE at the site

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to reallocate a booking whose charging driver reports the allocated
stall blocked on arrival to another free compatible EVSE at the site, so that the driver can charge there instead of
waiting for ops.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a blocked-stall report is stored for an active allocated booking (SS-133) and another EVSE at the site
   is free and compatible, the Allocation & rebooking component shall reallocate the booking to that EVSE (SS-101) and
   emit the reallocation event of SS-104, so that the station hold is placed on it and the charging driver is told the
   new EVSE.
2. [Unwanted] If no other compatible EVSE at the site is free when a blocked-stall report is stored, then the Allocation
   & rebooking component shall keep the booking's allocation and request no station hold on another EVSE.
3. [Unwanted] If two blocked-stall reports at a site compete for the only free compatible EVSE, then the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall reallocate exactly one of the two bookings to it and keep the other on its allocation.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall leave the blocked-stall exception and the failed-station-hold
   record of SS-133 in place whether or not it reallocates the booking.

## Notes
- CR-008: "If the stall is taken, move the booking to another free one" (SRC-C2@00:24:04) applied to a stall the driver
  reports blocked. Q-005: liability for a blocked stall is a partner-agreement term.
- Clause 3 relies on SS-102 and the database constraint of SS-103 (JS-020 S7).
- The exception and its stay in the queue (JS-020 S1, S6) are SS-133's; the station hold on the new EVSE uses that
  EVSE's method (SS-116, SS-118).
