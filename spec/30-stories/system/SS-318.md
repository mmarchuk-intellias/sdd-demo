# SS-318 · Driver identity: the registered mobile number only, with entitlement across fleets

As the Identity & access component, I need to identify a driver by the mobile number a fleet registered and to know
which vehicles that number may book, so that a driver books from the first shift with no sign-up, password, email or
card.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall identify a driver only by a mobile number in E.164 form that is
   registered to at least one fleet.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall require no sign-up form, password, email address or payment card
   from a driver on any channel.
3. [Event] When a message arrives from a number or a driver session acts, the Identity & access component shall resolve
   the number to its driver and give the booking engine the vehicles that each fleet the number is registered to allows
   that driver to book (SS-006).
4. [Unwanted] If a hold or booking command comes from a number registered to no fleet, then the Identity & access
   component shall refuse it and offer no sign-up or account.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall identify the driver from the registered number whichever phone it
   is used in, so a driver who puts the number in a new phone is identified as before.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall need no SoC, location or other vehicle data to identify a driver
   or to decide what the driver may book.

## Notes
- Depends on A-006 (identity by registered number, signed links), A-016 (several fleets), A-023 (fleets or ops register
  numbers) and Q-028.
- The booking records the fleet whose vehicle it uses and refuses a vehicle the policy does not allow: SS-006 (US-218
  S2, S4). Registration: SS-037, SS-040; booker-only numbers: SS-042.
- On a new phone, booking by text needs nothing more (US-218 S5); a booking page opened there shows the limited view
  until a one-time code is completed (SS-321). The reply to an unregistered number is SS-312.
