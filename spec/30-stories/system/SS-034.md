# SS-034 · Durable booking timers that survive restarts and take effect once

As the Booking workflows module, I need every booking timer to be durable and every step it triggers to take effect
exactly once, so that restarts and the overnight maintenance window never lose a hold expiry, a reminder, a station
hold or a release, and never apply one twice.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Booking workflows module shall run every booking timer (hold expiry, reminder, allocation, station
   hold, pre-arrival check and end of grace) as a durable timer of the booking's workflow, never as a scheduled job or
   an in-process timer.
2. [Event] When the workflow engine or its workers restart with timers pending, the Booking workflows module shall fire
   each pending timer after the restart, with none lost.
3. [Unwanted] If a timer's due time passes while the workflow engine or its workers are stopped, such as during planned
   downtime between 01:00 and 05:00 ET, then the Booking workflows module shall fire it as soon as they are running
   again and run its step once.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Booking workflows module shall call each step with a key made of the workflow id and the step, so
   that a retried or replayed step changes nothing a second time.
5. [Event] When a timer fires later than its due time, the Booking workflows module shall pass the due time and the
   actual time to the step, so the step can apply its rule for a late timer.
6. [Unwanted] If the workflow engine cannot be reached when a hold or booking is created, then the Booking workflows
   module shall start the booking's workflow once the engine can be reached, and fire at once any timer that fell due
   in the meantime.

## Notes
- Enabler for REQ-133 (no story). Constitution N5 and N10; ADR-007; A-028 (timers may fire late in the maintenance
  window but are never lost and take effect once).
- Clause 5 feeds the late-timer rules: a grace timer that fired late sends the fee decision to ops (REQ-180, SS-131)
  and never releases a booking with an arrival (REQ-177, SS-127). Code conventions rows 8 and 9.
- Verified by the staging test of N5 (stop the engine and workers with timers pending, restart, each fires once) and
  by workflow replay tests in CI.
