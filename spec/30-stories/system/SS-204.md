# SS-204 · Silent EVSE: unknown and out of allocation after the silence time, back on a non-fault status

As the EVSE status service, I need to mark an EVSE that falls silent while the rest of its site keeps reporting as
unknown, keep it out of new allocations and alert ops, and return it as soon as it reports a non-fault status again, so
that no driver is sent to an EVSE nobody can vouch for.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an EVSE at a connected site has received no status for the site's configured silence time, during all
   of which the site's status feed was available, the EVSE status service shall set the EVSE's status to unknown and
   exclude it from new allocations.
2. [Event] When the EVSE status service sets an EVSE to unknown under the silence rule, the EVSE status service shall
   raise one ops exception of type "silent EVSE" naming the site, the EVSE and the time it was last heard from.
3. [Unwanted] If an EVSE already set to unknown under the silence rule stays silent, then the EVSE status service shall
   raise no further silent-EVSE exception for it.
4. [State] While a connected site's status feed is unavailable (SS-203), the EVSE status service shall not apply the
   silence rule to any EVSE of that site.
5. [Optional] Where a site's live status source is the attendant view, the EVSE status service shall not apply the
   silence rule to its EVSEs.
6. [Event] When an EVSE set to unknown under the silence rule, and not excluded after a fault before it fell silent,
   reports a status other than faulted, the EVSE status service shall apply that status and return the EVSE to new
   allocations at once.
7. [Unwanted] If an EVSE set to unknown under the silence rule reports faulted, then the EVSE status service shall
   apply it as a fault (SS-208) and keep the EVSE out of new allocations until it returns to service (SS-209).
8. [Unwanted] If an EVSE that was excluded after a fault (SS-208) falls silent and later reports a status other than
   faulted, then the EVSE status service shall return it to new allocations only under the return-to-service rule of
   SS-209.

## Notes
- Covers JS-210 S3, S5, S6 (with SS-203) and S7.
- Settles bucket-5 item #35: a silent EVSE returns to allocation when it reports a non-fault status again; a faulted
  EVSE follows REQ-194 (SS-209).
- REQ-169 v2 (CR-004): the rule applies only while other EVSEs at the site still report. "Feed available" is the
  negation of SS-203 clause 1, so at least one EVSE of the site has a status age of 60 seconds or less. Clause 1 counts
  the silence time only while the feed is available, so an EVSE is not marked unknown the moment a feed comes back.
  The silence time must therefore be longer than 60 seconds.
- Bookings already allocated to the EVSE are left to the pre-arrival check and reallocation (SS-104, batch 2); REQ-169
  excludes it from new allocations only.
- Parameter: the site's configured silence time (REQ-169 "set time"; JS-210 S3 calls it the site's). Default in
  design.md §8 (A-059); the catalogue is SS-026.
- How AMPECO and ChargeLab report an EVSE that lost its connection (an offline status, a last-seen time, or no status)
  is not known: Q-061 (2). Who acts at night: Q-023.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 6 conflicted with SS-209 clause 1 for an EVSE that faulted and then fell silent (return at
  once versus after the stable-available time); clause 6 is narrowed and clause 8 hands that case to SS-209. Clause 2
  names the component.
