# SS-324 · Attendant site-scoped login on a site device, revocable by ops

As the Identity & access component, I need each site-staff member to have a personal login scoped to one site that ops
can revoke at once, so that the attendant acts on Amperlane bookings at the site under their own name and without
anyone else's access.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user creates a site-staff login, the Identity & access component shall create a personal login
   for one named person, scoped to one site, with the site-staff role.
2. [Event] When a site-staff user signs in on the site's device, the Identity & access component shall open a session
   limited to that site (SS-323).
3. [Event] When an ops user revokes a site-staff login, the Identity & access component shall refuse the next request
   of every open session of that login and every later sign-in with it.
4. [Unwanted] If a site-staff session asks for bookings or EVSEs of another site, then the Identity & access component
   shall refuse the request.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall attach the signed-in staff member's identity to every action of
   a site-staff session as its actor, so two staff members who share a device are recorded apart.
