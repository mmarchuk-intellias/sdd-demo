# SS-117 · Charger reservation rejected or unanswered: site-staff fallback and ops exception, booking unchanged

As the Station-hold dispatcher, I need to fall back to site staff and alert ops when a charger reservation is rejected
or unanswered, without changing the booking, so that the EVSE is still held when the driver arrives and Amperlane's slot
calendar stays the only judge of a booking.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Unwanted] If the CSMS rejects a charger reservation and the EVSE's status does not show it in use, then the
   Station-hold dispatcher shall send site staff a station-hold request for that EVSE's stall (SS-118) and raise a
   'charger reservation failed' ops exception for the booking (SS-219).
2. [Unwanted] If no answer to a charger reservation request arrives within the site's configured reservation answer
   timeout, then the Station-hold dispatcher shall send site staff a station-hold request for that EVSE's stall (SS-118)
   and raise a 'charger reservation failed' ops exception for the booking, marked as unanswered.
3. [Unwanted] If the CSMS rejects a charger reservation while the EVSE's status shows it in use, then the Station-hold
   dispatcher shall send no site-staff request for that EVSE and report the rejection to the Allocation & rebooking
   component for reallocation or site-full handling (SS-104, SS-105).
4. [Ubiquitous] The Station-hold dispatcher shall leave a booking's state, allocation, grace and auto-release unchanged
   when its charger reservation is rejected, unanswered, expired or cancelled at the CSMS.
5. [Event] When the CSMS integration passes a late acceptance of a charger reservation (SS-212) after the Station-hold
   dispatcher has sent the fallback site-staff request, the Station-hold dispatcher shall record the late acceptance,
   keep the site-staff request standing, and have both cleared when the booking ends (SS-120).
