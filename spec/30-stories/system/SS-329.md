# SS-329 · Times shown in the site's time zone with the zone indicated

As the Driver PWA, I need to show every booking time in the site's local time with the zone indicated, so that the
driver arrives at the right time across a daylight-saving change or with a phone set to another zone.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall show every booking time, slot start and end included, in the site's time zone
   with the zone abbreviation that applies at that instant.
2. [Unwanted] If the phone is set to a time zone other than the site's, then the Driver PWA shall still show every
   booking time in the site's time zone.
3. [Event] When a booking time falls in the hour that the autumn daylight-saving change repeats, the Driver PWA shall
   show it with the abbreviation that tells the two occurrences of that hour apart.
4. [Event] When a booking spans the spring daylight-saving change, the Driver PWA shall show its end in the site's
   local time after the change, so the booking shows its booked duration.

## Notes
- Depends on A-013 (per-site time zone; times stored as instants). Instants and daylight-saving arithmetic are SS-021;
  times in messages are SS-302 (clause 5).
- The daylight-saving changes fall at night, so clauses 3 and 4 rely on overnight bookings: REQ-152, A-028 (CF-015 is
  needs-decision).
