# SS-108 · Fault rebooking at the same site: allocated bookings rebooked, later bookings re-checked

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to rebook the bookings of a faulted EVSE to other compatible EVSEs at
the same site and re-check the rest of the day's bookings against the EVSEs left, so that drivers still charge at the
time they booked and nobody is recorded as a no-show because of a fault.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the EVSE status service emits a fault event for an EVSE (SS-208), the Allocation & rebooking component
   shall rebook each active booking allocated to that EVSE, in order of slot start, to another free compatible EVSE at
   the same site (SS-101), with the reason code 'moved — charger fault' and the system as actor, and emit the
   reallocation event of SS-104 for it, so that the old station hold is cleared (SS-120), the station hold is placed on
   the new EVSE (SS-116, SS-118) and the charging driver is told the new EVSE (SS-301).
2. [Event] When a fault event is emitted for an EVSE, the Allocation & rebooking component shall re-check each confirmed
   booking at the site with a slot later that day (in the site's time zone) and no allocation yet against the site's
   remaining compatible EVSEs, using the pool check of SS-003 without the faulted EVSE and with the bookable cap
   unchanged (A-050), and leave unchanged each booking that still fits.
3. [Unwanted] If a booking re-checked under clause 2 no longer fits, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall
   hand it to unplaced handling (SS-109) before its allocation offset is reached, taking the bookings confirmed last
   when only some of the overlapping bookings still fit.
4. [Unwanted] If no other compatible EVSE at the site is free for a booking allocated to the faulted EVSE, then the
   Allocation & rebooking component shall keep the booking on the faulted EVSE and hand it to unplaced handling
   (SS-109).
5. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall record no no-show for a booking because it was rebooked after
   a fault.
6. [Event] When the Allocation & rebooking component rebooks a booking after a fault, the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall make the new allocation the booking's only active allocation, so that arrival and grace are judged
   against it: a session start on the new EVSE inside the arrival window records arrival and one on the faulted EVSE
   does not (SS-125).
7. [Unwanted] If a fault event arrives for an EVSE whose fault rebooking has already started, then the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall rebook no booking a second time.
8. [Event] When the Allocation & rebooking component has handled every booking affected by a fault, the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall record and emit one outcome per affected booking, rebooked (from which EVSE to which) or
   handed to unplaced handling, as the only source of fault outcomes for the fault summary (SS-111), the station
   portal (SS-227) and reporting (SS-234).
9. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall apply clauses 1 – 6 with the same rule and reason code at
   sites of every supply pattern, whether the fault came from the CSMS or from the attendant.
10. [Event] When a booking whose outcome under clause 8 was "handed to unplaced handling" is later rebooked, to an
    alternative its driver accepted (SS-109 clause 4) or by ops (SS-110 clause 2, SS-114), or released by ops (SS-016
    clause 3), the Allocation & rebooking component shall record and emit that booking's final outcome, with the new
    site, time and EVSE where it has one, to the same consumers.

## Notes
- A-017 (automatic same-site rebooking; CF-022 resolved-provisional), A-050 (covers Q-038: a fault does not lower the
  bookable cap; a rebooking may use any free compatible EVSE, never one a walk-up is using). Constitution R6 holds
  through A-050.
- The faulted EVSE leaves new allocations through SS-208, which also raises the per-EVSE "fault" exception (REQ-189);
  this SS raises per-booking exceptions only, through SS-109. The saga that guarantees every affected booking ends
  rebooked, proposed or on its EVSE with an exception after a restart (JS-119 S5) is SS-036.
- Clause 3's "confirmed last" follows the rule A-055 sets for lowered limits; no requirement states which booking yields
  (GAP in the Step 3.3 log).
- A rebooked booking is excluded from an automatic no-show fee as "rebooked" (REQ-180, SS-131); the unplaced ones carry
  the "site faulted" exclusion (SS-109).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 1 now emits the reallocation event; before, nothing told the charging driver the new EVSE
  (JS-119 S1) or moved the station hold, unlike every other rebooking path (SS-104, SS-106, SS-107, SS-111). Clause 8 is
  the single source of per-booking fault outcomes; clause 10 adds the final outcome of an unplaced booking, which
  SS-111 (US-122 S1 "offered alternatives"), SS-227 clause 3 and SS-234 ("final outcome") need. SS-112 and SS-113 apply
  clauses 8 and 10 to the one-action and the maintenance paths.
- US-412 S1 (system actor, charger-fault code, no no-show anywhere) is clauses 1 and 5; US-205 S4 is clause 9; JS-330 S3
  is clause 1. The station operator's notice is SS-313; the Gowanus replay is SS-115.
