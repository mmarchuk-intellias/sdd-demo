# SS-109 · Unplaced fault-affected bookings: driver offers and ops exception within the set times

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to offer the driver of each fault-affected booking I could not place
alternatives at that site and nearby, and to put the booking in the ops queue with those offers and the driver's answer,
so that the driver chooses where to charge and ops sees every booking the fault left open.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fault-affected booking is handed to unplaced handling (SS-108, SS-112, SS-113), the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall request, within the configured fault offer time after the fault, an offer to its charging
   driver of the next free compatible slot at the same site (SS-009) and the nearest free compatible slots at other
   sites (SS-010), each accepted with one tap or a keyword (SS-011, SS-316).
2. [Event] When a fault-affected booking is handed to unplaced handling, the Allocation & rebooking component shall
   raise an 'unplaced after fault' ops exception for it (SS-219) within the configured unplaced-booking exception time
   after the fault, showing the alternatives offered and the driver's answer so far.
3. [Event] When the charging driver accepts or declines an offered alternative, the Allocation & rebooking component
   shall add the answer and its time to the booking's exception.
4. [Event] When the charging driver accepts an offered alternative (SS-011), the Allocation & rebooking component shall
   record the rebooking with the reason code of its cause ('moved — charger fault', or 'moved — maintenance' for
   SS-113), record no no-show for the original booking, mark its exception resolved and withdraw its proposal (SS-110).
5. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall mark each unplaced fault-affected booking with the
   "site faulted" exclusion, so that no no-show fee is created for it automatically (SS-131).
6. [Unwanted] If no free compatible slot exists at the site or at nearby sites, then the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall still raise the exception within the configured unplaced-booking exception time, stating that no
   alternative could be offered.

## Notes
- A-017, CF-022, A-050 (Q-038). Q-023: who works the queue out of hours is a client decision; the exception is raised
  whoever is on duty.
- "The fault" includes the placing of a maintenance hold (SS-113, A-018), which takes the same path.
- Slot taken before the driver accepts (JS-120 S3) is SS-011; only compatible slots (JS-120 S5) are SS-009 and SS-010;
  the message language (JS-120 S6) is SS-303 and SS-316. JS-120 S4 (no answer, no automatic fee) is clauses 2 and 5.
- Proposals for ops' bulk approval are SS-110.
