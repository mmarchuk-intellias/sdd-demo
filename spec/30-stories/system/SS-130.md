# SS-130 · Ops grace extension per booking: reason code, up to the site maximum

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to let ops extend the grace period of a single booking with a reason code, up
to the site's maximum in total, so that a driver a few minutes away keeps the booking without voiding the station
operator's grace term.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user extends the grace period of an active booking with no arrival whose end of grace has not
   passed, with a reason code from the catalogue and a total extension within the site's configured maximum grace
   extension, the Arrival & no-show component shall extend the booking's end of grace by that amount and record the
   extension with the ops user, the reason code and the time.
2. [Unwanted] If the booking's total extension would exceed the site's configured maximum grace extension, then the
   Arrival & no-show component shall refuse the extension and keep the current end of grace.
3. [Unwanted] If the extension has no reason code from the catalogue, then the Arrival & no-show component shall refuse
   it.
4. [Unwanted] If the booking's end of grace has passed or the booking is no longer active, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall refuse the extension and leave the booking as it is.
5. [Event] When the Arrival & no-show component extends a booking's grace period, the Arrival & no-show component shall
   emit the new end of grace, so that the release timer (SS-032), the charger reservation's expiry (SS-116) and the
   site-staff clear time (SS-118) follow it.
6. [Event] When arrival evidence comes after the original end of grace and before the extended one, the Arrival &
   no-show component shall record it as arrival within grace.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall apply an extension only to the booking it names.

## Notes
- A-020 (extension only by an ops override with a reason code, up to a maximum set per site with its grace), A-003,
  Q-004; CF-021 is needs-decision, its other options are parked (REQ-305 – REQ-307).
- The maximum is a per-site parameter (SS-026) and caps the total extension of one booking (US-108 S6, Step 3.1 red
  team).
- The ops reason codes for an extension belong to the catalogue of SS-020.
