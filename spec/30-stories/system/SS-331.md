# SS-331 · No location request without the driver's opt-in

As the Driver PWA, I need every booking step to work without the phone's location and to read it only after the
driver has opted in, so that a driver books, arrives and charges without sharing whereabouts.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [State] While the driver has no active location opt-in, the Driver PWA shall neither call the browser's location
   interface nor prompt for location permission.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall complete every step, booking, confirming, cancelling, running late, QR arrival,
   reports and directions included, without location access.
3. [Unwanted] If the browser blocks or denies location access, then the Driver PWA shall complete every step and show
   no location prompt.
4. [Optional] Where a location feature is included (REQ-173 or REQ-175, today I), the Driver PWA shall read the phone's
   location only while the driver's opt-in is active and only between the booking's pre-slot reminder and the end of
   its grace period.
5. [Event] When the driver withdraws the location opt-in, the Driver PWA shall stop reading location at once.

## Notes
- A-024, Q-016: any location needs the driver's opt-in, off by default, and only from the pre-slot reminder until grace
  ends. In the committed plan no K or Y requirement reads location, so clauses 1 – 3 are what the release delivers;
  clause 5 applies once a location feature is admitted (constitution P7).
- Arrival without location: QR scan SS-124, session start SS-125, attendant SS-126 (US-104 S1, S5). The server-side
  guard (no location stored without opt-in, vehicle location included, US-104 S3, S4, S6) is SS-341; minimisation is
  SS-340.
- Epic: EP-06 by majority of parents; REQ-255 and REQ-204 are EP-03 (ss-plan §5.2, problem 5).
