# SS-319 · Driver removal: no booking for that fleet on any channel from the moment of removal

As the Identity & access component, I need a driver's right to book for a fleet to end the moment the fleet removes the
driver, on every channel, so that a driver whose lease has ended cannot book on the fleet's account.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet manager or ops removes a driver number from a fleet, the Identity & access component shall
   withdraw that number's entitlement for that fleet with effect from the moment the removal is saved.
2. [Unwanted] If a hold or booking command for a vehicle of that fleet comes from the removed number after the removal,
   by SMS, WhatsApp, the driver PWA or a link issued before the removal, then the Identity & access component shall
   refuse it.
3. [Unwanted] If the removed number confirms, after the removal, a hold it placed for that fleet before the removal,
   then the Identity & access component shall refuse the confirmation.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall check the entitlement on every command, not only when a link or
   session was issued.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall leave the number's entitlements for its other fleets unchanged
   when one fleet removes it.
6. [Event] When a removal is saved, the Identity & access component shall emit a driver-removed event with the number,
   the fleet, the actor and the time.

## Notes
- Depends on A-023 (fleets, or ops for small fleets, register and remove numbers) and Q-028. Ops removing a driver for
  a small fleet (US-219 S6) is the same action.
- A-052 (working assumption for Q-045): the removed driver's future bookings for the fleet stand and are listed for the
  fleet manager or ops; the removed number can no longer change them (SS-322) and gets no further messages about them
  (SS-302 clause 10). The listing itself has no SS (GAP in the Step 3.3 log); US-219 covers new bookings only.
- The removal is an audited change (constitution N17, SS-332).
