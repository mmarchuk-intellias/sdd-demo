# SS-428 · Booking availability 99.9% from 05:00 to 01:00 ET, measured by a synthetic check

As the Platform & operations component, I need making bookings to be available in 99.9% of the minutes between 05:00
and 01:00 Eastern time, measured by a synthetic booking check every minute and reported monthly, so that the agreed
availability target is met and shown, not assumed.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall keep making bookings available in at least 99.9% of the
   minutes between 05:00 and 01:00 America/New_York in each calendar month.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall run a synthetic booking check every minute (A-033), from
   outside the production network, that creates and cancels a hold on a hidden test site.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall compute monthly availability as the share of minutes between
   05:00 and 01:00 America/New_York in which the check succeeded, counting planned maintenance inside that window as
   downtime.
4. [Unwanted] If the number of consecutive failed synthetic checks reaches its configured value, then the Platform &
   operations component shall raise the availability alert to the on-call engineer.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall keep the hidden test site and its holds out of every capacity
   view, report, invoice and message.
6. [Event] When a calendar month ends, the Platform & operations component shall send the month's availability report
   to the Amperlane CTO.
