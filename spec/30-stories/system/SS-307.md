# SS-307 · Delivery tracking and one resend on the other channel for time-critical messages

As the Messaging component, I need to track the delivery of every message, resend a time-critical message once on the
driver's other channel when it does not arrive, and show ops who was not reached, so that a driver still gets the
message needed to keep the slot, and never gets two confirmations.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall keep, for every message it hands to the messaging provider, the
   provider's message id, the recipient, the channel, the message type, the booking and the latest delivery status.
2. [Event] When a delivery report arrives, the Messaging component shall apply it once per provider message id and
   status and never replace a final status (delivered or undelivered) with an earlier one (queued or sent), so
   duplicated or out-of-order reports end in the same status as in-order delivery.
3. [Event] When a confirmation, reminder, allocation notice, re-offer or fault notice is reported undelivered, the
   Messaging component shall raise an "undelivered message" ops exception (SS-219) naming the driver, the booking and
   the message.
4. [Event] When a confirmation, reminder, allocation notice, re-offer or fault notice is reported undelivered, the
   Messaging component shall resend it once on the driver's other registered channel.
5. [Event] When a reminder, allocation notice, re-offer or fault notice has no delivery status within the configured
   delivery-status wait, the Messaging component shall resend it once on the driver's other registered channel.
6. [Unwanted] If a confirmation has no delivery status within the configured delivery-status wait, then the Messaging
   component shall not resend the confirmation on any channel.
7. [Unwanted] If the driver has no other registered channel, or that channel has no agreement in force (SS-304), then
   the Messaging component shall resend nothing and keep the exception open.
8. [Unwanted] If a resent message is reported undelivered, or has no delivery status within the configured
   delivery-status wait, then the Messaging component shall send it on no channel again and keep the exception open.
9. [Event] When a delivered report arrives for a message whose exception is open, the Messaging component shall record
   the delivery on that exception and send nothing further for that message.
10. [Event] When a WhatsApp message cannot be sent because no approved template exists in the driver's language
    (SS-308), the Messaging component shall handle it as reported undelivered on WhatsApp.
11. [Event] When the Messaging component cannot send a confirmation, reminder, allocation notice, re-offer or fault
    notice on any channel for want of agreement (SS-304), the Messaging component shall raise the exception of
    clause 3.
12. [Event] When a confirmation, reminder, allocation notice, re-offer or fault notice has no delivery status within the
    configured delivery-status wait, the Messaging component shall raise the exception of clause 3.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item #36: one resend, on the other channel, never back to the first; later failures stay on the ops
  list. REQ-219 v2 (CR-005): a confirmation is resent only when reported undelivered, never for a missing status
  (clause 6), so a late first delivery cannot give the driver two (REQ-089, US-223 S6, JS-226 S6).
- "Re-offer" covers the late-arrival offer and the site-full alternatives, "fault notice" the alternatives after a
  fault (SS-316). The exception type is raised here (ss-plan §5.1); the queue and its closing are SS-219; who acts on it
  at night is Q-023.
- Clause 2 is the delivery-report part of REQ-106 (JS-421 S7); the webhook signature is SS-317.
- WhatsApp is unconfirmed (U-10, Q-034, A-008): without it no driver has a second channel and only the ops listing
  applies (clause 7).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clauses 5 and 6 each gave two responses (raise the exception and resend or not); the exception
  for a missing status is now clause 12 for all five message types, clause 5 only resends and clause 6 only forbids
  resending a confirmation.
