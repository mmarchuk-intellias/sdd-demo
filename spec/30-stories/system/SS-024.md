# SS-024 · Station operator capacity-rule change in force at once, with walk-up EVSEs per window

As the Inventory & site configuration module, I need to put a station operator's capacity-rule change in force at
once without an Amperlane approval and report for each window how many EVSEs stay for walk-ups, so that a station
operator can protect walk-up capacity on a busy day without asking Amperlane first.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a station operator saves a valid capacity-rule change for one of its sites in the station portal, the
   Inventory & site configuration module shall put it in force for new holds and bookings from that instant, including
   the remaining slots of a window already under way, with no approval step by Amperlane.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Inventory & site configuration module shall apply a capacity-rule change to new holds and bookings
   only and request no change to any confirmed booking because of it.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Inventory & site configuration module shall report for each time window of a site the cap on
   bookable EVSEs and the number of the site's EVSEs that stay unbookable for walk-ups, which is the site's EVSE count
   less the cap.
4. [Event] When a window's cap or the site's EVSE count changes, the Inventory & site configuration module shall report
   the new walk-up figure for that window from that instant.
5. [Unwanted] If a station operator's capacity-rule change fails the validation of SS-023 (clauses 2 and 3), then the
   Inventory & site configuration module shall put no part of it in force, so new holds and bookings keep meeting the
   rules already in force.

## Notes
- Covers US-311 S1, S2, S6 (the cap applies at once; confirmed bookings above it stand and are listed by SS-019).
  Rule validation (US-311 S3, S4) is SS-023; another station operator's site (S5) is SS-323.
- A hold placed before a cap change and confirmed after it meets the new cap (bucket-5 #45, SS-003).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 5 is the [Unwanted] clause the SS lacked; it links the "in force at once" rule of clause 1
  to SS-023's refusal and adds no new check.
