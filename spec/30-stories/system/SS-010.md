# SS-010 · Cross-site alternatives ranked by distance from the booked site

As the Booking engine, I need to find the nearest free compatible slots at other partner sites for a booking that its
own site cannot serve, so that the driver can choose a slot elsewhere that fits the shift instead of being sent to a
site somebody else picked.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When another component asks for cross-site alternatives for a booking whose site is full at station-hold
   time or at the pre-arrival check, or which a fault or maintenance hold left unplaced, the Booking engine shall find,
   for each live partner site other than the booking's site, the earliest start time from the later of the booking's
   slot start and the time of the request to the end of that site's bookable hours that day at which a hold for the
   booking's vehicle and duration would be accepted.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall rank the sites found under clause 1 by straight-line distance between their
   recorded location and the location of the booking's site, nearest first and an earlier start time first on a tie,
   and return at most the configured number of cross-site alternatives.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall rank cross-site alternatives without reading any location of the driver or of
   the vehicle.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall not hold the cross-site alternatives it returns.
5. [Unwanted] If no other live site has a start time under clause 1, then the Booking engine shall return an empty list
   of cross-site alternatives, so the caller offers the same-site slot alone and the ops exception shows that no other
   site was free.

## Notes
- Covers the other-site part of JS-019 S1 and S3 and of JS-120 S1 and S2. Only compatible slots are found (SS-004,
  JS-019 S4, JS-120 S5). Acceptance, and a slot taken before the driver accepts, are SS-011.
- Settles bucket-5 #20: ranked by distance from the booking's site, starting near the booked time, up to a configured
  number of offers. The driver's location is never used: it is opt-in and off by default (A-024, REQ-255).
- REQ-148 v2 (CR-007) adds the pre-arrival check. Depends on A-019 (Q-033) and A-017.
- Parameter: number of cross-site alternatives (platform-wide, SS-026). Site locations are onboarding data (SS-028).
  The message is SS-316; the ops exceptions are SS-105 and SS-109.
