# SS-228 · Attendant view: today's due drivers by EVSE and station-hold requests to mark and clear

As the attendant view (Partner portals), I need to show the attendant which driver is due at which EVSE and when, and
each station-hold request with the stall to mark, when to mark it and when to clear it, so that the attendant holds
the right stall at the right time without ops phoning.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the attendant opens the attendant view, the attendant view shall list each allocated booking at the
   attendant's site that falls on the current day as SS-323 clause 5 defines it, with its EVSE, stall, start time and
   the driver due, showing only the driver data allowed for site staff (the vehicle's plate and the charging driver's
   first name).
2. [Event] When the station-hold dispatcher issues a site-staff station-hold request for the attendant's site, the
   attendant view shall show it with the stall to mark, when to mark it and when to clear it.
3. [Event] When the station-hold dispatcher issues an instruction to clear a stall at the attendant's site, the
   attendant view shall show it with the stall and the time to clear it.
4. [Event] When the attendant view has loaded a station-hold request or a clear instruction on the attendant's device,
   the attendant view shall report it to the station-hold dispatcher as delivered, with the time.
5. [Event] When the attendant acknowledges a station-hold request in the attendant view, the attendant view shall pass
   the acknowledgement with its time to the station-hold dispatcher.
6. [Event] When a listed booking is reallocated to another EVSE, the attendant view shall show it at its new EVSE and
   stall.
7. [Unwanted] If a listed booking is cancelled, released or rebooked to another site, then the attendant view shall
   remove it from the list.

## Notes
- Covers US-032 S1 and S5, US-025 S4, and the attendant-view delivery of US-025 S1. Arrival confirmation (US-032 S2 –
  S4) is SS-126; site scoping and "current day" (slot starting that calendar day in the site's time zone, or a station
  hold falling on it, bucket-5 #54) are SS-323 and SS-324.
- Content, timing and acknowledgement rules of station-hold requests are SS-118; clear instructions SS-120;
  unacknowledged requests SS-119. Clauses 4 and 5 give them the delivery and acknowledgement signals (A-046, A-048).
  Requests to JFK and Gowanus staff by SMS go through messaging.
- Driver data for site staff: A-053 (Q-048).
- R7: the attendant's use of the view is unconfirmed (integrations.md U-13): A-021, Q-010, Q-049, A-054.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 1 takes "current day" from SS-323 clause 5 (a 00:10 booking shows at its 23:55 station
  hold); clause 4 counts delivery when the request is loaded, as SS-118 clause 3 does (it said "first displays").
