# SS-429 · Planned downtime only between 01:00 and 05:00 ET

As the Platform & operations component, I need planned maintenance, and every deployment that interrupts booking, kept
between 01:00 and 05:00 Eastern time, so that drivers can book throughout the availability window without the cost of
zero-downtime deployment.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Unwanted] If a deployment or maintenance task marked as interrupting booking is started outside 01:00 – 05:00
   America/New_York, then the Platform & operations component shall refuse to start it.
2. [Unwanted] If the synthetic booking check (SS-428) fails during a deployment marked as not interrupting booking, then
   the Platform & operations component shall roll the deployment back.
3. [Unwanted] If a deployment or maintenance task marked as interrupting booking is still running at 05:00
   America/New_York, then the Platform & operations component shall alert the on-call engineer.
4. [Event] When a deployment or maintenance task ends, the Platform & operations component shall record in the
   deployment log its start, its end and whether it interrupted booking.

## Notes
- Enabler: REQ-129 has no story. Constitution N5 and code convention 15; A-028. Zero-downtime deployment is not
  required, so a deployment that does not interrupt booking may run at any time; clause 2 guards that claim.
- P3.4 critique: clauses 2 and 3 held two behaviours each. The alert in old clause 2 duplicated, with other timing,
  SS-428 clause 4 (alert after the configured number of failed checks), which now alerts alone; the downtime count in
  old clause 3 is SS-428 clause 3, which counts every failed minute of the window. The rollback in clause 2 is an
  Intellias mechanism for REQ-129, not a client statement.
- Booking timers may fire late during the window but are never lost and take effect once: SS-034 (REQ-133).
- The tech lead checks the deployment log monthly (N5). Who is on call: Q-039.
