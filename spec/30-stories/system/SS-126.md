# SS-126 · Attendant arrival confirmation within the arrival window

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to record a driver's arrival when the site's attendant confirms it within the
booking's arrival window, so that at a site without an API a driver who has arrived is not released as a no-show.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the attendant signed in for a site confirms a driver's arrival for a booking at that site inside the
   booking's arrival window (SS-124), the Arrival & no-show component shall record arrival on the booking at the
   confirmation's time, with the attendant as its source and actor, so the booking is not released at its end of grace
   (SS-127 clause 3).
2. [Unwanted] If the attendant confirms an arrival after the booking's end of grace, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall keep the booking recorded as a no-show and hand the confirmation to late-arrival handling (SS-128).
3. [Unwanted] If the attendant confirms an arrival before the booking's arrival window opens, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall record no arrival.
4. [Unwanted] If the attendant confirms an arrival for a booking at another site, then the Arrival & no-show component
   shall refuse the confirmation and record nothing.
5. [Event] When the attendant confirms an arrival for a booking that already has one, the Arrival & no-show component
   shall keep the first arrival and its time.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 13: the same window as a QR scan, from the site's configured arrival-window start before the
  slot to the end of grace; later confirmations are late arrivals (REQ-037).
- A-021, Q-010, Q-049 and A-054 (the attendant's device); R7: U-13 (nobody has confirmed the attendant will use the
  view). The attendant's site-scoped login is SS-324; the due list (US-032 S1, S5) is SS-228.
- US-104 S5 (no QR scan, no session start, attendant confirms) is clause 1; no location is involved (REQ-204).
