# SS-100 · Late allocation at the site's allocation offset, never at booking time

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to allocate each confirmed booking to one EVSE only when the site's
configured allocation offset before its slot is reached, so that the booking counts against the site's pool until
shortly before the slot and gets an EVSE that is free at that moment.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall hold no EVSE allocation for a booking, and emit no allocation
   event for it, before the site's configured allocation offset before the booking's slot start is reached.
2. [Event] When the site's configured allocation offset before the slot start of an active confirmed booking is reached,
   the Allocation & rebooking component shall allocate the booking to exactly one EVSE chosen under SS-101.
3. [Event] When a booking is allocated, the Allocation & rebooking component shall emit an allocation event naming the
   booking, the EVSE and its stall, so that Messaging sends the charging driver the allocation notice (SS-301) and the
   Station-hold dispatcher places the station hold (SS-116, SS-118).
4. [Unwanted] If no EVSE at the site is free and compatible with the booking's vehicle when the allocation offset is
   reached, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall leave the booking unallocated, emit no allocation event and
   hand the booking to site-full handling (SS-105).
5. [Unwanted] If a booking is cancelled or released before its allocation offset is reached, then the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall create no allocation for it.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall record every allocation it makes without a person's action
   with the system as actor and the allocation reason code (SS-020).
7. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall apply clauses 1 – 4 alike at sites of supply patterns A, B
   and C, each with its own configured allocation offset, so that only the source of EVSE status differs by pattern.

## Notes
- A-002 (allocation about T-15, configured per site, tuned on the simulator in Phase 0); CF-023 and A-014: before
  allocation only the pool check protects capacity (SS-003).
- The timer is the booking's workflow (SS-032); a timer that fires late after maintenance takes effect once
  (constitution N5, SS-034).
- Consumers of the allocation event: SS-301 (notice, also to a charging driver who is not the booker, US-017 S6), SS-116
  and SS-118 (station hold), SS-125 (session-start arrival on the allocated EVSE), SS-228 (attendant view).
- US-017 S2 follows from clause 1; US-017 S3 from clause 4; US-205 S1 (allocation part) from clause 7; JS-330 S1 – S3
  (no manual ops action on allocation) from clause 6.
