# SS-102 · No overlapping allocations on one EVSE, buffer included

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need never to give one EVSE to two bookings whose times overlap with the
site's buffer between them, so that a booked driver never finds another booked driver charging on their EVSE.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall give each allocation a time range from the booking's slot
   start to its slot end plus the buffer fixed for that booking at confirmation.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall treat two time ranges as overlapping only when each starts
   before the other ends, so that a booking whose slot starts exactly one buffer after an allocated booking's slot end
   can be allocated to the same EVSE.
3. [Unwanted] If an active allocation on an EVSE overlaps a booking's time range, then the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall not allocate the booking to that EVSE, and shall allocate it to another free compatible EVSE (SS-101)
   or hand it to site-full handling (SS-105) when none is free.
4. [Unwanted] If two overlapping bookings are allocated at the same moment and one EVSE is the only free compatible one
   for both, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall allocate that EVSE to exactly one of them and hand the
   other to site-full handling (SS-105).
5. [Event] When a booking is cancelled, released or rebooked to another EVSE, the Allocation & rebooking component shall
   deactivate its allocation at once, so that the EVSE can be allocated to another booking in that time range.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall never hold two active allocations on one EVSE whose time
   ranges (clause 1) overlap.

## Notes
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clause 1 defined the time range and stated the no-overlap rule; the rule is now clause 6.
- A-001 (buffer as a configured per-site value); CF-023 and A-014: before allocation only the pool check protects
  capacity (SS-003); this rule bites at allocation.
- Clause 4 holds even when two processes race: the database constraint of SS-103 lets exactly one allocation commit, and
  the loser is handled by clause 3.
- Constitution N7: integration test on real PostgreSQL, zero overlaps in the load test, daily production query.
