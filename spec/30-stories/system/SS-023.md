# SS-023 · Capacity rules and bookable hours cover each slot exactly once

As the Inventory & site configuration module, I need to hold each site's bookable hours and capacity rules and accept
only sets of rules that put every bookable slot under exactly one cap, so that the cap the station operator agreed is
always defined and never ambiguous.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Inventory & site configuration module shall hold for each site its bookable days and hours and a
   set of capacity rules, each with days of the week, a time window in the site's local time and a cap on bookable
   EVSEs.
2. [Unwanted] If a save of capacity rules or bookable hours would leave a slot within the site's bookable hours under
   no rule or under more than one rule, then the Inventory & site configuration module shall refuse the whole save,
   list the days and times left uncovered and name each overlapping rule, and keep the rules and hours in force
   unchanged.
3. [Unwanted] If a capacity rule's cap is higher than the number of EVSEs at the site, then the Inventory & site
   configuration module shall refuse the rule and keep the rules in force unchanged.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Inventory & site configuration module shall read a rule's or a bookable period's days of the week
   as the days on which its window starts, so a window whose end is earlier than its start runs past midnight and the
   slots after midnight belong to the window that began the evening before.
5. [Event] When a station operator or ops save a site's bookable days and hours, the Inventory & site configuration
   module shall put them in force from that instant for every open-slot query and hold.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Inventory & site configuration module shall keep bookable hours in the site's local time, so on a
   day the clocks change slots are offered between the same local hours.
7. [Event] When a save of bookable hours leaves confirmed bookings outside the new hours, the Inventory & site
   configuration module shall keep those bookings and emit a bookable-hours-reduced event so the Booking engine lists
   them (SS-019).

## Notes
- Covers US-002 S3, US-311 S3 and S4, US-310 S1, S2, S4 (cross-block 11: one SS for rule validation), US-033 S3 (the
  hours in force; the refusal of a request outside them is SS-001).
- Settles bucket-5 #2: a rule's days of the week name the day its window starts; slots after midnight belong to the
  window that began the evening before (Gowanus off-peak 10 PM – 5 AM, SRC-D1#7), and the station portal shows it that
  way.
- Clause 7 applies A-055 (reduced bookable hours) to confirmed bookings; nothing is dropped silently.
- Who may edit: the station operator for its own sites (scoping SS-323) and ops. A station operator's change in force
  at once is SS-024; idempotent saves SS-025; audit with before and after values SS-333 (REQ-262). Q-005 sets the caps
  per partner agreement; overbooking stays off (REQ-327 parked).
