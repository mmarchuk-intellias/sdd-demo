# SS-408 · Reconciliation queue: unmatched bookings and records held from invoicing until ops resolve them

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need every booking that ends without a matched session record, and every
session record on a booked EVSE and window without a matched booking, held in a reconciliation case and kept off every
invoice until it is matched or ops record an outcome, and every charge with no booking held the same way until ops link
it, so that the platform invoices and confirms no-shows only on evidence.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a booking is marked as without a session record (SS-407), the Ledger & invoicing component shall open a
   reconciliation case for it.
2. [Event] When a session record arrives whose start lies within the window of a booking allocated to that EVSE that
   has no recorded arrival, such as a booking released as a no-show, the Ledger & invoicing component shall open one
   reconciliation case holding the record and that booking.
3. [State] While a booking is in an open reconciliation case, the Ledger & invoicing component shall put none of its
   charges, its no-show fee line included, on any invoice.
4. [Event] When a session record is matched to the booking of an open case, the Ledger & invoicing component shall
   close the case and release the booking's charges to invoicing, as an adjustment on the next invoice if their month
   has closed.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall open no reconciliation case for a session record on an EVSE
   that no booking was allocated to at the record's start.
6. [Event] When an ops user records an outcome for an open case with a reason code (a manual match, a confirmed
   no-show, the record accepted as a walk-up, or the booking accepted without a session record), the Ledger & invoicing
   component shall apply the outcome and close the case.
7. [Unwanted] If an outcome is recorded without a reason code, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall refuse it
   with the code REASON_CODE_REQUIRED and keep the case open.
8. [Unwanted] If a user without the Amperlane ops role, a finance user included, tries to record an outcome, then the
   Ledger & invoicing component shall refuse it and keep the case open.
9. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall list each open case for finance and ops users with its booking
   or its charge, its session record where there is one, the reason it is open and when it was opened.
10. [Event] When month close leaves a charge off a fleet's invoice because its ledger entries reference no booking
    (SS-405 clause 4), the Ledger & invoicing component shall open one reconciliation case holding that charge and its
    ledger entries.
11. [Event] When an ops user links the charge of such a case to its booking with a reason code, the Ledger & invoicing
    component shall record the link as a new entry that references the charge's entries (SS-402 clause 2) and close
    the case.
12. [Event] When an ops user accepts at once, with one reason code, every open case of one site and one period whose
    booking is without a session record, the Ledger & invoicing component shall apply the outcome "accepted without a
    session record" to each of those cases and close each case on its own, as clause 6 does for one case.
