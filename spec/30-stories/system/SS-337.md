# SS-337 · Driver anonymisation on request, records kept under a pseudonym

As the Audit & data protection component, I need ops to be able to anonymise one driver's personal data on request
while bookings, audit entries and ledger entries stay under a pseudonym, so that Amperlane honours the request without
breaking fleet invoices, dispute evidence or the audit trail.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user anonymises a driver identified by a registered mobile number, the Audit & data protection
   component shall replace that driver's name, phone number and other personal data with one pseudonym in every
   fleet's records.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall keep the anonymised driver's bookings, audit entries and
   ledger entries, showing the pseudonym, with every amount and invoice total unchanged.
3. [Unwanted] If a user without the ops role asks to anonymise a driver, then the Audit & data protection component
   shall refuse it and change nothing.
4. [Unwanted] If no driver is registered with the given number, then the Audit & data protection component shall change
   nothing and tell the ops user that no driver matches.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall keep no link from an anonymised number to the
   pseudonymised history, so a later registration of that number creates a new driver record.
6. [Event] When an anonymisation completes, the Audit & data protection component shall append an audit entry with the
   ops user, the reason code and the time, referring to the pseudonym and holding none of the removed data.

## Notes
- The ledger and the audit trail are append-only (constitution N17, N19), so "delete" becomes pseudonymisation for them;
  entries refer to people by internal id (SS-332 clause 7). Backups follow A-058 (SS-336 clause 7).
- Q-058 (client decision): the driver's future bookings, verification of a request that does not come from the
  registered number, and evidence for an open no-show dispute (A-047). No clause assumes an answer; constitution R6
  flag for the engagement lead.
- Message bodies held by the messaging provider are vendor-held data (#51, SS-338). Depends on A-025, Q-022, A-006.
