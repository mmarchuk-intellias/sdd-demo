# SS-107 · Charge not started after arrival: rebook at the site or route to ops, no fee

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to act when a driver has arrived but no charging session starts on the
allocated EVSE, by rebooking to another EVSE at the site or routing the booking to ops as a fault case, so that the
driver is not left at an EVSE that does not work and is never charged a no-show for it.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the configured session-start wait has passed after the later of a booking's arrival and its slot start
   with no charging session started on its allocated EVSE, and another compatible EVSE at the site is free, the
   Allocation & rebooking component shall rebook the booking to that EVSE with the reason code
   'moved — session did not start' and emit the reallocation event of SS-104, so that the charging driver is told the
   new EVSE.
2. [Unwanted] If no compatible EVSE at the site is free when the session-start wait has passed, then the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall raise a 'charge not started' ops exception for the booking as a fault case for its
   allocated EVSE (SS-219).
3. [Unwanted] If no charging session starts within the session-start wait on the EVSE the booking was rebooked to under
   clause 1, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall raise the 'charge not started' ops exception instead of
   rebooking it again.
4. [Event] When a charging session starts on the allocated EVSE within the session-start wait, the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall neither rebook the booking nor raise an exception for it.
5. [State] While a booking with recorded arrival waits for its charging session to start, the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall keep the arrival on the booking, also across a rebooking under clause 1, so that the booking is not
   released at its end of grace (SS-127) and no no-show fee is requested for it (SS-131).

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 18: the catalogue reason code 'moved — session did not start', distinct from
  'moved — charger fault' (SS-020).
- Whether a session started is decided by the evidence of A-051: the CSMS session-start event where Phase 0 confirms
  one, otherwise the EVSE's status changing to charging or occupied, the attendant's "occupied" at LIC (SS-216, SS-200;
  Q-009, Q-012, Q-051; U-14).
- The exception does not mark the EVSE faulted: a fault comes only from status (SS-208) or ops (SS-112); a driver's
  report is never a status (REQ-191). Clause 2's exception is per booking; the per-EVSE "fault" exception of REQ-189 is
  raised by SS-208 (batch 3 contract).
- GAP in the Step 3.3 log: REQ-181 sets neither when the wait starts for an early arrival nor what follows a second
  failed start (clauses 1 and 3).
