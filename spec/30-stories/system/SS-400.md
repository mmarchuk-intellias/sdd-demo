# SS-400 · Generic double-entry ledger that records every business-model option

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need a generic double-entry ledger in which every monetary fact is a balanced
transaction and every amount is derived from entries, so that settlement can start before the business model is
decided (Q-001) and no figure depends on a stored balance.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall record every monetary fact as one transaction of two or more
   entries, each entry naming one ledger account, its side (debit or credit), its amount and currency, and the
   transaction's posting time.
2. [Unwanted] If the debits and credits of a submitted transaction are not equal in each currency, then the Ledger &
   invoicing component shall reject the whole transaction with the code TRANSACTION_UNBALANCED and post none of its
   entries.
3. [Unwanted] If a submitted transaction names a ledger account that does not exist, or an amount in a currency other
   than its account's, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall reject the whole transaction with the code
   ACCOUNT_INVALID and post none of its entries.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall compute every balance it reports, for an account, a fleet or an
   invoice, as the sum of the entries posted up to the time asked for.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall keep no stored balance that is updated when an entry is posted.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall accept the balanced sample month of each Q-001 option (booking
   fee, fleet subscription, energy resale at B2B cost, station fee, no-show fee with station share, payouts) with no
   schema change, any new account kind being added as data only.

## Notes
- Enabler: REQ-085 and REQ-210 have no story; US-316 and US-317 rely on this ledger (their notes name it).
- Clause 6 is one parameterised test over the six sample months of REQ-210; that every sample transaction balances is
  enforced by clause 2 (P3.4: the balance check was a second behaviour in clause 6). The months are test data, not the
  chosen model: Q-001 and CF-008 stay undecided, and the option-specific flows stay parked (REQ-270, REQ-278).
- Append-only entries and corrections by new entries: SS-402 (REQ-247). Fee lines: SS-401. Fleet position: SS-403.
- Constitution N19 (daily check for unbalanced transactions, with an alert) and code convention 11 (integer minor
  units, ISO 4217 currency, no floating point) apply and are not restated. Repeated postings follow constitution N8
  and code convention 8; the business rule "one fee line per no-show" is SS-401.
- ADR-008 (double-entry, append-only).
