# SS-409 · Invoice payment status and overdue flag

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need to keep every fleet invoice's payment status as the payment provider last
reported it, post each payment to the ledger, and flag every invoice still unpaid after its due date, so that finance
follow up only on invoices that are really overdue.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall hold for every issued fleet invoice its fleet, month, amount,
   due date and payment status as last reported by the payment provider, with the time of that report.
2. [Event] When the Payments integration reports an invoice paid (SS-413), the Ledger & invoicing component shall
   record it as paid with the payment time the provider reports and post the payment to the fleet's ledger.
3. [Unwanted] If the Payments integration reports a failed payment attempt, then the Ledger & invoicing component
   shall record the attempt and keep the invoice unpaid.
4. [Event] When the end of an invoice's due date in Eastern time has passed and the invoice is not paid, the Ledger &
   invoicing component shall flag it as overdue.
5. [Event] When the Payments integration reports an invoice paid with a payment time on or before the end of its due
   date, the Ledger & invoicing component shall leave the invoice unflagged, removing an overdue flag set before the
   report arrived.
6. [Event] When an invoice flagged as overdue is reported paid with a payment time after the end of its due date, the
   Ledger & invoicing component shall clear the flag and show when the invoice was paid.

## Notes
- Covers US-323 S1 (clause 1), S2 (4), S3 (5), S4 (3, 4), S5 (6). Payments posted by clause 2 feed the fleet position
  (SS-403).
- P3.4 critique: old clause 5 ("shall not flag ... also when the report arrives after the due date") could not be met
  together with clause 4, which flags at the end of the due date before a late report is known. Clause 5 now acts on
  the report and removes such a flag; clause 6 is limited to payments after the due date, so the two never share a
  trigger. With the payment check interval of SS-413 clause 5, an on-time payment is unflagged within one
  interval.
- The due date is 30 days after issue (REQ-083, SS-404); "end of the due date" is Eastern time (spec conventions
  row 11). Clause 5 keeps a late or lost webhook (SS-413) from leaving an invoice paid on time flagged.
- Duplicated and out-of-order provider events are handled before they reach this SS (SS-413). The finance view is
  SS-411; a fleet sees only its own invoices (REQ-258, SS-323).
- Q-021: per-fleet credit limits and an alert after a set number of overdue days are parked (REQ-280, REQ-281).
