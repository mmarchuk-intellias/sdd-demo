# SS-406 · Credit lines for fees reversed after their invoice was issued

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need a fee reversed after its invoice was issued to come back as one credit line
on the fleet's next invoice, and never as a change to the issued invoice, so that a fleet never has to replace or
re-check an invoice it has already processed.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a no-show fee line that is on an issued invoice is reversed (SS-402), the Ledger & invoicing component
   shall put a credit line for the reversed amount, within the limit of clause 5, on the first invoice of that fleet
   issued after the reversal, naming the original invoice and booking.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall refuse every change to, and every removal of, a line of an
   issued invoice with the code INVOICE_ISSUED, and leave the issued invoice unchanged.
3. [Event] When a fleet's invoice for a month is issued, the Ledger & invoicing component shall leave off it every
   no-show fee of that month that was reversed or waived before the issue.
4. [Unwanted] If month close runs again after a credit line was created for a reversed fee, then the Ledger &
   invoicing component shall create no second credit line for that fee.
5. [Unwanted] If the credit due to a fleet exceeds the charges of the fleet's invoice being built, then the Ledger &
   invoicing component shall put credit lines on that invoice only up to its charges, so that the invoice is issued
   at zero and its total still equals the sum of its lines (SS-405 clause 3).
6. [Unwanted] If a fee was reversed or waived before any invoice carrying it was issued, then the Ledger & invoicing
   component shall put no credit line for it on any invoice.
7. [Event] When credit is left over after an invoice is issued at zero (clause 5), the Ledger & invoicing component
   shall carry the rest, unrefunded, to the fleet's following invoices as carried credit naming the invoice it came
   from, until it is used up.

## Notes
- Covers US-320 S1 (clause 1), S2 (2), S3 (3, 6), S4 (4), S5 (1: the first invoice issued after the reversal); US-113
  S6 (clauses 1, 2).
- P3.4 critique: old clause 3 held two behaviours (not charged; no credit) and is split into clauses 3 and 6; old
  clause 5 issued an invoice "at zero" while its lines summed below zero, against SS-405 clause 3, and held two
  behaviours; it is now clauses 5 (credit capped at the charges) and 7 (the rest carried forward), and clause 1
  credits "within the limit of clause 5" so that the two never give different amounts for one invoice.
- Settles bucket-5 #42: unused credit is carried forward; no refund is made unless finance decide one. A refund has no
  requirement in v1: whether unused credit is ever refunded, who decides and how it is paid is Q-064 (client business
  decision, money flow); the baseline holds in every answer, and a refund path would enter through a CR.
- The reversal itself is SS-402; the net-zero fee of a reversal or waiver posted before issue is SS-402 clause 11.
- Depends on Q-002 (payer and amount) and A-047 (a fleet or driver can dispute within 30 days of the booking, so
  reversals after the invoice was issued are expected).
- A fee whose booking's slot started before the cut-over was invoiced by the pilot process; its reversal follows
  SS-410, not this SS.
