# SS-104 · Reallocation when the allocated EVSE is in use at station-hold time or the pre-arrival check

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to reallocate a booking whose allocated EVSE is in use when its station
hold falls due or at the pre-arrival check, without touching the session on it, so that the driver can still charge on
time and no walk-up is asked to leave.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the site's configured station-hold offset before a booking's slot start is reached and the booking's
   allocated EVSE is in use (occupied or charging), the Allocation & rebooking component shall reallocate the booking to
   another free compatible EVSE at the same site (SS-101) before the station hold is placed.
2. [Event] When the pre-arrival check runs at the site's configured pre-arrival check offset and the booking's allocated
   EVSE is in use, the Allocation & rebooking component shall reallocate the booking to another free compatible EVSE at
   the same site (SS-101).
3. [Event] When the Allocation & rebooking component reallocates a booking, the Allocation & rebooking component shall
   deactivate the old allocation and emit a reallocation event naming the old and the new EVSE and stall, so that the
   old station hold is cleared (SS-120), the station hold is placed on the new EVSE by its method (SS-116, SS-118) and
   the charging driver is told the new EVSE (SS-301).
4. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall send no stop command for any charging session, request no
   station hold for an EVSE in use, and request no message to site staff, ops or a driver that asks for a walk-up to
   leave.
5. [Unwanted] If the allocated EVSE is in use and no other compatible EVSE at the site is free, then the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall keep the booking's allocation, request no station hold for the EVSE in use and hand the
   booking to site-full handling (SS-105).
6. [Event] When the Station-hold dispatcher reports a charger reservation rejected while the EVSE's status shows it in
   use (SS-117), the Allocation & rebooking component shall reallocate the booking as in clause 1, or hand it to
   site-full handling as in clause 5.
7. [Unwanted] If arrival is already recorded on the booking when its station hold falls due or the pre-arrival check
   runs, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall not reallocate it.

## Notes
- A-002 (station hold about T-15, pre-arrival check about T-10, configured per site). "In use" comes from the status
  service (SS-202): CSMS status at Gowanus and JFK (A-031; Q-012 and U-8 for JFK freshness), the attendant's status at
  LIC (US-031 S5; A-021, Q-049, A-054, U-13). A stale feed is decided on the last known status (SS-101 clause 2,
  SS-203).
- Clause 4 is the station red line of REQ-020 (JS-018 S3); SS-118 clause 7 applies it to site-staff requests.
- JS-018 S5 (reservation cancelled on the first EVSE, new station hold by the new EVSE's method) follows from clause 3
  through SS-120 and SS-116.
- Specific over general (JS-023 S4): a rejection because the EVSE is in use leads to clause 6, not to a signage request
  (REQ-155).
- Where session-start arrival is off (A-051), a booked driver who plugs in early without scanning makes the allocated
  EVSE look in use at the pre-arrival check (Q-051). The allocation notice asks the driver to scan first (SS-301 clause
  10, Gate 3 edit 6e.2), so that clause 7 keeps the allocation.
