# SS-111 · Fault rebooking summary and undo of a single rebooking

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to give ops a summary of what I did with each booking after a fault and
to undo a single automatic rebooking when it is safe, so that ops can correct a rebooking that does not suit a driver or
that followed a false fault.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user requests the summary of a fault, the Allocation & rebooking component shall return each
   booking affected by it with the outcome recorded for it under SS-108 clauses 8 and 10, and nothing recomputed:
   rebooked (from which EVSE to which), or handed to unplaced handling and so in the ops exception queue, shown with
   the alternatives offered to its driver (SS-109) or that none could be offered, and with its final outcome once it
   has one.
2. [Event] When an ops user undoes an automatic fault rebooking (SS-108 clause 1) with a reason code from the catalogue,
   the Allocation & rebooking component shall return the booking to its original EVSE at its original time, record the
   undo with the ops user, the reason code and the time, and emit the reallocation event of SS-104, so that the charging
   driver is told the original EVSE and the station hold follows.
3. [Unwanted] If the original EVSE is still faulted or otherwise out of the pool, then the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall refuse the undo, tell ops that reason and keep the booking as rebooked.
4. [Unwanted] If another active allocation on the original EVSE overlaps the booking's time range, then the Allocation &
   rebooking component shall refuse the undo, tell ops that reason and keep the booking as rebooked.
5. [Unwanted] If arrival has been recorded on the booking, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall refuse the
   undo, tell ops that reason and keep the booking as rebooked.
6. [Unwanted] If the undo has no reason code from the catalogue, or names a rebooking that was not made automatically
   after a fault, then the Allocation & rebooking component shall refuse it and change nothing.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Allocation & rebooking component shall change only the booking named in an undo and leave the other
   rebookings of the same fault unchanged.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 21: undo returns the booking to its previous EVSE only while that EVSE is back in the pool and
  free for the slot, and before any arrival; otherwise ops are told why it is refused.
- A-017 and CF-022. The EVSE returns to the pool through SS-209 (JS-118). The undo records no no-show.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 1 listed three outcomes ("offered alternatives" and "in the queue" as separate ones) that
  SS-108 clause 8 does not emit; an unplaced booking is both in the queue and, usually, offered alternatives (SS-109
  clauses 1, 2, 6). Clause 1 now reads only SS-108's recorded outcomes (clauses 8 and 10), so the summary, the station
  portal (SS-227) and reporting (SS-234) cannot disagree; US-122 S1's three wordings map onto them.
