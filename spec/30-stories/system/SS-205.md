# SS-205 · Status history: status and source at any past moment

As the EVSE status service, I need to keep a time-stamped history of every EVSE status and answer what an EVSE's status
was at any past moment, with its source and how old it was then, so that a no-show dispute is decided on recorded
evidence.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The EVSE status service shall keep, append-only, every status it receives for an EVSE with its
   observed-at time, received-at time and source, and the times at which a status for that EVSE was received or
   fetched.
2. [Event] When asked for an EVSE's status at a past moment, the EVSE status service shall return the status with the
   latest observed-at time at or before that moment, with its observed-at time and its source.
3. [Unwanted] If a status event arrived after one with a later observed-at time, then the EVSE status service shall
   place it in the history at its own observed-at time.
4. [Unwanted] If no status was observed for the EVSE at or before the requested moment, then the EVSE status service
   shall answer that no status was recorded for that moment, in place of any later status.
5. [Event] When the EVSE status service returns the status of an EVSE at a connected site for a past moment, the EVSE
   status service shall include the status age at that moment, measured as A-031 defines, and flag the status as stale
   if that age exceeded 60 seconds.
6. [Optional] Where the status at the requested moment was set by the attendant, the EVSE status service shall return
   the attendant's report time and the attendant view as its source, with no stale flag.

## Notes
- Covers US-209 S1 – S5 and US-114 S6. The dispute screen that shows it is SS-135 (batch 2).
- A moment inside a feed outage returns the last status observed before it, flagged stale (US-209 S4, clauses 2 and 5).
- Retention of the history: A-025, Q-022; a moment older than the retention period is out of scope (US-209 notes).
  Storage form (time-series partitions, design.md §2.5) is a task decision.
