# SS-420 · Raw pilot export: read-only, named people only, deleted at the end of retention

As the Pilot migration & cut-over component, I need the raw pilot export kept read-only in a store of its own, readable
only by the people the Amperlane CTO names, and deleted when counsel's retention period ends, so that a file of driver
names and phone numbers cannot be altered, spread or kept longer than Amperlane may keep it.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall keep the raw pilot export in a store of its own that
   only the people named on the export's access list can read.
2. [Unwanted] If a person not named on the access list tries to read the export, through the platform or directly in
   its store, a production administrator included, then the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall refuse access.
3. [Unwanted] If anyone, a named person included, tries to change, overwrite or delete any part of the export before
   its retention period ends, then the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall refuse it and leave the export
   unchanged.
4. [Event] When the retention period counsel set for the export ends, the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall
   delete the export within one day (A-058).
5. [State] While counsel has not set a retention period for the export, the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall
   keep the export.
6. [Event] When the export is deleted, the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall record the deletion with its
   time.
7. [Unwanted] If anyone, a named person included, tries to open the export after its deletion, then the Pilot
   migration & cut-over component shall refuse it, also after a backup is restored (A-058).
8. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall list the raw pilot export in the data register (SS-336)
   with its retention period, shown as "not set" until counsel sets one.

## Notes
- Covers US-404 S1 (clause 1), S2 (2), S3 (3), S4 (4, 6, 7), S5 (5, 8).
- P3.4 critique: old clause 4 held three behaviours (delete, record, refuse) and old clause 5 two (keep, show in the
  register); they are now clauses 4 – 8; the numbers and the restore case of clauses 4 and 7 cite A-058 inline.
- Depends on A-025 (counsel sets the period), A-026 (the raw export is kept read-only) and Q-022. "Within one day" is
  A-058 (the purge runs at least daily); backups are not edited, they expire on their own schedule, and a restore
  re-applies the deletion (A-058).
- Access to the export is production data access, logged by SS-433 (REQ-120); not restated (US-404 note). The access
  list is defined in Terraform (constitution SC8) and changes through SS-426. Encryption at rest: SS-424.
- Copies taken for sharing or staging are de-identified (REQ-118, SS-339).
