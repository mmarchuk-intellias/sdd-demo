# SS-327 · One-tap actions: cancel, running late, report a faulted EVSE or a blocked stall, accept an offer

As the Driver PWA, I need each action a driver takes on a booking from a message or the driver page to take one tap,
with no dialog, sign-in or code, so that a driver can cancel, report running late, report a faulted EVSE or a blocked
stall, or take an offered slot while working.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall offer, on the driver page and on every page opened from a message about a booking,
   the one-tap actions the booking's state allows: cancel and running late while the booking is active and no arrival
   is recorded; report a faulted EVSE while the booking is active; report a blocked stall while the booking has an
   allocated EVSE; accept for each open offer.
2. [Event] When the driver taps a one-tap action, the Driver PWA shall send it at once, with no confirmation dialog,
   sign-in step or one-time code, on a limited view too (SS-321).
3. [Ubiquitous] The Driver PWA shall send one idempotency key per tapped action and the same key with every repeat of
   it, so a repeated tap or a retry gives one result.
4. [Event] When the result of an action returns, the Driver PWA shall show it: the booking cancelled, the running-late
   report with its time, the report received, or the booked slot.
5. [Unwanted] If the booking is no longer active when an action is tapped, then the Driver PWA shall show that the
   booking is no longer active and that nothing changed.
6. [Unwanted] If the platform refuses an action because the session is neither the booking's booker nor its charging
   driver, then the Driver PWA shall show the refusal and that the booking is unchanged.
