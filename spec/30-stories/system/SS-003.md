# SS-003 · Confirmation with pool feasibility, buffer, cap re-check and fixed terms

As the Booking engine, I need to confirm a held booking only if its slots stay within their caps and every overlapping
booking can still be given its own compatible EVSE with the site's buffer, so that a confirmed booking is a promise the
site can keep at allocation.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the booker confirms an unexpired hold, the Booking engine shall confirm the booking only if, at the
   moment of confirmation, every slot it covers stays within the cap of the capacity rule then in force and the pool
   check of clause 2 passes.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall pass the pool check for a booking only if, for every slot it covers, the new
   booking, the confirmed bookings overlapping that slot and the units held by manual holds (SS-017, SS-018) can be
   assigned to distinct EVSEs of the site, each booking to an EVSE compatible with its vehicle under SS-004, with no
   two bookings on one EVSE closer than the site's configured buffer.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall count in the pool check only the EVSEs that are in the site's pool for the
   slot: not under a maintenance hold for that slot, and, for slots that start on the current day in the site's time
   zone, not excluded from new allocations by the EVSE status service.
4. [Unwanted] If the pool check fails, then the Booking engine shall refuse the confirmation with the code
   NO_COMPATIBLE_EVSE_FREE, release the hold at once so its capacity returns, and request a same-site offer (SS-009)
   as for a full slot.
5. [Unwanted] If the cap re-check fails because the capacity rule was lowered after the hold was placed, then the
   Booking engine shall refuse the confirmation with the code SLOT_FULL, release the hold at once and request a
   same-site offer (SS-009).
6. [Event] When the Booking engine confirms a booking, the Booking engine shall record on the booking the version of the
   site's booking parameters (SS-026) and of its partner terms (SS-027) in force at that instant, and use those values
   for the booking from then on.
7. [Event] When the Booking engine confirms a booking, the Booking engine shall record it as Confirmed with the booker
   as actor, and emit one booking-confirmed event with the booking's site, window, vehicle, booker, charging driver and
   booking code (clause 8).
8. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall give every booking, at its confirmation, a booking code that is unique across
   all bookings.
9. [Event] When the Pilot migration & cut-over component requests a carried-over booking (SS-421 clause 1), the
   Booking engine shall record it as Confirmed with the driver, fleet, site, window and vehicle the request names (no
   vehicle where it names none), the versions of the site's booking parameters and partner terms in force at the
   cut-over instant, the system as actor and the reason code CARRIED_OVER_AT_CUTOVER, whatever the site's bookable
   hours, time grid, booking durations, cap and pool, the vehicle's compatibility and registration and whether the
   driver's number is registered (REQ-231 v2, A-055, A-061).

## Notes
- Covers US-003 S1, S4, S5; US-006 S1 (accepted against EVSEs with either connector type) and S2 (refused when no
  compatible EVSE has room); US-311 S1 (a hold confirmed after a cap change meets the new cap).
- Settles bucket-5 #3: a confirmation refused under REQ-147 releases the hold at once (REQ-145) and is answered as
  "slot full" under REQ-008 (clause 4). Settles #45: confirmation re-applies REQ-004 against the rule in force at
  confirmation (clause 5, REQ-077).
- The pool check is the Phase 0 prototype of A-014 (CF-023, resolved-provisional). Clause 3 limits status exclusions
  to the current day, matching the reach of fault rebooking (REQ-048, "later booking at the site that day"); a
  booking for a later day on a still-faulted EVSE is handled at its allocation (SS-104, SS-105).

  (SS-221); added beyond the catalogue title (ss-log-1). P3.4 (2026-10-28): the code moved out of clause 7 into its own
