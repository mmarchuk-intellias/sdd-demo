# SS-113 · Maintenance-hold rebooking as on a fault, 'moved — maintenance'

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to rebook the bookings a maintenance hold takes off their EVSE as on a
fault, with their own reason code, so that planned work never sends a driver to an EVSE out of service and never counts
as a no-show.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a maintenance hold (SS-017) is placed on an EVSE that has active allocations inside its window, the
   Allocation & rebooking component shall rebook each of those bookings as on a fault (SS-108 clause 1) with the reason
   code 'moved — maintenance' and record no no-show for it.
2. [Unwanted] If no other compatible EVSE at the site is free for such a booking, then the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall hand it to unplaced handling (SS-109) as on a fault.
3. [Event] When a maintenance hold is placed, the Allocation & rebooking component shall re-check each confirmed booking
   without an allocation inside the maintenance hold's window against the site's remaining compatible EVSEs as on a
   fault (SS-108 clauses 2 and 3), and hand each one that no longer fits to unplaced handling before its allocation
   offset.
4. [State] While an EVSE is under a maintenance hold, the Allocation & rebooking component shall allocate no booking to
   it for any time inside the maintenance hold's window.
5. [Event] When a maintenance hold's window ends, the Allocation & rebooking component shall consider the EVSE for
   allocation again, with no return-to-service step.
6. [Event] When the Allocation & rebooking component has handled every booking a maintenance hold affects, the
   Allocation & rebooking component shall record and emit one outcome per affected booking as in SS-108 clauses 8 and
   10, with the reason code 'moved — maintenance', for the station portal (SS-227).

## Notes
- A-018 and Q-035: a maintenance hold takes EVSEs out physically, so affected bookings are rebooked as on a fault
  (A-017); maintenance stays out of no-show figures (CTX-036).
- Placing, validating and counting the maintenance hold (US-130 S1, S5, S6) are SS-017; the audit of the maintenance
  hold (US-412 S7) is SS-020 and SS-332; the station operator's notice is SS-313. Fault return-to-service rules (SS-209)
  do not apply.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 6 gives SS-227 (station portal, "fault and maintenance outcomes", REQ-051) a source for
  maintenance outcomes; before, only fault outcomes were emitted (SS-108 clause 8).
