# SS-426 · Recorded change management for every production change

As the Platform & operations component, I need every production change, to code or to infrastructure, to pass a
recorded review and approval before the pipeline applies it, and any change made around that process to be detected, so
that a SOC 2 auditor can see who changed production, when, and who approved it.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall apply a change to production only through the pipeline, from
   the protected main branch, after an approving review by a person other than the change's author.
2. [Event] When the pipeline applies a change to production, the Platform & operations component shall record the
   change, its task id, its author, its reviewer and approver, and the time it was applied.
3. [Unwanted] If someone pushes to the main branch directly, or merges without the required approval, then the
   Platform & operations component shall refuse the push or merge.
4. [Unwanted] If the daily drift check finds that a setting of a production resource defined in Terraform differs
   from its Terraform definition, then the Platform & operations component shall report it as an unrecorded change to
   the Amperlane CTO and the on-call engineer.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall keep the change records where the people who make changes
   cannot edit or delete them.
