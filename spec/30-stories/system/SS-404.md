# SS-404 · Monthly invoice build at close: one net-30 invoice per fleet, late records carried

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need to build, at the configured invoice close offset after each month, one
invoice per fleet from that fleet's ledger entries for the month, due 30 days after issue, and to carry records that
arrive after the close to the next invoice, so that each fleet gets one consolidated net-30 invoice a month with no
spreadsheet and no manual step.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the configured invoice close offset has passed after the end of a month that ends after the cut-over
   instant (SS-422), the Ledger & invoicing component shall build one invoice for that month for each fleet registered
   in the platform during the month, from that fleet's ledger entries for the month only, leaving out the charges held
   from invoicing by SS-405 clause 4, SS-408 clause 3 and SS-410 clause 1, with no manual step, file or data entry.
2. [Event] When a fleet's invoice for a month is built, the Ledger & invoicing component shall issue it with the close
   date as issue date and a due date 30 days after it (net-30, REQ-083).
3. [Unwanted] If month close runs again for a month in which a fleet's invoice has already been issued, then the
   Ledger & invoicing component shall issue no second invoice for that fleet and month.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall assign each booking charge to the month in which the booking's
   final slot starts in the site's time zone.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall post each booking charge to the fleet whose vehicle the booking
   is for, whichever other fleets its driver is registered to.
6. [Event] When an entry is posted for a month whose close has passed, for a booking whose slot started at or after the
   cut-over instant and that is in no open reconciliation case, the Ledger & invoicing component shall put the entry on
   the fleet's next invoice as an adjustment line naming the original month and booking, leaving that month's issued
   invoice unchanged.
7. [Event] When a fleet's invoices for a month are built (clause 1) and the fleet has no charge in that month, the
   Ledger & invoicing component shall still issue the fleet's invoice for that month, at zero.
8. [Event] When a fleet invoice is issued, the Ledger & invoicing component shall request the Payments integration to
   submit it (SS-412).

## Notes
- Covers JS-319 S1 (clauses 1, 8; submission SS-412), S3 (6), S5 (4); US-318 S1 (1, 2), S3 (3), S4 (5). Line
  content and traceability (JS-319 S2, S4; US-318 S2, S5) are SS-405.
- P3.4 critique: clause 1 now names the cut-over restriction (SS-410 clause 6) and the charges held from invoicing,
  and clause 6 its two exceptions, so that no two clauses give different responses to one close; the submission
  request moved from clause 2 to clause 8 (one behaviour per clause). Clause 4 counts a rebooked booking by its final
  slot, as reporting does (#44, SS-230, SS-232).
- Settles bucket-5 #41: a month with no charges still gets an invoice, at zero (REQ-083 as written). Whether Stripe
  issues a zero-total invoice is Q-062 (1); fallback: the platform issues the zero invoice and shows it in the fleet
  portal, recorded as paid at zero, without submission.
- Clause 6 leaves out an entry for a booking whose slot started before the cut-over (pilot invoicing process, SS-410
  clause 5) and a booking in an open reconciliation case (invoiced after the case closes, SS-408 clause 4).
- The invoice close offset is the platform parameter of REQ-218 in SS-026's catalogue (name aligned in P3.4; default
  in design.md §8, A-059). Period boundaries are in the site's time zone (A-013); the close instant it gives is
  Eastern time.
- If the payment provider is down at close, the invoice is still issued with these dates and SS-412 retries the
  submission (REQ-245).
- What is charged per booking depends on Q-001 and Q-053 (what is invoiced from launch while Q-001 is open); in the
  baseline the only charge is the no-show fee line (REQ-036, REQ-211). Charges with no booking exist only under parked
  options. Invoice recipients are fleet finance contacts (A-010, Q-037).
