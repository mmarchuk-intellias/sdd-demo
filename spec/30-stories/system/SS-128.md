# SS-128 · Late arrival: kept as a no-show with the arrival time, re-offer triggered

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to keep a booking whose driver arrives after grace as a no-show with the late
arrival stored, and to trigger an offer of the earliest free compatible slot at that site, so that the station sees the
late arrival and the driver can still charge there.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When arrival evidence for a booking, a QR scan or an attendant confirmation, comes after its end of grace,
   ops extensions included, the Arrival & no-show component shall keep or record the booking as a no-show and store the
   evidence's time with it as a late arrival.
2. [Event] When the Arrival & no-show component stores a late arrival, the Arrival & no-show component shall request,
   within the configured late-arrival re-offer time, an offer to the driver of the earliest free compatible slot at
   that site (SS-009), sent by Messaging (SS-316) and accepted with one tap (SS-011).
3. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall keep a released booking recorded as a no-show with its late
   arrival whether or not the driver accepts the re-offer; an accepted re-offer is a new booking.
4. [Unwanted] If further arrival evidence for the same booking follows a stored late arrival, then the Arrival & no-show
   component shall store it and request no second re-offer.

## Notes
- Q-003: whether the no-show fee still applies after the driver accepts the re-offer is an undecided client decision
  (REQ-304 parked); this SS neither waives nor confirms the fee (ops can waive it, SS-402).
- A slot taken before the driver accepts (JS-106 S3) is SS-011; only compatible slots (JS-106 S5) is SS-009; when
  nothing is free for the rest of the site's bookable hours that day, the reply says so and nothing is held (bucket-5
  item 4, SS-009).
- A session start after release is not a late arrival (SS-125 clause 4).
