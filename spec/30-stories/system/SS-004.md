# SS-004 · Connector compatibility through native connector or allowed adapter

As the Booking engine, I need one rule that decides whether an EVSE is compatible with a vehicle, from the vehicle's
native connector and the adapters it may use, so that no slot is offered, held, confirmed or allocated on an EVSE the
vehicle cannot plug into.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall treat an EVSE as compatible with a vehicle only if at least one of the EVSE's
   connectors has the vehicle's native connector type, or a connector type for which the vehicle has a recorded
   adapter that it may use.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall take whether a vehicle may use its recorded adapters from the vehicle's
   adapter override where one is set, and otherwise from its fleet's adapter policy, as the Fleet & vehicles module
   reports them at the moment of the check (SS-039).
3. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall apply this rule, and no other, wherever it counts, offers or holds capacity
   for a vehicle: the open-slot view, holds, the pool check at confirmation, same-site offers, cross-site alternatives
   and re-offers.
4. [Event] When allocation or rebooking asks for the EVSEs of a site that are compatible with a booking's vehicle, the
   Booking engine shall return only EVSEs that meet clause 1, so an EVSE that needs an adapter the vehicle may not use
   is never returned.
5. [Unwanted] If no EVSE at the requested site is compatible with the vehicle, then the Booking engine shall refuse the
   hold with the code NO_COMPATIBLE_EVSE and make no offer at that site.
6. [Event] When a vehicle's connector profile, its adapter override or its fleet's adapter policy changes, the Booking
   engine shall apply the new values to every query, hold, confirmation and offer made after the change, and change no
   confirmed booking because of it.

## Notes
- Covers US-006 S1, S3, S5; US-304 S1 – S3 (S3's "no booking allocated" through clause 4); JS-004 S3; JS-106 S5;
  JS-120 S5; JS-019 S4 (clause 3).
- An EVSE with connectors of both types is compatible with a CCS1-only and a NACS-only vehicle under clause 1; such
  EVSEs exist only if Q-013 confirms them (A-004).
- Clause 6 follows A-055: confirmed bookings that a change leaves without a compatible EVSE stand and are listed by
  the Fleet & vehicles module (SS-038); at allocation they are handled as unplaced (REQ-022, SS-105).
- Records used: EVSE connectors SS-030, vehicle profile SS-038, adapter policy and override SS-039. The EVSE choice at
  allocation is SS-101.
