# SS-431 · Hosting draws AWS Activate credits first

As the Platform & operations component, I need every platform AWS account to draw on Amperlane's AWS Activate credits
before any paid usage, and the credit balance reported every month, so that hosting stays inside Amperlane's budget.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall place every platform AWS account, production and
   non-production, in the Amperlane AWS organisation to which the AWS Activate credits are applied.
2. [Unwanted] If a Terraform plan creates an AWS account outside that organisation, then the Platform & operations
   component shall fail the pipeline run.
3. [Unwanted] If a service the platform uses is not covered by the AWS Activate credits, then the Platform & operations
   component shall list it with its monthly cost in the hosting cost report.
4. [Event] When a calendar month ends, the Platform & operations component shall report to the Amperlane CTO the
   hosting cost per account, the credits drawn, the credits remaining and their expiry date.

## Notes
- Enabler: REQ-137 has no story. Budget envelope: CTX-080, Q-025. Which services the credits cover is AWS's rule and
  not in the sources; clause 3 reports any that are not covered instead of assuming.
- A-029 keeps the workflow engine self-hosted on AWS, so its cost also draws on the credits (SS-035).
