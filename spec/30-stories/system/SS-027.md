# SS-027 · Per-site no-show fee terms required to go live and fixed at confirmation

As the Inventory & site configuration module, I need to hold each site's no-show fee amount and the station
operator's share of it as versioned partner terms, required before the site goes live, so that every no-show is
charged on the terms its booking was confirmed under and no site runs without agreed terms.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Inventory & site configuration module shall hold for each site a no-show fee amount, in integer
   minor units with a currency, and the station operator's share of it, as a versioned partner-terms record.
2. [Event] When ops record or change a site's fee amount or station share, the Inventory & site configuration module
   shall store a new version of the site's partner terms in force from that instant.
3. [Event] When a component asks for the partner terms of a booking, the Inventory & site configuration module shall
   return the version that was in force at the instant the booking was confirmed.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Inventory & site configuration module shall record an agreed "no fee yet" as an explicit amount
   of zero, distinct from an amount that was never recorded.
5. [Unwanted] If ops try to mark live a site whose fee amount or station share was never recorded, then the Inventory
   & site configuration module shall refuse it and name the missing term.
6. [Unwanted] If a station share is less than none or more than all of the fee amount, then the Inventory & site
   configuration module shall refuse the change and keep the terms in force.
7. [Unwanted] If a user who is not ops tries to record or change a site's partner terms, then the Inventory & site
   configuration module shall refuse it and keep the terms in force.

## Notes
- Covers US-109 S4 (each site's own terms) and the terms behind S1 and S5. The fee line is SS-401 and the fee decision
  SS-131; the confirmation that states the terms is SS-300 (REQ-172).
- Settles bucket-5 #17: a booking's fee amount and station share are fixed at confirmation, as REQ-146 does for other
  parameters, and are the terms its confirmation states (REQ-172); ops cannot mark a site live until both are
  recorded; an agreed "no fee yet" is an explicit zero. The amounts stay with Q-002 and Q-005.
- Changes are audited with before and after values (REQ-262, SS-333). Who pays the fee is parked (Q-002).
