# SS-135 · Dispute evidence timeline; location evidence only while the dispute is open

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to give ops a disputed no-show's whole history in time order in one view,
with location evidence only while the dispute is open, so that ops decide disputes on evidence without exposing a
driver's location at any other time.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user opens the evidence view of a dispute, the Arrival & no-show component shall return in one
   view the booking's history in time order: each state with its actor and reason code, the station-hold attempts and
   their answers (SS-121), the EVSE status at each of those times with its source (SS-205), the arrival evidence with
   unidentified session starts marked (SS-125), the messages sent to the driver and the session record.
2. [Unwanted] If no session record was received for the booking, then the Arrival & no-show component shall state in the
   view that no session record exists.
3. [Unwanted] If an EVSE status shown in the view was older than 60 s at that time at a connected site, then the Arrival
   & no-show component shall show it as stale, with its age at that time.
4. [State] While a dispute on a booking is open, the Arrival & no-show component shall include the booking's
   location-derived arrival evidence in the dispute view and record each view of it in the audit trail with the ops user
   and the time (SS-335).
5. [Unwanted] If no dispute on the booking is open, because none was raised or it was decided and closed, then the
   Arrival & no-show component shall return no location evidence for the booking to any caller.

## Notes
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 1's trigger "an ops user opens a dispute" could be read as raising one (SS-134); it is the
  evidence view. Clause 5 spoke for "any ops view", which other components render; it now limits what this component
  returns.
- Clause 3 applies constitution N2 to past statuses; status at a past moment comes from SS-205 (REQ-164); view logging
  follows REQ-263 (SS-335).
- A-024: with location off by default most disputes have no location evidence; no K or Y requirement collects location
  (REQ-173 and REQ-175 are I), so clauses 4 and 5 are tested with seeded evidence until one is admitted. A-047 and
  A-025: evidence is kept at least until the dispute window closes.
- Session records are unconfirmed (U-6; Q-009, Q-012); clause 2 covers their absence.
