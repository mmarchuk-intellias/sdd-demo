# SS-123 · Manual mode per site: an ops timed list of station holds instead of automated ones

As the Station-hold dispatcher, I need a per-site manual mode in which I send no automated station holds and give ops a
timed list of the station holds to arrange, so that bookings at the site are still honoured when its automated station
holds cannot be relied on.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When an ops user switches a site to manual mode, the Station-hold dispatcher shall send no charger
   reservation and no site-staff request for any station hold at that site that falls due after the switch.
2. [Event] When an ops user switches a site's mode, the Station-hold dispatcher shall record the switch in the audit
   trail with the ops user as actor and its time.
3. [State] While a site is in manual mode, the Station-hold dispatcher shall give ops a timed list of the site's station
   holds to arrange, each with its booking, EVSE, stall, the time to mark it and the time to clear it.
4. [Event] When an ops user records that a station hold was arranged with the site, from the manual-mode list or from a
   'contact the site' exception (SS-119), the Station-hold dispatcher shall record the station hold with the method
   "ops", the ops user and the time, mark it placed and mark any open exception for it resolved.
5. [Event] When a booking whose station hold ops arranged under manual mode is released, cancelled or rebooked, the
   Station-hold dispatcher shall mark that station hold on ops' list as to be cleared now.
6. [Unwanted] If a station hold on the manual-mode list is not recorded as arranged before the booking's end of grace,
   then the Station-hold dispatcher shall record the booking's station hold as failed, so that no no-show fee is created
   for it automatically (SS-131).
7. [Event] When a site is switched to manual mode, the Station-hold dispatcher shall keep the station holds already sent
   and clear them through the normal path (SS-120).
8. [Event] When an ops user switches a site back from manual mode, the Station-hold dispatcher shall send automatically
   again the station holds that fall due after the switch.
9. [Unwanted] If a user who is not ops, such as a station operator or a fleet manager, tries to switch a site's mode,
   then the Station-hold dispatcher shall refuse it and leave the mode unchanged.
10. [State] While a site is in manual mode, the Station-hold dispatcher shall leave the slot calendar's holds,
    confirmations and capacity checks for that site unchanged.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 12 (clauses 1 and 7): station holds already sent stay and are cleared through the normal path;
  those that fall due after the switch go only to ops' list.
- Ops are the safety net for patterns B and C (SRC-C2@00:53:49); when to use manual mode is ops' call; ops hours are
  Q-023.
- Clause 4 also covers US-129 S3 at sites not in manual mode (deviation noted in the Step 3.3 log). Clause 6 applies the
  rule of bucket-5 item 10 to ops' list.
- US-029 S2 (holds at the cap refused as at any site) is clause 10 with the Booking engine's checks (SS-002).
