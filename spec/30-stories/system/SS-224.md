# SS-224 · Fleet portal no-shows by driver (rebooks and cancellations excluded)

As the fleet portal (Partner portals), I need to list the no-shows of the fleet's drivers, and only real no-shows, so
that a fleet manager can take them up with the drivers concerned.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet manager opens the fleet's no-shows, the fleet portal shall list each booking of the fleet's
   vehicles that ended as a no-show (released at the end of grace without arrival) with its charging driver, vehicle,
   site, date and slot time.
2. [Event] When the fleet manager narrows the no-shows to one driver, the fleet portal shall list only that driver's
   no-shows.
3. [Unwanted] If a booking was rebooked for any reason ('moved — charger fault', 'moved — site full', 'moved —
   maintenance' or another rebook reason code), then the fleet portal shall not list the rebook as a no-show.
4. [Unwanted] If a booking was cancelled, then the fleet portal shall not list it as a no-show.
5. [Ubiquitous] The fleet portal shall show each listed no-show that has a recorded late arrival with its late arrival
   time.
6. [Event] When a no-show dispute raised by the fleet is decided (SS-134 clause 9), the fleet portal shall show the
   decision and its reason code on the dispute, and mark the no-show as newly decided in the fleet's no-show view
   until a user of the fleet opens it.

## Notes
- Covers US-307 S1, S2, S3 and S5. S4 (a shared driver's no-shows on another fleet's vehicles) is SS-323 (REQ-258,
  A-016).
- Clause 3: a rebook keeps the same booking (glossary "Rebook", bucket-5 #44), so only its final outcome can be a
  no-show; the pilot showed rebooked bookings as no-shows (CTX-047), which this guards against.
- Clause 4 covers the cancellation before the cut-off of US-307 S3 and also a late cancellation, which A-049 records as
  a late cancellation and sends to ops as a fee decision, not as a no-show (Q-042; batch 3 log).
- Late arrival: REQ-037. Who pays the fee and whether the fleet sees it here: Q-002.
- Clause 6 is A-062 (Gate 3, 2026-10-28): it receives SS-134 clause 9 for a fleet raiser and covers US-113 S1's last
  Then ("the fleet manager receives the decision and its reason code"). A fleet without portal users raised its
  dispute through ops (A-023), and ops pass the decision on; no e-mail is sent, since no e-mail provider is in
  integrations.md. Parent US-113 and requirement REQ-045 added (both Y); KYIV stays Y, epic stays EP-08.
