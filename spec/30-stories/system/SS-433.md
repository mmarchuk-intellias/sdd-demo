# SS-433 · Production access: named people, roles, every access logged in a separate log account

As the Platform & operations component, I need production systems and data reachable only through roles held by named
people or named machine identities, and every access and refused attempt logged from the first release into a log
account that the logged roles cannot change, so that Amperlane can show a SOC 2 auditor and the rideshare platform who
reached production and what they did there.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall grant access to production systems and data only through
   least-privilege roles assigned to the single-sign-on identities of named people or to named machine identities.
2. [Event] When a person or machine identity accesses a production system or production data, a database console query
   outside the application included, the Platform & operations component shall log the identity, the role used, what
   was accessed and the time.
3. [Unwanted] If a person without a production role tries to reach a production system, then the Platform & operations
   component shall refuse access and log the refused attempt with the person and the time.
4. [Unwanted] If a person's role does not cover the attempted action, such as a direct database query by a person whose
   role covers the ops console only, then the Platform & operations component shall refuse it and log the refused
   attempt.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall let the deployment pipeline act in production only under its
   own machine identity, with a role and a named owner, and log its access like a person's.
6. [Unwanted] If a production role is to be assigned to an account that several people use, or that is not tied to one
   named person, then the Platform & operations component shall refuse the assignment, so that the account reaches no
   production system.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall write the production access log to storage in a separate log
   account in which no role that the log records can change or delete an entry.
8. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall have the access log recording before the first production
   release is deployed, so that the release is its first entry.
