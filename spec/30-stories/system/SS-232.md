# SS-232 · No-show and late-arrival rates per month, fleet and site

As the reporting module, I need to report no-shows and late arrivals per month, fleet and site as shares of the month's
bookings, without counting rebooks as no-shows, so that each fleet and station operator sees its true no-show rate.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the no-show report for a month is requested, the reporting module shall give for each fleet and for each
   site the number of no-shows and of late arrivals, each also as a share of all bookings whose final slot started in
   that month (clause 2).
2. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall place each booking in the month in which its final slot started, in the
   site's time zone, and count a rebooked booking once, in the month of its final slot.
3. [Unwanted] If a booking was rebooked, then the reporting module shall count it as a no-show only if its final slot
   ended in a release at the end of grace without arrival, and never because of the rebook.
4. [Unwanted] If a booking was cancelled, then the reporting module shall count it among the month's bookings and not as
   a no-show.
5. [Event] When a booking's arrival is recorded after the end of its grace period, including any ops extension, the
   reporting module shall count it both as a no-show and as a late arrival.
6. [Ubiquitous] The reporting module shall count a booking whose arrival was recorded within its grace period neither
   as a no-show nor as a late arrival.

## Notes
- Covers US-329 S1 – S5.
- Settles bucket-5 item #44: a rebook keeps the same booking (glossary "Rebook"), so it counts once in the month's
  bookings, in the month of its final slot. Clause 3 excludes fault, site-full and maintenance rebooks (REQ-092) and
  any other rebook reason code.
- Clause 4 covers US-329 S3 (cancelled before the cut-off) and also a late cancellation, which A-049 records as a late
  cancellation and sends to ops as a fee decision, not as a no-show (Q-042; batch 3 log).
- Pilot data is marked in this report too (REQ-225, SS-230). Depends on CF-018, Q-029 and Q-044. Late arrival:
  REQ-037; grace per site: A-003.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 1 said "bookings placed in that month", which could be read as the month the booking was
  made and contradicted clause 2 and REQ-092 ("whose slot fell in that month"). Clause 3 reworded to one response.
