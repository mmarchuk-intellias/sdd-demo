# SS-043 · Vehicle-data authorisation recorded by ops, visible to and revocable by the fleet

As the Fleet & vehicles module, I need to hold each fleet's authorisation as account owner for vehicle data and permit
vehicle-data reads only while it is in force, so that a fleet's vehicle data is never read without the account owner's
consent and reading stops when the fleet revokes it.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When ops record a fleet's authorisation as account owner, from the signed fleet agreement, with its date and
   its scope (battery level and charging state), the Fleet & vehicles module shall hold it as the fleet's authorisation
   in force from that moment.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall show a fleet's manager the fleet's authorisation with its date, its
   scope, who recorded it and whether it is in force.
3. [Event] When the fleet manager revokes the fleet's authorisation in the fleet portal, the Fleet & vehicles module
   shall end it at that instant and change no booking because of it.
4. [Optional] Where a vehicle-data source is connected for a fleet, the Fleet & vehicles module shall permit a read for
   a vehicle only while the vehicle's current fleet has an authorisation in force, and only for the data in its scope.
5. [Unwanted] If no authorisation is in force for the vehicle's current fleet, because none was recorded, it was
   revoked or the vehicle moved to another fleet, then the Fleet & vehicles module shall refuse every vehicle-data read
   for that vehicle, and its bookings proceed without vehicle data.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall never permit a read of a vehicle's location on the fleet's
   authorisation alone; a location read also needs the charging driver's opt-in in force (SS-341).

## Notes
- Covers US-013 S1 – S4.
- Settles bucket-5 #7: ops record the authorisation at onboarding from the signed fleet agreement (A-024), with date
  and scope; the fleet manager sees and revokes it in the fleet portal (REQ-257).
- Constitution R7: no vehicle-data source is confirmed for the MVP (Geotab U-9, Q-014; Tesla Fleet API and Smartcar
  parked, CF-004, Q-015), hence the [Optional] clause; tests use a simulated source. Q-016 (how the fleet authorises).
- Bookings never need vehicle data (REQ-019, SS-044).
