# SS-039 · Fleet adapter policy with per-vehicle override

As the Fleet & vehicles module, I need each fleet to say once whether its vehicles may use adapters and each vehicle
to override it, so that drivers are sent to an EVSE that needs an adapter only where the fleet accepts that, without
the fleet manager editing every vehicle.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall hold for each fleet an adapter policy, allow or forbid, and for each
   vehicle an optional override, allow or forbid.
2. [Event] When a fleet is created, the Fleet & vehicles module shall set its adapter policy to the configured default
   for new fleets.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall report a vehicle as allowed to use its recorded adapters according
   to its override where one is set, and otherwise according to its fleet's adapter policy, as they stand at the
   moment of the query.
4. [Event] When a fleet manager of the fleet, or ops, change the fleet's adapter policy, the Fleet & vehicles module
   shall apply it from that moment to every vehicle of the fleet without an override and keep every override unchanged.
5. [Event] When a vehicle's override is removed, the Fleet & vehicles module shall report the vehicle under its fleet's
   adapter policy from that moment.
6. [Unwanted] If a driver, or any user who is neither a fleet manager of the fleet nor ops, tries to change the fleet's
   adapter policy or a vehicle override, then the Fleet & vehicles module shall refuse it and leave the policy and the
   overrides unchanged.

## Notes
- Covers US-304 S1 – S5 (S4: role check here; a fleet manager of another fleet is also refused by SS-323). The
  matching that uses the result is SS-004.
- Ops may set the policy for a fleet without portal users (A-023; US-304 leaves it open; ss-log-1). The default for
  new fleets is a platform parameter of SS-026; its default is in design.md §8 (A-059).
- A change applies to new bookings only; affected confirmed bookings are listed under SS-038 (A-055). Changes are
  audited with before and after values (REQ-262, SS-333).
