# SS-304 · Messaging consent and STOP/START per channel

As the Messaging component, I need to send SMS or WhatsApp messages to a number only on a channel where its agreement
to booking messages is recorded and has not been ended, so that the platform meets carrier rules and the driver
decides what reaches the phone.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall send an SMS or WhatsApp message to a driver or booker-only number only on
   a channel for which that number's agreement to booking messages is recorded and has not been ended by STOP.
2. [Event] When an agreement to booking messages is recorded for a number, the Messaging component shall store the
   channel, the time, how it was given, from the configured list of agreement forms (Q-056), and who recorded it.
3. [Unwanted] If a message is due for a number on a channel without an agreement in force, then the Messaging component
   shall not send it on that channel, leaving the booking or action that triggered it unaffected.
4. [Event] When a STOP command arrives on a channel (SS-310), the Messaging component shall end the number's agreement
   on that channel at once and record the time.
5. [Unwanted] If a message for a number is scheduled or queued but not yet handed to the messaging provider when STOP
   arrives on that channel, then the Messaging component shall not send it on that channel.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall keep a STOP on one channel from changing the number's agreement on its
   other channel.
7. [Event] When a START command arrives on a channel where the number had sent STOP, the Messaging component shall
   record a new agreement on that channel with the time and the form "START reply", and resume messages on it.
8. [Event] When an agreement is first recorded for a registered number, the Messaging component shall emit an
   agreement-recorded event, so that a pending welcome can be sent (SS-306).
9. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall show ops, for each number and channel, whether an agreement is in force,
   when it was recorded or ended, and how it was given.
10. [Event] When the Messaging component does not send a message for want of agreement (clause 3), the Messaging
    component shall report it as not sent for want of agreement to SS-307 and to the component that requested it.

## Notes
- How the agreement is given and recorded, whether a pilot driver's past use of the pilot number counts, and whether
  the rule covers booker-only numbers are Q-056 (client decision). Clause 2 records whichever forms are configured; the
  forms have no default (design.md §8). Q-047 covers the carrier registration and the STOP acknowledgement text.
  Booker-only numbers are gated too until Q-056 says otherwise; a station operator's notice contact (SS-313) is a
  business contact, not a driver, and is outside REQ-248.
- START (clause 7) follows bucket-5 item #27 and is subject to Q-056; the keywords themselves, in any case (US-229
  S5), are SS-310.
- A driver who stopped every channel keeps a valid booking (US-229 S2); time-critical messages that could not be sent
  are listed for ops by SS-307 (JS-226 S5). Ops messages refused here tell the ops user why (SS-314, US-126 S5).
- Welcome after a late agreement (US-225 S2): SS-306.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): old clause 3 (do not send, and report) split into clauses 3 and 10.
