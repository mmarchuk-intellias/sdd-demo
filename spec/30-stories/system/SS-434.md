# SS-434 · Amperlane ownership: its repositories, Terraform-only AWS, full data export

As the Platform & operations component, I need all code, infrastructure, pipelines and data in Amperlane's own
repositories and AWS accounts under Amperlane's Terraform, and a full data export that Amperlane's engineers can run
alone, so that Amperlane owns its product outright and can change or leave its vendor at any time.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall keep all platform code, Terraform and its state, pipelines,
   container images and secrets in repositories and AWS accounts owned by Amperlane.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall define every cloud resource of every environment in
   Terraform kept in Amperlane's repositories.
3. [Unwanted] If the daily drift check finds a cloud resource, in any environment, that no Terraform definition covers,
   then the Platform & operations component shall report it to the Amperlane CTO as not defined in Terraform.
4. [State] While no Intellias account has access to Amperlane's repositories or AWS accounts, the Platform & operations
   component shall build, test, deploy and export the platform with Amperlane's accounts and repositories alone.
5. [Event] When an Amperlane engineer with the export role starts a full data export, the Platform & operations
   component shall export all platform data, bookings, migrated pilot history, the audit trail and the ledger included,
   in open formats readable without proprietary tools, with no step by Intellias.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Platform & operations component shall write the full data export only to storage in Amperlane's
   production AWS account.
7. [Unwanted] If the export cannot read part of a module's data, then the Platform & operations component shall fail
   the export as a whole and name the module, rather than finish with a partial export.
8. [Unwanted] If a person without the export role starts a full data export, then the Platform & operations component
   shall refuse it.
