# SS-011 · Offer acceptance by tap or keyword, with taken slot, expiry and decline

As the Booking engine, I need to turn a driver's acceptance of an offered slot into a booking or a rebooking, and to
handle a declined, unanswered, expired or already-taken offer, so that a driver who lost a slot can take another with
one tap or one keyword and no ops call.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the booker accepts a same-site offer whose hold has not expired, the Booking engine shall confirm a
   booking from that hold under SS-003.
2. [Event] When the charging driver accepts a re-offer made after a no-show release, the Booking engine shall create a
   new booking for the offered slot, with the same vehicle, duration, booker and charging driver, if a hold for it
   would be accepted at that moment, and leave the released booking a no-show.
3. [Event] When the charging driver accepts an alternative offered after a fault, a maintenance hold or a full site,
   the Booking engine shall rebook the same booking to the accepted site and slot with the reason code the offer
   carries, release its capacity in the original slot, record no no-show for the original slot, record the accepted
   site's parameters and partner terms in force at that instant, and emit one booking-rebooked event.
4. [Unwanted] If the accepted slot can no longer take a hold for the booking's vehicle and duration when the
   acceptance arrives, then the Booking engine shall make no booking for it and return a new same-site offer (SS-009).
5. [Unwanted] If an acceptance of a held same-site offer arrives after its hold expired, then the Booking engine shall
   handle it as a new hold and confirmation for the offered slot under SS-002 and SS-003, never confirming from the
   expired hold, so a slot that is full by then is answered with the next same-site offer (SS-009).
6. [Event] When the driver declines an offer, the Booking engine shall release any hold the offer placed, book nothing
   from it and record the decline on the offer.
7. [Event] When one slot of an offer is accepted, the Booking engine shall withdraw the other slots offered with it
   and release any hold placed for them.
8. [Unwanted] If an acceptance arrives for an offered slot whose start time has passed, then the Booking engine shall
   refuse it with the code OFFER_EXPIRED and return a new same-site offer (SS-009).
9. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall record every offer with the booking it was made for, the slots offered, the
   time it was made and the driver's answer (accepted, declined or none yet), and make them readable by the ops console.
10. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall give the same result to an acceptance or a decline whether it came from one
    tap on the booking page or from a keyword reply.

## Notes
- Covers JS-004 S1 (confirm within the hold time); JS-019 S2; JS-106 S1 – S3; JS-120 S1 – S4 (S4: the offers and the
  missing answer are readable for the ops queue, SS-109, SS-219); US-214 S3 and S4; JS-228 S3 and S4 (the Booking
  engine's part; keywords are SS-310, the replies Messaging).
- Reason codes on rebooking come from the requesting SS: 'moved — charger fault' (SS-108), 'moved — maintenance'
  (SS-113), 'moved — site full' (SS-105). Clearing the old station hold on rebooking is SS-120; the allocation notice
  is SS-301; timers follow the booking (SS-032).
- Whether a no-show fee still applies after a late driver accepts the re-offer is Q-003 (REQ-304 parked); clause 2
  neither creates nor waives a fee. No fee is created automatically for a rebooked booking (REQ-180, SS-131).
- REQ-241 v2 (CR-001): accepting an offered slot needs no one-time code (SS-321); REQ-242 limits keyword senders
  (SS-322). A repeated acceptance with the same key is SS-014 (JS-421 S6).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 5 held two branches (confirm, or offer again); it is now one rule (a new hold and
  confirmation), whose two outcomes follow from SS-002 and SS-003.
- Clause 8 (an unheld offer ends when its offered slot starts) is this batch's rule; no requirement sets an offer
  expiry (GAP in ss-log-1).
