# SS-012 · Afternoon and overnight slots confirmed together, each on its own

As the Booking engine, I need to confirm an afternoon slot and an overnight slot for one vehicle with one confirmation
while deciding each slot on its own, so that a cab's charging for the whole day is arranged in one step and one slot
that fails never takes the other down with it.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a booker confirms, in one confirmation, two held slots for the same vehicle, the Booking engine shall
   decide each under SS-003 on its own and confirm each that passes as a separate booking with its own site, start
   time, duration and booking code.
2. [Unwanted] If one of the two cannot be confirmed because its hold expired or its checks fail, then the Booking
   engine shall still confirm the other, refuse the failed one with its reason code, and name the failed slot in the
   answer, with a same-site offer (SS-009) where the reason is a full slot.
3. [Unwanted] If the booker selects a slot that is not open for the vehicle as the second slot of the pair, then the
   Booking engine shall refuse the hold on that slot and keep the hold on the other slot unchanged.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall emit one booking-confirmed event for each booking it confirms from a paired
   confirmation.
5. [Unwanted] If the two slots of a paired confirmation name different vehicles, then the Booking engine shall refuse
   the paired confirmation with the code PAIR_VEHICLE_MISMATCH and confirm neither.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall count and expire each hold of a pair on its own under SS-002, so a hold of the
   pair that expires stops counting against any cap from its expiry instant while the other hold stays in place.

## Notes
- Covers JS-008 S1 – S4 (S3's "capacity offered again": clause 6, added in P3.4 2026-10-28). S4's dates in the site's
  time zone are SS-021; the overnight slot itself is SS-001 clause 4 (REQ-152, A-028, CF-015 needs-decision:
  constitution R6 flag carried by JS-008).
- Settles bucket-5 #6: each slot is confirmed on its own; the answer names the failed slot with REQ-008's alternative.
- One confirmation message per booking to each recipient (bucket-5 #29) follows from clause 4 and is SS-300.
