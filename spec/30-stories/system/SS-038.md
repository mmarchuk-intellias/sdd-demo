# SS-038 · Vehicle profiles added, edited and retired with connector, adapters and power

As the Fleet & vehicles module, I need to keep each fleet vehicle's native connector, recorded adapters and maximum DC
power, and let the fleet manager or ops add, edit and retire vehicles, so that drivers are offered only EVSEs their
vehicle can charge at and a vehicle that has left the fleet can no longer be booked.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fleet manager or ops add a vehicle with its plate, a native connector of CCS1 or NACS, zero or more
   recorded adapters and a maximum DC charging power in kW, the Fleet & vehicles module shall register it to the fleet
   and report it as bookable.
2. [Unwanted] If a vehicle is saved without a native connector, with a connector type other than CCS1 or NACS, or
   without a positive maximum DC charging power, then the Fleet & vehicles module shall not save it and shall name the
   missing or invalid field.
3. [Unwanted] If a vehicle is added with a plate already used by an active vehicle of the same fleet, then the Fleet &
   vehicles module shall not add it and shall say the plate is already registered.
4. [Event] When a fleet manager or ops edit a vehicle's connector, adapters or power, the Fleet & vehicles module shall
   report the new profile to every check made after the change.
5. [Event] When a fleet manager or ops retire a vehicle, the Fleet & vehicles module shall report it as retired, and so
   not bookable on any channel, from that moment, and keep it linked to its past bookings, shown as retired.
6. [Event] When a vehicle is retired, or a change to its profile, its adapter override or its fleet's adapter policy
   leaves a confirmed future booking of it with no compatible EVSE at its site, the Fleet & vehicles module shall keep
   the booking and list it for the fleet manager, or for ops where the fleet has no portal user, to act on.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Fleet & vehicles module shall register and report as bookable a vehicle that has no telematics
   link, with no state of charge or location data.

## Notes
- Covers US-303 S1 – S4 and S6; US-301 S1 (vehicles part) and S4; US-006 S6 (vehicle side; connectors are SS-030).
  US-303 S5 (another fleet's vehicle) is SS-323.
- REQ-015's "allowed adapters" are the adapters recorded for the vehicle; whether it may use them is the adapter
  policy and override of SS-039 (REQ-072).
- Clause 6 is A-055: the change applies to new bookings; confirmed bookings stand, are listed and are acted on one by
  one; a booking no compatible EVSE can take at allocation is unplaced (REQ-022, SS-105).
- Changes are audited (REQ-262 fleet policies, SS-333). Plates and power values in tests are test data.
