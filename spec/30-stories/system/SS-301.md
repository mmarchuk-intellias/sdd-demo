# SS-301 · Allocation, reallocation and rebooking notices to the charging driver

As the Messaging component, I need to tell the charging driver which EVSE and stall a booking is on each time it is
allocated or reallocated, and the new site and time when ops rebook it to another slot or site, so that the driver goes
straight to the right EVSE at the right time although the booking was made against the site's pool.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a booking is allocated to an EVSE (SS-100), the Messaging component shall send the booking's charging
   driver one notice naming the site, the EVSE and its stall, and the booking's start time.
2. [Event] When a booking's allocation changes to another EVSE, whatever the cause (occupied EVSE, blocked stall,
   charger fault, maintenance hold or an ops rebook), the Messaging component shall send the charging driver one notice
   naming the new EVSE and its stall and saying that it replaces the EVSE named before.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall address every allocation and reallocation notice to the booking's
   charging driver, whoever the booker is.
4. [Unwanted] If a booking has no active allocation, then the Messaging component shall send no message that names an
   EVSE for that booking.
5. [Unwanted] If a booking is reallocated before its earlier notice was handed to the messaging provider, then the
   Messaging component shall send only the notice naming the current EVSE.
6. [Unwanted] If the same allocation is reported to the Messaging component more than once, then the Messaging
   component shall send no further notice for it.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall hold no message template, for drivers, site staff or ops, that asks a
   walk-up to leave, unplug or stop charging.
8. [Event] When a booking is rebooked to another slot or site by an ops rebooking (SS-114) or an approved rebooking
   proposal (SS-110), the Messaging component shall send the charging driver one rebooking notice in the driver's
   language naming the booking's new site, date and start time in the site's time zone and the rebook's reason code,
   with the booking's link and code.
9. [Unwanted] If the same rebooking is reported to the Messaging component more than once, then the Messaging component
   shall send no further rebooking notice for it.
10. [Optional] Where arrival from a session start is not switched on for the booking's site (SS-125 clause 7), the
    Messaging component shall ask the charging driver, in each allocation and reallocation notice (clauses 1 and 2), to
    scan the EVSE's QR code before plugging in.

## Notes
- Allocation is SS-100 and SS-101; reallocation is SS-104 (occupied), SS-106 (blocked stall), SS-108 (charger fault),
  SS-113 (maintenance hold) and SS-114 (ops rebook). An allocation notice is a time-critical message (REQ-219, SS-307).
- Clause 4 covers US-017 S2 and S3 (no EVSE named before allocation, or when allocation fails). JS-018 S3: clause 7 is
  the messaging part; no stop command and no site-staff request for an occupied EVSE are SS-104 and SS-118 (REQ-020).
- Language, link and booking code: SS-302, SS-303; agreement gate SS-304; channel SS-308.
- Depends on A-002 (allocation offset, per site), A-008 and Q-034 (the driver's channel; WhatsApp unconfirmed, U-10).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28, cross-batch fix): parents US-121 and US-129 and requirements REQ-188 and REQ-058 added; clauses 8
  and 9 are the driver message for a rebooking to another slot or site (US-121 S2, US-129 S2, requested by SS-110 clause
  2 and SS-114 clause 2; GAP-B2-5). A rebooking to another EVSE at the same time is a reallocation notice (clause 2). An
  alternative the driver accepts is answered by SS-316 clause 7, not by clause 8. GAP in p34-Y: no requirement states
  this message; REQ-200 covers EVSE allocation only. KYIV stays K.
- Gate 3 (2026-10-28, triage edit 6e.2): clause 10. Where session-start arrival is off (A-051), a booked driver who
  plugs in without scanning makes the allocated EVSE look in use at the pre-arrival check; a scan first records arrival
  (SS-124), and SS-104 clause 7 then keeps the allocation. The CSMS side is Q-051 and A-051 (2). The reallocation
  notice carries the same request, since it names the EVSE the driver will use.
