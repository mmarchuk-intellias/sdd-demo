# SS-416 · Pilot history loaded into the platform model, linked to live records

As the Pilot migration & cut-over component, I need to load the pilot history since October 2025 into the platform's
own booking model, linked to the same driver, fleet, site and EVSE records as platform bookings, so that pilot and
platform bookings are queried and analysed together instead of sitting in a CSV dump.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a migration run loads a pilot booking, the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall store it in the
   platform's booking model, marked as migrated pilot data with its source system and source id, and linked to the
   platform's driver, fleet, site and EVSE records.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall store migrated bookings so that a query of bookings by
   driver, fleet, site or EVSE returns pilot and platform bookings together in one result, each pilot booking marked as
   pilot data.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall load each pilot booking with its slot, its outcome
   (SS-418), the arrival time where the pilot recorded one, and its EVSE.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall link each pilot booking to the driver record of its
   mobile number, creating that record when no fleet has registered the number yet, so that later bookings from that
   number fall under the same record.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall map a pilot Sheet column to an EVSE only through the
   documented column-to-EVSE mapping of its site, so that pilot and platform bookings on one EVSE share one EVSE
   record.
6. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall attach each pilot booking to the fleet that Airtable
   records for that booking.
7. [Unwanted] If a pilot booking names a site that the documented site mapping does not match to Gowanus, JFK or LIC,
   then the Pilot migration & cut-over component shall not load it and shall put it on the rejected list with the
   reason "unknown site".
8. [Unwanted] If a pilot booking sits in a Sheet column that the mapping does not map to an EVSE, then the Pilot
   migration & cut-over component shall not load it and shall put it on the rejected list with the reason "Sheet
   column maps to no EVSE".
9. [Event] When a migration run loads a pilot booking that Airtable holds and no Sheet cell matches, the Pilot
   migration & cut-over component shall load it with its driver, fleet, site and slot and show its EVSE as not
   recorded.
10. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall process every source booking record from October 2025
    on, and skip none for its date.
11. [Ubiquitous] The Pilot migration & cut-over component shall start no booking workflow and request no message for a
    migrated pilot booking.

## Notes
- Covers US-400 S1 (clauses 1 – 5), S2 (7), S3 (8), S4 (9; its conflict-list entry is SS-419 clause 9, which now
  lists US-400 as a parent), S5 (4), S6 (6), S7 (2, 5), S8 (10; the early records also carry the data-quality flag,
  SS-418).
- Depends on A-026 (Airtable and the Sheets since October 2025) and Q-020 (scope); A-004 and Q-013 (a pilot column is
  one EVSE); A-006 (a driver is a registered mobile number) and A-016 (a driver may drive for several fleets). Stripe
  Billing history and Twilio logs are parked (REQ-314).
- The pilot records the fleet per booking, not the vehicle (CTX-042), so migrated bookings carry no vehicle.
- A driver record created for an unregistered number gives no right to book; when a fleet registers the number
  (Fleet & vehicles), it attaches to that record (US-400 S5).
- P3.4 critique: clause 9 had a state as its trigger and two behaviours (load; list); the listing is SS-419 clause 9.
  A pilot booking whose slot starts at or after the cut-over instant becomes a carried-over booking that replaces its
  migrated copy (SS-421 clause 11), so clause 11 here never applies to it.
- No ledger entry is posted for migrated bookings (SS-410). Outcomes per record and count reconciliation: SS-417;
  re-classification: SS-418; Sheet versus Airtable: SS-419. Reports mark pilot data (REQ-225, Reporting).
- Driver names and numbers in the history are personal data under the retention matrix (A-025).
