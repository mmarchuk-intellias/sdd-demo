# SS-316 · Re-offer and alternatives message within the set time, accepted by one tap or keyword

As the Messaging component, I need to send the charging driver the slots the booking engine offers after a late
arrival, a full site or a charger fault, in time to act on them and each accepted by one tap or a keyword, so that the
driver still charges soon instead of being lost.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When the booking engine produces offers for a booking (late arrival SS-128, site full SS-105, unplaced after
   a fault SS-109), the Messaging component shall send the charging driver one message listing each offered slot with
   its site, date and start time in the site's time zone, a one-tap accept link and an accept keyword with the slot's
   option number, and how to decline.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall hand a site-full offer message to the messaging provider within the
   dispatch target of constitution N11 after the offers are produced.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall hand a late-arrival offer message to the messaging provider within the
   configured late-arrival re-offer time (REQ-038) of the late arrival being recorded.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall hand a fault alternatives message to the messaging provider within the
   configured fault offer time (REQ-049) of the charger fault being recorded.
5. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall write the offer message and its keywords in the charging driver's
   language (SS-303).
6. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall state in the offer message that the offered slots are not held for the
   driver and are booked only if still free when accepted (SS-009 clause 3, SS-010 clause 4, SS-011 clause 4).
7. [Event] When the driver taps an offer's accept link or replies with its keyword, the Messaging component shall pass
   the acceptance to the booking engine (SS-011) and reply with the booked slot, or with the next free slot offered
   when the chosen one was taken first.
8. [Unwanted] If the booking engine produces no offer, then the Messaging component shall tell the charging driver that
   no compatible slot is free and, for a site-full or fault case, that the booking stands and is with ops.
9. [Ubiquitous] The Messaging component shall send offer messages whether or not any ops user is signed in.
10. [Unwanted] If the same offers for a booking reach the Messaging component more than once, then the Messaging
    component shall send the offer message once.

## Notes
- Which slots are offered, compatibility, ranking of "nearest" (#20) and holding them are SS-009, SS-010 and SS-011;
  the site-full case and its ops exception are SS-105, which also settles what happens when no alternative is accepted
  (#14: the booking stands); late arrival SS-128; unplaced fault bookings SS-109 (A-017).
- REQ-148 v2 (CR-007) says "immediately"; clause 2 reads it as the dispatch target of constitution N11 (A-036).
  Clauses 3 and 4 name the set times of REQ-038 and REQ-049; their defaults are in design.md §8 (A-059).
- One-tap accept needs no one-time code (REQ-241 v2, SS-321, SS-327). Offer messages are time-critical (REQ-219,
  SS-307). Whether the no-show fee still applies after a re-offer is accepted is Q-003 (not assumed).
- Depends on A-019 (Q-033) for the site-full outcome and Q-023 for ops hours (JS-019 S5 needs only clause 9).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 6 said "how long the offered slots are held", but the offers this SS sends (late arrival,
  site full, fault) are not held (SS-009 clause 3, SS-010 clause 4); it now says so, and acceptance re-checks the slot
  (SS-011 clause 4).
- P3.4: clause 3's parameter renamed to SS-026's "late-arrival re-offer time".
