# SS-120 · Clearing station holds on release, cancellation or rebooking

As the Station-hold dispatcher, I need to clear every station hold I placed as soon as its booking is released,
cancelled or rebooked, by cancelling the charger reservation or telling site staff to clear the stall, so that no EVSE
stays blocked for walk-ups after its booking ends.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a booking for which a site-staff station-hold request was sent is released after grace, cancelled (a
   force-release included) or rebooked to another EVSE, site or time, the Station-hold dispatcher shall send site staff,
   on the request's channel and within the site's configured clear-instruction time, an instruction to clear that stall
   for walk-ups.
2. [Event] When a booking with a charger reservation is released after grace, cancelled (a force-release included) or
   rebooked to another EVSE, site or time, the Station-hold dispatcher shall request cancellation of the charger
   reservation through the site's CSMS integration (SS-212).
3. [Unwanted] If the CSMS integration returns the result "cancellation unconfirmed" for a charger reservation
   cancellation (SS-212), then the Station-hold dispatcher shall raise a 'station hold not cleared' ops exception for
   the booking (SS-219) and leave the booking's new state unchanged.
4. [Unwanted] If a clear instruction is not delivered within the site's configured site-staff delivery timeout, then the
   Station-hold dispatcher shall raise a 'station hold not cleared' ops exception for the booking (SS-219).
5. [Unwanted] If arrival is recorded on a booking with a site-staff station hold, then the Station-hold dispatcher shall
   send no clear instruction for it at the end of grace.
6. [Unwanted] If a booking is cancelled before any station hold was requested for it, then the Station-hold dispatcher
   shall send no clear instruction and request no reservation cancellation for it.
7. [Ubiquitous] The Station-hold dispatcher shall match every site-staff station-hold request it sends with exactly one
   clear instruction once its booking is released, cancelled or rebooked.

## Notes
- Settles bucket-5 item 11 (clauses 3 and 4). The retries of an unanswered cancellation, until the CSMS confirms it or
  the reservation expires, are SS-212's (batch 3 contract); this SS acts only on its "cancellation unconfirmed" result.
  Item 9: a booking with both a late-accepted reservation and a site-staff request gets both clauses 1 and 2. Item 12:
  station holds sent before a switch to manual mode are cleared here.
- REQ-114 is stated for Gowanus; clause 7 applies at every site and is checked over a day at Gowanus (US-027 S6, A-048
  with Q-059).
- A-012, Q-009 (cancelling through AMPECO). A force-release clears station holds as a cancellation does (US-128 S2,
  REQ-057); its fee is SS-016 (item 23). No release after arrival: SS-127.
