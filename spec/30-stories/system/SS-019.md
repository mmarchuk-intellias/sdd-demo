# SS-019 · Bookings above a lowered limit stand and are listed for action

As the Booking engine, I need to keep confirmed bookings that a capacity cut leaves above the new limit and list each
of them for whoever made the cut, so that no driver loses a confirmed slot without a person deciding it.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a capacity rule's cap is lowered (SS-024), a fleet-priority hold is placed (SS-018) or a site's
   bookable hours are reduced (SS-023) so that confirmed bookings in a slot exceed the new limit, the Booking engine
   shall keep every one of those bookings confirmed.
2. [Event] When a slot exceeds its new limit under clause 1, the Booking engine shall list the slot with its bookings
   and the number by which it is over, marking the bookings confirmed last as above the limit, for the station
   operator who made the cut in the station portal, or for ops where ops made it.
3. [Unwanted] If a hold request covers a slot that is over its limit, then the Booking engine shall refuse it with the
   code SLOT_FULL as for a full slot (SS-002 clause 3).
4. [Ubiquitous] The Booking engine shall never cancel, release or rebook a booking because it is above a lowered limit;
   any later change to such a booking is made by a person's action and recorded with its actor and reason code.
5. [Event] When a booking in an over-limit slot is cancelled, released or rebooked, the Booking engine shall recompute
   the slot's excess and remove the slot from the list once it is within its limit.

## Notes
- Covers JS-312 S1 – S3, S5 and US-131 S3. JS-312 S4 (other station operators see nothing) is SS-323.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 3 was a [State] clause with no [Unwanted] clause in the SS; it is now the refusal in
  [Unwanted] form, with the same code and offer as any full slot.
- A-018 and Q-035; A-055 extends the rule to reduced bookable hours (clause 1) and names which bookings are above the
  limit (those confirmed last). The station operator can keep a listed booking or refer it to ops; only ops rebook or
  release it, with a reason code (SS-016), and the driver is told (REQ-057).
- Clause 4 holds against timers too: allocation, station hold and reminder run as for any booking (SS-032). A booking
  that no compatible EVSE can take at allocation is unplaced (REQ-022, SS-105), not dropped.
