# SS-410 · Cut-over invoicing: each booking invoiced once, by slot start against the cut-over instant

As the Ledger & invoicing component, I need to invoice only bookings whose slot starts at or after the cut-over instant,
and to hand the pilot invoicing process the platform bookings whose slots started before it, so that every booking
around the cut-over is invoiced exactly once.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall put on a platform invoice only charges of bookings whose slot
   starts at or after the cut-over instant configured in the Pilot migration & cut-over component (SS-422).
2. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall post no ledger entry for a migrated pilot booking (SS-416).
3. [Ubiquitous] The Ledger & invoicing component shall invoice a pilot booking carried over at cut-over (SS-421) as a
   platform booking, once, under its one booking id, also after it is rebooked.
4. [Event] When the configured invoice close offset after a month's end (SS-404 clause 1) is first reached after the
   cut-over instant, the Ledger & invoicing component shall produce a one-time export, for the pilot invoicing process,
   of every booking run in the platform whose slot started before the cut-over instant, with booking id, fleet, site,
   slot and outcome.
5. [Unwanted] If an entry is posted after that export for a booking whose slot started before the cut-over instant, a
   fee, a reversal or a waiver, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall put it on no platform invoice and list it
   for the pilot invoicing process.
6. [Unwanted] If a month ends before the cut-over instant, then the Ledger & invoicing component shall issue no
   platform invoice for it.

## Notes
- Covers US-408 S1 (clauses 1, 4), S2 (2), S3 (3), S4 (1), S5 (3), S6 (1: a slot starting exactly at the instant is
  invoiced by the platform).
- Settles bucket-5 #57 (REQ-232 as written): bookings run in the platform during REQ-269's period (SS-423) with slots
  before the cut-over are invoiced by the pilot process, Stripe Billing run by Amperlane outside the platform
  (SRC-C2@00:03:28), from the export of clause 4. Amperlane finance confirm its fields and timing in Phase 0 (A-010,
  Q-037).
- The instant is SS-422's single configured value (#46); it changes with CF-027, and the clauses hold for any instant.
- "Billable" is the invoicing unit, a client decision (Q-017; Q-053 for what is invoiced from launch while Q-001 is
  open); the clauses hold under every definition. Migrated history carries no ledger entry; Stripe Billing history is
  parked (REQ-314).
- SS-404 clauses 1 and 6 now state the exceptions of clauses 1, 5 and 6 themselves (P3.4 critique: before, the two SS
  gave different responses to one close or one late entry). Clause 4's trigger names the invoice close offset, which is
  reached even for a month that gets no platform invoice (clause 6).
