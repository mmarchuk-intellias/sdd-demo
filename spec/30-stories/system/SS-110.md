# SS-110 · Bulk rebooking proposals for unplaced bookings, approved by ops

As the Allocation & rebooking component, I need to propose a rebooking for each fault-affected booking I could not place
and to apply the proposals ops approve in one action, so that ops settle a fault in a few actions instead of rebooking
each booking by hand.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a fault-affected booking is handed to unplaced handling, the Allocation & rebooking component shall
   attach to its exception a proposed rebooking to the first alternative offered to its driver (SS-109), without holding
   that slot's capacity.
2. [Event] When an ops user approves a set of proposals in one action, the Allocation & rebooking component shall rebook
   each booking whose proposed slot is still free as proposed, with the reason code of its cause ('moved — charger
   fault', or 'moved — maintenance' for a booking unplaced by a maintenance hold, SS-113) and the ops user as actor,
   request the rebooking notice to its charging driver in the driver's language (SS-301 clause 8), and mark its
   exception resolved.
3. [Unwanted] If a proposed slot is no longer free when the proposal is approved, then the Allocation & rebooking
   component shall leave that booking unchanged, keep it in the queue marked "not placed" and still apply the other
   approved proposals.
4. [Unwanted] If the charging driver accepted an alternative before ops approve the booking's proposal, then the
   Allocation & rebooking component shall refuse the approval of that proposal and keep the booking as the driver chose.
5. [Event] When an ops user rejects a proposal, the Allocation & rebooking component shall remove it and keep the
   booking's exception open without a proposal.
6. [Unwanted] If no free compatible slot exists at the site or at nearby sites for an unplaced booking, then the
   Allocation & rebooking component shall attach no proposal and mark the exception "no proposal possible".

## Notes
- A-017 and CF-022: ops bulk-approve proposals only for the bookings the platform could not place. A-050 (Q-038): a
  proposal may use any free compatible EVSE at the site, never one a walk-up is using.
- After clause 5 or 6 ops can rebook the booking themselves (SS-114) or release it (SS-016).
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 2 always used 'moved — charger fault', while SS-109 clause 4 gives a maintenance booking
  'moved — maintenance' (same booking, two codes depending on who rebooks it); clause 2 now takes the cause's code, as
  REQ-185 requires.
- Clause 2's driver message: the catalogue has no Messaging SS for a rebooking to another slot or site (SS-301 names an
  allocated EVSE); logged as a GAP for batch 4 in the Step 3.3 log.
