# SS-336 · Retention purge: personal data deleted or anonymised when its period ends

As the Audit & data protection component, I need personal data to be deleted or replaced by a pseudonym automatically
once its retention period ends and no dispute or finance need holds it, so that the platform keeps only what disputes
and finance need without anyone having to remember to purge.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall keep a data register listing every personal-data field with
   its data type, class and retention period, or "not set" where counsel has set none (constitution SC4), and any
   finance retention rule that holds the field past that period.
2. [Event] When the retention run, at least daily (A-058), finds personal data whose retention period has ended, to
   which no open dispute refers (clause 4) and which no finance retention rule in the data register still holds, the
   Audit & data protection component shall delete it or replace it with a pseudonym, without any person acting.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Audit & data protection component shall never delete a booking, an audit entry or a ledger entry,
   nor change a ledger amount, in a retention run; those records keep the driver's internal id, which then shows the
   pseudonym.
4. [Unwanted] If an open dispute, or a dispute window still running (A-047), refers to the data, then the Audit & data
   protection component shall keep the data that dispute needs until the dispute is closed and its window has passed.
5. [Unwanted] If no retention period is set for a data type, then the Audit & data protection component shall delete
   nothing of that type and show its period as "not set" in the data register.
6. [Event] When a driver books again, the Audit & data protection component shall count the retention period of that
   driver's personal data from the driver's latest activity (A-025).
7. [Event] When a backup is restored, the Audit & data protection component shall re-apply every deletion and
   anonymisation made since that backup before the restored data is used (A-058).
8. [Event] When a retention run ends, the Audit & data protection component shall record in the audit trail how many
   records of each data type it deleted or anonymised, with no personal data.

## Notes
- Depends on A-025 (retention matrix; counsel sets the periods), A-058 (daily run, backups) and Q-022. The dispute
  window is A-047 (Q-043). The ledger is append-only (constitution N19).
- Location rules (opt-in, arrival events only) are SS-340 and SS-341; one driver's request to be anonymised is SS-337.
  The vendors each field is sent to are added to the same register by SS-338 (#51); sensitive identifiers by SS-342.
- P3.4 (2026-10-28): clause 2 depended on an undefined "finance need" and could not be tested; finance needs (REQ-125)
  are now finance retention rules recorded in the data register (clause 1). What they are is still the open question of
  ss-log-4 (NEW-Q 2).
