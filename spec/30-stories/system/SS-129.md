# SS-129 · Running-late report: recorded for ops, no effect on grace, release or fee

As the Arrival & no-show component, I need to record a driver's running-late report on the booking for ops without
changing grace, release or the fee, so that ops can see the driver is on the way and decide whether to give more time.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Event] When a driver reports running late for an active booking, with one tap on the page opened from any message
   about that booking or on the driver page (SS-327), or by keyword (SS-310), the Arrival & no-show component shall
   record the report with its time and the driver as actor on the booking, shown in the ops console.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall leave a booking's end of grace and its auto-release unchanged by a
   running-late report.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Arrival & no-show component shall not treat a running-late report as an exclusion from the no-show
   fee, so that a booking released after grace with no arrival gets its fee as any other no-show (SS-131).
4. [Unwanted] If the booking was already released or cancelled, then the Arrival & no-show component shall add no
   running-late report to it.

## Notes
- A-020: a running-late report changes nothing automatically; ops may extend grace (SS-130). CF-021 is needs-decision;
  its option of a report that releases or extends the station hold is REQ-305 (parked V).
- REQ-241 v2 (CR-001): one tap with no one-time code (constitution N23). Q-042 is covered by A-049 (SS-132); Q-002 does
  not change clause 3.
