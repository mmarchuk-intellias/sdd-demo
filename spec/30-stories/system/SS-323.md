# SS-323 · Organisation-scoped access for fleet, station-operator and attendant users

As the Identity & access component, I need every request from a fleet, station-operator or site-staff user to be
limited to that user's own organisation, site and day, in pages, exports and the API alike, so that fleets and station
operators share one platform without seeing each other's data and no station operator sees a driver's phone number.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall bind each fleet, station-operator and site-staff user to one
   organisation and one role, and check every read and write request against that scope, whether it comes from a
   portal page, an export or the API.
2. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall limit a fleet user to that fleet's vehicles, driver
   registrations, bookings for its vehicles, no-shows, invoices and ledger position.
3. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall limit a station-operator user to its own sites and their EVSEs,
   capacity rules, bookable hours, bookings, faults and fault outcomes.
4. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall return to station-operator and site-staff users no driver's
   phone number, and of driver data only the vehicle's plate and the charging driver's first name (A-053).
5. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall limit a site-staff user to bookings at their own site whose slot
   starts on the current calendar day in the site's time zone or whose station hold falls on that day.
6. [Event] When midnight passes in a site's time zone, the Identity & access component shall shift the scope of that
   site's site-staff users to the new day.
7. [Unwanted] If a user asks to read or change anything outside their scope, by list, search, id, link, export or API,
   then the Identity & access component shall refuse it with the same response as for an item that does not exist,
   and change nothing.
8. [Ubiquitous] The Identity & access component shall show a fleet user a driver registered to several fleets only
   with that fleet's registration and bookings, and with nothing that reveals the driver's other fleets.
9. [Event] When a request is refused as out of scope, the Identity & access component shall record a security event
   with the user, the request and the time (SS-334).
