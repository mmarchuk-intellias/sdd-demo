# US-215 · Book and get booking messages on WhatsApp

As a driver who uses WhatsApp all day, I want to book and get my booking messages on WhatsApp, so that I deal with
Amperlane in the messaging app I already live in instead of switching to SMS.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Booking on WhatsApp
  Given a registered driver whose preferred channel is WhatsApp and who has agreed to booking messages on WhatsApp
  When the driver sends a booking request on WhatsApp
  Then the driver gets the reply on WhatsApp, with a confirmed booking or the next free compatible slot to accept

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Booking messages arrive on WhatsApp in the driver's language
  Given a driver whose preferred channel is WhatsApp and whose language is Bengali has a confirmed booking
  When the platform sends the booking's confirmation and reminder
  Then both arrive on WhatsApp in Bengali

Scenario: S3 (failure) A message the platform starts outside the 24-hour window
  Given the driver has not written to Amperlane on WhatsApp in the last 24 hours
  When the platform sends the driver a reminder on WhatsApp
  Then the reminder is sent as a pre-approved template in the driver's language

Scenario: S4 (failure) No approved template exists for the message in the driver's language
  Given the driver has not written to Amperlane on WhatsApp in the last 24 hours
  And no pre-approved WhatsApp template exists for that message in the driver's language
  When the message is due
  Then it is not sent on WhatsApp as a free-form message
  And it is handled as undelivered on WhatsApp (REQ-219)

Scenario: S5 (failure) WhatsApp reports the message undelivered
  Given a driver whose preferred channel is WhatsApp
  When WhatsApp reports a booking message as undelivered
  Then the message is handled as undelivered (REQ-219)

Scenario: S6 (edge case) The 24-hour window closes before a message is sent
  Given the driver's last WhatsApp message opened a 24-hour window that was still open when the reminder was scheduled
  When the reminder is sent after that window has closed
  Then the reminder is sent as a pre-approved template in the driver's language

## Notes

- Depends on Q-034: WhatsApp is not used in the pilot and the Twilio WhatsApp sender is not set up (integrations.md
  U-10). A-008: Twilio stays the provider. Pre-approved templates in English, Spanish and Bengali are an external
  dependency (WhatsApp approval).
- S1 assumes the WhatsApp booking request works like SMS (REQ-061, US-214) or through the message link (US-211).
- S4 and S5 hand over to REQ-219 (JS-226), which resends on the driver's other registered channel and lists the driver
  for ops. Agreement to messages is REQ-248 (US-229).
- S6: the window is judged when the message is sent, not when it was scheduled.
