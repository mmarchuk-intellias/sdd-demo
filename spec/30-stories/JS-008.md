# JS-008 · Afternoon and overnight slots in one confirmation

When I lease both shifts of a cab and book its afternoon charge, I want to add the overnight slot for the same cab and
confirm both at once, so I can arrange the cab's charging for the whole day in one step between rides.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Two slots for one vehicle confirmed with one confirmation
  Given a booker has selected an afternoon slot and an overnight slot for the same vehicle
  And both slots are held for the booker
  When the booker confirms once
  Then two bookings are confirmed, each with its own site, start time and duration, for that vehicle

Scenario: S2 (failure) The overnight slot has no room
  Given the overnight slot the booker wants is not open for the vehicle
  When the booker selects slots for the day
  Then the overnight slot cannot be selected
  And the afternoon slot can still be confirmed on its own

Scenario: S3 (failure) One of the two holds expires before the confirmation
  Given both slots were held and the hold on the overnight slot expired before the booker confirmed
  When the booker confirms
  Then the overnight booking is not confirmed
  And its capacity is offered again in the open-slot view

Scenario: S4 (edge) The overnight slot falls on the next calendar day
  Given the afternoon slot is today and the overnight slot starts between 01:00 and 05:00 Eastern time tomorrow
  When the booker confirms once
  Then both bookings are confirmed, each shown with its own date in the site's time zone

## Notes

- Depends on: overnight bookings being allowed (REQ-152, US-033; constitution N4 and A-028). A-028 is the working
  assumption for CF-015, which is **needs-decision**; constitution R6 flag for the engagement lead.
- When one slot of the pair cannot be confirmed (S3), whether the other is confirmed on its own or both fail is a
  design detail for Step 3.3. S3 asserts only what is certain: the failed slot is not confirmed.
- Confirmation messages to booker and charging driver are REQ-088 (EP-06).
