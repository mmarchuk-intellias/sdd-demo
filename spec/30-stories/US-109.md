As a station operator, I want every booking released after grace with no arrival to carry exactly one no-show fee line
at my site's agreed amount and share, so that an EVSE held for a driver who never came is paid for.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · A release after grace creates one fee line
  Given a site whose no-show fee amount and station operator share are configured
  And a booking at that site is released after grace with no arrival and no exclusion of REQ-180
  When the release is recorded
  Then exactly one no-show fee line item is created for the booking, at the site's configured amount
  And the line records the station operator's share configured for the site
  And the line is separate from any energy line

Scenario: S2 · failure · An excluded booking goes to ops review instead
  Given a booking whose station hold failed is released after grace with no arrival
  When the release is recorded
  Then no no-show fee line is created automatically
  And the booking appears in the ops exception queue for a fee decision (JS-110)

Scenario: S3 · failure · A release processed twice still gives one line
  Given the release of a booking after grace is processed twice, for example after a retried timer
  When both are recorded
  Then the booking has exactly one no-show fee line

Scenario: S4 · edge case · Each site uses its own terms
  Given site A and site B have different no-show fee amounts and station operator shares
  When a booking at each site is released after grace with no arrival
  Then each fee line uses the amount and the share of its own site

Scenario: S5 · edge case · A release at the end of an ops extension
  Given ops extended a booking's grace period
  When the extended grace period ends with no arrival
  Then exactly one no-show fee line is created for the booking

## Notes

- Q-002: who pays the fee (fleet, driver, or fleet pays and deducts) is undecided; the line exists in every option and
  the payer options are parked (REQ-298 – REQ-302). Q-005: amounts and shares come from the partner agreements.
- Constitution N19 and REQ-211: a no-show fee is a separate line from energy. Exclusions are JS-110 and JS-111.
- Which fee terms apply when a site's fee amount changes between a booking's confirmation and its release (REQ-146
  lists grace and other parameters, not the fee), and what happens at a site whose fee amount or station share is not
  configured yet (Q-005: partner terms pending), are design details for Step 3.3. S1 – S5 can pass while such a site
  creates fee lines of zero or none.
