# US-303 · Fleet manager keeps the fleet's vehicle profiles up to date

As a fleet manager, I want to add, edit and retire the fleet's vehicles in the fleet portal, each with its native
connector, allowed adapters and maximum DC charging power, so that my drivers are offered only EVSEs their vehicle can
actually charge at, and a vehicle that has left the fleet can no longer be booked.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Adding a vehicle
  Given a fleet manager signed in to the fleet portal
  And the fleet allows adapters
  When the fleet manager adds a vehicle with its plate, native connector NACS, an allowed CCS1 adapter and its maximum DC charging power
  Then the vehicle is listed among the fleet's vehicles
  And slots for it are offered only at EVSEs with a NACS or a CCS1 connector

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Editing a vehicle's profile
  Given a registered NACS vehicle with an allowed CCS1 adapter, in a fleet that allows adapters
  When the fleet manager removes the CCS1 adapter from the vehicle
  Then the vehicle's profile shows no allowed adapter
  And bookings made for it after the change are accepted and allocated only where a NACS EVSE can take it

Scenario: S3 (happy path) Retiring a vehicle
  Given a registered vehicle with past bookings
  When the fleet manager retires it
  Then no new booking can be made for it on any channel
  And its past bookings stay visible to the fleet, with the vehicle shown as retired

Scenario: S4 (failure) A vehicle without its connector profile is refused
  When the fleet manager saves a vehicle without a native connector or without a maximum DC charging power
  Then the vehicle is not saved
  And the fleet manager is shown the missing field

Scenario: S5 (failure) Vehicles of another fleet cannot be changed
  When a fleet manager tries to edit or retire a vehicle of another fleet
  Then the request is refused and the vehicle is unchanged

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A vehicle with no telematics
  Given a vehicle with no telematics, such as a Five Boroughs cab
  When the fleet manager adds it with only its plate and connector profile
  Then it is registered and can be booked with no state of charge or location data

## Notes

- Depends on A-023. The vehicle fields and the matching rule are REQ-015 and REQ-013 (EP-01); vehicles without
  telematics are REQ-019. This story is the fleet manager's way to keep that data current.
- A vehicle's confirmed future bookings when it is retired, or when an edit of its connector or adapters leaves a
  confirmed booking with no compatible EVSE at its site, follow A-055: they stand and are listed for the fleet manager
  or ops, who act on each one; a booking that no compatible EVSE can take at its allocation time is handled as
  unplaced (REQ-022). These AC do not test it.
- The fleet-level adapter policy and its per-vehicle override are US-304 (REQ-072).
- S1 uses NACS with a CCS1 adapter after the example on the call (SRC-C2@00:41:53); the plate and power values in tests
  are test data.
- Red team: S2 used to edit the maximum DC charging power and check that matching used it, but matching is by connector
  and adapter only (REQ-013), so any build passed. S2 now edits a field whose effect on matching can be observed.
