# JS-026 · Unacknowledged station-hold request goes to ops

When site staff have not acknowledged a station-hold request by the pre-arrival check, I want to get an exception to
contact the site, so I can still get the stall marked before the driver arrives.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) No acknowledgement by the pre-arrival check
  Given site staff at JFK Lot 9 received a station-hold request for a booking
  And they have not acknowledged it
  When the pre-arrival check for that booking runs
  Then an exception to contact the site appears in the ops queue for that booking
  And the booking stays confirmed and allocated

Scenario: S2 (failure) Ops does not act before the slot starts
  Given an exception to contact the site is open for a booking
  When the slot starts and the driver scans the QR code at the allocated EVSE within grace
  Then the booking is still valid and the arrival is recorded

Scenario: S3 (failure) Site staff acknowledge after the pre-arrival check
  Given an exception to contact the site was raised for a booking
  When site staff acknowledge the station-hold request afterwards
  Then the acknowledgement is recorded on the booking with its time
  And the booking stays valid

Scenario: S4 (edge) An acknowledged request raises no exception
  Given site staff acknowledged a station-hold request before the pre-arrival check
  When the pre-arrival check runs
  Then no exception to contact the site is raised for that booking

Scenario: S5 (edge) A charger reservation the charger accepted needs no acknowledgement
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub whose charger reservation the charger accepted
  When the pre-arrival check runs
  Then no exception to contact the site is raised for that booking
