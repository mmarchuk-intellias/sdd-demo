# JS-020 · Report a blocked stall on arrival and get another free EVSE

When I arrive and the stall allocated to me is blocked, I want to report it on the spot, so I can be given another
free EVSE at the site when there is one, get help from ops when there is not, and not be charged automatically as a
no-show for a stall I could not use.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The report is stored and ops is alerted
  Given a charging driver arrives at the site of an allocated booking and finds the allocated stall blocked
  When the driver reports the stall as blocked from the booking's page
  Then the report is stored with its time and the booking's arrival evidence
  And an exception for the booking appears in the ops queue
  And the booking counts as a failed station hold

Scenario: S2 (failure) Grace ends without a session after the report
  Given the driver reported the allocated stall as blocked
  When grace ends and no charging session has started
  Then no no-show fee is created automatically for the booking
  And the booking is in the ops exception queue for a decision on the fee

Scenario: S3 (failure) A report from someone not on the booking is refused
  Given a person who is neither the booking's charging driver nor its booker
  When that person tries to report the booking's stall as blocked
  Then the report is refused
  And the booking is unchanged

Scenario: S4 (edge) The report sits next to the station-hold record
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub whose charger reservation the charger had accepted
  When the driver reports the stall as blocked
  Then the booking's history shows the accepted charger reservation and the blocked-stall report, each with its time

Scenario: S5 (happy path) The booking is reallocated to a free compatible EVSE at the site
  Given a charging driver arrives at Gowanus FastHub for a booking allocated to EVSE A and finds its stall blocked
  And EVSE B at the same site is free and has a connector the vehicle can use
  When the driver reports the stall as blocked
  Then the booking is reallocated to EVSE B
  And the station hold is placed on EVSE B
  And the charging driver is told EVSE B

Scenario: S6 (failure) No compatible EVSE is free at the site
  Given a charging driver arrives at JFK Lot 9 and finds the allocated stall blocked
  And no other EVSE at the site with a connector the vehicle can use is free, while an EVSE whose connector the
    vehicle cannot use is free
  When the driver reports the stall as blocked
  Then the booking is not reallocated
  And no station hold is placed on another EVSE
  And the exception for the booking is in the ops queue with the report

Scenario: S7 (edge) Two blocked-stall reports and one free compatible EVSE
  Given two bookings at JFK Lot 9 whose charging drivers each find their allocated stall blocked
  And exactly one other EVSE at the site is free and has a connector both vehicles can use
  When both drivers report their stalls as blocked at the same moment
  Then exactly one of the bookings is reallocated to that EVSE
  And the other booking is not reallocated and its exception stays in the ops queue

## Notes

- Depends on: Q-005 (liability for a blocked stall is a partner-agreement term; linked from REQ-046 and REQ-336),
  A-024 (arrival evidence without location: QR scan, CSMS session start or the attendant).
- The fee rule for a failed station hold is REQ-180 (EP-03, K); this story only makes the booking count as one. The
  station-hold record is US-028 (REQ-154). Authorisation of the reporter: constitution SC3, REQ-242 (EP-06).
- S5 – S7 follow REQ-336 (CR-008): the COO's rule "If the stall is taken, move the booking to another free one"
  (SRC-C2@00:24:04) now covers a stall the charging driver reports blocked on arrival, as REQ-021 does for an EVSE the
  platform sees occupied at station-hold time or at the pre-arrival check (JS-018). The report still raises the ops
  exception and counts as a failed station hold (REQ-046), so S1 holds whether or not the booking is reallocated. The
  station hold on the new EVSE uses that EVSE's method (charger reservation, US-021, or site staff, US-025). S7 relies
  on constitution N7 (no overlapping allocations on one EVSE).
- What is stored when the driver reports with no arrival evidence yet is a design detail for Step 3.3.
- Reporting a blocked stall is a one-tap action that needs no one-time code (REQ-241 v2, CR-001), so S1 and S5 hold
  on a phone without a recent code.
