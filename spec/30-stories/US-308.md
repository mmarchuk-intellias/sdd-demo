# US-308 · Monthly usage report per vehicle in the fleet portal

As a fleet manager, I want a monthly report per vehicle of its bookings, no-shows, completed bookings and the charging
energy of its matched session records, so that I can see how each vehicle of the fleet uses Amperlane and spot
vehicles that book but do not charge.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The report for a month
  Given a month in which the fleet's vehicles had bookings
  When the fleet manager opens the vehicle report for that month
  Then each vehicle with a booking that month has one row with its bookings, no-shows, completed bookings and the energy in kWh of its matched session records

Scenario: S2 (failure) A booking without a matched session record adds no energy
  Given a vehicle's booking with a recorded arrival but no matched session record
  When the report is shown
  Then that booking adds no energy to the vehicle's total
  And the row shows how many of the vehicle's bookings have no matched session record yet

Scenario: S3 (failure) Only the fleet's own vehicles
  Given a driver registered to this fleet and to another fleet
  When the fleet manager opens the report
  Then it contains no vehicle and no booking of the other fleet

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A booking on the last evening of the month
  Given a booking that starts late on the last evening of a month in the site's time zone, when it is already the next month in UTC
  When the report for that month is shown
  Then the booking is counted in that month

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Completed bookings follow the configured definition
  Given the completed-booking definition configured by ops
  When ops changes that definition during the current month
  Then the report for the current month counts completed bookings under the new definition
  And the report states which definition it uses

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A vehicle retired during the month
  Given a vehicle with bookings earlier in a month that the fleet manager retired later that month
  When the report for that month is shown
  Then the retired vehicle still has its row for that month

## Notes

- R7: per-session records are unconfirmed at all three pilot sites (U-6, U-7: Q-009, Q-011, Q-012). Where none are
  available, energy stays empty and S2 applies.
- "Completed" is counted under REQ-224 (US-326); the definition itself is a client decision (Q-017). A-013 for month
  boundaries.
- S3 relies on REQ-258 and A-016.
- Red team: S5 is limited to the current month, because whether months already reported are recounted after a
  definition change is Q-054 (a client decision, with Q-017; US-327).
