# JS-226 · A time-critical message that does not arrive is resent and shown to ops

When a message I need to keep my slot, such as a reminder or a re-offer, does not reach my phone, I want it sent
again on my other channel and ops to know, so I can still act on it before the slot is lost.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A reminder is reported undelivered
  Given a driver with SMS and WhatsApp both registered
  When a reminder sent by SMS is reported undelivered
  Then the reminder is resent on WhatsApp
  And the driver is listed among undelivered messages in the ops console

Scenario: S2 (failure) No delivery status arrives in time
  Given a re-offer was sent to a driver by SMS
  When no delivery status arrives within the set time
  Then the re-offer is resent on the driver's other registered channel
  And the driver is listed among undelivered messages in the ops console

Scenario: S3 (failure) The driver has only one channel
  Given a driver with only SMS registered
  When an allocation notice is reported undelivered
  Then nothing is resent
  And the driver is listed among undelivered messages in the ops console

Scenario: S4 (failure) The resend is undelivered too
  Given a fault notice was resent on WhatsApp after the SMS was undelivered
  When the WhatsApp message is also reported undelivered
  Then it is not sent back to SMS again
  And the driver stays listed among undelivered messages in the ops console

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The other channel has been stopped
  Given a driver with SMS and WhatsApp registered replied STOP on WhatsApp
  When a confirmation sent by SMS is reported undelivered
  Then nothing is sent on WhatsApp
  And the driver is listed among undelivered messages in the ops console

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A confirmation with no delivery status is listed but not resent
  Given a driver with SMS and WhatsApp both registered
  When a confirmation sent by SMS has no delivery status within the set time
  Then nothing is resent on WhatsApp
  And the driver is listed among undelivered messages in the ops console

## Notes

- Covers confirmations, reminders, allocation notices, re-offers and fault notices only, as REQ-219 lists them.
  "The set time" is a configured value.
- Depends on WhatsApp (Q-034, A-008); without it no driver has a second channel and only the ops listing applies.
- S4 limits the resend to one; how often a message is resent is a design detail for Step 3.3. S5 applies REQ-248
  (US-229).
- The undelivered list sits in the ops exception queue (REQ-189, FT-07.1). Who acts on it at night is Q-023.
- REQ-219 v2 (CR-005): a confirmation is resent only when it is reported undelivered (S5), never for a missing
  delivery status (S6), so a late first delivery cannot give the driver two (REQ-089, US-223). Other messages are
  still resent when no status arrives (S2).
