# US-219 · A removed driver can no longer book for the fleet

As a fleet manager, I want a driver I remove to lose the right to book for my fleet the moment I remove them, on
every channel, so that a driver whose weekly lease has ended cannot book charging on my fleet's account.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A removed driver texts a booking
  Given the Borough Green fleet manager removed a driver from the fleet
  When, right after the removal is saved, the driver texts a booking request from the same number
  Then no booking or hold is created for Borough Green

Scenario: S2 (failure) A removed driver uses a link received before removal
  Given a driver received a booking message link before being removed
  And the link would otherwise still work
  When the driver opens it after the removal and tries to book for the fleet
  Then no booking or hold is created for the fleet

Scenario: S3 (failure) A removed driver uses WhatsApp
  Given a driver was removed from the fleet
  When the driver sends a booking request on WhatsApp
  Then no booking or hold is created for the fleet

Scenario: S4 (failure) Removal while a hold is open
  Given a driver has picked a slot and holds it for the fleet's vehicle
  And the fleet manager removes the driver before the driver confirms
  When the driver taps confirm within the site's hold time
  Then no booking is created

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The driver is still registered to another fleet
  Given a driver is registered to Borough Green and to a second fleet
  When Borough Green removes the driver
  Then the driver can still book the second fleet's vehicles that its policy allows
  And cannot book any Borough Green vehicle

Scenario: S6 (edge case) Ops removes the driver for a small fleet
  Given ops manages Five Boroughs' drivers on the fleet's behalf
  When ops removes a driver
  Then the driver can no longer book for Five Boroughs on any channel, as in S1 to S4

## Notes

- Depends on A-023 (fleets, or ops for small fleets, register and remove numbers) and Q-028.
- Q-045 (are the removed driver's future bookings cancelled or handed back to the fleet?) is covered by A-052: they
  stand and are listed for the fleet manager, or ops, to reassign or cancel, and the removed number can no longer
  change them (US-222 S6). This story covers new bookings only.
- The removal itself is a fleet-portal action (FT-08.1) and an audited change (constitution N17).
