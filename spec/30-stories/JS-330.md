# JS-330 · Five Boroughs bookings run with no manual ops action

When Five Boroughs' cabs come in to charge ahead of the shift change, I want each booking to reach arrival,
auto-release or an automatic rebook without anyone in ops acting on it, so my team does not have to pick up the phone
to get the cabs charged.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Arrival with no ops action
  Given a Five Boroughs booking at a site with a free compatible EVSE
  When the driver arrives
  Then the booking reaches arrival
  And its audit trail shows no manual ops action

Scenario: S2 (happy path) No arrival: auto-release with no ops action
  Given a Five Boroughs booking with no arrival by the end of grace
  When grace ends
  Then the booking is released automatically
  And its audit trail shows no manual ops action

Scenario: S3 (happy path) A charger fault: automatic rebook
  Given the booking's EVSE faults while another compatible EVSE at the site is free
  When the fault is recorded
  Then the booking is rebooked to that EVSE automatically with the reason code "moved — charger fault"
  And its audit trail shows no manual ops action

Scenario: S4 (failure) Site full or faulted with no free compatible EVSE
  Given no compatible EVSE at the site is free for the booking
  When the booking is due
  Then it goes to the ops exception queue
  And the ops action taken on it is recorded with its actor and reason code, as an allowed exception

Scenario: S5 (failure) A manual ops action outside the exception
  Given a Five Boroughs booking whose site had a free compatible EVSE throughout
  And an ops person confirms, extends grace on or rebooks that booking in the ops console
  When the month's Five Boroughs bookings are checked against their audit trails
  Then the action is found on that booking with the ops person, not the system, as its actor, and its reason code
  And the booking counts against this outcome

Scenario: S6 (edge case) Site staff and the driver are not ops
  Given the attendant at a pattern C site records the driver's arrival, or the driver reports running late
  When the booking's audit trail is checked
  Then it shows the attendant or the driver as the actor
  And the booking has no manual ops action
