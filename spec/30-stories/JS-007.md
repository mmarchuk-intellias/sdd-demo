# JS-007 · Book for the driver who will charge

When I book a charge for a cab that another driver of my fleet will take out, I want to name that driver as the
charging driver, so I can hand the cab over knowing the booking and its messages are his.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The day driver books for the night driver
  Given the day driver and the night driver are mobile numbers registered to Five Boroughs EV Cab Co.
  When the day driver books a slot for one of the fleet's cabs and names the night driver as charging driver
  Then the booking records the day driver's number as booker and the night driver's number as charging driver

Scenario: S2 (happy path) The owner books from a booker-only number
  Given the fleet has registered the owner's mobile number as a booker-only number
  When the owner books a slot for one of the fleet's cabs and names a driver of the fleet as charging driver
  Then the booking is made for the fleet with the owner's number as booker and the named driver's number as charging driver

Scenario: S3 (failure) A booker-only number must name the charging driver
  Given a booker-only number registered to a fleet
  When it tries to book a slot without naming a charging driver
  Then no booking is made

Scenario: S4 (failure) The charging driver is not registered to the booking's fleet
  Given a mobile number that is not registered to the fleet of the booked vehicle
  When a booker names that number as charging driver
  Then the booking is refused

Scenario: S5 (failure) The booker is not registered to the booking's fleet
  Given a mobile number that is not registered to the fleet of the vehicle, for example a driver the fleet has removed
  When that number tries to book a vehicle of that fleet
  Then the booking is refused

Scenario: S6 (edge) A driver of two fleets cannot name a driver of the other fleet
  Given a driver registered to two fleets
  When the driver books a vehicle of the first fleet and names a charging driver registered only to the second fleet
  Then the booking is refused

Scenario: S7 (edge) A driver booking for himself is both booker and charging driver
  Given a registered driver books a slot without naming anyone else
  When the booking is confirmed
  Then the booking records the driver's number as both booker and charging driver

Scenario: S8 (failure) A booker-only number cannot be the charging driver
  Given the fleet has registered the owner's mobile number as a booker-only number
  When a booker names that number as the charging driver of a booking
  Then the booking is refused

## Notes

- Depends on: A-023 (fleets register driver and booker-only numbers; ops does it for small fleets), A-016 (a driver may
  drive for more than one fleet), Q-028 (driver identity), Q-045 (what happens to the bookings of a removed driver,
  covered by A-052).
- REQ-009 v2 (CR-002) also allows a fleet-portal or ops user as booker (US-009, US-127); the AC here cover bookers
  that are registered numbers, which CR-002 leaves unchanged.
- Messages to the booker and the charging driver are REQ-088 (EP-06); reminders and allocation notices go to the
  charging driver (REQ-067, EP-06; REQ-200, US-017). Removal of a driver is REQ-202 (EP-06).
- S3 and S8 read "booker-only" in REQ-201 and A-023 ("a booker-only number books for the fleet and names the driver
  who will charge") literally: such a number must name a charging driver and can never be one (confirmed in the Step
  3.1 red-team pass). Without S8, a booker-only number could be its own charging driver and the night driver would get
  no reminder or allocation notice.
