When a charger fault leaves no compatible EVSE for my booking at my site, I want to get the next free slot there and
the nearest free slots at other sites, each accepted with one tap or a keyword, so I can pick what fits my shift
instead of being sent to a site I did not choose.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · Accept the next slot at the same site with one tap
  Given a booking cannot be placed at its site after a fault
  When the set time after the fault passes
  Then its charging driver has a message offering the next free compatible slot at that site and the nearest free compatible slots at other sites
  When the driver accepts the same-site slot with one tap
  Then the driver has a booking for that slot with the reason code 'moved — charger fault'
  And no no-show is recorded for the original booking

Scenario: S2 · happy path · Accept a slot at another site by keyword
  Given the driver got alternatives after a fault
  When the driver replies with the keyword for a slot at another site
  Then the driver has a booking for that slot at the other site with the reason code 'moved — charger fault'

Scenario: S3 · failure · The chosen slot was taken first
  Given the driver got alternatives after a fault
  And another booker took the chosen slot before the driver accepted it
  When the driver accepts it
  Then no booking is made for the taken slot
  And the driver is offered the next free compatible slot at that site

Scenario: S4 · failure · The driver does not answer
  Given the driver got alternatives after a fault and has not answered
  When an ops user looks at the booking in the ops exception queue
  Then it shows the alternatives offered and that the driver has not answered
  And no no-show fee line is created automatically for the booking

Scenario: S5 · edge case · Only compatible alternatives are offered
  Given the driver's vehicle can charge only through a NACS connector or an adapter its fleet allows
  When the alternatives are sent
  Then every offered slot is one that the vehicle can be allocated to

Scenario: S6 · edge case · The message is in the driver's language
  Given the charging driver's language is Spanish
  When the alternatives are sent
  Then the message and its keywords are in Spanish

## Notes

- A-017. In the Gowanus fault, ops sent a cab driver to JFK at shift change and lost him (CTX-047); the driver chooses
  here.
- S3 applies REQ-008 (FT-01.2); S4 applies REQ-050 (US-121) and REQ-180 (JS-110); S6 applies REQ-199 and REQ-220.
  Messages may be SMS or WhatsApp (U-10, Q-034, A-008).
- How "nearest" is ranked (distance from the faulted site or from the driver) and how many alternatives are offered
  are a design detail for Step 3.3.
- REQ-241 v2 (CR-001) makes accepting an alternative a one-tap action that needs no one-time code, so S1's one tap
  (REQ-049) holds on any phone; the keyword path (S2) is governed by REQ-242.
