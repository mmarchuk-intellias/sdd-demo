As Amperlane ops, I want to keep capacity at a site for a time window for one named fleet's bookings only, so that a
fleet whose drivers must charge in a set window, such as cabs at the shift change, still finds capacity when other
fleets' bookings would fill the site.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · The named fleet books the held capacity
  Given a fleet-priority hold for fleet X at a site for a time window
  When a driver of fleet X books a slot in that window
  Then the booking is accepted against the held capacity
  And the holds and bookings overlapping each slot it covers, the fleet-priority hold included, stay within the cap of the site's capacity rule for that slot (REQ-004)

Scenario: S2 · failure · Another fleet cannot use the held capacity
  Given a fleet-priority hold for fleet X at a site for a time window
  And all capacity in that window outside the hold is booked
  When a driver of fleet Y tries to book a slot in that window
  Then the booking is refused as full, and the driver is offered the next free compatible slot
  And the held capacity stays kept for fleet X

Scenario: S3 · failure · A hold placed below confirmed bookings
  Given other fleets' confirmed bookings in the window already use more capacity than the hold leaves them
  When an ops user places the fleet-priority hold
  Then those confirmed bookings stand
  And ops see each booking above the new limit, to act on it

Scenario: S4 · failure · An incomplete hold is refused
  Given an ops user enters a fleet-priority hold with no fleet named, or with no reason code
  When the ops user places it
  Then the hold is refused

Scenario: S5 · edge case · The named fleet beyond its held capacity
  Given fleet X's bookings in the window already use all of the held capacity
  When another driver of fleet X books a slot in that window
  Then the booking is accepted only from the capacity open to every fleet, under the site's capacity rules

Scenario: S6 · edge case · The hold ends
  Given a fleet-priority hold ends at the end of its window
  When a driver of any fleet books a slot after the window
  Then the capacity is open to every fleet

## Notes

- A-018, Q-035: a fleet-priority hold applies to new bookings only; confirmed bookings stand and ops act on each one
  above the new limit (S3, REQ-186, FT-09.1). S2 applies REQ-008.
- S1's last line was added in the Step 3.1 red-team pass: without it, a fleet-priority hold could be built as capacity on
  top of the cap, which passes S1 – S6 but takes EVSEs from walk-ups and breaks REQ-004 and the station's terms.
- The benefit is our reading of the launch success "Five Boroughs' cabs are charged at five o'clock" (CTX-139); the
  hold itself was proposed by Intellias (SRC-C2@00:53:00).
- Whether held capacity that fleet X has not booked is released to other fleets before or during the window is a
  design detail for Step 3.3.
