# US-316 · No-show fee as its own line, reversed by a new entry

As Amperlane finance, I want each no-show fee recorded as its own line, and a reversed fee undone by a new entry that
leaves the original fee entry and every energy line untouched, so that a disputed fee can be settled on its own and the
ledger still shows what was charged, when, and why it was reversed.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A no-show creates a separate fee line
  Given a booking released as a no-show that gets a no-show fee
  And an energy line for the same fleet in the same month, where the business model has energy lines
  When the fee is recorded
  Then the ledger holds the fee as its own line, linked to the booking
  And no ledger line combines the fee with any energy charge

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A disputed fee is reversed
  Given a no-show fee line and an energy line for the same fleet and month
  When ops decides a dispute in the fleet's favour and the fee is reversed
  Then a new reversing entry for the same amount is recorded with its reason code, actor and time
  And the original fee entry is still in the ledger, unchanged
  And every energy line is unchanged

Scenario: S3 (failure) Ledger entries cannot be changed or deleted
  Given a posted ledger entry
  When any user or platform process tries to change its amount or delete it
  Then the attempt is refused and the entry is unchanged
  And the only way to correct it is a new entry

Scenario: S4 (failure) A fee cannot be reversed twice
  Given a no-show fee that has already been reversed
  When a second reversal of the same fee is attempted
  Then the attempt is refused and no further entry is recorded

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A reversal in a later month
  Given a fee charged in one month and reversed in the next
  When the fleet's ledger is viewed as of the last day of the first month
  Then it still shows the fee as owed on that day
  And the reversal counts only from the day it was recorded

## Notes

- Holds for every payer option of the fee (Q-002); amounts are test data.
- Energy lines exist only where the business model sells energy (Q-001; REQ-270, REQ-273, REQ-274 are parked). Tests use
  the sample months of REQ-210, which is an enabler for Step 3.3, as is the double-entry structure itself (REQ-085).
- The dispute decision is REQ-045 (EP-03); waiving a fee before it is invoiced is REQ-182 (EP-03). A reversal after the
  fee's invoice was issued becomes a credit line on the next invoice (US-320). Constitution N19 applies.
