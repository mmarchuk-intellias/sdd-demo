As a driver, I want to report that I am running late with one tap from my booking's message or page, so that ops can
see I am still on my way and decide whether to give me more time.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · One tap from a booking message
  Given a driver with a confirmed booking opened the page from a message about that booking
  When the driver taps "running late"
  Then the report and its time appear on the booking in the ops console
  And the driver needed no further tap, sign-in or confirmation step

Scenario: S2 · failure · The report does not stop the auto-release
  Given a driver reported running late
  And ops did not extend the booking's grace period
  When the grace period ends with no arrival
  Then the booking is released as a no-show

Scenario: S3 · failure · The report does not waive the no-show fee
  Given a driver reported running late
  And no exclusion of REQ-180 applies to the booking
  When the booking is released after grace with no arrival
  Then a no-show fee line is created for the booking, as for any other no-show

Scenario: S4 · failure · A report on a booking that has ended
  Given a booking was already released or cancelled
  When the driver taps "running late" on its page
  Then no running-late report is added to the booking

Scenario: S5 · edge case · From the driver page, without a message
  Given a driver opens the driver page directly
  When the driver taps "running late" on a confirmed booking
  Then the report and its time appear on that booking in the ops console

Scenario: S6 · edge case · A report during grace leaves the end of grace unchanged
  Given a booking's slot has started and its grace period is running with no arrival recorded
  When the driver taps "running late"
  Then the report and its time appear on the booking in the ops console
  And the booking's end of grace stays where it was until an ops user extends it (US-108)

## Notes

- A-020: under the working assumption, a running-late report changes nothing automatically; ops may extend grace
  (US-108). CF-021 is needs-decision; its option (c), a report that releases or extends the station hold, is REQ-305
  (parked V). S2 and S6 hold under A-020 (option b); if the client chooses option (c), they change through a CR.
- Step 3.1 red-team pass: S6 used to test cancelling before the cut-off, which is REQ-171 (US-112), not linked here,
  and hung on Q-042 (now covered by A-049). It now tests the loophole that matters for this story: an automatic grace
  extension on a running-late report (REQ-305, parked) would pass S1 – S5.
- REQ-241 v2 (CR-001) makes "running late" a one-tap action that needs no one-time code, as constitution N23 and
  REQ-040 give it, so S1 holds on any phone, with or without a recent code.
- Q-002 (payer) does not change S3: the fee line exists in every option. The SMS keyword for running late is REQ-220
  (FT-06.5). Messages may be SMS or WhatsApp (U-10, Q-034, A-008).
- S1 counts taps as constitution N23 does.
