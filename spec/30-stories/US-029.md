# US-029 · Manual mode for a site's station holds

As a member of Amperlane ops, I want to switch a site to manual mode, in which the platform keeps the slot calendar and gives me a
timed list of station holds to arrange instead of automated ones, so that bookings at that site are still honoured when
its automated station holds cannot be relied on.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Switching a site to manual mode
  Given a site whose station holds run automatically
  When ops switches the site to manual mode
  Then the platform sends no automated station holds for its bookings, neither charger reservations nor requests to site staff
  And ops sees a timed list of the station holds to arrange, each with its booking, EVSE, stall and when to mark and clear it
  And the switch is recorded in the audit trail with ops as actor and its time

Scenario: S2 (failure) The slot calendar keeps its rules in manual mode
  Given a site is in manual mode
  When a booker tries to place a hold for a slot that is at the cap of the site's capacity rule
  Then the hold is refused as at any site

Scenario: S3 (failure) Only ops can switch a site to manual mode
  Given a station operator or a fleet manager is signed in
  When that user tries to switch a site to manual mode
  Then the action is refused

Scenario: S4 (edge) Switching back to automated station holds
  Given a site is in manual mode
  When ops switches it back
  Then station holds that fall due afterwards are sent automatically again

## Notes

- Ops is the safety net for patterns B and C (SRC-C2@00:53:49). When to use manual mode is ops' call; ops hours are
  Q-023.
- What happens to station holds already sent, or falling due at the moment of the switch, is a design detail for
  Step 3.3.
