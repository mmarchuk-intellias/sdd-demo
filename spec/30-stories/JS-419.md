# JS-419 · Honour the next bookings by phone during a platform outage

When the platform is down during the availability window, for example around the 4:30 PM shift change, I want to see
each site's next bookings from a copy kept outside the platform and refreshed at a set interval, so I can honour those
bookings by phone with drivers and site staff instead of going back to Airtable and the Sheets.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Ops opens the copy during an outage
  Given the platform's application and its database are both unavailable at 4:30 PM
  When an ops person opens the outage copy
  Then it opens without the platform
  And it shows, per site, every booking whose slot starts within the set time after its last refresh, each with the
    driver, the driver's contact number, the window and the EVSE where one is allocated
  And it shows when it was last refreshed

Scenario: S2 (failure) The copy has not been refreshed for longer than the set interval
  Given the refresh of the outage copy has failed for longer than the set interval before the outage
  When an ops person opens it
  Then it shows its last refresh time
  And it shows that it is older than the set interval, so ops know later bookings may be missing

Scenario: S3 (failure) Someone outside ops tries to open the copy
  Given a person without an ops role
  When that person tries to open the outage copy
  Then access is refused
  And the attempt is recorded

Scenario: S4 (edge case) The outage lasts past the next refresh time
  Given the platform is still unavailable when the next refresh of the outage copy is due
  When the refresh runs and cannot reach the platform
  Then the copy keeps the last complete refresh
  And it is not replaced by an empty or partial one

## Notes

- After launch "nobody opens Airtable" (SRC-C1@00:33:22), so the pilot tools cannot be the fallback. The
  availability window and its measurement are constitution N4 and A-033; the shift-change burst is N6.
- The copy holds driver phone numbers outside the primary system, so N14 (encryption) and REQ-120 (role-based access,
  US-410) apply to it.
- An automatic reply to drivers during an outage is REQ-223 (I, out of scope). Who in ops is on duty is Q-023.
- How ops record in the platform, once it is back, what they arranged by phone during the outage is a design detail
  for Step 3.3.
- Red team: a copy kept in the same database, or behind the same application, passes a test that stops only one
  of them; S1 stops both. "The next bookings" without a horizon is met by one booking per site; S1 uses a configured
  time ahead of the last refresh. If ops sign in to the copy through the platform's own identity service, S1 and S3
  cannot both hold during an outage; the solution architect decides how ops are authenticated there (design detail for Step 3.3).
