# US-003 · Book a slot with a hold and a confirmation

As a booker, I want the slot I pick held for me while I confirm, and confirmed only if an EVSE that fits my vehicle can
still be found for it, so that a confirmed booking is a promise the site can keep when the driver arrives.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Pick a slot and confirm within the hold time
  Given a booker has chosen a site, a vehicle, a start time on the site's time grid and one of the site's configured booking durations
  When the booker picks that slot
  Then a hold on one unit of capacity for that vehicle, site and window is placed
  And the hold counts against the cap of the site's capacity rule
  When the booker confirms before the site's configured hold time runs out
  Then the booking is confirmed with that site, start time, duration and vehicle

Scenario: S2 (failure) The hold expires before confirmation
  Given a booker picked a slot and its hold was placed
  When the site's configured hold time runs out without a confirmation
  Then the hold expires and its capacity is offered again in the open-slot view
  And another booker can place a hold on it
  And a confirmation of the expired hold is refused

Scenario: S3 (failure) A start time off the grid or an unconfigured duration is refused
  Given a site's configured time grid and booking durations
  When a booker requests a start time that is not on the site's time grid, or a duration the site has not configured
  Then no hold is placed
  And the request is refused

Scenario: S4 (failure) Confirmation is refused when the pool can no longer fit every overlapping booking
  Given the cap of the capacity rule for a slot at a site still has room
  And every EVSE at the site that a vehicle with a NACS native connector and no allowed adapter can use is needed by confirmed bookings overlapping that slot, with the site's buffer
  When a booker confirms a held booking for another such vehicle in that slot
  Then the booking is not confirmed

Scenario: S5 (edge) The site's buffer counts in the pool check
  Given the only EVSE at a site that a vehicle can use has a confirmed booking that ends less than the site's configured buffer before the requested start
  When a booker confirms a held booking for that vehicle at that start
  Then the booking is not confirmed
  And a booking for that vehicle that starts at least the site's configured buffer after the other booking ends can be confirmed

## Notes

- Depends on: A-001 (grid, durations, buffer and hold time are configured values), A-005 (holds as database rows),
  A-014 and CF-023 (the pool check before allocation is a working hypothesis, prototyped in Phase 0; resolved-provisional).
- The answer when the requested slot is full is JS-004 (REQ-008). Two-tap booking is REQ-100 (EP-06). Repeated taps
  with the same idempotency key are constitution N8.
- When a confirmation is refused under REQ-147 (S4, S5), whether the hold is released at once or kept until it
  expires, and whether the booker gets an alternative (REQ-008 covers only "the requested slot is full"), are a design
  detail for Step 3.3.
