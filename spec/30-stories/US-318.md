# US-318 · One consolidated monthly invoice per fleet, every item listed

As a fleet finance contact, I want one consolidated invoice a month for the whole fleet, payable net-30, that lists
every charged item with its booking id, site, date, vehicle and driver, so that I pay Amperlane once a month on our
usual terms and can check each line against our own vehicle and driver records.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) One invoice for the month
  Given a fleet with charge lines in a month across several sites, vehicles and drivers
  When the month is closed
  Then the fleet receives exactly one invoice for that month
  And its due date is 30 days after its issue date

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Every charged item is itemised
  Given the fleet's invoice for the month
  When the fleet finance contact opens it
  Then every charged item of the month is a line with its booking id, site, date, vehicle and charging driver
  And the line amounts add up to the invoice total

Scenario: S3 (failure) No second invoice for the same month
  Given the fleet's invoice for a month has been issued
  When month close runs again for that month
  Then no second invoice is issued for that fleet and month

Scenario: S4 (failure) A booking of another fleet never appears
  Given a driver registered to this fleet and to another fleet
  When this fleet's invoice is issued
  Then it contains only charges for bookings of this fleet's vehicles

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Booker and charging driver differ
  Given a booking made by a booker-only number for a named charging driver
  When the fleet's invoice is issued
  Then the line for that booking names the charging driver

## Notes

- What is charged per booking depends on the business model (Q-001): the pilot's fee per completed booking (REQ-271),
  the fleet subscription (REQ-293) and energy (REQ-270) are parked. The no-show fee line (REQ-036, REQ-211) is the only
  baseline charge, and its payer is open (Q-002). The AC therefore speak of charge lines of any kind, which holds under
  any answer to Q-053 (what the platform invoices from launch if Q-001 is still open).
- Depends on A-010 and Q-037 (fleet finance contacts as invoice recipients). The invoice reaches the fleet and is paid
  through Stripe (US-321); its build and submission at month close are JS-319.
- Whether a fleet with no charges in a month receives an invoice is a design detail for Step 3.3. A charge with no
  booking (only under a parked option, e.g. a subscription) could not carry a booking id as REQ-217 requires.
