# US-418 · Anonymise one driver on request without breaking records

As an Amperlane ops person handling a driver's request to have their data deleted, I want to anonymise that one
driver's personal data while their bookings, audit entries and ledger entries stay, with a pseudonym in place of the
driver, so that I can honour the request without breaking fleet invoices, dispute evidence or the audit trail.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Ops anonymises a driver who asked
  Given a driver who asked, from the registered mobile number, to have their personal data deleted
  When an ops person anonymises that driver
  Then the driver's name, phone number and other personal data no longer appear anywhere in the platform
  And the driver's bookings, audit entries and ledger entries remain, showing a pseudonym
  And every fleet invoice total that included the driver's bookings is unchanged

Scenario: S2 (failure) A user who is not ops tries to anonymise a driver
  Given a Borough Green fleet manager
  When the fleet manager tries to anonymise one of the fleet's drivers
  Then the action is refused
  And the driver's data is unchanged

Scenario: S3 (failure) The request names no known driver
  Given a request that gives a mobile number registered to no driver
  When an ops person tries to anonymise by that number
  Then nothing is changed
  And the ops person is told that no driver matches

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A driver registered to two fleets
  Given a driver registered to both Borough Green and Five Boroughs
  When an ops person anonymises the driver
  Then the driver's personal data is gone from both fleets' records
  And both fleets' bookings for that driver show the same pseudonym

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The same number is registered again later
  Given a driver who was anonymised
  When a fleet later registers the same mobile number
  Then a new driver record is created
  And it is not linked to the anonymised history

Scenario: S6 (edge case) The anonymisation is itself on record
  Given an ops person anonymises a driver
  When the action completes
  Then the audit trail shows the ops person, the reason code and the time, under the pseudonym
  And it holds none of the removed personal data

## Notes

- The ledger and audit trail are append-only (CTX-095, CTX-110; constitution N17, N19), so "delete" becomes
  pseudonymisation for them. Depends on A-025 and Q-022. A driver is identified by the registered mobile number
  (A-006).
- What happens to the driver's future bookings when ops anonymises the driver on request, and how a request is
  verified when it does not come from the registered number, are Q-058, a client decision. The AC hold under any
  answer: S1 uses a request from the registered number and does not decide the future bookings.
- Automatic purge at the end of a retention period is JS-417.
- Whether evidence naming a driver with an open no-show dispute (A-047) is kept until the dispute closes or anonymised
  at once is also Q-058; JS-417 S2 keeps such data on the automatic path, and no scenario here decides it. Backups
  follow A-058.
