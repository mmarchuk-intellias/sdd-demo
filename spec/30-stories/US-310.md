# US-310 · Station operator sets each site's bookable days and hours

As a station operator, I want to set the days and hours in which each of my sites accepts bookings, in the station
portal, so that booked drivers come only when my site is ready to serve them, and I can change it myself when the
site's opening changes.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Setting a site's bookable days and hours
  Given a site of the station operator whose capacity rules cover every weekday from morning to evening
  When the station operator sets the site to accept bookings on weekdays only, between those hours
  Then slots at that site are offered on weekdays within those hours only
  And no slot at that site is offered on a weekend

Scenario: S2 (failure) Hours with no capacity rule are refused
  Given the site's capacity rules cover weekdays only
  When the station operator adds Saturday to the site's bookable days
  Then the change is refused, listing the days and times that no capacity rule covers
  And the site's bookable days and hours stay as they were

Scenario: S3 (failure) Another station operator's site
  When a station operator tries to change the bookable days or hours of a site that is not theirs
  Then the request is refused and nothing changes

Scenario: S4 (edge case) The day the clocks change
  Given a site that accepts bookings between a set morning and a set evening hour, site time
  When the clocks change for daylight saving time
  Then on that day slots are offered between the same hours of the site's local time

## Notes

- Depends on Q-005 (per-site terms with each station operator) and A-013 (site time zones).
- S2 follows REQ-005 (every slot within the bookable hours falls under exactly one capacity rule, EP-01). S3 relies on
  REQ-258 (EP-13).
- REQ-076 does not say what happens to confirmed bookings that fall outside newly reduced hours; A-055 extends A-018
  to them: the change applies to new bookings, and the confirmed bookings it affects stand and are listed for the
  station operator or ops, who act on each one, as JS-312 does for a lowered cap. These AC do not test it.
- Until the portal is used, ops can set the hours for a site (KYIV reason of REQ-076; per-site parameters by ops,
  REQ-146).
