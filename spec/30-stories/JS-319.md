# JS-319 · Month close builds and submits fleet invoices from the ledger

When a month ends, I want every fleet's invoice built from the ledger and submitted to the payment provider at the
configured close time, with no one typing or uploading anything, and records that arrive after the close carried to
the next invoice, so I can close the month without a spreadsheet and still trace every invoice line to its booking.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Invoices are built and submitted at close
  Given the configured close time after the end of a month has come
  When month close runs
  Then each fleet's invoice is built only from that fleet's ledger entries for the month
  And it is submitted to the payment provider with no manual step, no file and no data entry

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Each line traces to its ledger entries and its booking
  Given month close has built a fleet's invoice
  When the invoice is issued
  Then every line references the ledger entries it was built from and the booking they belong to

Scenario: S3 (failure) A record that arrives after the close
  Given a no-show fee that ops creates after reviewing an exception, for a booking of a month already closed
  When the fee is recorded
  Then the issued invoice of that month is unchanged
  And the fee appears on the fleet's next invoice as an adjustment naming the original month and booking

Scenario: S4 (failure) A line that cannot be traced is not issued
  Given a charge of the month whose ledger entries do not reference a booking
  When month close runs
  Then the untraceable charge is on no issued invoice
  And every line of every invoice issued at that close references its ledger entries and its booking

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Period boundaries in the site's local time
  Given a booking that starts late on the last evening of the month in the site's time zone, when it is already the next month in UTC
  When month close runs
  Then the booking's charges are on that month's invoice

## Notes

- The close time is a configured value (requirements.md conventions). Depends on A-013 (site time zones) and Q-001
  (what is charged; see US-318) and Q-053 (what is invoiced from launch while Q-001 is open).
- Late records also include session records from the LIC CSV export, made by hand about twice a day (CTX-062); they
  carry invoice amounts only where the business model invoices energy (Q-001).
- S4 checks only what REQ-109 states: no line is issued untraced. What happens to the untraceable charge (hold the
  fleet's whole invoice for correction, which delays the K monthly invoice, REQ-083, or issue the invoice without it
  and carry it to the next one) is a design detail for Step 3.3. The earlier S4 assumed the first.
- Payment-provider outages and retries at submission: JS-322. Invoicing across the pilot cut-over: REQ-232 (EP-12).
