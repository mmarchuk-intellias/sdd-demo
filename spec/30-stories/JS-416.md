# JS-416 · Scope a suspected breach and follow the SHIELD notification procedure

When a breach of personal data is suspected (a staff or portal account in the wrong hands, an export sent to the wrong
place, an exposed copy of a data store), I want the platform to list the individuals affected and which of their data
elements were exposed, and a breach-notification procedure that meets the NY SHIELD Act ready to follow, so I can
notify the right people about the right data without guessing.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A compromised fleet portal account
  Given a suspected breach scoped to one fleet manager's account over a stated time window
  When the Amperlane CTO asks the platform for the affected individuals
  Then the platform lists each driver whose personal data that account viewed or exported in the window
  And for each driver it lists the data elements exposed, such as name and phone number

Scenario: S2 (failure) No procedure is ready at launch
  Given the launch readiness review
  When no written breach-notification procedure exists that Amperlane counsel has reviewed against the NY SHIELD Act
    and that names Amperlane's notification owner
  Then launch readiness is not confirmed
  And the missing procedure is listed as an open go-live item

Scenario: S3 (failure) The platform cannot resolve part of the scope
  Given a suspected breach scope that includes a period or a copy the platform holds no records for
  When the affected individuals are listed
  Then the list states which part of the scope it could not resolve
  And it is not presented as complete

Scenario: S4 (edge case) SHIELD-covered data elements in scope
  Given a suspected breach scope that includes credentials, licence numbers or account numbers
  When the affected individuals are listed
  Then each of those data elements is marked as covered by the NY SHIELD Act

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Drivers who were already anonymised
  Given a suspected breach scope that includes drivers anonymised before the breach window
  When the affected individuals are listed
  Then those drivers are listed by pseudonym only, as already anonymised
  And they are not re-identified

Scenario: S6 (edge case) An exposed copy of a data store
  Given a suspected breach scoped to a copy of one data store as it was at a stated time
  When the affected individuals are listed
  Then every individual whose personal data was in that store at that time is listed with the data elements stored for them

## Notes

- The person in the situation is the Amperlane CTO together with Amperlane counsel (stakeholders.md). Constitution N18
  (incident runbook naming Amperlane's breach-notification owner, reviewed before launch) applies. Retention periods:
  Q-022.
- S1 relies on the access records of REQ-263 (JS-415) and the organisation scope of REQ-258 (US-411).
- Data held only by vendors (for example message bodies at Twilio) cannot be listed by the platform; the vendor
  register that would show it is REQ-264 (I, out of scope). How the data register lists the vendors each field is
  sent to is a design detail for Step 3.3.
- Storage controls for SHIELD-covered fields (REQ-253) and the rule against holding licence or account numbers without
  an approved purpose (REQ-265) are enablers.
- Red team: "meets the NY SHIELD Act" has no tester; S2 makes it counsel's review, as constitution N18 has the
  runbook reviewed before launch.
- Gate 3 (2026-10-28, decision G3-3 option (b), Mykola Marchuk): S2 is the launch check of constitution N18, a human
  check of the procedure document, not component behaviour. SS-338 clause 8 makes a missing procedure visible in the
  go-live record (SS-423 clause 7); the procedure is proposed as a candidate go-live criterion when Q-055 is asked. S2
  does not depend on Q-055. Scenario text unchanged.
