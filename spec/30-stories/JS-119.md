When the EVSE my booking is on faults, I want to be rebooked automatically to another compatible EVSE at the same
site, with no no-show recorded against me, so I can still charge at the time I booked without calling anyone.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · An allocated booking is rebooked at the same site
  Given a booking is allocated to EVSE A at a site
  And EVSE B at the site is free and compatible with the booking's vehicle for the booking's slot
  When EVSE A faults
  Then the booking is rebooked to EVSE B with the reason code 'moved — charger fault'
  And the charging driver gets a message naming EVSE B
  And no no-show is recorded for the booking

Scenario: S2 · happy path · Later bookings that still fit are left alone
  Given bookings at the site later that day are not allocated yet
  When EVSE A faults
  Then each of them is re-checked against the site's remaining compatible EVSEs
  And each one that still fits keeps its booking unchanged

Scenario: S3 · failure · A later booking that no longer fits is handled before its allocation time
  Given a booking later that day no longer fits the site's remaining compatible EVSEs after EVSE A faults
  When the re-check runs
  Then before the booking's allocation time its driver gets alternatives (JS-120)
  And it appears in the ops exception queue (US-121)

Scenario: S4 · failure · No compatible EVSE is free for an allocated booking
  Given a booking is allocated to EVSE A and no other compatible EVSE at the site is free for its slot
  When EVSE A faults
  Then its driver gets alternatives (JS-120)
  And it appears in the ops exception queue (US-121)
  And no no-show is recorded for it

Scenario: S5 · failure · Rebooking stops partway
  Given fault rebooking for EVSE A stopped after only some of its bookings were handled, for example because the platform restarted
  When the rebooking resumes or is abandoned
  Then every affected booking ends rebooked, proposed to ops, or on EVSE A with an ops exception
  And no affected booking is left in any other state

Scenario: S6 · edge case · A vehicle that needs NACS or an adapter
  Given the booking's vehicle can charge only through a NACS connector or an adapter its fleet allows
  When EVSE A faults
  Then the booking is rebooked only to an EVSE with a connector that vehicle can use

Scenario: S7 · edge case · A fault during the booking's grace period
  Given EVSE A faults after the booking's start and before arrival is recorded
  When the booking is rebooked to EVSE B
  Then the rebooking records no no-show
  And a session start on EVSE B inside the arrival window records arrival
  And a session start on EVSE A does not

## Notes

- A-017 (automatic same-site rebooking); CF-022 is resolved-provisional, for Dana Whitfield to confirm. Q-038 (may a
  rebook use capacity beyond the bookable cap?) is covered by A-050: a fault does not lower the site's cap, and a
  rebook may use any free compatible EVSE at the site, never one a walk-up is using; a booking that finds none is
  unplaced. S1 holds under it (constitution R6).
- Walk-ups are never displaced (REQ-020): "free" means not in use by anyone. The station operator's notice is REQ-051
  (FT-09.2). A maintenance hold takes the same path with 'moved — maintenance' (US-130).
- The Gowanus fault replay (REQ-141, enabler) is the regression test for this story. The allocation message is REQ-200
  (FT-06.4).
