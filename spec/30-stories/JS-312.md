# JS-312 · Bookings above a lowered limit stand and are shown for action

When capacity at a site is cut below the bookings already confirmed, because the station operator lowers a cap or ops
adds a fleet-priority hold, I want those bookings to stand and each one above the new limit shown to whoever cut the
capacity, so I can deal with each of them and no driver loses a confirmed slot without someone deciding it.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A station operator lowers a cap below confirmed bookings
  Given a weekday afternoon window with confirmed bookings
  When the station operator lowers the window's cap below the number of bookings confirmed in some of its slots
  Then every confirmed booking stays confirmed
  And the station operator sees, for each slot over the new cap, the bookings in that slot and by how many the slot is over
  And no new booking is accepted in those slots

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Ops adds a fleet-priority hold below confirmed bookings
  Given confirmed bookings of several fleets in a window
  When ops adds a fleet-priority hold for one fleet that leaves the other fleets less capacity than they have confirmed
  Then those bookings stay confirmed
  And ops sees each slot over the new limit with its bookings

Scenario: S3 (failure) Nothing is dropped silently
  Given bookings above a lowered limit
  When the platform's timers for those bookings run: allocation, station hold and reminder
  Then none of them is cancelled, released or rebooked because of the lower limit
  And any later change to one of them is made by a person and recorded with its actor and reason code

Scenario: S4 (failure) Other station operators see nothing
  Given bookings above a lowered limit at a site
  When a station operator of another site opens the station portal
  Then none of these bookings is shown

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A cancellation brings a slot back within the limit
  Given a slot one booking over its new cap
  When one of its bookings is cancelled by its driver
  Then the slot leaves the over-limit list
  And the freed capacity is not offered to new bookings, because the slot is now at its cap

## Notes

- Depends on A-018 (confirmed bookings stand; the station operator or ops acts on each one), Q-035 and Q-005.
- Which actions the station operator has on a booking above the limit, and whether its driver is told, are covered by
  A-055: the station operator can keep a listed booking or refer it to ops; only ops rebook or release it, with a
  reason code, and the driver is told (REQ-057). The red team removed "its driver gets no message" from S1, which
  assumed the answer.
- Which bookings in an over-cap slot are "above the limit": A-055 lists those confirmed last. S1 shows the whole slot
  and its excess, which holds under it.
- Ops' fleet-priority hold is REQ-335 and ops overrides REQ-056 (EP-07). A maintenance hold is different: it takes EVSEs
  out physically and rebooks as on a fault (REQ-185; the station operator's side is JS-313). S4 relies on REQ-258.
