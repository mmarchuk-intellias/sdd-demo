# US-329 · No-show and late-arrival rates per month, fleet and site

As Amperlane's CEO, I want no-show and late-arrival rates per month, per fleet and per site, with fault, site-full and
maintenance rebooks not counted as no-shows, so that I can show each fleet and station operator their true no-show
rate and see whether cutting no-shows lifts completed bookings.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Rates per month, fleet and site
  Given bookings whose slots fell in a month
  When the CEO opens the no-show report for that month
  Then for each fleet and for each site it shows the no-shows and the late arrivals, each as a share of all bookings whose slot fell in that month

Scenario: S2 (failure) Rebooks are not no-shows
  Given bookings rebooked with the reason codes "moved — charger fault", "moved — site full" or "moved — maintenance"
  When the report is shown
  Then none of them is counted as a no-show

Scenario: S3 (failure) Cancellations are not no-shows
  Given a booking cancelled by its driver before the site's cancellation cut-off
  When the report is shown
  Then it is among the month's bookings but not counted as a no-show

Scenario: S4 (edge case) Arrival after grace
  Given a driver who arrived after the booking's grace period, including any ops extension
  When the report is shown
  Then the booking counts as a no-show and as a late arrival
  And a booking whose driver arrived within grace counts as neither

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A slot that crosses into the next month
  Given a booking whose slot starts on the last evening of a month, site time, and ends in the next month
  When the reports of both months are shown
  Then the booking counts only in the month in which its slot started

## Notes

- Depends on CF-018 and Q-029 (the pilot's 17% and 22% are uncleaned) and Q-044 (targets or baselines). The cut-off in
  S3 is Q-042, covered by A-049 (it defaults to the slot start). S4 follows REQ-037 and the per-site grace of A-003.
- Pilot data is marked in this report too (REQ-225, linked from US-327 and US-328 in FT-11.1).
- Whether a rebooked booking and the booking that replaced it both count in the month's bookings (the denominator) is
  a design detail for Step 3.3.
- S5 is our reading of "whose slot fell in that month"; red team to confirm.
