When I reach the site after my booking was released as a no-show, I want to get an offer of the earliest free
compatible slot there that I can accept with one tap, so I can still charge at that site without starting a new
booking from scratch.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · A late driver gets a re-offer and accepts it
  Given a booking was released as a no-show at the end of its grace period
  When the driver records arrival at the booking's site by scanning a QR code
  Then the booking stays recorded as a no-show, with the arrival time stored as a late arrival
  And within the set time the driver gets an offer of the earliest free compatible slot at that site
  When the driver accepts the offer with one tap
  Then the driver has a confirmed booking for the offered slot
  And the released booking stays a no-show

Scenario: S2 · failure · The driver does not accept the re-offer
  Given a late driver got a re-offer
  When the driver does not accept it
  Then no new booking is made
  And the released booking stays a no-show with its late arrival stored

Scenario: S3 · failure · The offered slot is taken before the driver accepts
  Given a late driver got a re-offer
  And another booker took that slot before the driver accepted
  When the driver accepts the offer
  Then no booking is made for the taken slot
  And the driver is offered the next free compatible slot at the site

Scenario: S4 · edge case · Arrival after an ops extension is still late
  Given ops extended the booking's grace period and the extended grace ended with no arrival
  When the driver records arrival at the booking's site
  Then the booking stays a no-show with the arrival stored as a late arrival
  And the driver gets the re-offer

Scenario: S5 · edge case · Only compatible slots are offered
  Given the late driver's vehicle can charge only through a NACS connector or an adapter its fleet allows
  When the re-offer is made
  Then the offered slot is one that the vehicle can be allocated to under connector compatibility

## Notes

- Q-003: whether the no-show fee still applies after the driver accepts the re-offer is an undecided client decision
  (REQ-304 is parked). This story neither waives nor confirms the fee; ops can waive it (US-115).
- S3 applies REQ-008 (FT-01.2). Arrival by QR scan after grace is stored by US-101 S4.
- What a late driver gets when no compatible slot is free at the site for the rest of the site's bookable hours that
  day is a design detail for Step 3.3.
- REQ-241 v2 (CR-001) makes accepting an offered slot a one-tap action that needs no one-time code, so S1's one tap
  (REQ-038) holds on any phone.
