# US-301 · Ops onboards a fleet and registers its drivers and vehicles on its behalf

As Amperlane ops, I want to onboard a fleet and register its drivers' mobile numbers and its vehicles on the fleet's
behalf, and hand this over to a fleet manager later, so that a fleet that will not use a portal, such as Five Boroughs,
can book from launch day, and a fleet that later wants the portal does not have to start from scratch.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Ops onboards a fleet that has no portal user
  Given a fleet that has no fleet portal user
  When Amperlane ops creates the fleet and registers its drivers' mobile numbers and its vehicles, each vehicle with its native connector, allowed adapters and maximum DC charging power
  Then a booking from any of those mobile numbers for one of those vehicles is accepted under the usual capacity and compatibility rules
  And the fleet's drivers and vehicles are listed under that fleet for ops
  And no fleet portal sign-in was needed at any step

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A fleet manager takes over in the fleet portal
  Given a fleet that ops onboarded on its behalf
  When a person of that fleet is given a fleet manager login and signs in to the fleet portal
  Then the fleet manager sees every driver number and vehicle that ops registered for the fleet
  And the fleet manager can add, edit and retire them from then on
  And the bookings already made for the fleet are unchanged

Scenario: S3 (failure) An invalid mobile number is refused
  Given ops is registering drivers for a fleet
  When ops enters a mobile number that is not a valid phone number
  Then that number is not registered
  And ops is shown which number was refused and why

Scenario: S4 (failure) A vehicle without its connector profile is refused
  Given ops is registering vehicles for a fleet
  When ops saves a vehicle without a native connector or without a maximum DC charging power
  Then the vehicle is not registered
  And ops is shown which field is missing

Scenario: S5 (failure) Other organisations cannot register for the fleet
  Given a station operator user and a fleet manager of another fleet
  When either of them tries to register a driver or a vehicle for this fleet
  Then the request is refused and nothing is registered

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A driver who already drives for another fleet
  Given a mobile number already registered to another fleet
  When ops registers the same number for this fleet
  Then the number is registered to both fleets
  And it can book vehicles of either fleet, each booking belonging to the fleet whose vehicle it names
  And neither fleet sees the other fleet's bookings for that number

## Notes

- Depends on A-023 (fleets register numbers and vehicles; ops does it for small fleets), Q-028 (how a driver is
  identified and authorised) and A-016 (a driver can drive for more than one fleet).
- Booker-only numbers (REQ-201) and removing a driver (REQ-202, Q-045) belong to other features; this story only
  registers. Registration is not messaging consent: the driver gets no SMS or WhatsApp until REQ-248 holds.
- The vehicle fields come from REQ-015 (EP-01). S5 and S6 rely on organisation-scoped access (REQ-258, EP-13), which is
  not linked here.
- The fleet manager's own registration paths are US-302, US-303 and US-305 (file import).
