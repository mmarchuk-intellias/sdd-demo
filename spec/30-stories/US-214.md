# US-214 · Book by texting a site code and a time

As a driver, I want to text a site code and a time, such as "GOW 1645", and get back either a confirmed booking or
the next free slot to accept, with nobody in ops involved, so that I can book the way I do today but get an answer
at once instead of waiting for ops to confirm by hand.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The requested slot is free
  Given a registered driver whose vehicle and duration for text bookings are known (see Notes)
  And the slot starting at 16:45 at Gowanus has capacity for that vehicle and duration
  When the driver texts "GOW 1645" to Amperlane's SMS number
  Then the driver gets a reply with a confirmed booking at Gowanus starting at 16:45, naming the vehicle and duration
  And no ops person took any action on the request

Scenario: S2 (failure) The requested slot is full
  Given the slot starting at 16:45 at Gowanus has no capacity left for the driver's vehicle
  When the driver texts "GOW 1645"
  Then the driver gets a reply offering the next free compatible slot at Gowanus, held for the site's hold time
  And no booking exists until the driver accepts it

Scenario: S3 (happy path) The driver accepts the offered slot in time
  Given the driver was offered the next free compatible slot by reply
  When the driver replies with the accept keyword within the site's hold time
  Then the driver gets a reply with the confirmed booking for the offered slot

Scenario: S4 (failure) The driver accepts after the hold expired
  Given the driver was offered the next free compatible slot by reply
  When the driver replies with the accept keyword after the site's hold time
  Then no booking is created from the expired hold
  And the driver gets a reply with a confirmed booking for that slot if it is still free, or the next free
    compatible slot to accept

Scenario: S5 (failure) The site code is unknown
  Given no site has the code "XYZ"
  When the driver texts "XYZ 1645"
  Then no booking or hold is created
  And the message goes to ops as not understood, and the driver gets an acknowledgement (REQ-222)

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A time that is not on the site's time grid
  Given Gowanus books on its configured time grid and 16:50 is not a start time on it
  When the driver texts "GOW 1650"
  Then no booking is created for 16:50
  And the driver gets a reply offering the next free compatible slot on the site's time grid

## Notes

- Times are the site's local time (A-013). Text bookings use Twilio (A-008); inbound messages are processed once
  (REQ-090, JS-216).
- Which vehicle and duration a text booking uses when the text names neither (a driver may book several vehicles,
  REQ-198, and a site has several durations, A-001), which day "1645" means when that time has already passed today,
  and what the reply is when no compatible slot is free for the rest of the day are design details for Step 3.3.
- S4 applies REQ-061 again to the late accept; REQ-008 (FT-01.2) sets the hold.
- requirements.md lists REQ-061 among the rows to reconsider if K exceeds the budget; it is Y.
