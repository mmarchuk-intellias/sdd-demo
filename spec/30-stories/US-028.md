# US-028 · Station-hold record kept per booking

As a member of Amperlane ops, I want each booking to show the station-hold method used, every request sent, the answer from the
charger or site staff, and their times, so that I can show what was done to hold the stall when a no-show is disputed.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A charger reservation is recorded
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub is allocated to an EVSE flagged as reservable
  When the charger reservation is sent and the charger accepts it
  Then the booking's record shows the method "charger reservation", the request with its time and the acceptance with its time

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A site staff request and its acknowledgement are recorded
  Given a booking at JFK Lot 9 is allocated
  When the station-hold request is sent and site staff acknowledge it
  Then the booking's record shows the method "site staff", the request with its time and the acknowledgement with its time

Scenario: S3 (failure) A rejected reservation and the fallback are both recorded
  Given a charger reservation for a booking is rejected and the platform sends site staff a signage request
  When ops opens the booking's record
  Then it shows the rejected reservation, the signage request and their answers, each with its time, in the order they happened

Scenario: S4 (failure) A request that was never answered or delivered is recorded as such
  Given a station-hold request for a booking was not delivered, or got no answer
  When ops opens the booking's record
  Then it shows the request with its time and that no delivery or no answer followed

Scenario: S5 (edge) A reallocated booking keeps the attempts for both EVSEs
  Given a booking was reallocated from one EVSE to another before its slot
  When ops opens the booking's record
  Then it shows the station-hold attempts on both EVSEs, each with its EVSE and time
