When I find the EVSE I came to charge at not working, I want to report it in one tap from my booking page or by
message, so I can get ops onto it without phoning anyone while I wait at the site.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · One tap from the booking page
  Given a driver at the site has a booking allocated to EVSE A
  When the driver reports EVSE A as faulted with one tap on the booking page
  Then an ops alert linked to the site and EVSE A appears in the ops console
  And the alert shows the driver's booking and the time of the report

Scenario: S2 · happy path · A report by message
  Given a driver has a booking allocated to EVSE A
  When the driver replies to a message about the booking to report the EVSE as faulted
  Then an ops alert linked to the site and EVSE A appears in the ops console

Scenario: S3 · failure · A message the platform cannot understand
  Given a driver sends a free-text message about a broken EVSE that the platform cannot understand
  When the message is received
  Then it reaches ops in the ops console with the driver's booking and language shown
  And the driver gets an acknowledgement in the driver's language

Scenario: S4 · failure · A retried tap creates one alert
  Given the driver's report from the booking page is sent again because the phone retried the request
  When both requests arrive
  Then one ops alert exists for the report

Scenario: S5 · edge case · The CSMS still reports the EVSE as available
  Given the CSMS reports EVSE A as available
  When the driver reports EVSE A as faulted
  Then the ops alert is created
  And it shows the CSMS status of EVSE A with the time it was observed

## Notes

- Drivers are today's fault detectors, and at LIC the only signal besides the attendant (CTX-048).
- The report raises an alert only. Automatic fault rebooking starts from a CSMS fault status or the LIC attendant
  (REQ-047, REQ-192, JS-116); ops can rebook all bookings of the EVSE in one action (JS-123).
- S3 applies REQ-222 (FT-06.2); S4 applies REQ-236 (FT-14.3). Messages may be SMS or WhatsApp (U-10, Q-034, A-008).
- REQ-241 v2 (CR-001) makes reporting a faulted EVSE a one-tap action that needs no one-time code, so S1's one tap
  (REQ-191) holds on any phone.
