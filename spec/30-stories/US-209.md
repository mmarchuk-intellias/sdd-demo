# US-209 · EVSE status at any past moment

As Amperlane ops deciding a no-show dispute, I want to see what an EVSE's status was at any past moment and where
that status came from, so that I can decide the dispute on recorded evidence rather than on anyone's memory.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Status at a past moment
  Given an EVSE's status changed several times during a day
  When ops asks for its status at a moment between two of those changes
  Then the platform shows the status in force at that moment
  And it shows when that status was observed and its source (CSMS webhook, CSMS poll or attendant)

Scenario: S2 (failure) A moment before any status was recorded
  Given an EVSE whose first status was recorded at a known time
  When ops asks for its status at an earlier moment
  Then the platform shows that no status was recorded for that moment
  And it does not show the first recorded status in its place

Scenario: S3 (failure) Events arrived out of order
  Given a status event with an earlier observation time arrived after one with a later observation time
  When ops asks for the status at a moment between the two observation times
  Then the platform shows the status with the earlier observation time

Scenario: S4 (failure) The moment falls in a feed outage
  Given a connected site's status feed was unavailable for a period
  When ops asks for an EVSE's status at a moment inside that period
  Then the platform shows the last status observed before the outage, with its observation time
  And it shows that the status was older than 60 seconds at that moment (constitution N2)

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Status reported by the attendant
  Given the LIC attendant set an EVSE to faulted and later to free
  When ops asks for the EVSE's status at a moment between the two reports
  Then the platform shows "faulted", the time the attendant reported it and the attendant view as its source
