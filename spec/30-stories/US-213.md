# US-213 · Book a vehicle that has no telematics

As a shift driver of a medallion cab with no telematics, I want to book the cab from my own phone by SMS or the
driver PWA without giving its SoC, location or any other vehicle data, so that the cab is charged at the shift
change even though nothing reports its state.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Booking in the driver PWA
  Given a Five Boroughs driver whose cab has no telematics link
  When the driver books the cab in the driver PWA
  Then the booking is confirmed
  And no step of the booking asked for SoC, location or other vehicle data

Scenario: S2 (happy path) Booking by SMS
  Given a Five Boroughs driver whose cab has no telematics link
  When the driver texts a site code and a time, such as "GOW 1645", from the registered number
  Then the driver gets a confirmed booking or the next free compatible slot to accept by reply (REQ-061)
  And no reply asks for SoC, location or other vehicle data

Scenario: S3 (failure) The driver has not opted in to location
  Given a driver who has not opted in to location
  When the driver books a slot
  Then the booking is confirmed
  And no location is read or stored for it

Scenario: S4 (failure) No vehicle data is available at all
  Given a vehicle whose telematics provider returns no data, or that has no telematics link
  When the driver books it
  Then the booking is confirmed on the same terms as for any other vehicle

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The driver chooses to enter SoC
  Given a driver booking a cab with no telematics in the driver PWA
  When the driver enters the cab's SoC and confirms
  Then the booking is confirmed on the same terms as a booking made without SoC
