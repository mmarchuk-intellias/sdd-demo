# US-006 · Bookings match the vehicle's connector and adapters

As a driver, I want every booking matched to EVSEs whose connector my vehicle can use, natively or through an adapter
my vehicle is allowed, so that I never arrive at an EVSE I cannot plug into.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A vehicle with an allowed adapter can use both connector types
  Given a vehicle registered to the booking's fleet records a NACS native connector, an allowed CCS1 adapter and its maximum DC charging power
  And each EVSE connector at Gowanus FastHub records its connector type, CCS1 or NACS, and its maximum power in kW
  When a booker books that vehicle at Gowanus FastHub
  Then the booking names that vehicle
  And it is accepted against the EVSEs with a NACS or a CCS1 connector
  And at allocation it gets an EVSE with one of those connectors

Scenario: S2 (failure) No EVSE with a matching connector has room
  Given a vehicle with a CCS1 native connector and no allowed adapter
  And in the requested window the only EVSEs with room at the site have NACS connectors only
  When a booker tries to book that vehicle for that window
  Then the booking is refused

Scenario: S3 (failure) An adapter the vehicle is not allowed does not count
  Given a vehicle with a NACS native connector whose allowed adapters do not include CCS1
  When a booker tries to book it at JFK Lot 9, whose EVSEs have CCS1 connectors only
  Then the booking is refused

Scenario: S4 (failure) A vehicle of another fleet cannot be named
  Given a vehicle registered to another fleet
  When a booker books for the booker's own fleet and names that vehicle
  Then the booking is refused

Scenario: S5 (edge) An EVSE with connectors of both types counts for either vehicle
  Given an EVSE whose connectors include both CCS1 and NACS
  When a booking for a CCS1-only vehicle and a booking for a NACS-only vehicle are checked against the site's EVSEs
  Then that EVSE counts as compatible for both

Scenario: S6 (failure) A connector or vehicle record without a usable type or power is not saved
  Given an EVSE connector or a vehicle is being recorded
  When its connector type is anything other than CCS1 or NACS, for example the pilot's free text "CCS", or its maximum power is missing
  Then the record is not saved
  And bookings keep being matched against the records already saved

## Notes

- Depends on: A-023 (vehicles registered by the fleet or by ops), A-004 and Q-013 (whether the sources' "ports", "chargers" and
  "stalls" map one to one to EVSEs, and whether an EVSE carries two connector types; S5 holds only if Q-013 confirms such EVSEs exist).
- The fleet's adapter policy, overridable per vehicle, is REQ-072 (EP-08); vehicle records are maintained under REQ-070
  (EP-08) and EVSE connectors at site onboarding (REQ-165, US-014). Power-based choice among compatible EVSEs is
  REQ-016 (US-017).
- S6 (added in the Step 3.1 red-team pass) closes a loophole: with a free-text connector field, as in the pilot
  prototype ("CCS", SRC-C2@00:16:36), a CCS1 vehicle would silently match nothing or the wrong EVSE while S1 – S5 still
  pass on clean test data. A vehicle with no allowed adapter is a valid record (REQ-015).
- Pilot incidents this story prevents: vehicles with a NACS connector booked onto CCS1 EVSEs without an adapter, and a
  CCS1 vehicle on a NACS EVSE (CTX-049).
