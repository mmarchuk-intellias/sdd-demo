# JS-313 · Station operator is told when a fault or maintenance hold changes bookings

When an EVSE at my site faults, or Amperlane puts EVSEs on a maintenance hold, while bookings are due on them, I want a
notice within a set time and a list of each affected booking and what happened to it, so I know which booked drivers
are still coming and to which EVSE, instead of finding out from a driver at the site.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A charger fault with bookings
  Given an EVSE at the station operator's site with bookings later today
  When the platform records a charger fault on it and rebooks the affected bookings
  Then the station operator gets a notice within the set time of the fault
  And the station portal lists each affected booking with its slot and its outcome: rebooked to another EVSE at the site, rebooked to another time or site, or sent to ops

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A maintenance hold
  Given an EVSE with allocated bookings
  When ops places a maintenance hold that takes that EVSE out of use
  Then the station operator gets the same notice and list, with the reason code "moved — maintenance"

Scenario: S3 (failure) A booking the platform could not place
  Given an affected booking that could not be rebooked automatically
  When the station operator opens the list
  Then the booking is shown as sent to ops
  And its outcome is updated in the list once ops resolves it

Scenario: S4 (failure) No driver phone numbers
  When the station operator opens the list of affected bookings
  Then no driver's phone number is shown

Scenario: S5 (edge case) Bookings that still fit are not reported as changed
  Given later bookings at the site that still fit the remaining EVSEs
  When the charger fault is recorded
  Then those bookings are not listed as changed

## Notes

- "A set time" is a configured value (requirements.md conventions).
- The channel and recipient of the notice (station portal only, e-mail, SMS) are a design detail for Step 3.3;
  integrations.md lists no e-mail provider.
- The rebooking itself is REQ-048 and REQ-185 (EP-04, EP-07); same-site rebooks follow A-017 (CF-022), and Q-038 asks
  whether a rebook may use walk-up capacity (A-050: it may, but never an EVSE a walk-up is using). Bookings that
  still fit stay (INV-2 in data-model.md).
- S4 relies on REQ-258; which other driver data the station operator may see is Q-048 (covered by A-053).
- The count of bookings each fault cost is US-314 (REQ-080).
