# US-315 · Station operator sees upcoming bookings and their allocated EVSE

As a station operator, I want to see the upcoming bookings at my sites and the EVSE each one is allocated to, so that
my site staff know which EVSEs are booked and when, instead of learning it from a phone call or a driver.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Upcoming bookings with their EVSE
  Given confirmed bookings at the station operator's site
  When the station operator opens the site's upcoming bookings
  Then each booking is listed with its slot and the EVSE it is allocated to
  And a booking not allocated yet is shown as not yet allocated until the site's allocation offset before its slot

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A reallocation is shown
  Given a booking whose allocated EVSE is still occupied at the pre-arrival check
  When the platform reallocates it to another EVSE
  Then the list shows the new EVSE

Scenario: S3 (failure) No driver phone numbers
  When the station operator opens the upcoming bookings
  Then no driver's phone number is shown

Scenario: S4 (failure) Other station operators' sites stay hidden
  When a station operator asks for the upcoming bookings of a site that is not theirs
  Then nothing is shown

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A cancelled booking leaves the list
  Given an upcoming booking allocated to an EVSE
  When its driver cancels it
  Then it is no longer listed as upcoming
  And its EVSE no longer shows that booking

## Notes

- Bookings are made against a pool and allocated about T-15 (A-002, REQ-003); reallocation at the pre-arrival check is
  REQ-021 (EP-02).
- S3 and S4 rely on REQ-258; which driver data a station operator may see is Q-048 (covered by A-053).
- At JFK and LIC, station-hold requests already tell site staff what is due (KYIV reason of REQ-079); this view adds the
  full list.
