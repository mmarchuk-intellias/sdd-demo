# US-307 · Fleet manager sees the no-shows of the fleet's drivers

As a fleet manager, I want to see the no-shows of my fleet's drivers, so that I can take them up with the drivers
concerned myself instead of hearing about them only when Amperlane shows me.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) No-shows are listed by driver
  Given the fleet's bookings include no-shows
  When the fleet manager opens the fleet's no-shows
  Then each no-show is listed with its charging driver, vehicle, site, date and slot time
  And the list can be narrowed to one driver

Scenario: S2 (failure) A rebook is not a no-show
  Given a booking rebooked after a charger fault, a site-full case or a maintenance hold
  When the fleet manager opens the fleet's no-shows
  Then that booking is not listed

Scenario: S3 (failure) A cancellation is not a no-show
  Given a booking its driver cancelled before the site's cancellation cut-off
  When the fleet manager opens the fleet's no-shows
  Then that booking is not listed

Scenario: S4 (failure) No-shows on another fleet's vehicles stay hidden
  Given a driver registered to this fleet and to another fleet
  When the driver no-shows a booking of the other fleet's vehicle
  Then that no-show is not shown to this fleet's manager

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A driver who arrived after grace
  Given a driver who arrived after the booking's grace period had ended
  When the fleet manager views the fleet's no-shows
  Then the booking is listed as a no-show together with the recorded late arrival time

## Notes

- The pilot showed rebooked bookings as no-shows (CTX-047, SRC-C2@00:52:53); S2 guards against repeating that.
- S3 depends on the cancellation cut-off (Q-042, covered by A-049; REQ-171); S5 follows REQ-037. S4 relies on REQ-258 and A-016.
- Who pays a no-show fee and whether the fleet sees it here are open (Q-002); this story shows the no-shows only.
