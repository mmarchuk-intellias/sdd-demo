# US-027 · Station holds cleared when the booking ends

As a station operator, I want every station hold Amperlane placed at my site, a charger reservation or a marked stall,
to be cleared as soon as its booking is released, cancelled or rebooked, so that no EVSE stays blocked for walk-ups
after its booking ends.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A marked stall is cleared after release at the end of grace or cancellation
  Given a booking at JFK Lot 9 has a physical station hold on a stall
  When the booking is released after grace without an arrival, or cancelled after its station hold was requested
  Then site staff are told within the set time to clear that stall for walk-ups

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A charger reservation is cancelled with the booking
  Given a booking at Gowanus FastHub has a charger reservation
  When the booking is cancelled, or released after grace
  Then the platform cancels the charger reservation through the CSMS

Scenario: S3 (failure) A booking rebooked to another EVSE leaves no station hold behind
  Given a booking has a station hold on its first EVSE
  When the booking is rebooked to another EVSE
  Then site staff are told within the set time to clear the first stall if it was marked
  And the charger reservation on the first EVSE is cancelled if there was one

Scenario: S4 (failure) A booking with a recorded arrival is not cleared at the end of grace
  Given the driver's arrival is recorded for a booking with a physical station hold
  When the booking's grace period ends
  Then site staff get no instruction to clear that stall

Scenario: S5 (failure) A booking cancelled before any station hold gets no instruction
  Given a booking is cancelled before its station-hold offset
  When the cancellation is confirmed
  Then site staff get no instruction to clear a stall and no charger reservation is cancelled for it

Scenario: S6 (edge) Every Gowanus physical station hold is matched by a clear instruction
  Given the physical station holds the platform requested at Gowanus FastHub over a day
  When each of their bookings has been released, cancelled or rebooked
  Then each of those requests has a matching instruction to clear the stall
  And no stall is left marked by an instruction without a matching clear instruction after its booking ended

## Notes

- REQ-153 belongs to FT-02.2, but it is linked here because the three requirements give the station operator one
  outcome: every station hold is cleared when its booking ends, whichever method held it.
- Depends on: A-012, Q-009 (cancelling reservations through AMPECO), A-048 with Q-059 (Gowanus site staff receive and

  (CTX-139).
- No release after an arrival is REQ-177 (EP-03). "Within the set time" is a configured value; its default is a
  design detail for Step 3.3.
- What happens when a clear instruction cannot be delivered to site staff, or the CSMS does not confirm a reservation
  cancellation, is a design detail for Step 3.3 (REQ-026 covers only undelivered station-hold requests).
