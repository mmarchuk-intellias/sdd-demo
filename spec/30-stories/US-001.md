# US-001 · Open-slot view shows only bookable slots

As a booker, I want the open-slot view to show, per site and time, only the slots that could be held for the vehicle
and duration I chose, so that I do not spend taps between rides on slots the site would refuse.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A slot with room for a compatible vehicle is shown as open
  Given a booker has chosen a site, a vehicle registered to the booker's fleet and one of the site's configured booking durations
  And for every slot a booking of that duration would cover, the holds and bookings overlapping the slot are below the cap of the site's capacity rule for that slot
  And the site has EVSEs with a connector the vehicle can use natively or through an allowed adapter
  When the booker opens the open-slot view for that site
  Then the start times of those bookings are shown as open
  And a hold for that vehicle and duration placed on any of those start times is accepted

Scenario: S2 (failure) A slot at its cap is not shown
  Given the holds and bookings overlapping one slot at a site already equal the cap of the capacity rule for that slot
  When a booker opens the open-slot view for that site
  Then no start time whose booking of the chosen duration would cover that slot is shown as open

Scenario: S3 (failure) A vehicle the site cannot serve sees no open slot there
  Given every EVSE at JFK Lot 9 has only CCS1 connectors
  And the chosen vehicle has a NACS native connector and no allowed adapter
  When the booker opens the open-slot view for JFK Lot 9 with that vehicle
  Then no slot at JFK Lot 9 is shown as open for that vehicle

Scenario: S4 (edge) The chosen duration decides which start times are open
  Given a slot at its cap follows slots with room at a site
  When the booker changes the chosen duration
  Then a start time is shown as open only if no slot covered by a booking of the new duration from that start time is at its cap

## Notes

- Depends on: A-001 (time grid and durations are configured values), A-023 (vehicles registered to fleets).
- Overnight slots (REQ-152) were split out to US-033 in the Step 3.1 red-team pass: REQ-152 holds only under A-028,
  the working assumption for CF-015 (**needs-decision**), and would have kept this K story from being Ready
  (constitution R6).
- The view filters by capacity rules (REQ-004, US-002) and connector compatibility (REQ-013, US-006) only, which is
  what REQ-001 names. A slot shown as open can still fail the pool check at confirmation (REQ-147, US-003, A-014) or be
  taken by another booker first; the "so that" promises fewer refusals, not none.
- The hours a site accepts bookings are set under REQ-076 (EP-09). Two-tap booking from this view is REQ-100 (EP-06).
