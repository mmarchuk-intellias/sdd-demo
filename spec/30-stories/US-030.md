# US-030 · Site staff sign in with their own site-scoped login

As an attendant, such as the one at LIC Garage Depot, I want my own sign-in to the platform on the site's device, scoped to my site,
so that I can act on Amperlane bookings at my site without ops calling me and without using anyone else's access.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The attendant signs in on the site device
  Given ops has created a site-scoped login for the LIC attendant
  When the attendant signs in on the site's device
  Then the attendant view opens for LIC Garage Depot only

Scenario: S2 (failure) A revoked login stops working at once
  Given the attendant is signed in on the site's device
  When ops revokes the attendant's login
  Then the attendant's next action is refused
  And the attendant can no longer sign in

Scenario: S3 (failure) The login does not reach another site
  Given the attendant is signed in for LIC Garage Depot
  When the attendant tries to open bookings or EVSEs of another site
  Then access is refused

Scenario: S4 (edge) Two staff members share one site device
  Given two site staff members each have their own login for the same site
  When each signs in on the site's device in turn and acts in the attendant view
  Then the audit trail records each action against the staff member who signed in
