# US-218 · The registered mobile number is the driver's only identity

As a driver, I want the mobile number my fleet registered to be all Amperlane needs to know who I am and which
vehicles I may book, so that I can book on my first shift from the phone I already drive with, instead of giving up
at a sign-up step I will not do between rides.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) A registered driver books
  Given a driver's mobile number is registered to Borough Green
  And Borough Green's policy allows the driver to book a vehicle
  When the driver books that vehicle from the registered number, by text or from a message link
  Then the booking is made for Borough Green with that vehicle
  And no sign-up form, password, email address or payment card was asked for

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A driver registered to two fleets
  Given a driver's mobile number is registered to two fleets
  When the driver opens the open-slot view
  Then the driver can choose any vehicle that either fleet's policy allows the driver to book
  And a booking records the fleet whose vehicle it uses

Scenario: S3 (failure) A number that is not registered
  Given a mobile number that is not registered to any fleet
  When someone tries to book from that number
  Then no booking or hold is created
  And the person is not offered a sign-up form or an account

Scenario: S4 (failure) A vehicle the fleet policy does not allow
  Given a registered driver whose fleet policy does not allow a particular vehicle
  When the driver tries to book that vehicle
  Then the booking is refused for that vehicle
  And the vehicles the policy allows can still be booked

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The driver changes phone but keeps the number
  Given a registered driver puts the same mobile number in a new phone
  When the driver books by text from the new phone
  Then the driver is identified as before and the booking is made
  And no sign-up form, password, email address or payment card is asked for on the new phone

## Notes
