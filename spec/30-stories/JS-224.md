# JS-224 · A reminder before the slot

When my slot is coming up and I am out driving for Uber or Lyft, I want a reminder at a set time before it, so I can
head to the site in time, or cancel or report running late while the slot can still go to someone else.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) The reminder arrives at the site's lead time
  Given a confirmed booking at a site with a configured reminder lead time
  When that lead time before the slot is reached
  Then the charging driver receives one reminder on their channel, in their language
  And it carries a link to the booking

Scenario: S2 (failure) The booking was cancelled before the reminder
  Given a booking was cancelled before its reminder time
  When the reminder time is reached
  Then no reminder is sent

Scenario: S3 (failure) The booking was rebooked before the reminder
  Given a booking was rebooked to a later slot before its reminder time
  When the lead time before the new slot is reached
  Then the charging driver receives the reminder for the new slot
  And no reminder is sent for the old slot

Scenario: S4 (failure) The reminder falls in planned downtime
  Given a booking's reminder time falls in planned downtime between 01:00 and 05:00 ET
  When the platform resumes
  Then the reminder is sent, possibly late, and exactly once (constitution N5)

Scenario: S5 (edge case) The booker is not the charging driver
  Given a day driver booked a slot for a night driver
  When the reminder time is reached
  Then the night driver, as charging driver, receives the reminder

Scenario: S6 (edge case) Two sites with different lead times
  Given two bookings at sites whose configured reminder lead times differ
  When each booking's reminder is sent
  Then each is sent at its own site's lead time before its slot

## Notes

- The reminder default is still to be set (A-011); the lead time is per site.
- What happens when a booking is made after its reminder point (a slot starting sooner than the lead time) is a
  design detail for Step 3.3.
- Rebooking (S3) is EP-04; the durable timer behind S4 is REQ-133 (FT-14.2). "Their channel" is the driver's preferred
  channel (SMS or WhatsApp, Q-034); a driver who replied STOP gets no reminder on that channel (REQ-248, US-229).
