# JS-309 · Know before the shift change whether each booked vehicle is charging

When my cabs are booked to charge ahead of the shift change, I want to see for each vehicle booked today
whether it arrived and whether charging started, without having to sign in to the fleet portal, so I can sort out a
cab that is not charging before the next shift needs it.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Arrival and charging appear as they happen
  Given a fleet manager of a fleet with a vehicle booked for this afternoon
  When the driver's arrival is recorded and the site then reports charging on the allocated EVSE
  Then the fleet's view of today's bookings shows the vehicle as arrived and then as charging, each with its time, as soon as the platform has recorded the evidence

Scenario: S2 (happy path) A small fleet follows a booking without the fleet portal
  Given Five Boroughs' owner has no fleet portal login and booked a cab for the night-shift driver by message
  And the owner opens the booking's link on a phone that completed a one-time code for the owner's number within the
    set period (REQ-241)
  When the cab arrives and starts charging
  Then the owner can see from the booking's message, without any portal sign-in, that the cab arrived and is charging
  And Amperlane ops, looking the booking up for the owner, sees the same arrival and charging times

Scenario: S3 (failure) No arrival by the end of grace
  Given a vehicle's booking reaches the end of the site's grace period with no arrival
  When the fleet looks at today's bookings
  Then the booking is shown as released with no arrival, with the time of release

Scenario: S4 (failure) Arrived, but charging did not start
  Given the driver's arrival was recorded
  And no charging session started within the set time
  When the fleet looks at today's bookings
  Then the vehicle is shown as arrived and not charging
  And the outcome, a rebook at the site or a fault case with ops, is shown once it is decided

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A site where the attendant reports charging
  Given a vehicle booked at a pattern C site, where charging is reported by the attendant and not by a CSMS
  When arrival is recorded but the attendant has not reported charging yet
  Then the vehicle is shown as arrived, with charging not yet reported, and the time of the last update

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A rebook after a charger fault
  Given the vehicle's booking was rebooked to another EVSE or time after a charger fault
  When the fleet looks at today's bookings
  Then the view follows the new booking and shows the reason code of the rebook

## Notes

- R7: "charging started" comes from the EVSE status at pattern A and B sites and from the attendant at pattern C.
  Session-start events, ChargeLab freshness and the attendant's device are unconfirmed (U-8, U-13, U-14): Q-009,
  Q-012, Q-049, Q-010; A-051 (with Q-051) defines the session-start evidence until they are answered. The view
  always shows when the status was observed (constitution N2).
- REQ-074 shows evidence only; whether the booking counts as completed is Q-017 (REQ-224).
- REQ-243 also covers booking without the portal: booking by message and by a booker-only number is delivered in
  EP-01 and EP-06 (REQ-019, REQ-201, REQ-011), ops booking on behalf in EP-07 (REQ-055), ops look-up by REQ-195. This
  story covers seeing whether each booked charge took place.
- S2 assumes the booker reaches the booking's status from the confirmation sent to the booker (REQ-088) through a
  signed link (A-006); A-023 for booker-only numbers. Whether a status message is also pushed is a design detail for Step 3.3.
  Red team: on a phone without a recent one-time code the link shows only site, time and one-tap actions (REQ-241 v2,
  CR-001), not arrival or charging, so S2 states the verified phone.
- S4 follows REQ-181 (EP-03). "As soon as the evidence is recorded" has no latency target of its own; N1 bounds status
  age at connected sites.
