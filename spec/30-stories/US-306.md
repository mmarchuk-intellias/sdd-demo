# US-306 · Fleet manager sees every booking of the fleet

As a fleet manager, I want to see in the fleet portal every upcoming and past booking of my fleet with its driver,
vehicle, site, time and current state, so that I can answer my drivers' and my own questions about the fleet's
charging without calling Amperlane.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Upcoming and past bookings are listed
  Given a fleet with bookings in the past and in the coming days
  When its fleet manager opens the fleet's bookings in the fleet portal
  Then every booking of the fleet is listed with its charging driver, vehicle, site, date and time, and current state
  And bookings made by the driver, by a booker-only number, by a fleet manager and by ops are all included

Scenario: S2 (happy path) The state follows the booking
  Given an upcoming confirmed booking of the fleet
  When it is allocated, its driver arrives, or it is rebooked after a charger fault
  Then the fleet manager sees its new state
  And for a rebook, the reason code and the booking that replaced it

Scenario: S3 (failure) Other fleets' bookings stay hidden
  When a fleet manager asks for a booking of a fleet that is not theirs, by list, search or booking id
  Then nothing of that booking is shown

Scenario: S4 (failure) A shared driver's other bookings do not leak
  Given a driver registered to this fleet and to another fleet
  When the fleet manager lists the fleet's bookings
  Then only that driver's bookings of this fleet's vehicles are shown

Scenario: S5 (edge case) An overnight booking is shown in the site's time zone
  Given a booking that starts before midnight and ends after it, site time
  When the fleet manager views it
  Then its start and end are shown in the site's time zone, each with its own date

## Notes

- Depends on A-016 (shared drivers) and A-013 (site time zones). S3 and S4 rely on REQ-258 (EP-13).
- "Driver" is the charging driver; the booker may differ (REQ-009). Booking states and reason codes come from EP-01 to
  EP-04 (REQ-053).
- Whether migrated pilot bookings appear among past bookings follows REQ-115 (EP-12); not covered here.
