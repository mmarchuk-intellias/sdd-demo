As a driver, I want my booking confirmation to tell me the grace period and the no-show fee terms, and to cancel
without a fee before the site's cancellation cut-off, so that when a better-paying ride comes in I can give the slot
back without paying for it.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 · happy path · The confirmation states the terms
  Given a site with a configured grace period, no-show fee terms and cancellation cut-off
  When a driver's booking at that site is confirmed
  Then the confirmation tells the driver, in the driver's language, the grace period and the no-show fee terms that apply to the booking, including the cancellation cut-off

Scenario: S2 · happy path · A cancellation before the cut-off carries no fee
  Given a driver has a confirmed booking
  When the driver cancels it before the site's cancellation cut-off
  Then the booking is cancelled
  And no no-show fee line is created for it

Scenario: S3 · failure · A released booking cannot be cancelled to avoid the fee
  Given a booking was released as a no-show
  When the driver tries to cancel it
  Then the booking stays a no-show
  And its no-show fee line is unchanged

Scenario: S4 · failure · A cancellation after the cut-off is not exempt under this rule
  Given a confirmed booking whose site's cancellation cut-off has passed
  When the driver cancels it
  Then the booking is cancelled
  And the booking's history records that the cancellation came after the cut-off

Scenario: S5 · edge case · Booker and charging driver differ
  Given the booker and the charging driver of a booking are different people
  When the booking is confirmed
  Then each of them gets the grace period and the no-show fee terms in their own language

## Notes

- Q-042 (the cancellation cut-off) is covered by A-049: each site's cut-off is a per-site value that defaults to the
  slot start; a cancellation after it is recorded as a late cancellation and goes to ops as a fee decision, with no
  fee line created automatically. S4 records it only, which holds under A-049 (constitution R6).
- Q-002: who pays and what else the driver sees is undecided; the confirmation states the terms configured for the
  site, which holds in every option.
- S1 reads the cut-off as part of the "no-show fee terms" of REQ-172: it decides whether a cancellation carries a fee.
  Confirmed in the Step 3.1 red-team pass. One-tap cancel is REQ-039 and the SMS
  keyword is REQ-041 (FT-01.2); both confirmations come from REQ-088 (FT-06.4).
