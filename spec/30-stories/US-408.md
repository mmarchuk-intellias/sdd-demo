# US-408 · Each booking invoiced once across the cut-over

As a fleet manager, I want each booking around the cut-over invoiced exactly once, by the pilot process if its slot
started before the cut-over and by the platform otherwise, so that I am never charged twice for one booking during the
switch and can pay the last pilot invoice and the first platform invoice without reconciling them against each other.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) Bookings on either side of the cut-over
  Given a fleet with billable bookings whose slots started before the cut-over and billable bookings whose slots started after it
  When the pilot process invoices its last period and the platform produces the fleet's first monthly invoice
  Then each billable booking appears on exactly one of the two invoices
  And the bookings whose slots started before the cut-over are on the pilot invoice only

Scenario: S2 (failure) Migrated pilot history is not invoiced again
  Given the migrated pilot history holds bookings already invoiced in the pilot
  When the platform produces any fleet invoice
  Then no booking whose slot started before the cut-over is on it

Scenario: S3 (failure) A booking taken in the pilot for a slot after the cut-over
  Given a pilot booking carried over at cut-over (JS-407) whose slot started after the cut-over
  When both invoices for the fleet are issued
  Then the booking is on the platform's invoice
  And it is not on the pilot invoice

Scenario: S4 (edge case) A slot that spans the cut-over
  Given a booking whose slot started before the cut-over and ended after it
  When the platform produces the fleet's first monthly invoice
  Then the booking is not on it
  And it is invoiced by the pilot process only

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A carried-over booking that is later rebooked
  Given a pilot booking carried over at cut-over that is later rebooked to another slot after the cut-over
  When the platform produces the fleet's invoice
  Then the booking appears on it once

Scenario: S6 (edge case) A slot that starts exactly at the cut-over
  Given a billable booking whose slot starts at the cut-over instant
  When both invoices for the fleet are issued
  Then the booking is on the platform's invoice
  And it is not on the pilot invoice

## Notes

- "Billable" is whatever the billing unit is: today $2 per completed booking (CTX-051), but the definition of a
  completed booking is a client decision (Q-017). The rule here holds under every definition; no story assumes one.
- The monthly invoice itself is REQ-083 and REQ-084 (EP-10). The pilot process is Stripe Billing, run by Amperlane
  outside the platform (SRC-C2@00:03:28).
- The cut-over instant (it moves with CF-027), and which process invoices bookings run in the platform during the
  pre-launch period of real bookings (REQ-269) whose slots start before the cut-over, are design details for Step 3.3.
  What is invoiced from launch while Q-001 is open is Q-053; the rule here holds under any answer.
- Red team: S6 fixes the boundary: "started before the cut-over" (REQ-232) excludes a slot that starts at the instant
  itself, so both processes cannot claim it and neither can leave it out.
