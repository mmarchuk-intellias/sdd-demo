# JS-322 · Invoices survive payment-provider outages, retries and lost webhooks

When the payment provider is down, times out or loses a webhook while invoices are being submitted or paid, I want
bookings and fee lines still recorded, each pending invoice retried until it is accepted but never created twice, and
missed payment updates caught up, so I can trust that each fleet gets exactly one invoice a month and that each
invoice's status matches the provider's.

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: S1 (happy path) An outage at month close
  Given the payment provider is unavailable when month close submits the fleet invoices
  When bookings, arrivals and no-show fees keep happening
  Then they are recorded as usual
  And each pending invoice submission is retried at the configured interval until the provider accepts it

Scenario: S2 (failure) A submission times out after the provider created the invoice
  Given an invoice submission that the provider accepted but whose response never reached the platform
  When the platform retries the submission
  Then the retry carries the same idempotency key as the first attempt
  And the provider holds one invoice for that fleet and month, which is the one the platform records

Scenario: S3 (failure) A lost payment webhook
  Given a fleet paid its invoice and the provider's payment webhook never reached the platform
  When the scheduled reconciliation check runs
  Then the invoice is shown as paid
  And it was not shown in the wrong state for longer than one check interval

Scenario: S4 (failure) A duplicated or out-of-order webhook
  Given the provider sends the same payment event twice, or a later event before an earlier one
  When the platform processes them
  Then the payment is recorded once
  And the invoice ends in the state the provider holds

Scenario: S5 (edge case) A retry across a platform restart
  Given a pending invoice submission when the platform is restarted in its maintenance window
  When the platform is back
  Then the submission is retried
  And the fleet still gets exactly one invoice for the month

## Notes

- Depends on A-007 (Stripe behind an interface) and A-035 (idempotency keys kept at least 7 days). Constitution N8, N9
  and N5 apply.
- Payouts to station operators exist only in some business models (Q-001); the parts of REQ-244 and REQ-110 about
  payouts apply only if the client chooses such a model, and are not tested here.
- Red team: an outage longer than the key retention of A-035 must still not create a second invoice; the key's lifetime
  is settled in Step 3.3. The check interval is a configured value.
