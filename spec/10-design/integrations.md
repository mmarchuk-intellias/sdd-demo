# Integrations inventory, v1

Step 1b (prompt P1.3), revised after Steps 3 and 4 (playbook §10.1, CR-012). The **facts** about what exists between
Amperlane and each system are those of v0: no new source has arrived since Step 1. v1 adds:
- what the approved requirements, system stories and tasks do with each system (section "MVP use");
- the platform services the system stories and tasks rely on (I-20 – I-30);
- the questions, assumptions and conflicts registered since Step 1;
- the capabilities the system stories assume (U-15 – U-24), and the fallback each assumed capability now has.

**Status** means what exists today between Amperlane and the system: *in use* · *access granted* · *promised* ·
*proposed* (Intellias' idea, not agreed) · *agreed* · *not available*. **Risk** is H/M/L with the reason.
"Unconfirmed" means no source shows that the capability works for Amperlane. KYIV values are provisional (Gate 2,
CR-011).
