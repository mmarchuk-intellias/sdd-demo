# Open questions and assumptions

Unknowns go here; nothing below is a fact. `Q-###` is a question someone must answer, and `A-###` is a working
assumption we proceed on until it is confirmed or replaced. Where a question repeats one already sent to the client,
the "Asked" column gives where (SRC-C1@00:47:44 list, SRC-C2@00:56:45 list, SRC-D2#18).

The business-model workshop was requested for 2026-10-27 (SRC-R1#c1). It did not take place that day (Gate 1 review,
2026-10-27), and no new date is set; see CF-008.

No client answers are expected before Step 2 (Gate 1 review, 2026-10-28). Two rules apply until they arrive:

- **Product and technical questions** get an Intellias working assumption (A-017 onwards), marked as proposed. The
  person in "Validate by" confirms it when the client is next consulted; the question stays open.
- **Client business decisions** (Q-001, Q-002, Q-003, Q-021, Q-025, the SOC 2 date in Q-018, and, since the second
  Gate 1 review, Q-017, Q-023, the March integration scope in Q-036 and CF-025, and the back-end operator question
  in Q-010) are not assumed.
  Step 2 puts the requirements that hold in every option into the baseline, and the option-specific ones into the
  parking lot, linked to the question and priced as options in the proposal. Answers that arrive after Gate 2 enter
  through a change request.

A second, independent review at Gate 1 (2026-10-28) revised A-017 – A-026, A-028 and A-030, withdrew A-027 and
added Q-038.
